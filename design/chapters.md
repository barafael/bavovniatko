# Chapters

Status: the slate is being chosen (2026-10-01). The principles are settled. More than 13 chapters is fine.

## Principles

1. **Each chapter is a diorama.** It is one bounded historical episode: a compact map, a fixed time window and
   a few iconic set pieces you can take in at a glance.
2. **The world runs by itself.** If the player does nothing, the chapter still unfolds along its historical
   course. Both sides' AI and the scripted historical events (arrivals, cut-offs, weather, orders from above)
   drive it, and player decisions matter at the margin.
3. **Winning means reproducing history.** The win condition is reaching the outcome that actually happened.
   For Ukrainian defeats that means the historical result, not holding the ground: for example, Azovstal holds
   until the negotiated surrender in mid-May, and Bakhmut costs Russia months and its assault reserves before
   the withdrawal.
   - **Contested outcomes.** Where the historical outcome itself is disputed (Spiderweb's damage, Vivaldi's area),
     the win band spans the credible range, and the debrief shows who claims what.

**Vignettes.** These are short micro-dioramas (minutes, not hours) for single famous encounters that are too small
for a chapter: a tank duel, an ambush. They can sit between chapters or inside one as a set piece.

## Spiderweb: how it plays

It is the war's purest covert operation, so it plays as two very different halves.

1. **The long game** (18 months, compressed). It looks like a logistics board across Russia:
   - Drones and wooden mobile cabins move through a front company, carried by drivers who don't know the cargo.
     Routes, timing and cover stories all have to be chosen.
   - A **detection meter** rises with every risk: rushed routes, an extra truck, a careless contact.
   - Left alone, the operation follows its historical plan and schedule.
   - The player's lever is the trade-off between speed, scale and risk.
2. **The strike** (one morning). It shows split-screen dioramas of the five airfields (Olenya, Belaya, Dyagilevo,
   Ivanovo, Ukrainka):
   - The cabin roofs open, and FPVs fly over Russian mobile networks from pilots in Ukraine.
   - The player assigns operators and targets (Tu-95, Tu-22M3, Tu-160, A-50) against link latency and the time
     before Russian guards react.
3. **Debrief.** It shows the claims side by side, with the win band set accordingly: the SBU's 41 hit, about 20 hit
   and 10 destroyed by a US estimate, and 12–13 destroyed by independent counts.

Corrections from the research:
- **Snake Island:** the "13 dead" defenders were a myth. About 80 were captured.
- **Mariupol air bridge:** Time's "82 km from Dnipro" is wrong; the distance is about 233 km.
- **Avdiivka pipeline infiltration:** it was in mid-to-late January 2024, not February.
- **Border Patriot ambush:** the documented one was over Bryansk on 13 May 2023. No source was found for a
  May 2024 ambush on Su-34s or Ka-52s.
- **Bradleys at Avdiivka:** the 3rd Assault and 67th Brigades also used Bradleys there, so the 47th was not the
  only user.
