# The Brovary ambush (vignette)

Evening of 9 March and 10 March 2022. The M01 Kyiv–Chernihiv highway at Skybyn and Kalynivka, on the north-eastern edge of Brovary, about 26 km from central Kyiv. A set piece inside chapter 1, the march on Kyiv.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

In early March 2022 the Russian 90th Guards Tank Division pushed down from southern Chernihiv Oblast. It took Bohdanivka, Zalissia and Velyka Dymerka, and on the evening of 9 March a column of its 6th Tank Regiment drove south-west down the M01 into Skybyn [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. The 72nd Mechanised Brigade had prepared ambushes and pre-planned artillery and mortar targets along the road. Its 1st and 3rd Mechanised Battalions were there, the 3rd under Maj. Roman Darmohrai, alongside Azov and Territorial Defence units [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary] [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov]. The ambushers fired anti-tank missiles at the lead vehicle and the trail vehicle, then artillery fell on the vehicles bunched on the road, where they had left the open fields for a stretch of highway lined with houses [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]. A Ukrainian drone filmed it. The clip shows many armoured vehicles driving away apparently unharmed while others burn, and a TOS-1 moving among the exploding tanks [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]. Its voice-over is a Russian officer's report: "Lots of losses. They waited for us. The Head of the convoy got into the ambush. Regiment commander killed in action" [src:interestingengineering-2022-brovary-convoy].

The trap was only partly closed, because many vehicles escaped down the four-lane road [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Ukrainian intelligence (HUR) claimed that the regiment's commander, Col. Andrei Zakharov, had been killed [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov]. ISW judged that the column had been "badly damaged", disrupting Russian efforts to encircle or assault Kyiv from the east [src:isw-2022-03-10-roca].

**The outcome the player must reproduce:**
- The Russian column is stopped at Skybyn and Kalynivka and pulls back. Brovary is not entered.
- About **19 Russian armoured vehicles are knocked out**, and 49 others withdraw. Russian losses also include 3 IFVs, 1 BTR-82A and up to 55 soldiers, among them the regimental commander. Ukraine loses 1 killed and 5 wounded. These are the battalion commander's figures, relayed by [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].
- Several abandoned tanks in working order are towed or driven off by Ukrainians [src:rferl-2022-brovary-tank-convoy] [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
- **Contested points.**
  - *Losses:* HUR gave no numbers. A Ukrainian eyewitness called the strike a partial success, since many vehicles drove away [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
  - *The date:* "the evening of 9 March" [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]; "Wednesday", which was 9 March [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]; 10 March [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]. ISW covers Russian losses at Brovary over 9–10 March [src:isw-2022-03-10-roca].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Map box.** About 4 × 4 km on the M01 between Kalynivka (50.564, 30.823) and the northern exit of Skybyn (50.581, 30.832, the OSM village centre) [src:osm-2026-brovary-geodata].
- *The road:* in OSM the M01 (E95/E101) is a dual carriageway, two lanes each way, signed as Kyivska Street. It passes about 180 m from Skybyn's centre point [src:osm-2026-brovary-geodata], matching MWI's "four-lane highway", which gave room to escape [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- *Distances:* Skybyn is 8.3 km north-east of central Brovary and 26 km from central Kyiv. Zalissia, on the approach, lies 6.5 km to the north-east [src:osm-2026-brovary-geodata]. One report puts the ambush "about 22 miles (35 km)" from central Kyiv, which is probably a road distance or an error [src:interestingengineering-2022-brovary-convoy].
- *Approach:* open fields give way to a stretch of highway between two rows of houses. That is where the Ukrainians struck [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
- *The wider ground:* the east side of Kyiv has no water obstacle like the Irpin. Its high-speed routes are the P69, M01 and M03 [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].

**Weather and light** (ERA5 reanalysis, a model, not observations [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-brovary-mar-2022]).
- *9 Mar:* overcast, about −4 to +1 °C, with 0.5 mm of precipitation, a light snow shower around dusk and clearing skies by night.
- *10 Mar:* clearing to cloudless by midday, −7 to −2 °C. A thin snow cover of about 3 cm on both days.
- *Light:* sunrise about 06:23 and sunset about 17:50 local time (EET, UTC+2). An "evening" ambush on 9 March therefore fell around or after dusk.

## 4. Forces

**Russia.**
- *Formation:* 90th Guards Tank Division (Central MD), with the 6th Guards Tank Regiment (Chebarkul, CO Col. Andrei Zakharov) and the 239th Tank Regiment [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]. Two of the division's BTGs fought near Brovary [src:wikipedia-2026-90th-guards-tank-division].
- *Column:* tanks moving slowly without infantry support, mixed with self-propelled artillery and a TOS-1 [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary] [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]. One blog counts "over 20" tanks and APCs in the drone clip [src:dronedj-2022-crumley-brovary].
- *Tank model:* the battalion commander, relayed via Wikipedia, says mostly T-72B3 [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]. The Washington Post, also via Wikipedia, says older T-72s [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]. This is unresolved.
- *Total:* the column was about 68 vehicles if one adds the commander's 19 knocked out to the 49 that withdrew [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].

**Ukraine.**
- *72nd Mechanised Brigade:* its 1st and 3rd Mechanised Battalions. The 3rd, under Maj. Roman Darmohrai, had 32 BMP-2s, a 120 mm mortar battery, DShKs, NLAW and Javelin, plus two Corsar and two Stuhna ATGM teams [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]. Former MP Tetiana Chornovol, a Stuhna operator in the 72nd, took part [src:wikipedia-2026-tetiana-chornovol].
- *72nd's artillery group:* mortar batteries and four artillery battalions with 2S1, 2S3, BM-21 Grad and MT-12 guns, which kept firing and moving to avoid counter-battery fire [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- *Others credited:* Azov (the SSO unit "Azov-Kyiv") and National Corps [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov]; the 49th Infantry Battalion, Territorial Defence, the 128th TDF Battalion and SSO [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary] [src:lb-2022-kyiv-region-defense-forces].
- *Wider context:* RUSI credits massed fire from two Ukrainian artillery brigades, more than ATGMs, with stopping the thrust on Kyiv [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Head-and-tail ambush.** The ambushers hit the first and last vehicles to trap the rest [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]. Darmohrai describes layered ambushes, with firepower building as the enemy pushes deeper (relayed by [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]).
- **Pre-planned indirect fire on a road choke.** Artillery from farther back, anti-tank missiles from close range, "pretty much everything they had" [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
- **Drone overwatch.** A Ukrainian drone watched and filmed the strike [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks]. Around Kyiv, civilian drone operators were tied to the Delta centre with named areas of interest from the first or second week [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Delta itself began as an Aerorozvidka project [src:csis-2024-bondar-delta-cjadc2]. The sources here do not say whose drone filmed the Brovary clip or whether it adjusted fire (see section 11).
- **Russian errors.** Artillery travelled in front of the tanks, there was no infantry screen, and the column bunched in a built-up stretch [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].
- **Russian counters.** A four-lane road let much of the column escape [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The column enters Skybyn at dusk.** Tanks and self-propelled artillery, a TOS-1 among them, move without infantry down the M01 into the village [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
2. **The trap.** An ATGM hits the lead vehicle and another the tail, then shells "rain down" on the stalled column [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
3. **Breakout and retreat.** Many vehicles reverse and escape along the four-lane road, while others burn [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
4. **Trophies.** Ukrainian soldiers start up an abandoned Russian tank and drive it away, filmed by a Reuters videographer [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].

## 7. Decision points

- **Ambush in depth or a linear defence.** The 3rd Battalion's commander chose ambushes and fire positions in depth, ahead of the villages, over a linear line (relayed by [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary]).
- **When to spring it.** The ambushers aimed to fire on the first and last vehicles. Because the tail was not sealed, many vehicles escaped, and the result was only a partial success [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].
- **Russian column order.** Putting self-propelled artillery up front without infantry baffled the defenders. "The whole scenario was weird," said Private Berezenko [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- *The radio intercept:* no Russian official account of the ambush appears in these sources. The voice-over on the released clip, said to be a Russian officer, reports: "Sixth regiment lost… Lots of losses. They waited for us… Regiment commander killed in action." He guesses that Bayraktar drones were involved [src:interestingengineering-2022-brovary-convoy].
- *Honours:* in 2018 the regiment was named the 6th Guards "Lvov" Tank Regiment [src:wikipedia-2026-90th-guards-tank-division].
- *Zakharov's death:* reported as a Ukrainian intelligence claim and not independently confirmed in these sources [src:isw-2022-03-10-roca].
- *Russia's claimed hit:* on 12 March Russia claimed a precision strike on a Ukrainian radio-intelligence centre in Brovary [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].
