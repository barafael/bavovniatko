# The march on Kyiv: Hostomel, the convoy and the Irpin line
24 Feb – 6 Apr 2022 (epilogue to 2023): Kyiv Oblast north and west of the capital: Antonov airport at Hostomel, Bucha, Irpin and Moshchun on the Irpin River, the convoy road from Prybirsk to Hostomel, and the eastern approaches at Brovary, with Chernihiv as context.

Accessed 2026-10-01. New sources are in `db/staging/sources/c01-march-on-kyiv.yaml`, cited inline by source id in square brackets. The player side is Ukraine. Russian claims are given as Ukrainian and Western outlets report them. The Brovary ambush is a separate vignette (`research/chapters/v01-brovary-ambush.md`), and the air war over Kyiv is a vignette set inside this chapter (sections 5 and 6).

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Russia's plan was a coup de main. Intelligence operatives inside Kyiv would seize government buildings. The VDV would take Antonov airport at Hostomel for an airbridge, and mechanised forces would advance on both banks of the Dnipro, with a new regime installed within two or three days [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Most units carried 3–4 days of supply [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].

**Hostomel.** On 24 February about 34 helicopters landed 200–300 VDV at Hostomel. Fewer than 200 National Guard conscripts held them for more than an hour before withdrawing. The follow-on Il-76 airlift was aborted in mid-air [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river]. A Ukrainian counterattack at sunset retook the field by 21:00 and cratered the runway. The Russians had the airport back on 25 February, but only when the mechanised column from Belarus arrived [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:isw-2022-02-25-roca].

**The convoy and the Irpin line.** Behind the airport the column stretched more than 40 miles (64 km) back towards Prybirsk and stalled. Fuel, food, spare parts, mud, broken-down vehicles and Ukrainian ambushes are all blamed [src:independent-2022-james-convoy-40-miles] [src:cnn-2022-mcgee-convoy-stalled] [src:bbc-2022-why-convoy-stopped]. Ukrainian engineers blew most bridges on the western outskirts by 27 February. On 25 or 26 February a dam breach at Kozarovychi flooded the Irpin floodplain [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:wikipedia-2026-kozarovychi-dam]. That left Russia to attack through Bucha, Irpin and above all Moshchun, where an understrength battalion of the 72nd Mechanised Brigade held the river line [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].

**The east and the turn.** East of the Dnipro the Russian columns were stopped at Chernihiv and at Brovary [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. ISW judged on 19 March that the campaign to seize the capital had failed [src:isw-2022-03-19-roca]. On 29 March Russia announced in Istanbul that it would "drastically reduce" activity around Kyiv and Chernihiv [src:euromaidan-2022-day-35-kyiv-attacks]. On 2 April Deputy Defence Minister Maliar declared the whole Kyiv region liberated [src:aljazeera-2022-kyiv-region-retaken] [src:cnn-2022-murphy-antonov-withdrawal-maxar].

**Epilogue.** The withdrawal revealed the killings in Bucha [src:hrw-2022-trail-of-death-bucha] [src:bbc-2022-bucha-satellite-image].

**The outcome the player must reproduce:**
- **Kyiv is never entered.** Russia takes no ground inside the city limits [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. The Irpin line holds from Stoianka to Demydiv. The Russians reach Bucha, Hostomel, Vorzel, part of Irpin and the north-western half of Moshchun, but never cross in force east of the river south of Moshchun [src:wikipedia-2026-northern-front] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-irpin] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Hostomel: lost, retaken, lost.** The airport falls about 13:00 on 24 Feb, is retaken by 21:00, and is lost for good on 25 Feb. It is denied as an airbridge: no Il-76 lands that day, and the runway is cratered [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:isw-2022-02-25-roca]. Russian helicopter losses in the assault are **contested**:
  - 2 shot down in the first wave [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons];
  - at least 4 of 34 [src:forbes-2022-axe-mig-pilots-kyiv-day-one];
  - 6–7 of 34 [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv];
  - about 17%, with Oryx confirming 6 [src:pravda-2026-dotsenko-helicopters-drones].
- **The An-225 Mriya is destroyed** in its hangar. Ukroboronprom announced it on 27 Feb [src:ukroboronprom-2022-an225-destroyed]. Who fired the fatal rounds is not established [src:kyivindependent-2022-ponomarenko-an225-chief-pilot].
- **The convoy stalls.** Roughly 64 km long from 28 Feb, it makes "little discernible progress" for days and has dispersed into forests and firing positions by 10 Mar [src:independent-2022-james-convoy-40-miles] [src:cnn-2022-mcgee-convoy-stalled] [src:aljazeera-2022-convoy-dispersed].
- **The milestones on the Irpin line:**
  - Moshchun is held, then cleared 19–21 Mar [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv];
  - Makariv is retaken 22 Mar [src:pravda-2022-kalatur-makariv-deoccupied];
  - Irpin is liberated 28 Mar [src:pravda-2022-petrenko-irpin-liberated];
  - Bucha is free 31 Mar [src:pravda-2022-tyshchenko-bucha-liberated];
  - the Russians are out of Antonov airport by 31 Mar (Maxar) [src:cnn-2022-murphy-antonov-withdrawal-maxar].
- **The Kyiv region is declared free on 2 Apr.** More than 30 towns and villages are retaken [src:aljazeera-2022-kyiv-region-retaken]. The end of the withdrawal from Kyiv Oblast is **contested**: 2 Apr (MoD) or 6 Apr (MWI) [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. ISW, 3 Apr: "Ukraine has won the Battle of Kyiv" [src:isw-2022-04-03-roca].
- **Losses.** MWI says no reliable estimates exist for the Battle of Kyiv [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Ukrainian losses for the northern front are given as 530+ killed, as an aggregated low-confidence floor [src:wikipedia-2026-northern-front].
- **Bucha's dead: contested.** The UN (OHCHR) documented at least 73 unlawful killings [src:wikipedia-2026-bucha-massacre]. Local officials count 458 dead in the Bucha community [src:babel-2022-andreikovets-bucha-458]. HRW documented 16 cases itself [src:hrw-2022-trail-of-death-bucha].
- **Win band.** Kyiv untouched, the Irpin line held, and the Russian withdrawal complete by 2–6 April, with the convoy never re-forming as a striking column. A Ukrainian "victory" that retakes Hostomel for good on 24 Feb or destroys the convoy outright is outside the historical record.

**Distances quoted for the airport.** They vary with the reference point: "about 12 miles" (WOTR) [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel], 18 mi (CNN) [src:cnn-2022-murphy-antonov-withdrawal-maxar], 20 km (ISW) [src:isw-2022-02-24-roca] and 10 km NW of the city edge (BAR) [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].

**The airport.** It has a 3,500 m runway, one large hangar and a separate outdoor shelter for the Mriya, a control tower, and a five-storey office south-east of the strip [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river]. A railway with a single road crossing separates it from the National Guard base, and the five-storey staff flats stand outside the gate [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river]. It was chosen for the runway, its closeness to Kyiv and the E373 highway [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].

**The convoy road.** The convoy ran from Prybirsk through Ivankiv to Hostomel, on the P02 [src:wikipedia-2026-russian-kyiv-convoy] [src:guardian-2022-beaumont-convoy-siege]. Prybirsk to the airport is 46.7 km straight and about 72 km by road (OSRM), so the 64 km figure matches the road length [src:osm-2026-c01-kyiv-geodata]. Vehicles stood two or three abreast in places [src:abc-au-2022-convoy-what-we-know]. Camps sat around Zdvyzhivka, and on 2 Mar forward logistics camps were at Borodianka, Katiuzhanka and Havronshchyna [src:guardian-2022-beaumont-convoy-siege] [src:isw-2022-03-02-roca].

**The Irpin line.**
- *The river:* "only ten to twenty meters wide in many places", hard to ford and easy to pontoon. Irrigation ditches keep vehicles on roads and bridges [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- *The only vehicle crossings, clockwise:* four-lane E40 at Stoianka; two-lane P30 south of Irpin; four-lane E373 between Bucha and Horenka; two-lane P02 south of Demydiv, 3 km above the dam; two single-lane track bridges at Moshchun and Chervone [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. OSM puts the Moshchun bridge 6.4 km from the E373 bridge, against MWI's "six and a half" [src:osm-2026-c01-kyiv-geodata].
- *Moshchun:* a village of 794 people (MWI) or about 1,500 (WaPo), "ringed by a pine forest, a river and a lake", with WWII pillboxes in the woods [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
- *The suburbs:* Irpin had 70,000 people, Bucha 37,000 and Hostomel 17,000 [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. The Bucha River separates Bucha and Irpin, and its last bridge was on Vokzalna Street [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].

**The water.**
- *The dam:* the Kozarovychi dam is 1.4 km long and separates the drained Irpin floodplain from the Kyiv Reservoir, which stood 3 m higher [src:wikipedia-2026-kozarovychi-dam]. The floodplain had been drained in the 1960s [src:guardian-2022-mundy-irpin-hero-river].
- *The flood:* the river would grow from 10–20 m wide into a lake "over four hundred meters wide in some places", and in the end more than 31 billion gallons flowed in [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- *Contested area:* more than 2,800 ha [src:wikipedia-2026-kozarovychi-dam] or 13,000 ha [src:guardian-2022-mundy-irpin-hero-river].
- *Contested rise:* 0.5–0.7 m (RUSI) [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons], 1.5–2 m [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-moshchun] or 2 m [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-battle-of-kyiv-tactics].
- *Demydiv:* about 750 households, roughly 50 of them flooded. The village "never became a front line" [src:nyt-2022-kramer-demydiv-flood].

**Ground.** The Russian maps were old. "Whole towns did not exist when the maps they were using were made" [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. Temperatures hovering just above freezing turned the ground to mud and bogged vehicles down [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. Photos showed vehicles stuck when they left the road [src:abc-au-2022-convoy-little-progress].

**Weather and light.** ERA5 reanalysis at the airport: a model, not observations. Times are local; Ukraine moved to summer time on 27 Mar [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-c01-hostomel].

## 4. Forces

**Russia.**
- **Strength (contested).**
  - About 50,000 against Kyiv in all (MWI). The Northern Front had about 30,000 from the 29th, 35th and 36th Combined Arms Armies of the Eastern Military District, staged in Belarus on the west bank. The Northeastern Front had about 20,000 from the 41st and 2nd CAAs of the Central MD, on the east bank [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - Pavliuk claims about 70,000 men and 7,000 armoured vehicles came from Belarus alone [src:ukrinform-2023-pavliuk-30-sof].
  - RUSI gives a 12:1 ratio on the Gomel axis [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **Plan.** Group "V" formed at Gomel for the right bank, and group "O" at Bryansk to encircle from the left bank, both under the Eastern MD command post [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. Units moved in administrative column, were told to bypass resistance and expected to switch to stabilisation by D+10 [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **Command.** Col. Gen. Aleksandr Chaiko, commander of the Eastern MD [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons] [src:isw-2022-03-27-roca].
- **The air assault.** The 31st Guards Air Assault Brigade and the 45th Guards Spetsnaz Brigade flew from Bolshoy Bokov, about 170 km north [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel]. The 31st likely committed about 2,000 men in 2 BTGs around Kyiv. Mediazona counted 34 of its dead from 25 Feb to 7 Mar, and HUR says up to 50 were killed at Hostomel [src:moscowtimes-2022-beardsworth-31st-brigade]. The airlift on standby was the 76th Guards Air Assault Division at Pskov (18 Il-76) [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel].
- **VDV and motor rifles on the west bank.**
  - The 76th Division's 234th Regiment, the 56th Air Assault Regiment and the 64th Separate Motor Rifle Brigade at Bucha [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-bucha].
  - The 64th (Khabarovsk) deployed about 1,500 and has at least 44 named dead for February–March [src:rferl-2022-krutov-64th-brigade].
  - The 331st Guards Parachute Regiment lost its commander and at least 39 others [src:bbc-2022-urban-331st-regiment].
  - The 106th VDV Division had 2 BTGs withdrawn on 27 Mar (General Staff) [src:isw-2022-03-28-roca].
  - The 37th MRB and 5th Tank Brigade (36th CAA) pushed on Makariv and Yasnohorodka [src:isw-2022-03-09-roca].
  - The 155th and 40th Naval Infantry Brigades came in as replacements by 19 Mar [src:isw-2022-03-19-roca]. Some naval infantry units reportedly lost up to 90% [src:isw-2022-03-20-roca].
  - Rosgvardia OMON/SOBR (Kemerovo) and Chechen units, the latter geolocated at Borodianka [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-irpin] [src:isw-2022-03-24-roca].
  - The 165th Artillery Brigade (35th CAA) [src:isw-2022-03-27-roca].
- **The east bank.** The 90th Guards Tank Division (6th and 239th Tank Regiments) with the 2nd and 41st CAAs, against Brovary and Chernihiv [src:isw-2022-03-10-roca] [src:interfax-2022-hur-brovary-zakharov]. The 41st CAA had about 30,000 at Chernihiv [src:wikipedia-2026-siege-of-chernihiv].
- **The convoy.** An estimated 15,000 troops [src:guardian-2022-beaumont-convoy-siege], with fuel tankers, food and ammunition trucks, towed and self-propelled artillery, BMP-3s and tanks [src:guardian-2022-beaumont-convoy-siege] [src:independent-2022-james-convoy-40-miles]. Pavliuk mentions about 2,000 convoy guards [src:ukrinform-2023-pavliuk-30-sof].
- **Changes over the window.**
  - 27 Feb – 2 Mar: a 72-hour pause, then ad hoc groupings of battalions [src:isw-2022-03-02-roca].
  - 2–5 BTGs committed at a time from 9 Mar [src:isw-2022-03-09-roca].
  - Digging in from 19 Mar [src:isw-2022-03-19-roca], and damaged units rotated to Belarus from 27 Mar [src:isw-2022-03-27-roca].
  - Withdrawal behind a screen of the 38th and 37th MRBs and the 5th Tank Brigade from 1 Apr [src:euromaidan-2022-day-38-kyiv-recaptured].
  - By 4–6 Apr the force was "likely a spent force" [src:isw-2022-04-04-roca] [src:isw-2022-04-06-roca].

**Ukraine.**
- **Strength (contested).** Formal units numbered 3,000–5,000, and the whole force up to 18,000, mostly Territorial Defence (TDF) and volunteers [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Wikipedia's infobox gives 20,000 soldiers and 18,000 irregulars [src:wikipedia-2026-northern-front].
- **The pre-war appreciation.** More than 10 brigades, about half the manoeuvre force, were in Donbas, and Kyiv held one manoeuvre brigade and two artillery brigades [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **Command.** Col. Gen. Oleksandr Syrskyi commanded the defence of Kyiv. The Kyiv Defence Council (Syrskyi, Mayor Klitschko, Lt. Gen. Pavliuk) formed on 24 Feb [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Syrskyi set up two defensive rings, suburbs and city, with sectors under generals from the military schools [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. From 15 Mar Pavliuk headed the Kyiv Regional Military Administration [src:isw-2022-03-16-roca].
- **72nd Mechanised Brigade** (Col. Oleksandr Vdovychenko, from Bila Tserkva about 85 km south) [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - The 1st and 3rd Battalions were on the east side, covering about 30 km [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - The 2nd Battalion was on the Irpin, covering about 22 km, and some companies had only 22 men. Its 4th Company held Bucha–Irpin and the E373, its 5th Company Moshchun–Chervone (fewer than 30 men at first), and its 6th Company Huta-Mezhyhirska–Liutizh [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - Its artillery group had 2S1, 2S3, BM-21 and MT-12 battalions [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **National Guard, 4th Rapid Reaction Brigade** (Maj. Vitalii Rudenko) at Hostomel. The rear echelon left behind numbered about 200 (WOTR), fewer than 200 (BAR) or about 300 (WaPo). They had small arms, a few BTRs, Igla MANPADS, a ZU-23-2 and two D-30s in Horenka [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river] [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
- **Other army units.**
  - The 80th and 95th Air Assault Brigades: the 24 Feb counterattack, Makariv, and reinforcement at Moshchun [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:pravda-2022-romanenko-makariv-liberated] [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-battle-of-kyiv-tactics].
  - The 14th Mechanised Brigade at Makariv [src:pravda-2022-romanenko-makariv-liberated].
  - The 70th Support Brigade on bridge demolition [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-irpin].
  - Training-centre artillery, including 2S7 Pions, moved out of its bases [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
- **Special forces and intelligence.**
  - The SSO's 3rd Special Purpose Regiment [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel]. Pavliuk credits a 30-man SSO unit with disabling the convoy's lead three vehicles [src:ukrinform-2023-pavliuk-30-sof].
  - HUR teams, with the Georgian Legion mostly under HUR at the airport [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv] [src:wikipedia-2026-georgian-legion].
  - The police KORD unit at Huta-Mezhyhirska [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Aerorozvidka.** About 30 special-forces troops and drone operators on quad bikes, by Lt. Col. Yaroslav Honchar's account [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy]. It had 50 R18 octocopter sets by 24 Feb [src:wikipedia-2026-aerorozvidka].
- **Territorial Defence and volunteers.**
  - The Kyiv city 112th TDF Brigade had nine battalions, full within three days and manning hundreds of roadblocks [src:wikipedia-2026-112th-territorial-defense-brigade].
  - Almost 100,000 had volunteered for the TDF by 6 Mar [src:wikipedia-2026-territorial-defence-forces].
  - 25,000 rifles and 10m rounds were handed out in Kyiv [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - The International Legion was created on 27 Feb. Kuleba claimed 20,000+ volunteers, against 1,500–2,000 actual foreign fighters per 2023 investigations [src:wikipedia-2026-international-legion].
- **Air.**
  - The 40th Tactical Aviation Brigade's MiG-29s flew from Vasylkiv, dispersed before the first strike; Ukraine had about 50 MiG-29s and 32 Su-27s in all [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons] [src:forbes-2022-axe-mig-pilots-kyiv-day-one].
  - Su-24 and Su-25 flew strikes [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - "Fewer than a dozen" TB2s were operational [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Chernihiv (context).** The 1st Tank Brigade (Col. Leonid Khoda, about 2,000), the 58th Motorised Brigade and the 27th National Guard Regiment, about 6,000 in all [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv] [src:wikipedia-2026-siege-of-chernihiv].
- **Artillery balance.** Russia had 2,433 barrel systems against Ukraine's 1,176. Ukraine kept artillery parity for about the first 1.5 months, and "blunted Russia's attempt to seize Kyiv using massed fires from two artillery brigades" [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. For a few days the Kyiv forces risked running out of 152 mm ammunition [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Artillery as the decisive arm.**
  - Massed fires from two artillery brigades blunted the drive on Kyiv [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
  - The 72nd's 2S3 batteries rotated between four positions each [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - Artillery fired on the runway at Hostomel [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river] and on pontoon sites plotted in advance [src:nyt-2022-kramer-demydiv-flood].
  - Fire was called in over WhatsApp [src:swj-2022-rice-untold-story-battle-kyiv] or from civilian phone calls: an artillery strike destroyed a refuelling point after a woman phoned it in [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Demolition and water.** Bridges were blown on 25–27 Feb, though few on day one [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Two dam and sluice breaches flooded the floodplain [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. Russian intelligence "did not foresee the use of hydrotechnical structures" [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. The Russians made "a dozen or more wet gap crossings" with pontoons, and most were destroyed by fire [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. RFE/RL says three pontoons at Moshchun, two of them destroyed [src:rferl-2023-dzhulay-furlong-moshchun-dam].
- **Head-and-tail ambushes.**
  - Vokzalna: an ATGM on the lead vehicle, an RPG on the trail vehicle, then artillery for 10–20 minutes on a column in "parade formation" [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - Irpin: NLAWs on the lead and trail vehicles [src:swj-2022-rice-untold-story-battle-kyiv].
  - Brovary (v01) [src:nyt-2022-kramer-brovary-tanks].
  - Javelins at the convoy's head made "a roadblock", according to a US official [src:abc-au-2022-convoy-dispersed].
- **Drones.** Aerorozvidka's night teams carried thermal drones, 1.5 kg bomblets, night-vision goggles, sniper rifles and remote mines [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy]. They launched when Russian jamming was off, ran after launch, prioritised EW vehicles and fed Delta and Starlink to the artillery [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy]. A Furia UAV cued fire on the Rakivka pontoon [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. Russian jamming at Moshchun "made Ukrainian drones inoperable" [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. TB2s hit SAMs, guns and trucks early on, but their emissions made them trackable [src:thenational-2022-harding-drone-strikes].
- **Air defence and the air war (the vignette).**
  - *Opening:* jamming and decoys hit S-300 and Buk hardest "to the north of Kyiv along the Hostomel/Irpin and Chernihiv axes". Fighters carried the air defence for the first days [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. Two SAM sites screening the Dnipro were destroyed, which opened the helicopter route [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
  - *Russian SAM chaos:* Russian SAMs assumed "anything flying was Russian" [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - *Ukrainian SAMs recover:* lone Buk TELARs and surviving S-300s acted as "pop-up" threats and made medium altitude "prohibitively dangerous" from 3 Mar [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - *MANPADS:* the VKS flew at 500 ft or below and lost at least eight jets in a week [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. Stinger and Igla were "a major factor" [src:twz-2022-trevithick-terrible-day].
  - *Russian counters:* Su-35S CAPs with R-77-1s outranged Ukraine's R-27s, at "up to 15:2" odds (6:2 per Zabrodskyi) [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war] [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. S-400s and a Podlet-K1 radar in Belarus forced Ukrainian jets below 100 ft [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. From 9 Mar the VKS switched to night Su-34 sorties, because MANPADS crews lacked night-vision goggles [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - *Deconfliction:* time slots until 3 Mar, then areas after friendly fire [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
- **Russian helicopters.** Ka-52s led the gunships and suffered disproportionately. They often failed to dispense flares when engaged by MANPADS [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
- **Russian logistics.** Columns carried 3–4 days of supply [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] and used Belarusian railways [src:isw-2022-02-27-roca]. They were short of fuel, food and spare parts [src:defenseone-2022-copp-convoy-stalled]. The BBC adds tyres, the mud and C2 failures [src:bbc-2022-why-convoy-stopped]. By 22 Mar some troops had frostbite for lack of winter gear [src:kyivindependent-2022-pentagon-short-food-fuel].
- **Withdrawal technique.** Russia covered the withdrawal with heavy artillery, mined the routes and blew bridges Ukraine had failed to destroy on day one [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv] [src:euromaidan-2022-day-38-kyiv-recaptured].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The helicopter stream.** About 34 helicopters run low up the Dnipro past the Kyiv dam. A Ka-52 and a Mi-24 go down there, then MiG-29s dive in, then the stream reaches Hostomel through low cloud [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:forbes-2022-axe-mig-pilots-kyiv-day-one] [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].
2. **The Igla gunner.** A 25-year-old guardsman lets a strafing Ka-52 pass inside minimum range, re-acquires it and drops it onto the runway as an extra obstacle [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
3. **The sunset counterattack.** Artillery and Su-24 bombs fall on the airfield at dusk. The 4th Rapid Reaction Brigade raises a shrapnel-torn flag by 21:00, then the Ukrainians crater the runway and pull out [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].
4. **The Mriya.** The An-225 burns in its hangar while the Russians hold the airport. It was ready to fly by 22:00 on 23 Feb, but no order came [src:kyivindependent-2022-ponomarenko-an225-chief-pilot] [src:wikipedia-2026-antonov-an-225-mriya].
5. **The 64 km column.** Tankers, trucks and guns stand two or three abreast on the P02, stalled for days. At night quad bikes slip out of the forest near Ivankiv, and drones bomb the lead vehicles and then the depot [src:abc-au-2022-convoy-what-we-know] [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy].
6. **Vokzalna Street.** The Bucha column is trapped "in what would best be described as a parade formation" between two hits and destroyed by artillery [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. A pensioner fires a captured grenade launcher at a fuel truck [src:rferl-2022-levchenko-bucha-pensioner-rpg].
7. **Opening the dam.** Two civilian 4×4s carry nearly a ton of explosives, and the dam goes at 13:30. The river widens into a lake [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
8. **The broken bridge at Romanivka.** Civilians climb over the rubble of the P30 bridge and run about 100 yards in the open under mortar fire [src:nyt-2022-addario-kramer-irpin-family] [src:hrw-2022-irpin-fleeing-civilians].
9. **Moshchun.** Russian pontoons go in at Rakivka. Every tube in range fires on 6 Mar, and on 11 Mar comes the assault to within 10 m of the trenches. Two tanks arrive, and a Russian voice comes over the radio: "We cannot storm" [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
10. **Brovary.** The v01 vignette [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-brovary].
11. **The Ghost of Kyiv** (an air-war vignette). Low-level MiG-29 sorties under Russian CAPs, a Su-27 lost over the city on 25 Feb, and MANPADS teams hunting low jets. The debrief explains the myth [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war] [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-kyiv-2022] [src:kyivpost-2024-shashkova-ghost-of-kyiv-origins].
12. **Epilogue: Bucha.** The convoys roll north into Belarus. On Yablunska Street lie bodies that satellites had photographed on 19 March [src:bbc-2022-bucha-satellite-image] [src:hrw-2022-trail-of-death-bucha].

## 7. Decision points

- **Ukraine: where to put the main effort (pre-war).** Kyiv was treated as economy of force and the northern Russian group as a diversion. The redeployment north came about 7 hours before the invasion [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. Within the 72nd, two battalions went east of the Dnipro because the main thrust was expected via Chernihiv, and the 2nd Battalion stayed at Bila Tserkva so as not to alarm locals [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].
- **Ukraine: disperse before the first strike.** Command posts went to the field a week out, and aircraft and SAMs were dispersed hours before. The Russian opening strikes found about 10% of mobile SAM sites [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv] [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **Russia: an air assault on a position, not an axis.** RUSI calls this "extremely dangerous" [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. The assault had no armour or artillery, and the troops thought they were going to Grodno [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:moscowtimes-2022-beardsworth-31st-brigade].
- **Russia: abort the airlift.** The reason is debated: the slow seizure, the cratered runway, or fear for the transports after the helicopter losses [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel] [src:bar-2024-sladden-collins-connable-irpin-river].
- **Zaluzhnyi: counterattack Hostomel at once**, over Vdovychenko's objection that he lacked the forces [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. Then retake, crater and withdraw rather than hold against the column [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel].
- **Zelensky: stay in Kyiv.** His guards and Arestovych urged him to leave. He argued that flight would collapse the government [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
- **Arm civilians.** Within days officials decided to hand out rifles. Military leaders bristled and later blamed it for friendly fire [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. Panics over "saboteurs" followed in Obolon (25 Feb) and near the zoo (26 Feb) [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-kyiv-2022].
- **Blow the bridges: when and which.** Few went on day one, which let the Russians reach the Irpin quickly. The E373 bridge was half-dropped and mined, and the Moshchun bridge was overlooked on 27 Feb [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Syrskyi: flood the Irpin (25/26 Feb), then enlarge the breach (7–8 Mar).** A civilian proposed it, and Syrskyi "immediately endorsed it" [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. The risk was sweeping away villages downstream, and the first attempt failed [src:wikipedia-2026-kozarovychi-dam]. The flooding funnelled the Russians into Hostomel, Bucha and Irpin [src:nyt-2022-kramer-demydiv-flood].
- **Ukraine: how to hit the convoy.** Air and TB2 strikes against the column's air defences were weighed, and small night teams at its head and depots were chosen [src:thenational-2022-harding-drone-strikes] [src:bbc-2022-why-convoy-stopped] [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy].
- **Moshchun: hold or withdraw** (about 14 Mar; 21 Mar per SWJ). Zaluzhnyi refused the withdrawal. The fix was rotation every three days or less, a new battalion, and fire on the crossings [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv] [src:swj-2022-rice-untold-story-battle-kyiv]. The Ukrainians hesitated to shift forces west after Brovary, and moved only once the east went quiet (counterattack on 19 Mar) [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Russia (Chaiko): break through or withdraw.** He first tried to sever the roads west of Kyiv, took heavy losses under fire from both flanks, and withdrew when the left-bank effort failed [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. Reinforcement by the 5th CAA was "refused" because of losses in the south (General Staff claim) [src:isw-2022-03-16-roca].
- **Russia: reframe and leave** (25 and 29 Mar). Rudskoi's "first stage" claim came first, then the Istanbul pledge [src:isw-2022-03-25-roca] [src:euromaidan-2022-day-35-kyiv-attacks]. The General Staff at first read the pullback as "probably a rotation" [src:euromaidan-2022-day-35-kyiv-attacks].
- **Ukraine: pursue or not.** Gunners first took the covering barrage for a new offensive. By the time they realised, the bridges were down [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **Was Kyiv a feint? (debated).**
  - Putin (2024): "no political decision to storm" the city [src:pravda-2024-kizilov-putin-no-storm].
  - MWI: the plan had a single phase, the rapid seizure of Kyiv [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - ISW, 1 Apr: Kremlin claims that the north-east attacks were only meant to degrade Ukrainian forces are "false" [src:isw-2022-04-01-roca].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Hostomel.** The Russian MoD claimed the capture involved some 200 helicopters and killed about 200 Ukrainians, with no Russian losses. The claim was met with scepticism [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-antonov-airport]. A Ka-52 pilot told Russian TV: "In the first attack, we immediately lost the leader of our group" [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv]. A captured paratrooper said of the 26 Feb barrage: "Almost nobody survived that day" [src:moscowtimes-2022-beardsworth-31st-brigade].
- **The plan.** A captured 1st Guards Tank Army synchronisation matrix expected the "blocking and destruction of individual scattered units" by D+10 [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. Planners assumed Ukraine could generate only about 40,000 more troops [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **The turn.**
  - Kremlin-run media claimed paratroopers crossed the Irpin on 16 Mar; ISW judged it likely false [src:isw-2022-03-16-roca].
  - Rudskoi, 25 Mar: "the main tasks of the first stage of the operation" are complete and the "main goal" is Donbas [src:isw-2022-03-25-roca].
  - Medinsky, 29 Mar: two "de-escalatory steps", including drastically reducing activity towards Kyiv and Chernihiv [src:euromaidan-2022-day-35-kyiv-attacks].
  - Russia presented the drawdown as a goodwill gesture [src:aljazeera-2022-kyiv-region-retaken].
- **Bucha.** Lavrov called it "staged". The MoD claimed the bodies were "moving" and that the mass grave held victims of Ukrainian strikes [src:wikipedia-2026-bucha-massacre]. Satellite imagery from 19 Mar contradicts the claims [src:bbc-2022-bucha-satellite-image]. Putin honoured the 64th Brigade on 18 Apr 2022 [src:rferl-2022-krutov-64th-brigade].
- **Losses as seen from Russia.** The Kostroma VK memorial pages for the 331st Regiment said "The 331st regiment is disappearing" [src:bbc-2022-urban-331st-regiment]. Leaked hospital data show 898 servicemen treated in Gomel and Khoiniki after the march on Kyiv (an RFE/RL investigation, relayed) [src:pravda-2025-kizilov-898-wounded-belarus].
- **Hindsight.** Putin (June 2024): "There was no political decision to storm the three-million-strong city" [src:pravda-2024-kizilov-putin-no-storm].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Three acts on one map.**
  - *The coup de main (24–27 Feb):* Hostomel, the bridges, the dam, Vokzalna.
  - *The siege attempt (28 Feb – 21 Mar):* the convoy, Moshchun, Irpin, Brovary, the air war.
  - *The turn and withdrawal (22 Mar – 6 Apr):* counterattacks, Russian covering fire and mines, Bucha.
  - MWI gives the same phasing [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **The player's levers.**
  - Which bridges to blow, and when: early demolitions deny crossings but strand Ukrainian units [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - Whether to breach the dam, and how much (risk to villages downstream) [src:wikipedia-2026-kozarovychi-dam].
  - Where to put scarce artillery, with 152 mm stocks as a meter that nearly ran dry [src:wapo-2022-sonne-battle-for-kyiv].
  - Night drone-and-quad-bike raids on the convoy's head and depots [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy].
  - When to shift reserves from the east bank to Moshchun [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **The Russian AI.**
  - It is road-bound in mud, with 3–4 days of supply that degrade into fuel and food shortages from 27 Feb [src:isw-2022-02-27-roca] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - It commits 2–5 BTGs at a time and makes repeated pontoon attempts at Rakivka and Moshchun [src:isw-2022-03-09-roca] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
  - It digs in after 19 Mar and withdraws from 29 Mar behind artillery and blown bridges, whatever the player does [src:isw-2022-03-19-roca] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv].
- **The win band.**
  - Kyiv city never entered, and the Irpin line holds from Stoianka to Demydiv.
  - Hostomel is lost on 25 Feb but denied as an airbridge, and the convoy is stalled.
  - The Kyiv region is clear by 2–6 Apr.
  - The Hostomel helicopter tally should land in 2–7 [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons] [src:wotr-2023-collins-kofman-spencer-hostomel]. Moshchun is held at about 118 Ukrainian dead (Ukrainian figure) [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-moshchun].
- **Civilians as a constraint.** Firing near evacuation routes draws Russian fire onto civilians (Romanivka) [src:hrw-2022-irpin-fleeing-civilians]. The Bucha discoveries change the next chapters' politics: giving up settlements became unacceptable [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
- **The air-war vignette.**
  - Phase 1 (24–27 Feb): fighters carry the air defence.
  - Phase 2 (28 Feb – 8 Mar): SAMs recover and the VKS goes low into MANPADS.
  - Phase 3 (from 9 Mar): night Su-34 raids, and Ukrainian jets pinned below 100 ft by the S-400 umbrella.
  - All three phases come from [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. The debrief puts the claims side by side: the "Ghost's" six claimed kills, the SBU's 10, The Times's 40, and the Air Force's "collective image" [src:wikipedia-2026-ghost-of-kyiv] [src:kyivpost-2022-neplii-ghost-of-kyiv].
- **Light and weather.** Dusk at about 17:30 for the 24 Feb counterattack. An unfrozen, wet topsoil until 8 Mar keeps the AI on the roads. The hard frost of 9–12 Mar lets vehicles leave the road briefly [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-c01-hostomel].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Total losses.** No reliable totals for the Battle of Kyiv exist (MWI) [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. No count of vehicles destroyed on the convoy road was found. Oryx (`oryx-russian-equipment-losses`) has no breakdown by road, and its aircraft list gives no places [src:oryx-2022-aircraft-losses-list].
- **No km² figure** for the ground regained in the north was found. Note that the KB source `kyivpost-2022-60-settlements` is about Kherson, not Kyiv.
- **The Kozarovychi blast** is contested on three counts:
  - the date: 25 or 26 Feb;
  - the actor: a volunteer team with an army engineer (MWI), the Armed Forces (Wikipedia), or HUR and "The Diver" (WaPo);
  - the area (2,800 or 13,000 ha) and the rise (0.5 to 2 m).
  The Ukrainian-language sources were not used.
- **Hostomel numbers** are contested:
  - helicopters: 20 in two waves (RUSI), or 34;
  - Il-76s: 18 or 41;
  - troops in the airlift: 1,000 to 5,000;
  - defenders: 200 to 300;
  - border crossing: 08:00 or 09:30.
  Which of Oryx's six airport-perimeter helicopters fell on 24 Feb is unclear [src:oryx-2022-janovsky-hostomel-destination-disaster].
- **Who destroyed the Mriya**, and exactly when between 24 and 27 Feb, is not established [src:kyivindependent-2022-ponomarenko-an225-chief-pilot].
- **Aerorozvidka.**
  - The claim is the commander's own and was not independently verified [src:guardian-2022-borger-aerorozvidka-convoy]. It is undated and located only "near Ivankiv".
  - **The KB claim 05-0009-02 reverses the order.** The Guardian original has the lead vehicles first, then the supply depot. Defense Express's rewrite put the depot first [src:defenseexpress-2022-aerorozvidka-convoy].
  - Pavliuk's "30 SSO soldiers" disabling the lead three vehicles is probably the same action [src:ukrinform-2023-pavliuk-30-sof].
- **The tyre theory** (cheap tyres, poor maintenance) is carried only by the BBC and a Wikipedia relay of Forbes [src:bbc-2022-why-convoy-stopped] [src:wikipedia-2026-russian-kyiv-convoy].
- **Units, unconfirmed.**
  - The 98th VDV Division's presence.
  - The 155th Naval Infantry at Moshchun, which rests on Ukrainian identifications.
  - Which unit died at Vokzalna.
  - The numbers of the "two artillery brigades" in Kyiv and of the TDF brigades other than the 112th.
- **Makariv.** Credit for the 2 Mar recapture is contested: the 14th Mechanised (ISW) or the 95th Air Assault (MWI) [src:isw-2022-03-02-roca] [src:mwi-2025-collins-spencer-battle-of-kyiv]. The town was a "gray zone" again on 25 Mar [src:wikipedia-2026-battle-of-makariv].
- **The Moshchun hold-or-withdraw exchange** is dated about 14 Mar by most sources and 21 Mar by SWJ.
- **Air losses near Kyiv** could not be placed. The Su-27 crash street of 25 Feb and the S-400 attribution for Oksanchenko are unconfirmed.
- **Unfetched sources.**
