# The Kupiansk gas-pipeline infiltration

Late August 2025 – 12 January 2026 (epilogue to September 2026). Kupiansk and the villages north of it on the right (west) bank of the Oskil, Kharkiv Oblast, and the disused Shebelynka–Ostrohozhsk gas main that runs under the river from Lyman Pershyi to Radkivka.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

From late 2024 Russian forces crossed the Oskil by boat near Novomlynsk and Dvorichna, about 20 km north of Kupiansk. By mid-August 2025 they had taken the villages ringing the town from the north (Moskovka, Radkivka, Kindrashivka, Holubivka) [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. In late summer 2025 they found a simpler route: the decommissioned Shebelynka–Ostrohozhsk gas main. It runs a couple of kilometres north of the town, crosses under the Oskil and connects to a compressor station in Kupiansk [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. Troops entered it near Lyman Pershyi on the Russian-held left bank and climbed out through pre-cut openings near Radkivka on the right bank. Some crawled for kilometres inside the 1.2 m pipe and others rode improvised wheeled boards. The walk is about 10 km [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline], and DeepState said the transit took about four days, with rest areas and food stores along the way [src:meduza-2025-crawl-of-duty]. Once out, small groups of 2–6 hid in apartment blocks, scouted, and waited for reinforcements and drone operators. Ukrainska Pravda's sources estimate that 100–250 got into the town at the start [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].

On 20 November 2025 Gerasimov told Putin that Kupiansk had been taken [src:isw-2025-11-21-roca]. By then Ukraine's counter-operation had already been running for weeks. Khartiia's search-and-destroy group and the Kupiansk Tactical Group cleared the forests and villages to the north, then pushed to the Oskil, cut the pipeline route and hunted the infiltrators building by building [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours] [src:isw-2025-12-13-roca]. On 12 December Zelensky filmed himself at the town's entrance sign, and Khartiia announced that about 200 Russians were encircled inside [src:isw-2025-12-12-roca]. On 13 December the 429th "Achilles" regiment released video of the pipeline being blown with about three tonnes of explosives [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed].

**The outcome the player must reproduce:**
- **Kupiansk held and cleared, not lost.** About 90% of the town was retaken by December 2025 [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline]. The Ukrainian flag went up over the City Council on 12 January 2026, and by late February 2026 99% of the town was under Ukrainian control, with up to 10 buildings still uncleared [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Area.** ISW counts 305 km² lost to Russian control in late December 2025 as Ukraine liberated "much of Kupyansk and its surroundings" [src:isw-2026-02-15-roca].
- **The pipeline route closed, and the boat route not closed.** The pipeline was brought "almost fully under control" within the first couple of months, but Ukraine never shut the second route, the boat crossings near Dvorichna, and had to drop a push towards Dvorichna for lack of men [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. A Russian bridgehead on the west bank north of Kupiansk still existed in September 2026 [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment].
- **Losses.** Ukraine claims Russia lost nearly 2,000 soldiers: Khartiia's group killed more than 1,500 and the Kupiansk Tactical Group about 400 [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. The Times put the kill ratio at 1:27 in Ukraine's favour (as relayed by [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]). These are Ukrainian claims.
- **Contested points.**
  - *When the pipeline was cut:* Butusov says Ukrainian forces reached the Oskil and cut the northern supply lines by 21 October 2025, while Khartiia announced the breakthrough to the river on 12 December [src:isw-2025-12-13-roca] [src:isw-2025-12-12-roca].
  - *How many Russians were inside:* about 40 per the Ukrainian military on 21 November [src:wikipedia-2026-kupiansk-offensive]; 100–250 at the start per Ukrainska Pravda's sources [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]; about 200 encircled per Khartiia on 12 December [src:isw-2025-12-12-roca]; about 500 earlier, with about 100 trapped, per the Achilles regiment [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed]; 300 falling to about 50, per a December frontline report [src:euromaidanpress-2025-frontline-kupiansk-pipeline-killzone].

**The pipeline.** It is the Shebelynka–Ostrohozhsk main gas pipeline, a Soviet-built line for gas between Kharkiv Oblast and Russia that was cut off during the 2009 gas war [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. Near Kupiansk four abandoned pipelines merge into two across the Oskil, with a diameter of 1 to 1.2 m [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines]. Lengths vary with the endpoints chosen:
- "a walk of about 10 km in darkness" that exits in a forest [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline];
- "more than 10 km" for the civilians led through it [src:kyivpost-2026-khartiia-pipeline-civilians];
- 15 km from the Russian rear to Ukrainian lines [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines];
- 10.5 km by our own computation (method below).

*Method (computed 2026-10-01; OSM data as of 2026-05-31).* OSM maps an underground gas line (ways 310352107 and 310350544) that passes 0.95 km from Lyman Pershyi, crosses the Oskil at about 49.760, 37.643, 0.8 km north-east of Holubivka, and passes 0.45 km from Radkivka. Measured along the line, the stretch from the point nearest Lyman Pershyi to the point nearest Radkivka is 10.5 km (8.5 km east of the junction and 2.1 km west). OSM labels the eastern segment "Trans-Balkan", which is probably a mis-tag [src:osm-2026-kupiansk-pipeline-geodata]. Inside, the fumes of gas condensate left some men "half-dead" [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. Russian soldiers emerge "intoxicated" from inhaling chemical residue [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines].

**Terrain.**
- *Woods and fields.* Dense woodland around Radkivka and Kindrashivka hid the infiltrators while the trees were in leaf, from late summer into autumn, and was the hardest ground to clear [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. East of Kupiansk the forest-steppe fields are larger and less regular, wooded balkas cut across them, and the shelterbelts run WNW–ESE and NNE–SSW [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample].
- *The town.* Multi-storey blocks and an industrial zone. Russians hid in groups of 2–6, with one or two men on an upper floor to watch and the rest in the basement [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- *Water and bridges.* The pre-war Oskil bridge is destroyed, and Ukrainian aircraft strike Russian pontoons whenever they appear [src:euromaidan-2025-mukhina-oskil-crossing]. Kupiansk is a road and rail hub, with links to Kharkiv and south via Izium to Sloviansk and Kramatorsk [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Pipeline infiltration (RU).**
  - *The cycle:* enter on the left bank, walk or ride for days, exit through cut openings, regroup in forest, then filter into town [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours] [src:meduza-2025-crawl-of-duty].
  - *Disguise:* civilian clothes and false documents, which may amount to perfidy [src:isw-2025-09-15-roca].
  - *Reconnaissance first:* the infiltrators rarely engaged and some wore no body armour. They prepared for a "full takeover" [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
  - *Flag raising for the cameras:* clips that did not change who controlled the ground [src:isw-2025-11-21-roca].
- **Pipeline denial (UA).**
  - Drone surveillance and fire control over the exits, flooding, and filling holes with earth [src:isw-2025-09-14-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines].
  - Every attempt to destroy the pipe during the active phase failed: drones, MLRS, airstrikes, and charges inside it. That left hunting the shifting exits as they were used [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. Then came the demolition with about 3 t of explosives on 13 Dec 2025 [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed].
  - Infantry positions close to the exits rule out artillery, and drones lack the punch for permanent damage [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines].
- **Search-and-destroy clearance (UA).** The town was split into sectors, and radio intercepts betrayed hidden men; one hid in a bath for two days. The Russians mined cleared areas remotely by drone [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Operational security (UA).** Khartiia kept its gains out of the news and mostly off DeepState until 12 December [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **The drone kill zone, and UGVs.** UGV lifespan near Kupiansk fell to 3–4 missions, worn down by mines, FPVs and dedicated hunter crews [src:euromaidanpress-2026-ugv-persian-elephants].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **Men from under the ground.** Achilles footage shows Russians climbing out of pre-cut holes near Radkivka "as if from underground", some of them half-dead from condensate fumes [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. In the game, the pipe's exits are hidden spawn points that move.
2. **The exit kill zone.** A drone spots an emergence, and artillery or bomber drones saturate the hole. Of about 600 who tried in 100 days, eight got through [src:euromaidanpress-2025-frontline-kupiansk-pipeline-killzone] [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
3. **Clearing the forests.** Code 9.2 hits complacent Russians in the Radkivka and Kindrashivka woods. An intercepted voice says: "The khokhly are counter-attacking". The unit claims up to 30 kills on some days [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
4. **Block by block.** Six sectors, men hiding in wardrobes and a bath, and a 127th Brigade soldier, unarmed and in civilian clothes, walking two children and four adults out under drone watch [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
5. **The lie cracks.** Gerasimov's "liberated" (20 Nov) and Putin's Hero star (9 Dec), against Zelensky's video at the entrance sign on 12 Dec [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours] [src:isw-2025-12-12-roca].
6. **Blowing the pipe.** About three tonnes of explosives, 13 Dec 2025 [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed].
7. **The flag on the City Council**, 12 Jan 2026 [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
8. **Coda: the pipe as an escape route** (27 Sep 2026). Russians retreat into the pipe among civilians, and Khartiia holds its fire [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline].

## 7. Decision points

- **Redeploying Khartiia (Jul–Aug 2025).**
  - *Who decided:* Khartiia proposed blocking the infiltration routes, taking the high ground and clearing the town. Syrskyi was "initially sceptical" but agreed [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
  - *The rival account:* Drapatyi's team also asked for authority and set up the Kupiansk Tactical Group. Two accounts compete over who started the operation [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Reporting it as a "police operation".** Khartiia officers say the 10th Army Corps reported a counter-sabotage task so that it would not have to tell the president the town was lost. Others blame a lack of trained infantry. The 10th Corps did not respond [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Silence versus publicity.** Ukraine kept its gains off the news and mostly off DeepState, which let the Russian claim of capture stand until 12 December [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Destroy the pipe, or hunt its exits.**
  - *Attempts to destroy it:* engineers studied the pipe with former local employees, but every attempt failed during the active phase [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. A demolition with about 3 t of explosives was claimed on 13 Dec [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed].
  - *By Sept 2026:* the line was "damaged in sections, not destroyed", because Ukraine does not control the whole route [src:gwara-2026-diachenko-pipeline-damaged].
- **Dvorichna.** An early rush on Dvorichna was abandoned for lack of reinforcements to hold it, and the boat route stayed open [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Do not fire on the civilian column** (27 Sep 2026). Khartiia documented it for a war-crimes case instead [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- *The claim of capture:* Gerasimov said Russian forces had seized all of Kupiansk, and Kuzovlev credited the 68th Division. Putin said Kupiansk was an example Russia would "inevitably" repeat elsewhere [src:isw-2025-11-21-roca]. Putin later said the town had been under full Russian control for weeks (2 Dec), as relayed by [src:wikipedia-2026-kupiansk-offensive].
- *Dissent among the milbloggers:* Kremlin-affiliated bloggers saw no evidence of control south of Svativska Street or in the industrial zone, and one Northern Group blogger called the claim unconfirmed [src:isw-2025-11-21-roca].
- *The deadline slips:* after Zelensky's visit, Kuzovlev pushed the "liberation" back to "January or February" [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- *Celebrating the tactic:* a section of the Sudzha pipe went on show in a 2025 exhibition celebrating that operation [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours]. A Russian blogger admitted lung damage and suffocation in the Sudzha pipe [src:kyivindependent-2025-zadorozhnyy-pipeline-tunnels].
- *Inside the pipes:* an Astra video speaks of "dozens" who suffocated, killed themselves or died in panic in the Kupiansk pipes [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines].
- *Admitting perfidy:* a Russian milblogger acknowledged the use of civilian disguise near Kupiansk [src:isw-2025-09-15-roca].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **The pipe is a hidden, moving spawn.** It is a 10.5 km underground edge with a transit of about four days, its exits shifting [src:meduza-2025-crawl-of-duty] [src:osm-2026-kupiansk-pipeline-geodata]. The player's ISR (drones, intercepts, locals) uncovers exits, and fire control over an exit drives its throughput towards zero. Destroying the pipe should be hard: most attempts failed [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **There are two routes in.** Closing the pipe is not enough, because the Dvorichna boat route must also be policed and, historically, it was never closed [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **The win band.**
  - Kupiansk at least 90% Ukrainian by end-December 2025 and the flag on the council by 12 January [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-kupiansk-pipeline] [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours];
  - north-side villages (Radkivka, Kindrashivka) retaken [src:isw-2025-12-12-roca];
  - about 305 km² swing in late December [src:isw-2026-02-15-roca];
  - the Russian bridgehead at Dvorichna surviving [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment];
  - Russian losses in the low thousands, the Ukrainian claim being about 2,000 [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **The information layer.** Russian claims (the 20 Nov "liberation") and Ukrainian silence are a mechanic. Publicising early helps morale but tips off the enemy [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Urban fog.** Infiltrators sit in groups of 2–6 among civilians and in civilian clothes. Clearing is slow and sector-based, and radio emissions give men away [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours] [src:isw-2025-09-15-roca].
- **Pacing.** Script one tick per day from late Aug 2025 to 12 Jan 2026, with weather events for foliage cover (Sep–Oct), rain (27 Oct) and fog (about 20 Nov) [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours] [src:isw-2025-11-21-roca].
- **Precedent tutorials.** Avdiivka (Jan 2024) and Sudzha (Mar 2025) can teach the same mechanic in miniature [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:twz-2025-newdick-kursk-pipeline].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **The exact exit points** near Radkivka are not published: UP shows only approximate points, and they changed regularly [src:ukrpravda-2026-kyrylenko-kupiansk-is-yours].
- **Length.** The quoted figures (10, more than 10, 15 km) depend on which endpoints are counted. Ours (10.5 km) rests on an OSM line whose naming looks wrong [src:osm-2026-kupiansk-pipeline-geodata].
- **The date of the cut** (21 Oct per Butusov or 12 Dec per Khartiia) and the strength of the Russian force (40 to 500) are unresolved (see section 1).
- **The 305 km²** in ISW's late-December figure may include ground beyond the immediate Kupiansk box. ISW gives no breakdown [src:isw-2026-02-15-roca].
- **The 3 t demolition.** It is unclear whether it destroyed the river crossing or only one section, since a pipe that is "damaged in sections, not destroyed" was still in use in 2026 [src:defenseexpress-2025-achilles-pipeline-destroyed] [src:gwara-2026-diachenko-pipeline-damaged].
- **Russian units that went through the pipe.** None is named in these sources; only "Veterans" specialists appear [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-kupiansk-pipelines].
- **Ukrainian losses** for the operation are not published.
- **Avdiivka pipe dimensions** conflict: 0.8 m and about 2 km in pro-Russian claims [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-avdiivka-pipe], against 3.7 km and up to 1.4 m per United24 [src:united24-2026-kabachynskyi-pipelines].
