# The Kharkiv counteroffensive (the Slobozhanshchyna offensive)
29 Aug – early Oct 2022, focused on the breakthrough of 6–12 Sep 2022. Eastern Kharkiv Oblast: Balakliia → Kupiansk → Izium, then the Oskil river line and Lyman (Donetsk Oblast).

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

On 29 August Ukraine announced an offensive in Kherson Oblast after weeks of public talk about it. Russia had already been moving units south, including artillery of the 1st Guards Tank Army, and the Izium–Balakliia sector was left as an economy-of-force front [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:isw-2022-09-06-roca; src:isw-2022-09-09-roca]. Its defenders were a depleted mix of 11th Army Corps troops, Rosgvardia SOBR units, LNR conscripts and BARS volunteer reservists [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base; src:isw-2022-09-08-roca; src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv].

Colonel General Oleksandr Syrskyi had pitched the attack as a diversion for the Kherson effort. Its idea was a single fast blow north of Balakliia and Izium that bypassed towns and cut roads [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive]. At 03:30 on 6 September, after hours of rocket and artillery fire, the 25th Airborne Brigade advanced about 18 km to the road junction at Volokhiv Yar. Other brigades, including the 92nd Mechanised and the 3rd Tank, encircled Balakliia, which was retaken on 8 September [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive; src:isw-2022-09-08-roca; src:wikipedia-3rd-heavy-mechanized-brigade].

The exploitation echelon drove east along the N26 to Shevchenkove and Kupiansk, the rail hub on the Oskil. The 92nd raised the flag over Kupiansk city hall on 10 September [src:isw-2022-09-07-roca; src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv]. Cut off from the north, the Izium grouping fled rather than withdrew. On 10 September the Russian MoD called the retreat a "regrouping" to Donetsk Oblast, and on 11 September it showed a map conceding all of Kharkiv Oblast west of the Oskil [src:isw-2022-09-10-roca; src:isw-2022-09-11-roca].

Ukraine then halted the main advance on the Oskil, around 11–12 September, and widened the gains piecemeal:
- a Borova bridgehead on 13 September;
- the east bank of Kupiansk on 16 September;
- Kupiansk-Vuzlovyi on 26 September;
- a pincer on Lyman, which fell on 1 October, the day after Putin's annexation ceremony.

By 4 October the Oskil no longer worked as a Russian defensive line [src:isw-2022-09-13-roca; src:isw-2022-09-16-roca; src:pravda-2022-roshchina-kupiansk-vuzlovyi; src:isw-2022-10-01-roca; src:isw-2022-10-04-roca]. The rout pushed Moscow into partial mobilisation on 21 September [src:wikipedia-2022-russian-mobilization; src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**The box.** The playable area runs from about 48.9 to 50.3 N and 36.6 to 38.2 E. It reaches from the Chuhuiv–Balakliia start line to the Oskil and Svatove in the east, and from the Belgorod border at Vovchansk to Lyman and Sviatohirsk in the south (see the table) [src:osm-overpass-2026-c06-places].

**The map's logic is a triangle between two rivers.**
- **The Siverskyi Donets.** It runs NW–SE past Balakliia and Izium to Sviatohirsk and Lyman. Its right bank is high, with chalk cliffs at Sviati Hory. Its left bank is low, with swamps, oxbows and pine forest on sandy terraces, as at Lyman and Kreminna [src:wikipedia-siverskyi-donets; src:kyivindependent-2022-ponomarenko-donbas-woods].
- **The Oskil.** It runs N–S through Kupiansk to join the Donets near Izium, with the Oskil Reservoir between them [src:wikipedia-kupiansk-vuzlovyi; src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv]. Pine massifs line both rivers [src:matsala-2024-war-protective-plantations].
- Bielieskov notes that the Oskil Reservoir covered the Ukrainian flank and ruled out Russian manoeuvre east of Izium [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv]. Novaya Gazeta Europe described the Izium grouping as "stranded between two rivers" once Kupiansk and Kunie were threatened [src:novayagazeta-2022-fedorov-dual-pushback].

- Kupiansk-Vuzlovyi is the junction. It has lines to Kharkiv, Sviatohirsk and Sievierodonetsk, and via Kupiansk to Stary Oskol in Russia; the Belgorod–Vovchansk–Kupiansk line dates from 1901 [src:wikipedia-kupiansk-vuzlovyi].
- Novaya Gazeta Europe names two lines from Kupiansk into Russia, via Vovchansk to Belgorod and via Valuyki. They fed Russian forces at Kharkiv, Sloviansk, Bakhmut and Donetsk [src:novayagazeta-2022-fedorov-dual-pushback].
- After Kupiansk fell, Russia had to route logistics through the Valuyki line [src:isw-2022-09-16-roca]. Syniehubov called Kupiansk-Vuzlovyi "one of the biggest logistical railway routes" in the oblast [src:pravda-2022-roshchina-kupiansk-vuzlovyi].
- Lyman is a rail hub carrying up to 30% of Donetsk railway cargo [src:wikipedia-lyman-ukraine]. Wikipedia lists Balakliia, Shevchenkove, Kupiansk and Izium as the key logistics nodes Russia had held since the spring [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
- Russian Railway Troops rebuild bridges, for example a pontoon rail bridge at Kupiansk [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].

**Bridges and crossings.**
- Russia blew the Donets bridge at Bairak before 30 Aug, and bridges east of Balakliia and over the Serednia Balakliika on 6 Sep [src:isw-2022-09-06-roca].
- Ukrainian strikes left the Kupiansk road bridge over the Oskil unusable by 9 Sep [src:isw-2022-09-09-roca].
- Crossings east of the Oskil were won by fording and pontoon at Borova, Dvorichna and Kupiansk [src:isw-2022-09-13-roca; src:isw-2022-09-19-roca].
- In the south, the 103rd TDF crossed the Donets at Kryva Luka on 3 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-lyman-front].

**Fields.** East of Kupiansk the shelterbelt grid runs WNW–ESE and NNE–SSW: 0.62 km of belt per km², with main belts about 1.1 km apart (measured from OSM) [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample].

**Weather and light** (ERA5 reanalysis, local time) [src:openmeteo-2026-c06-weather]:
- **6–11 Sep at Balakliia and Kupiansk was dry**: 0.0–0.2 mm a day, lows of 5–11 °C and highs of 15–20 °C, with mean cloud cover of 33–77% until it closed in on 10–11 Sep.
- Sunrise was about 05:55 and sunset about 19:05 on 6 Sep. The 03:30 H-hour was in darkness.
- **Rain set in on 12–14 Sep**: 7–14 mm a day at Kupiansk on 12–13 Sep, with 92–100% cloud. This coincided with the halt on the Oskil; no source links the two.
- Around Lyman, 28 Sep – 3 Oct was mild (highs 19–24 °C) and wet: 10 mm on 28 Sep, 8.5 mm on 1 Oct and 13 mm on 2–3 Oct.
- Kyiv Post reported Ukrainian units in the Sviati Hory forests advancing "at night, in fog or rain" [src:kyivpost-2022-korshak-lyman-surrounded].

## 4. Forces

**Ukraine (Kharkiv and Slobozhanske grouping).**
- **Command.** Col Gen Oleksandr Syrskyi, commander of the Ground Forces, planned and ran the operation; Brig Gen Oleksandr Tarnavsky was his deputy. C-in-C Zaluzhnyi was above them [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive; src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
- **Manoeuvre brigades**, with their roles as reported:
  - **25th Airborne**: Volokhiv Yar on day 1, Izium on 10 Sep, then Korovii Yar and into Lyman from the Drobysheve side [src:wikipedia-25th-airborne-brigade].
  - **92nd Mechanised**: from Andriivka to Kupiansk, taken on 10 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
  - **80th Air Assault**: at the Oskil at Senkove on 8 Sep; later Oskil village, Yatskivka, Pisky-Radkivski and Borova [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:wikipedia-80th-air-assault-brigade].
  - **3rd Tank**: Nova Husarivka, Bairak, Shchurivka and Balakliia; elements at Izium per Neil Hauer [src:wikipedia-3rd-heavy-mechanized-brigade].
  - **14th Mechanised**: Ternova on the border on 12 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
  - **81st Airmobile**: Bohorodychne by 11–12 Sep; Novoselivka near Lyman on 28 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:wikipedia-2022-lyman-front].
  - **54th Mechanised**: also listed [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
  - Wikipedia's 4th Guards Tank Division article cites a Ukrainian **4th Tank Brigade** trying to encircle that division outside Izium [src:wikipedia-4th-guards-tank-division].
- **Territorial Defence.**
  - 113th (Shevchenkove, Velykyi Burluk, border near Vovchansk) and 112th (45 km advance to Vovchansk by 12 Sep).
  - 128th, 124th, 107th (93rd Bn at Velyka Komyshuvakha), 127th (north of Kharkiv) and 111th.
  - 103rd, whose battalions worked the Donets and Lyman flank from 3 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
- **Fires.** 26th, 40th, 43rd and 44th Artillery Brigades, the 107th Rocket Artillery Brigade and the 15th Artillery Reconnaissance Brigade, as named by Zelensky on 14 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. Each attacking brigade had at least eight M777s, some delivered the night before H-hour [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Special and security forces.**
  - HUR (Main Intelligence Directorate) [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. Its Kraken unit is reported as key at Izium [src:wikipedia-kraken-regiment].
  - Special forces, including the "Bohun" unit whose press officer was Taras Berezovets [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick].
  - Military-intelligence and SBU units acting as "raid squads" in the exploitation echelon [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv].
  - The Kharkiv special-forces detachment of the Azov Regiment [src:isw-2022-09-12-roca].
  - The National Guard's 15th Regiment and the volunteer battalion "Slobidska Sich" [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:mvs-2022-slobidska-sich-counteroffensive].
- **Strength.** No total is published. WaPo says "thousands" of soldiers were assembled [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive], and Bielieskov says Ukraine "concentrated everything we could" in the Balakliia–Kupiansk–Izium area [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv]. A typical lead company was about 100 men in columns of three IFVs [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Ammunition.** About 32,500 artillery rounds were used in five days, against a feared need of over 100,000 [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].

**Russia (Western Group of Forces, then partly Lapin's command).**
- **Command.**
  - Lt Gen Andrei Sychevoy commanded the western grouping until 26 Aug, then Lt Gen Roman Berdnikov.
  - From about 11 Sep, Col Gen Alexander Lapin (Central MD) took over the western grouping.
  - Western MD commander Zhuravlyov was replaced by Berdnikov, as reported on 3 Oct.
  - [src:isw-2022-09-11-roca; src:isw-2022-10-03-roca]
- **The Izium grouping.**
  - It peaked at 24 BTGs, about 18,000 troops, and had at least halved by August [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive]. Syrskyi gives about 9,000 in about 12 BTGs in August [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. Other estimates are "more than 10,000" near Izium [src:novayagazeta-2022-fedorov-dual-pushback] and 10,000 per David Axe [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
  - Main formations: elements of the 1st Guards Tank Army, namely the 4th Guards Tank Division (not fully reconstituted) and the 2nd Guards MRD, plus the 11th Army Corps and the 64th Motor Rifle Brigade. The 2nd Guards MRD's 1st MRR left fatigue letters dated 30 Aug [src:wikipedia-4th-guards-tank-division; src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:isw-2022-09-19-roca].
- **The Balakliia force.** It was commanded by Col Ivan Popov of the 11th Army Corps (Baltic Fleet), from a bunker in a vehicle-repair complex [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base].
  - Units: the 9th MRR at Hrakove, elements of the 336th Marine Brigade, Luhansk conscripts (one corporal was 64, and some carried Mosin rifles) and the 9th BARS brigade.
  - On 30 Aug it was at 71% strength overall. The 2nd assault battalion had 49 of 240 men and the 9th BARS was at 23%.
  - Its equipment fell from 8 APCs to 3 and from 24 Fagot ATGMs to 4. It had 2 drones left, its only Zoopark counter-battery radar was gone, and two of three jammers were broken.
  - [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base]
- **Rosgvardia.** SOBR and Rosgvardia units held Balakliia and lost it without coordination or adequate artillery [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca; src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. Bielieskov describes a single defensive tier north of Balakliia, held mainly by SOBR teams [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv].
- **The Lyman garrison**, late September:
  - BARS-13 and BARS-16 ("Kuban"), DNR and LNR units, the 752nd MRR and rear troops of the 20th CAA, and the 3rd Guards Spetsnaz Brigade [src:wikipedia-2022-lyman-front; src:kyivpost-2022-korshak-lyman-surrounded].
  - Reinforcements came late: the partly mobilised 503rd MRR (58th CAA) at Torske [src:isw-2022-09-30-roca], and 144th MRD elements on the Oskil and at Svatove [src:isw-2022-09-26-roca].
  - Strength was "up to 5,000" encircled [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive], or about 5,500 per Berezovets [src:kyivpost-2022-korshak-lyman-surrounded].
- **Force ratio.** The only explicit figure is the occupation head Ganchev's "8 times" Ukrainian superiority [src:aljazeera-2022-ganchev-eight-times]. It is an interested excuse, not a count.
- **Context.** Bielieskov put Russia at 200,000–250,000 troops on a front of more than 1,300 km, too few for a tiered defence [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv]. Russia held 25,000 [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive] to 25–30,000 [src:novayagazeta-2022-fedorov-dual-pushback] on the Dnipro right bank.
- **Change over the window.** The units that had left came back as reinforcements by road and by helicopter on 9 Sep [src:isw-2022-09-09-roca]. LNR units withdrawn from Kharkiv Oblast were sent to Donetsk Oblast rather than to the Oskil [src:isw-2022-09-14-roca]. The LNR 206th Regiment was screening the Valuyki rail line inside Russia [src:isw-2022-09-16-roca].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Deep fires on command and logistics before H-hour.**
  - HIMARS and M270 hit Russian depots and command posts as far back as Kupiansk and Kivsharivka in the weeks before the attack [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. The Balakliia papers record HIMARS strikes on command posts from late July [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base].
  - On the night of 5–6 Sep the M270 fire left Russian and proxy units unable to receive orders [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
  - ISW judged that Western systems were "necessary but not sufficient": what made the difference was how they were used inside a well-designed campaign [src:isw-2022-09-11-roca].
  - This is the background picture: after July's HIMARS strikes, Russia could no longer stockpile shells beside its guns [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
- **Targeting.** US geospatial intelligence returned precise locations for target types Ukraine requested. Extra drones were issued to the brigades to save ammunition [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Breach, then a mobile exploitation echelon that bypasses towns.**
  - A combined-arms breach by tank, mechanised and light motorised units under long-range artillery and MLRS, followed by "mobile raiding groups" (Zaluzhnyi) [src:ukrinform-2023-zaluzhnyi-balakliia-anniversary].
  - Syrskyi's rule was to encircle population centres rather than assault them [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive]. Bielieskov describes the "classical" breakthrough development echelon on wheeled armour, cutting roads rather than taking towns [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv].
  - Special forces and light infantry infiltrated at night around Balakliia ahead of the armour [src:kyivpost-2022-korshak-lyman-surrounded].
- **Deception and OPSEC.**
  - The public Kherson announcement [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick; src:isw-2022-09-11-roca].
  - A purge of informants in Ukrainian-held Kharkiv Oblast [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick].
  - Pontoon bridges moved to suggest a direct Izium assault [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
  - The Ukrainian side stayed silent while Russian milbloggers broke the news first [src:isw-2022-09-06-roca].
- **Russian counters, and their absence.**
  - Demolition of bridges, and requests for artillery on abandoned posts [src:isw-2022-09-06-roca; src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base].
  - Reserves rushed in by road and helicopter [src:isw-2022-09-09-roca].
  - Off-the-shelf Mavic drones with uninstalled software, and EW sets out of action [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base].
  - At Lyman the defenders fought: "all the artillery they had" fell on the 25th's company [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **EW.** A Zhitel was destroyed and a Borisoglebsk-2 captured in Kharkiv Oblast in September 2022 [src:militarnyi-2022-kushnikov-zhitel-kharkiv].
- **Captured equipment as a resource.** Ukrainian units reused captured T-72s [src:isw-2022-09-19-roca]. Analysts saw captured ammunition as more valuable than the guns, given Ukraine's shortage of Soviet-calibre shells [src:kyivindependent-2022-kossov-captured-vehicles].
- **Russian reprisal and new weapons.**
  - On 11 Sep, Kalibr strikes on the power grid [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive].
  - On 13 Sep, the first Geran-2 (Shahed-136) fragment, recovered at Kupiansk [src:isw-2022-09-13-roca; src:csis-2026-bondar-shahed-geran].
- **Communications.** A Ukrainian company commander used Starlink in Izium [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **03:30, 6 September: the silent start.** A rendezvous in quiet farmland feels "off" to the troops. Then come hours of M270 fire on headquarters and depots. Columns of three IFVs roll out in the dark, and by evening the lead company is at the ravine town of Volokhiv Yar. One resident had woken to four Russian tanks outside his house, their crews drinking beer in his gazebo and "talking about where to run" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
2. **The Balakliia bunker.** Encirclement rather than assault. Precision strikes hit the HQ, and the whole site "erupted in flames". Wounded Russians limp out along the road, and Luhansk conscripts trail behind the Russian column. The basement is left with burned papers and a notebook: "If you sit and look at the river for long enough, you will eventually see your enemies floating by" [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base]. On 8 Sep the flag goes up on the city council [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca].
3. **The dash down the N26 to Kupiansk.** Shevchenkove is bypassed or taken, Kupiansk is shelled at night, and the Oskil bridge is dropped [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca; src:isw-2022-09-09-roca]. On 10 Sep the 92nd's 1st Battalion poses with the flag on the city-hall roof while Russians flee from the east bank [src:euromaidan-2022-shandra-kharkiv; src:isw-2022-09-10-roca].
4. **Entering Izium.** On day 6 the company is told "we're entering Izium" instead of halting on the ridge. A Z-marked Zhiguli drives into the checkpoint and is hit by an RPG. Inside the city are working tanks, guns ready to fire and fuel tankers "filled up to the eyeballs" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
5. **"Regrouping."** Konashenkov announces a "three-day operation" to transfer the Izium–Balakliia group to Donetsk [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick], while Moscow holds City Day fireworks [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. The next day's MoD map gives up the oblast west of the Oskil [src:isw-2022-09-11-roca].
6. **The border run.** The 112th and 113th TDF and the 14th Mechanised drive north through Velykyi Burluk to Vovchansk and Ternova, and the Belgorod border is closed behind fleeing civilians [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:aljazeera-2022-ganchev-eight-times].
7. **Korovii Yar.** The 25th's company, heading for Lyman, is hit by three Russian tanks in a tree line: 5 killed and 12 wounded. A squad leader with a shattered jaw leads for four more hours. The advance turns "tougher and slower" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
8. **The Lyman pocket.** Ukrainian jaws close from Drobysheve and Stavky in the north-west and Yampil in the south-east. One road to Torske and Kreminna remains, under artillery fire. One night a big column is heard and shelled [src:isw-2022-09-30-roca; src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive]. On 1 Oct, the day after the annexation ceremony, a flag goes up at the town sign [src:isw-2022-10-01-roca].
9. **Trophy: the T-90M.** Russia's newest tank is found intact on 18 Sep [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:kyivindependent-2022-kossov-captured-vehicles].

## 7. Decision points

- **Where to strike (summer 2022).**
  - The General Staff asked for diversions to support Kherson. Syrskyi chose the seam between the Izium stronghold and the Belgorod bases, because the Russians thought "you'd have to be crazy" to strike there [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
  - RUSI judges the choice by three factors: Russian weakness, Izium's role as a springboard for attacks on Donbas, and Russian artillery in range of Kharkiv [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Zelensky approved the plan in mid-August as "a large swath of territory with minimal resources and losses" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **How to fight: bypass and speed over reduction.** Syrskyi: "Everything depended on the first day — how far we could break through." The stated risk was Russian reserves arriving from across the border and cutting off Ukrainians behind the lines [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Stripping other sectors.** Ukraine took M777s and drones from hotter sectors to give each attacking brigade at least eight howitzers, with tens of thousands of rounds against a feared need of over 100,000 [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Was it a surprise? (debated).**
  - RUSI: Russia saw the build-up but had no forces to respond. Ukraine surprised it on timing, not intent [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - The Balakliia papers show Russian officers expected an attack "to take control of Balakliia" from late July [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base]. Milbloggers had warned for days [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca].
  - Syrskyi says the information "didn't reach anyone or it wasn't taken into account" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Exploit into Izium early (about 9–10 Sep).** At battalion level the plan was changed from halting on a ridge north of Izium to entering the city, once intelligence showed "half the units in Izium… are simply fleeing" [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive].
- **Russia: stand or run (9–11 Sep).** Moscow first sent convoys and helicopters [src:isw-2022-09-09-roca], then ordered withdrawal behind the Oskil and called it a regrouping [src:isw-2022-09-10-roca].
  - ISW judged that Russia lacked the reserves to build an Oskil line, and that prudence demanded pulling forces from other sectors to a line further east [src:isw-2022-09-11-roca]. Instead, Lapin kept attacking at Bakhmut [src:isw-2022-09-12-roca].
  - Posen argues the opposite: Russia had deliberately economised in Kharkiv, may have "chosen to accept the loss", and saved most of its units [src:foreignpolicy-2023-posen-breakthrough].
- **When to stop: the Oskil (11–13 Sep).**
  - Ukraine halted the main advance at the Oskil around 11 Sep [src:isw-2022-09-16-roca]. ISW repeatedly said Ukraine could cross "if they choose", because Russia was too weak to hold the whole river [src:isw-2022-09-10-roca; src:isw-2022-09-13-roca].
  - Defence Minister Reznikov stressed the need to secure the retaken ground against a counterattack on stretched supply lines [src:aljazeera-2022-ganchev-eight-times].
  - The chosen course was local bridgeheads (Borova, Kupiansk, Dvorichna) rather than a second leap [src:isw-2022-09-13-roca; src:isw-2022-09-19-roca]. The sources found give no fuller account of the Ukrainian reasoning.
- **Next objective: Lyman.** After a week in Izium, the 25th was redirected south-east to Lyman [src:wapo-2022-khurshudyan-inside-counteroffensive]. The pincer came from Sviatohirsk and the Oskil, and cut the Drobysheve–Torske road rather than storming the town [src:wikipedia-2022-lyman-front; src:isw-2022-09-30-roca].
- **Russia: reinforce Lyman or the south?** ISW assessed that the decision not to reinforce Kupiansk and Lyman was almost certainly Putin's, prioritising Kherson and Zaporizhzhia [src:isw-2022-10-01-roca].
- **Russia: mobilise.** The breakthrough exposed how stretched Russia was, and mobilisation followed on 21 Sep [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons; src:wikipedia-2022-russian-mobilization].
- **The lesson that misled 2023.** RUSI says 2023 planners believed Russia's Kharkiv collapse came from poor morale, when it really came from a shortage of troops [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Silence, then "regrouping".**
  - For days the MoD did not acknowledge the attack. Its only statement was a claimed strike on a "Ukrainian depot" in Balakliia, which admitted the town's loss [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca]. Peskov refused to comment on "the situation around Balakliya" [src:isw-2022-09-09-roca].
  - On 10 Sep the MoD said a "three-day operation" had transferred the Izium–Balakliia group to Donetsk Oblast "to bolster efforts" there, with "a powerful fire defeat" inflicted to protect the withdrawal [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick]. ISW: "These Russian statements have no relation to the situation on the ground" [src:isw-2022-09-10-roca].
- **The Kremlin admits defeat.** On 13 Sep the Kremlin admitted defeat for the first time in the war, deflecting blame from Putin onto advisers and the MoD [src:isw-2022-09-13-roca].
- **Excuses from the occupation administration.** Ganchev claimed eightfold Ukrainian superiority and organised the evacuation of about 5,000 civilians to Russia [src:aljazeera-2022-ganchev-eight-times].
- **Russian sources on who held the line.** Russian sources said the front was held by lightly armed DNR militia [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive]. The Ukrainian expert Kuzan said they were Russian professionals [src:guardian-2022-koshiw-southern-offensive-trick]. Novaya Gazeta Europe (independent) said they were mainly National Guard, plus DNR/LNR residents and volunteers [src:novayagazeta-2022-fedorov-dual-pushback].
- **Milbloggers and politicians.**
  - Some milbloggers said Balakliia was given up "in good order"; others said Ukraine had "completely outplayed" the command [src:isw-2022-09-08-roca].
  - Kadyrov threatened to go to the Kremlin [src:isw-2022-09-11-roca]. Girkin, Kots, Duma deputies and Zyuganov called for mobilisation [src:wikipedia-2022-kharkiv-counteroffensive; src:isw-2022-09-13-roca].
- **After Lyman.** The MoD called it a withdrawal "to more advantageous positions" to avoid encirclement. Kadyrov and Prigozhin attacked Lapin, and Kremlin-aligned outlets then cast Lapin as the saviour of the trapped WMD units [src:isw-2022-10-01-roca; src:isw-2022-10-04-roca].
- **Inside view from the Balakliia papers.** Units were at 20–71% of strength, soldiers asked to be released for "physical and moral fatigue", there was desertion and a self-inflicted wound, and no ammunition or drone resupply arrived in late August [src:reuters-2022-saito-balakliia-base; src:isw-2022-09-19-roca].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **The win band** is reaching the Oskil from Vovchansk to Izium by about day 7 (13 Sep), and holding Kupiansk and Izium. The area band is about 6,000–8,500 km², and the debrief shows ISW's 2,500–3,000, Zelensky's 6,000 and 8,000, and Maliar's 8,500, plus Ukraine's later 12,000 for the whole operation. An optional second act ends with Lyman on 1 Oct and Borova by 3–4 Oct.
- **A front-density model** is the core mechanic. Russia's line is one tier of units at 20–71% strength, Rosgvardia and BARS, with no reserves nearby. Once the line is broken, exploitation is limited by fuel, roads and time to the border reserves, not by enemy combat power. The scripted Russian AI sends reinforcement convoys (9 Sep), then orders a general withdrawal (10–11 Sep).
- **Speed is the main lever.**
  - A "first day" depth score drives the Russian collapse meter, reflecting Syrskyi's "everything depended on the first day".
  - Bypassing towns costs nothing, while assaulting them costs time and casualties.
  - Cutting the N26, E40, R78 and R79 and the Kupiansk rail node isolates Izium.
  - A historical pace of about 15–20 km per day for lead elements is the default.
- **Deception before H-hour.** The player allocates OPSEC and deception (the Kherson noise, moved pontoons, informant sweeps). These lower the Russian readiness value, but per RUSI they buy surprise in timing, not intent: Russia sees the build-up but cannot react.
- **The ammunition budget** is about 32,500 rounds for five days. M777s are drawn from other sectors at the cost of risk elsewhere. Pre-H-hour HIMARS and M270 strikes on command posts degrade Russian command and control.
- **The stop decision.** At the Oskil, rain from 12 Sep and stretched supply lines argue for consolidation and limited bridgeheads. Overextending past the Oskil should raise counterattack risk, though the sources do not say what would have happened.
- **The trophy economy.** Captured tanks, IFVs and shells feed the Ukrainian pool (100+ tanks in three weeks). Russian losses can be counted against Oryx-confirmed numbers.
- **The Lyman act changes the tone.** Defenders fight back (Korovii Yar), and the encirclement mechanic is to cut the last road and shell the egress rather than storm the town.

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Ukrainian losses and strength.** No figures were found for casualties, total troops, brigade strengths or vehicle numbers.
- **Russian strength on 6 Sep.** Only fragments exist: the Balakliia force's percentages, and an Izium grouping of about 9,000–10,000. Total Russian personnel losses exist only as claims.
- **The precise area for 6–12 Sep.** ISW's daily figures stop at "over 3,000 km²". The 6,000 and 8,000 km² figures mix the south and later days. A DeepState-based computation for 6–13 Sep only, inside Kharkiv Oblast, would settle it; it was not run here.
- **Date conflicts.**
  - Balakliia: 8 Sep (ISW) against formal control on 10 Sep (Wikipedia).
  - Izium: 10 Sep (Wikipedia, WaPo) against 11 Sep (ISW geolocation).
  - Kharkiv Oblast "completely liberated" on 4 Oct (NGU), against 6% still occupied on 27 Sep (Syniehubov) and Russian presence east of the Oskil afterwards.
- **The start line.** Pryshyb (Wikipedia, about 9 km NW of Balakliia by OSM, not the 15 km stated) or Andriivka for the 92nd.
- **Unit roles.** The roles of the 3rd and 4th Tank Brigades and of Kraken rest on Wikipedia citing Hauer and Butusov; they need primary confirmation. HUR and SSO actions are almost undocumented in English.
- **Not fetched.**
  - The Forbes (Axe) pieces on 4th GTD tank losses and the "10,000 surrounded" story returned 403, so they are cited only via Wikipedia.
  - The CNN source of the "338" Oryx figure was not fetched.
  - The FT interactive did not render without JavaScript.
- **The Ukrainian reasoning for halting at the Oskil** is not documented in the sources found. Only Reznikov's caution and ISW's "if they choose" are available.
- **Rail detail.** Exact Russian railheads and their daily throughput in Kupiansk-Vuzlovyi and Izium before the attack are unknown.
- **Weather** is ERA5 reanalysis, not station data. There are no sources on fog or visibility for 6 Sep.
