# The defence of Bakhmut

1 Aug 2022 – 20 May 2023 (Wagner's ground assault to Prigozhin's declaration of capture; Soledar, 4–25 Jan 2023, is part of it), Bakhmut and its flanks, Bakhmut raion, northern Donetsk Oblast. Coda: the Wagner handover and the Ukrainian flank counterattacks, 9 May – July 2023. Epilogue: Andriivka and Klishchiivka, 14–17 Sep 2023.

Accessed 2026-10-01. English-language sources, with Ukrainian outlets paired with Western analysis and OSINT. Russian statements appear only as Ukrainian, Western or independent Russian outlets report them. Loss figures are **claims**, and the claimant is always named.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Bakhmut was a salt-and-gypsum city of about 70–75,000 people on the Bakhmutka river. It lay at the junction of the roads from Sloviansk (M03), Siversk (T0513) and Kostiantynivka (T0504) [src:bbc-2022-bachega-zelensky-visits-bakhmut] [src:bbc-2023-landale-gozzi-bakhmut-deputy-mayor] [src:isw-2023-01-07-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca]. From early July 2022 Russia relied on the Wagner Group to carry the attack on it [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-15-roca].

- **The convict army.** Wagner began recruiting in Russian prisons on 1 July 2022 and enlisted at least 48,000 convicts before the scheme ended in February 2023 [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut]. It threw them at the city's eastern and southern edges from August in small assault groups, with executions for retreat [src:isw-2022-08-01-roca] [src:cnn-2023-lister-wagner-tactics-report].
- **The autumn grind.** Through the autumn the front barely moved: Prigozhin's own figure was 100–200 m a day [src:isw-2023-01-03-roca]. The city became a symbol. Zelensky visited on 20 December and took the defenders' flag to the US Congress the next day [src:president-2022-zelensky-bakhmut-visit-awards] [src:pbs-2022-freking-zelensky-dc-visit].
- **The winter pincers.** In January Wagner, joined by VDV and other regular troops, took Soledar to the north-east (12–16 Jan). It then swung round the city's north (Krasna Hora, Paraskoviivka, Berkhivka, Yahidne) and south (Klishchiivka, Ivanivske) [src:isw-2023-01-13-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-27-roca].
- **The decision to hold.** Ukraine debated withdrawing, and the US advised it [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops]. In early March Ukraine gave up the east bank of the Bakhmutka but chose to reinforce the rest [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
- **The fall of the city.** Wagner took the city block by block: the AZOM plant about 28 March, the city hall on 2 April, about 76% of the city by 11 April [src:isw-2023-03-29-roca] [src:isw-2023-04-03-roca] [src:isw-2023-04-11-roca].
- **The feud.** Prigozhin fought the MoD in public over shells, and on 5 May threatened to leave [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca].
- **The end.** From 9 May Ukrainian brigades counterattacked the weak Russian regulars on the flanks [src:isw-2023-05-10-roca]. Wagner took the last western blocks anyway, and on 20 May Prigozhin declared the city captured [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca]. Wagner handed over to the army from 25 May and mutinied a month later [src:isw-2023-05-25-roca] [src:meduza-2023-prigozhin-aborted-rebellion].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Footprint.** The diorama is a box of about 25 × 25 km centred on Bakhmut. It runs from Soledar and Yakovlivka in the north-east to Kurdiumivka and Andriivka in the south, and from Chasiv Yar in the west to Zaitseve and Kodema in the south-east. The 2022 fighting sits on the eastern and south-eastern approaches. The winter pincers reach about 13 km north (Soledar, Zaliznianske) and about 14 km south-west (Kurdiumivka, Bila Hora). The 2023 flank counterattacks stay within about 10 km of the city [src:isw-2022-08-04-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-08-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca]. ISW counted only about 500 km² gained by Russia in the whole Bakhmut area from 4 July 2022 to mid-February 2023 [src:isw-2023-02-15-roca]. The city itself is about 40 km² by ISW's terrain count: 30.68 km² was 76.5% of it on 11 April 2023 [src:isw-2023-04-11-roca].

**Terrain.**
- **A city in a bowl.** Bakhmut lies low in the Bakhmutka valley (SRTM about 85–110 m). The ground rises west to about 150 m at the western blocks and Ivanivske, and to about 246 m at Chasiv Yar [src:opentopodata-srtm30m]. Klishchiivka's heights, about 154 m, overlook the southern approaches [src:opentopodata-srtm30m]. Meduza's experts describe the Ukrainian garrison as "half-encircled in a city in low ground" [src:meduza-2024-75000-dead]. Kofman and Lee argue that Ukraine "could have held the high ground west of the city with far fewer units" [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance]. In May 2023 Ukraine reported retaking "dominant heights" north and south of the city [src:isw-2023-05-21-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-27-roca].
- **The Bakhmutka.** The river "bisects the city". Locals call the east bank Zabakhmutka [src:pravda-2022-kyrylenko-bridge-bisects-bakhmut]. The UK MoD called the crossing "a killing zone" [src:youtube-2023-thesun-satellite-bridges-bakhmut]. By 22 March some Russians had forded it [src:isw-2023-03-23-roca].
- **Districts.**
  - **North:** the AZOM complex, "a heavily built-up complex of multiple buildings" [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca], and the Stupky rail area [src:isw-2023-02-08-roca].
  - **East:** the "Tavr" meat plant [src:isw-2023-03-04-roca], the T-34 tank monument on Maksyma Horkoho St [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca], and Artwinery [src:freeradio-2023-nazarova-artwinery-ruins-video].
  - **Centre:** the city administration, the Bakhmut-1 and Bakhmut-2 stations and the Avangard stadium [src:isw-2023-04-21-roca] [src:aljazeera-2023-psaropoulos-wagner-advances-bakhmut].
  - **South-west:** the Mariupolske cemetery [src:isw-2023-03-20-roca].
  - **West:** multi-storey blocks at the entrance from Ivanivske, the MiG-17 monument in Druzhba Square (the "Airplane" area) and Yuvileina St, which leads out to Khromove [src:pravda-2023-mazurenko-mig17-monument-destroyed] [src:isw-2023-04-26-roca].
- **Underground.**
  - **Artwinery** was founded in 1950 in the gypsum tunnels. They hold 13–14 °C and 85–90% humidity over 26 ha [src:wikipedia-artwinery]. The Guardian describes a "250,000 sq metre expanse of caves" under more than 80 m of earth [src:guardian-2017-miller-artwinery-brut-force].
  - **Soledar's salt mines** have about 200 km of tunnels at about 260 m depth [src:iwpr-2023-soledar-salt-mines].
  - Prigozhin claimed tanks could move through them. Artemsil said military use was "unlikely" because the shafts are deep and narrow [src:iwpr-2023-soledar-salt-mines]. Treat the "underground city" as a claim, not a mechanic.
- **The Siverskyi Donets–Donbas canal** runs north–south about 8–12 km west and south-west of the city. It crosses the T0504 west of Ivanivske, where the bridge was down by 11 Feb 2023 [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca], and passes Klishchiivka and Kurdiumivka [src:osm-2026-bakhmut-geodata]. In July 2023 Ukraine crossed it near Klishchiivka [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-gains-bakhmut].
- **Ruin.**
  - **26 Dec 2022:** more than 60% of the infrastructure was damaged or destroyed [src:kyivindependent-2022-governor-60pct-bakhmut-infrastructure].
  - **4 Mar 2023:** "not a single building" was untouched [src:bbc-2023-landale-gozzi-bakhmut-deputy-mayor].
  - **Jun 2023:** a Sentinel-1 analysis found the worst damage in the western districts that fell last [src:reuters-2023-zafra-mcclure-bakhmut-destruction-analysis].
  - **Apr 2023:** both sides demolished high-rises, Ukraine with GBU-62 JDAMs [src:forbes-2023-axe-jdams-bakhmut].

**Roads and bridges.** The road refs are as ISW uses them [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca] [src:isw-2023-04-21-roca].
- **T0504 Kostiantynivka–Chasiv Yar–Bakhmut.** The southern road of life, via Ivanivske. ISW called it "Ukrainian forces' last remaining GLOC into Bakhmut that Russian forces are not fully interdicting" [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca]. The 3rd Assault Brigade calls the T0504 through Khromove "the last paved route" [src:youtube-2023-3oshbr-road-of-life-trench-battle].
- **O0506 Chasiv Yar–Khromove–Bakhmut.** The northern road, entering the city as Yuvileina St [src:isw-2023-04-26-roca]. The Kyiv Independent counts two roads of life: "one to the north, through … Khromove, and the T0504 further south through Ivanivske". Both were within 1 km of Russian positions in April, and the Khromove road had been out of active use for weeks by 21 April [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-bakhmut-flanks-hold].
- **Which road is "the road of life".** Sources disagree [src:businessinsider-2023-baker-jankowicz-unyielding-city] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-ukraine-strikes-back-last-streets]. Treat both as supply routes.
- **M03 (E40) Sloviansk–Bakhmut.** The northern supply road. Russia came within 2.5 km of it by 8 February 2023 [src:isw-2023-02-08-roca].
- **T0513 Siversk–Bakhmut.** Cutting it would require advances west of Soledar [src:isw-2023-01-07-roca].
- **T1302.** Runs north-east via Bakhmutske [src:isw-2022-11-15-roca].
- **Bridges.**
  - **22 Sep 2022:** the central Bakhmutka bridge was destroyed, cutting off the east bank [src:pravda-2022-kyrylenko-bridge-bisects-bakhmut].
  - **3 Mar 2023:** two bridges went, one over the Bakhmutka in the north-east and one on the Khromove road [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca]. Cherevatyi blamed Russia for the Khromove bridge; ISW could not tell who blew it [src:isw-2023-03-04-roca].
  - **4 May 2023:** a bridge over the canal west of the city was down [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca].
- **Fire control.**
  - RUSI puts Russian artillery control of the supply roads from February 2023 [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - The Kyiv Independent says Russia had "fire control over all the remaining roads" by early March [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-russia-takes-bakhmut].
  - ISW judged Russian interdiction claims exaggerated throughout [src:isw-2023-04-08-roca].

## 4. Forces

### Russia

**Command.**
- **The theatre.** Surovikin was theatre commander until 11 Jan 2023. ISW says his tenure "increased Prigozhin's influence", as Wagner received significant military supplies and took over the Bakhmut offensive [src:isw-2023-04-30-roca]. Gerasimov took over on 11 Jan, in part to reassert the MoD over Prigozhin [src:isw-2023-01-11-roca]. Col. Gen. Mikhail Teplinsky's VDV cooperated with Wagner. He was reportedly reappointed in April after a reported clash with Gerasimov [src:isw-2023-04-17-roca] [src:isw-2023-04-30-roca].
- **Wagner.**
  - Financed and led in public by Yevgeny Prigozhin.
  - Its Soledar commander was the ex-GRU officer Anton Yelizarov ("Lotos"), per the Dossier Center [src:babel-2023-yelizarov-soledar-commander].
  - From May 2023 Mikhail Mizintsev was its deputy commander [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca] [src:cnn-2023-lendon-prigozhin-fat-cats].

**Wagner's strength.**
- **Two tiers.** Professionals wore "M" tags. Convicts wore "K" tags numbered by colony, hence "Kashniki" [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut].
- **Estimates of size:**
  - US, January 2023: about 50,000 in Ukraine, of whom 10,000 were contractors and 40,000 convicts [src:guardian-2023-kirby-wagner-tco].
  - Prigozhin, May 2023: 35,000 men plus 50,000 convicts [src:euromaidan-2023-prigozhin-video-losses].
  - Mediazona: at least 48,000 convicts recruited, the last on 7 Feb 2023 [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut].
- **Casualty estimates:**
  - White House, 17 Feb 2023: more than 30,000 casualties, about 9,000 of them killed. About 90% of those killed since December were convicts [src:reuters-2023-shalal-wagner-30000-casualties].
- **Frontage.** Prigozhin said Wagner's front shrank from 85 km to under 5 km [src:euromaidan-2023-prigozhin-video-losses].

**Other Russian units reported in the sector.**
- **Aug 2022:** the LNR 6th Cossack Regiment, with Wagner [src:isw-2022-08-25-roca].
- **Oct 2022:** the MoD convict unit "Shtorm" (unit 08807) at Soledar. Of a batch of 130, about 40 were left [src:cnn-2023-walsh-mod-convicts-storm].
- **From late Dec 2022 and Jan 2023:** VDV elements. The MoD named elements of the 76th and 106th Airborne Divisions on 24 Feb [src:isw-2023-02-24-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
- **On the northern flank in April:** the 106th VDV and the 2nd LNR Army Corps held a line from Zaliznianske through Sakko i Vantsetti, Mykolaivka and Yakovlivka to Berestove [src:isw-2023-04-17-roca].
- **Wagner-trained brigades.** Wagner was training up to three motor rifle brigades of mobilised men for its flanks [src:isw-2023-04-17-roca].
- **On the flanks in May:**
  - the 72nd Motor Rifle Brigade (3rd Army Corps) to the south-west [src:isw-2023-05-09-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-10-roca]
  - the 200th Motor Rifle Brigade (14th Army Corps) to the north-west [src:isw-2023-05-12-roca]
  - the LNR 4th Motor Rifle Brigade near Ivanivske [src:isw-2023-05-16-roca]
  - the 31st and 83rd VDV Brigades south of the city, which Syrskyi said had lost combat capability by September [src:isw-2023-09-18-roca]
- **Other formations:**
  - **PMC "Potok".** Accused of abandoning positions in April [src:isw-2023-04-26-roca].
  - **The "Nevsky" volunteer battalion** [src:isw-2023-05-09-roca].
  - **DNR units, including the 132nd Brigade.** These replaced Wagner in the city from late May [src:isw-2023-05-25-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-27-roca].

**Equipment and fires.**
- **Wagner's assets.** A Wagner infographic showed a TOS-1A, self-propelled guns and mortars, armour and an Su-25. ISW read these as MoD assets that Wagner depended on [src:isw-2023-01-20-roca]. The MoD sent T-90s in March–April [src:isw-2023-05-11-roca].
- **Artillery.** RUSI puts Russia's artillery advantage at Bakhmut at 5:1 [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **Precision and EW.**
  - Lancet loitering munitions emerged as a key precision tool [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Russia fielded at least one major EW system per 10 km of front [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
  - The Strelets reconnaissance and fire-control system was widely used by the VDV and Wagner [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].

**How the force changed.**
- **Aug – Dec 2022.** Wagner assault companies were fed by prison recruitment, first in companies of about 60, then platoons of about 15 [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder]. By late December losses had reduced them to squad-sized groups [src:isw-2022-12-27-roca].
- **January 2023.** The MoD cut off penal recruitment, the VDV was committed at Soledar, and the "Wagner offensive culminated" but was revived by regulars [src:isw-2023-05-11-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca].
- **March 2023.** Wagner committed its elite and its "main part" inside the city [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca]. Prisoners were used "to a much more limited extent" [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
- **April – May 2023.** A division of labour formed: Wagner in the city, regulars on the flanks [src:isw-2023-04-11-roca]. After the May counterattacks Russia moved VDV, motor-rifle and special forces in from other sectors. Maliar said "most of their reserves" went there [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].

- **Vehicles:** Wolfhound MRAPs in street fighting [src:newsweek-2022-vanbrugen-drone-bakhmut-video].
- **Drones:** reconnaissance drones throughout [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-hell-in-high-definition].

**How the force changed: attrition and rotation.**
- **Dec 2022:** one company was down to 7 of 93 combat-ready after two weeks [src:pravda-2022-kyrylenko-invincibility-centre-bakhmut].
- **Soledar:** a 46th Airmobile battalion that withdrew lost about 100 killed and 400 wounded of about 500. Its commander said the experienced men "are all already dead or wounded" [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops].
- **Oct 2023:** a 93rd company of 110 had four left in action [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-bakhmut-lags].
- **Kofman and Lee:** experienced brigades stayed on the line with "minimal rotation" so that new brigades could form for the counteroffensive [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance]. The best formations were kept out: a Ukrainian official said the Bakhmut units were "not slated" for the offensive anyway [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**Wagner's assault method.** The account comes from a Ukrainian intelligence report published by CNN; it is single-source and its intercepts are unauthenticated [src:cnn-2023-lister-wagner-tactics-report]:
- **The groups.** Assault groups are "about a dozen or fewer", with RPGs and Motorola radios, and drones as the "key element".
- **The waves.** Convicts make up the first wave and take up to 80% losses, by the Ukrainian claim. Veterans with thermal sights follow and consolidate in foxholes under artillery.
- **The rules.** "Unauthorized withdrawal … is punishable by execution on the spot." The wounded are left, and retreat is allowed "only at night".
- **Adoption.** The report says these were "the only" effective tactics for mobilised men, and that the army was copying them [src:cnn-2023-lister-wagner-tactics-report].

Corroboration:
- **RUSI.** Disposable infantry attacked in fire teams of 2–5, and some were "shot from Russian positions when attempting to retreat". Wagner assault companies "are either lost or succeed". Four UAVs flew over each assault group [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
- **Ukrainian defenders.** They describe a 10-hour assault by about 200 men against about 20 defenders. Groups of about 10 move 30 m, dig in, and the next group goes through [src:cnn-2023-lister-zombie-movie].
- **Russian soldiers at Soledar.** Commanders were heard shouting "Not a step back. We will shoot" [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-hellish-soledar]. Barabanov describes anti-retreat units of experienced fighters [src:novayagazeta-2022-morozov-convicts-in-arms].
- **ISW.** It saw Russia's "most elite units" use "smaller formations using urban infiltration tactics" by February [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca]. It traced the MoD's permanent assault detachments to Wagner's model [src:isw-2023-02-27-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-13-roca].
- **Storm-Z.** The MoD's convict units ("Storm", then Storm-Z from February 2023) worked the same way. The MoD document "On Storm Z" authorised shooting deserters [src:rferl-2024-belovodyev-storm-gladiator]. After 7 Feb 2023 convicts went to Storm-Z instead of Wagner [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut].

**The Ukrainian defence.**
- **Houses as forts.** Prigozhin: every house a "fortress", with lines "every 10 meters" [src:isw-2023-01-03-roca]. A border-guard commander: "houses for you are mass graves" [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-bakhmut-flanks-hold].
- **Trading ground for time.** The defence gave up blocks deliberately:
  - It withdrew from the east bank behind blown bridges [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca].
  - It held the roads west rather than the centre, forcing Wagner "into urban warfare and grinding directly through the city" [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
  - Rodnianskyi said the area west of the city had been fortified so that a withdrawal would not mean a quick Russian advance [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca].
- **Keeping the roads open.** Engineers "pave new routes" around interdicted sections, and special forces and artillery stop Russia cutting the road [src:euromaidanpress-2023-zoria-road-of-life-cherevatyi]. Vehicles run them fast at dawn [src:businessinsider-2023-baker-jankowicz-unyielding-city].
- **Active defence.** From May, flank counterattacks were aimed at Russian concentrations, not at the city [src:isw-2023-05-14-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-16-roca].

**Artillery and the ammunition war.**
- **Russian fire rates.**
  - Summer 2022: about 20,000 rounds a day, with peaks above 32,000. Ukraine fired fewer than 6,000 [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons].
  - Early 2023, falling, but the figures disagree by source. Budanov: from 60,000 to 19,000–20,000 a day [src:kyivindependent-2023-ponomarenko-artillery-gasps]. US officials: down "as much as 75%", to about 5,000 a day, while Ukraine averaged 4,000–7,000 [src:cnn-2023-bertrand-russian-artillery-down-75]. RUSI: 12,000–38,000 a day in Q1 2023 [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder]. RUSI gives 5:1 at Bakhmut itself [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **Ukrainian rationing.**
  - A 24th Brigade battery went from 150 full loads a day to 30: "We need every single 122-millimeter round anywhere in the world" [src:kyivindependent-2023-ponomarenko-artillery-gasps].
  - Infantry in March: "If we keep getting 10 shells, Bakhmut will quickly be surrounded" [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-bakhmut-troops-not-protected].
  - Palisa: "painful to see how we pay with our lives for the lack of ammunition" [src:nyt-2023-kramer-final-blocks].
  - Reznikov put Ukrainian use at about 110,000 155 mm shells a month, a quarter of Russia's [src:kyivindependent-2023-cherevatyi-denies-russian-shortage].
  - The Pentagon warned Kyiv against wasting ammunition [src:nyt-2023-engelbrecht-bakhmut-success].
  - In June Zelensky said 50,000 shells were fired there every day; it is unclear by whom [src:ukrinform-2023-zelensky-best-weapons-bakhmut].
- **Western supply.**
  - US 155 mm output was about 15,000 a month, aiming for 70,000 [src:cnn-2023-britzky-ukraine-burning-ammo].
  - On 20 Mar 2023 the EU agreed €2 bn for one million 155 mm rounds within a year [src:reuters-2023-gray-eu-million-shells]. By 23 May it had delivered 220,000 [src:reuters-2023-gray-eu-220000-shells].
- **Russian "shell hunger" as politics.**
  - Prigozhin claimed 70% of needed ammunition was missing (4 May), then that he got "only 10%" (10 May) [src:cnn-2023-lendon-prigozhin-fat-cats] [src:reuters-2023-osborn-prigozhin-10pct-shells].
  - Ukraine called the shortage "fake", counting 524 Russian strikes a day [src:ukrinform-2023-cherevaty-ammo-shortage-fake].
  - Rob Lee suggested the MoD was rationing ahead of the Ukrainian counteroffensive [src:reuters-2023-light-prigozhin-lick-wounds].

**Reconnaissance-strike.**
- **Kill-chain times.** Russian kill chains ran 3–5 minutes from drone detection to fire, and 20–30 minutes from an EW detection [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
- **Drone and munition attrition.**
  - Ukraine lost about 10,000 UAVs a month [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
  - Excalibur's accuracy began to collapse under jamming in this period [src:kyivpost-2024-chiu-gps-weapons].
  - Russian FPVs first appeared [src:justsecurity-2026-bidochko-drone-superpower].
- **Ukrainian drone teams** spotted for heavy guns from inside the city's industrial buildings [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-hell-in-high-definition].

**Demolition of the city.** Both sides levelled high-rises that offered observation. Ukraine dropped four GBU-62s on one complex on 26 April [src:forbes-2023-axe-jdams-bakhmut]. Ukrainian SOF accused Russia of using phosphorus and incendiaries [src:pravda-2023-romanenko-phosphorus-bakhmut-sof].

**Civilians.** The civilian population fell as follows:
- about 12,000 on 10 Dec 2022 [src:guardian-2022-beaumont-bakhmut-meat-grinder]
- about 5,900 on 2 Feb 2023 [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-one-night-in-bakhmut]
- about 4,000 on 4 Mar [src:bbc-2023-landale-gozzi-bakhmut-deputy-mayor]
- about 500 on 31 May [src:kyivindependent-2023-tsurkan-bakhmut-mayor-casualties]

Children were evacuated by force from 8 Mar [src:euromaidanpress-2023-midttun-day378-forced-evacuation-children]. Drone footage showed Russian soldiers removing civilians at gunpoint [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **Zelensky in Bakhmut (20 Dec 2022) and the flag in Congress (21 Dec).**
   - **The visit.** On day 300 of the invasion Zelensky goes to "frontline positions of one of the mechanized brigades … on the approaches to the city" and hands out awards [src:president-2022-zelensky-bakhmut-visit-awards]. He leaves before the visit is announced [src:bbc-2022-bachega-zelensky-visits-bakhmut]. The soldiers give him a flag "to pass on" [src:president-2022-address-indomitable-bakhmut].
   - **The flag.** The next day he presents it to Congress: "Let this flag stay with you" [src:pbs-2022-freking-zelensky-dc-visit]. It is a battle flag signed by the defenders [src:bbc-2022-chatterjee-zelensky-visit-russia-reaction].
   - **Weather.** The visit day is frosty and dry, with sunset at 16:34 [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-bakhmut-2022-23].
2. **The salt town (4–16 Jan 2023).**
   - **The streets.** Wagner fights street by street through Soledar. A Wagner fighter is filmed at the Artemsil plant, while a Ukrainian correspondent poses at the salt mine in the north-west [src:isw-2023-01-07-roca].
   - **The fall.** The town falls in hard frost, −13 °C on 10 Jan [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-bakhmut-2022-23].
   - **The quarrel.** It ends with the Russian MoD's announcement that leaves Wagner out, and a correction six hours later [src:isw-2023-01-13-roca].
3. **Withdrawal across the Bakhmutka (3–7 Mar 2023).**
   - **The bridges.** Two bridges go down on 3 Mar, one over the Bakhmutka and one on the Khromove road [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca].
   - **The east bank.** Over four days Ukraine pulls back across the river, and the river becomes the front [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca].
   - **The flag.** Wagner hangs its flag on the T-34 monument in the east [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
   - **The order.** Zelensky meanwhile orders reinforcements in [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
4. **AZOM (10–28 Mar 2023).**
   - **The assault.** Wagner assaults the built-up metals plant, including its "underground sections" [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-13-roca].
   - **The fall.** It takes about two and a half weeks [src:isw-2023-03-29-roca]. A Ukrainian missile then strikes the Russian-held plant [src:isw-2023-04-03-roca].
5. **The flag on city hall (night of 2 Apr 2023).**
   - **The flag.** Prigozhin raises a Russian flag in front of the city administration building, inscribed to the killed milblogger Maksim Fomin.
   - **The claim.** He declares Bakhmut "legally" taken, though Ukrainians still hold the west [src:isw-2023-04-03-roca] [src:aljazeera-2023-wagner-legal-control-bakhmut].
   - **Three weeks later.** The building is "completely flattened … flags literally on top of the rubble" [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-bakhmut-flanks-hold].
6. **The road of life.**
   - **The run.** Vehicles weave at near top speed between craters on the last roads in, through Khromove and Ivanivske. Satellite imagery shows "thousands of shell holes" at the bend nearest the Russians [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-bakhmut-flanks-hold]. Drivers make dawn dashes "on tires frayed to almost nothing" [src:businessinsider-2023-baker-jankowicz-unyielding-city].
   - **The trench fight.** The 3rd Assault Brigade's trench fight to free the road from fire control is filmed on bodycams [src:youtube-2023-3oshbr-road-of-life-trench-battle].
7. **Prigozhin among the corpses (4–5 May 2023).** In front of rows of dead Wagner fighters, Prigozhin curses Shoigu and Gerasimov and sets a 10 May deadline to leave the city. Four days later he backs down [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-09-roca].
8. **The flank strikes back (9–18 May 2023).**
   - **The breakthrough.** The 3rd Assault Brigade breaks the Russian 72nd Motor Rifle Brigade south-west of the city, and Prigozhin says the 72nd "fled" [src:pravda-2023-3rd-assault-72nd-flees] [src:isw-2023-05-10-roca].
   - **The reservoir.** Russian soldiers are filmed running from Ukrainian artillery on the Berkhivka reservoir [src:isw-2023-05-12-roca].
   - **The other side's view.** Prigozhin admits that he can no longer encircle anything [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
9. **The last blocks: the Nest and the Airplane (17–21 May 2023).**
   - **The barrage.** Wagner tanks and artillery pound "the last block of the high-rise buildings" at the western entrance [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-wagnerites-high-rises].
   - **The declaration.** Prigozhin declares victory at the last multi-storey block near the MiG-17 monument. The monument itself was blown up in March [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca] [src:pravda-2023-mazurenko-mig17-monument-destroyed].
   - **The holdout.** Ukrainians still hold the "Airplane" area and the south-western outskirts for weeks [src:euromaidanpress-2023-zoria-maliar-airplane-area] [src:isw-2023-06-01-roca].
10. **Coda: Andriivka and Klishchiivka (14–17 Sep 2023).** The 3rd Assault Brigade encircles Andriivka. The 80th Air Assault Brigade, the 5th Assault Brigade and the police "Liut" brigade clear Klishchiivka on the heights [src:militarnyi-2023-andriivka-taken] [src:militarnyi-2023-klishchiivka-cleared].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Russia: give Bakhmut to Wagner and its convicts (summer – autumn 2022).**
   - The MoD relied on Wagner for the Bakhmut effort "since July" [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca].
   - Prigozhin later said that he and Surovikin organised "Operation Bakhmut Meatgrinder" in October 2022 "to provoke Kyiv into throwing Ukrainian forces into Bakhmut en masse" [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca].
   - ISW records Prigozhin's consistent line, from November 2022, that taking the city lacked strategic value. It notes that this line contradicts his fight to complete the capture [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca].
2. **Russia: cut off Wagner's prison recruitment and centralise command (January 2023).**
   - ISW: the MoD "likely began a broader deprioritization of the Bakhmut effort by January 2023 when the MoD cut off Wagner Group penal recruitment" [src:isw-2023-05-11-roca].
   - On 11 Jan Gerasimov became theatre commander, in part to reassert the MoD over Prigozhin [src:isw-2023-01-11-roca].
   - The VDV and other conventional forces were fed in from late January [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca].
3. **Ukraine: hold or leave (from January 2023).** The sources set out the debate rather than settle it.
   - **US advice.** A US official told the Washington Post that "the United States has been advising Ukraine to retreat from the city since at least January" [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops]. On 20 Feb Zelensky said Ukraine would hold "not at any cost". ISW read that as a rhetorical concession to US officials, not a change of strategy [src:isw-2023-02-20-roca].
   - **The commanders' view.** It is contested. Zelensky said on 6 March that his commanders "responded not to withdraw, but to strengthen" and were unanimous. Bild, citing government sources, reported that Zaluzhnyi had recommended retreat weeks earlier [src:bbc-2023-greenall-bakhmut-defenders-double-down].
   - **The decision.** After the meeting with Zaluzhnyi and Syrskyi on 6 March, Zelensky ordered reinforcements [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca]. The Stavka confirmed it on 15 March: "Reinforce this sector and inflict maximum possible damage" [src:politico-2023-dettmer-zelenskyy-digs-in].
   - **The rationale.** Syrskyi: "We need to buy time to prepare reserves … Some people defend, others prepare" [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops]. Zelensky warned of an "open road" to Kramatorsk and Sloviansk [src:cnn-2023-picheta-zelensky-open-road]. RUSI adds that the General Staff feared Chasiv Yar would be next [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
   - **The alternatives argued.**
     - Kofman and Lee said the exchange was "not nearly as favorable as it was before" [src:politico-2023-dettmer-zelenskyy-digs-in]. Lee: "Bakhmut is no longer a good place to attrit Russian forces" [src:politico-2023-dettmer-zelenskyy-digs-in].
     - Austin called a withdrawal no "significant strategic setback" [src:isw-2023-03-06-roca].
     - ISW judged the defence "strategically sound" [src:isw-2023-02-20-roca].
     - Petraeus and Hertling backed holding [src:politico-2023-dettmer-zelenskyy-digs-in].
   - **What followed, by later verdicts.**
     - RUSI: the decision was "initially … vindicated". Once Russian artillery controlled the roads in February, losses turned unfavourable, and the "optimal tactical course" was to withdraw then. Pending Western decisions on tanks and munitions made that politically hard [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
     - Kofman and Lee: the defence kept experienced brigades on the line with "minimal rotation" while new brigades formed, and there were "no cost-free or risk-free options" [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance].
4. **Ukraine: a controlled withdrawal from the east bank (3–7 Mar 2023).**
   - Ukraine blew the bridges and gave up the city east of the Bakhmutka rather than the whole city [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca].
   - It kept fighting for the roads west of the city. ISW credits this with forcing "Wagner forces into urban warfare and grinding directly through the city itself" [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
   - Prigozhin had offered on 3 March to let Zelensky withdraw [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
5. **Russia: Wagner in the city, regulars on the flanks (March – April 2023).**
   - The MoD and Prigozhin agreed on 11 April that Wagner was the main effort inside the city, with the VDV on the flanks [src:isw-2023-04-11-roca].
   - ISW read this as a way to take the city "while minimizing casualties among conventional Russian forces" [src:isw-2023-04-11-roca].
   - Prigozhin complained that the flanks were held only by "irregular and degraded units" [src:isw-2023-05-11-roca]. That weakness is what the May counterattacks exploited.
6. **Russia: the shell squeeze and Prigozhin's ultimatum (February – May 2023).**
   - Prigozhin claimed "550 attempts" to get ammunition, all ignored [src:isw-2023-03-13-roca].
   - On 4–5 May he set the 10 May deadline. On 7 May he announced an MoD promise of supplies [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca]. On 9 May he stayed, saying the MoD had threatened him with treason [src:isw-2023-05-09-roca].
   - ISW judged that the MoD had deprioritised Bakhmut to prepare for Ukraine's counteroffensive [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca].
7. **Ukraine: counterattack the flanks, not the city (from 9 May 2023).**
   - Cherevatyi: the aim was "to destroy Russian concentration areas and encircle the city, not to conduct frontal assaults" [src:isw-2023-05-14-roca].
   - Syrskyi called it "active defense" [src:isw-2023-05-16-roca].
   - What followed: about 17–20 km² was retaken in a week [src:isw-2023-05-13-roca] [src:ukrinform-2023-maliar-20-sq-km]. The city still fell on 20 May.
   - Maliar said Russia had to commit "most of their reserves" to the area [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
8. **Russia: hand the city over and leave (20 May – 5 Jun 2023).**
   - Prigozhin announced the handover for 25 May and a two-month rest from 1 June [src:isw-2023-05-22-roca].
   - ISW called it "strategically questionable" on the eve of the counteroffensive [src:isw-2023-05-27-roca].
   - DNR and regular units took over the city [src:isw-2023-05-25-roca].
9. **Ukraine: keep attacking the flanks into the summer (June – September 2023).**
   - Kofman and Lee saw the Bakhmut flank as the first axis of the 2023 offensive, which drew in most of Russia's VDV [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance].
   - RUSI says reinforcing it reflected "strong political direction to demonstrate progress" and that it "devolved into fights for treelines" [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
   - What followed: Andriivka and Klishchiivka were retaken in September [src:isw-2023-09-18-roca], but not the city.

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **The capture.**
  - **Prigozhin, 20 May (noon):** "Bakhmut was completely taken … from house to house." Cherevatyi: "This is not true" [src:reuters-2023-trevelyan-prigozhin-claims-bakhmut]. The Kyiv Independent says the video was filmed about 3 km from the fighting [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-russia-takes-bakhmut].
  - **The MoD, that evening:** the "liberation of Artyomovsk has been completed" by Wagner, supported by Southern Group artillery and aviation [src:reuters-2023-mod-claims-bakhmut].
  - **Putin, overnight:** congratulations to "Wagner's assault groups" and to the army units "who provided … cover on their flanks", with a promise of state awards [src:reuters-2023-putin-congratulates-wagner].
  - **State television:** the "unassailable fortress" myth was "crushed", with comparisons to Berlin in 1945 and talk of an open road to Kramatorsk and Dnipro [src:apnews-2023-litvinova-russia-tv-berlin].
- **The credit fight.**
  - **Soledar.** The MoD's first announcement left Wagner out, and a second one six hours later credited "volunteers of assault detachments" [src:isw-2023-01-13-roca] [src:reuters-2023-russia-claims-soledar-wagner-nod]. Putin then credited the MoD and the General Staff [src:isw-2023-01-16-roca]. Zelensky: they were "fighting among themselves over who should be credited" [src:reuters-2023-russia-claims-soledar-wagner-nod].
  - **Paraskoviivka (Feb).** The MoD again called Wagner only "volunteers of assault detachments", and Prigozhin said the MoD was "spitting at" Wagner [src:isw-2023-02-20-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-22-roca].
  - **Bloomberg's sources** said the MoD would not let Prigozhin take credit for the city on state media [src:isw-2023-03-23-roca].
- **Prigozhin's narrative.**
  - **The battle's purpose.** The "Bakhmut meat grinder" was planned with Surovikin in October 2022 to draw in and exhaust the Ukrainian army, not to take the city [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca] [src:cnn-2023-knight-nothing-left-to-grind].
  - **Earlier admissions.** At the turn of the year he admitted Wagner could not break through [src:isw-2023-01-03-roca].
  - **The ammunition feud.** He accused the MoD of "treason" over shells and published photos and videos of his dead [src:kyivindependent-2023-prigozhin-shell-hunger-photo] [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca].
  - **Dramatised threats.** He claimed Ukraine had 200,000 reserves ready to strike [src:isw-2023-03-23-roca]. Late in May he warned of "a revolution, just like in 1917" [src:cnn-2023-picheta-prigozhin-revolution].
  - **The leak.** A leaked US document said he had offered to give Ukrainian intelligence Russian positions in exchange for a Ukrainian withdrawal. He denied it [src:isw-2023-05-16-roca].
- **Claims of Ukrainian losses.**
  - Prigozhin: 11,000 Ukrainians killed in February [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca] and 50,000 Ukrainian dead over the battle [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut].
  - The MoD: 700 killed in three days at Soledar [src:cnn-2023-picheta-soledar-explainer]. Russia also claimed 220 Ukrainians killed in one day in March [src:bbc-2023-wright-heavy-losses-bakhmut].
  - Against these, the UALosses name counts are 4,557–5,501 [src:meduza-2024-75000-dead] [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut].
- **The operational stakes as Russia framed them.** Pushilin said taking Bakhmut would open the way to Kramatorsk and Sloviansk [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca]. Shoigu made the same claim on 7 March, and Prigozhin rejected it [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca]. ISW judged Russia had no reserve to exploit the capture [src:isw-2023-03-08-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-21-roca].
- **Accounts of atrocities.**
  - Two former Wagner fighters said they had been ordered to kill civilians in Soledar and Bakhmut, including children [src:isw-2023-04-17-roca].
  - Ukraine's prosecutor opened a case over Prigozhin's removal of families with children from the city [src:isw-2023-04-21-roca].
  - The defector Andrey Medvedev publicised Wagner's brutal disciplinary practices [src:isw-2023-01-16-roca].
- **Western analysis.**
  - RUSI: Russia "traded lives for time" at Bakhmut while it built its southern defences [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - ISW: Prigozhin's final blocks were "purely symbolic" [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca].
  - Western officials called the battle "quite a small tactical event" with "a unique opportunity to kill a lot of Russians" [src:bbc-2023-adams-western-officials-30000].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Win band.**
  - **Hard conditions:**
    - Bakhmut is lost, but not before mid-May. Historically the east bank goes on 7 Mar, the city centre on 2 Apr and the last blocks on 20 May [src:isw-2023-03-07-roca] [src:isw-2023-04-03-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-20-roca].
    - The garrison is never encircled, and a western road stays usable to the end [src:isw-2023-05-18-roca].
  - **The flank counter-blow:** the flanks are pushed back by roughly 17–24 km² before or after the fall [src:isw-2023-05-13-roca] [src:isw-2023-07-10-roca].
  - **Losses scoring band** (Russian dead; the debrief names each claimant):
    - floor of about 19,500: Wagner's own records [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut]
    - middle of about 21,000: Meduza's all-Russian estimate [src:meduza-2024-75000-dead]
    - Ukrainian claims above that [src:ukrinform-2023-zelensky-wagner-100000]
  - **Exchange ratio:** keep it inside 3:1 to 7:1 [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance] [src:nyt-2023-lopez-biggest-battle].
  - **Hidden score:** the quality of the Ukrainian dead matters. The debrief should show RUSI's point that Ukraine lost trained men while Russia spent convicts [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **Self-running script.** The AI should repeat the historical phases, with these scripted events:
  - Wagner recruits convicts (Jul 2022) and loses penal recruitment (Feb 2023).
  - Gerasimov takes command (11 Jan).
  - The VDV arrives on the flanks (late Jan).
  - The MoD shell squeeze peaks (Feb and May).
  - Prigozhin's ultimatum (5–9 May).
  - The handover (25 May).
  - Sources: [src:mediazona-2024-price-of-bakhmut] [src:isw-2023-01-11-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-05-roca] [src:isw-2023-05-25-roca].

- **The player's levers.**
  - **Rotation.** How long to leave a brigade in the city before it is "dead or wounded", against keeping fresh brigades for the counteroffensive [src:wapo-2023-khurshudyan-short-of-skilled-troops] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-perseverance].
  - **Shells.** A daily ration on the order of 4,000–7,000 rounds against a Russian 5:1 [src:cnn-2023-bertrand-russian-artillery-down-75] [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - **Flank holding.** Losing Soledar or Klishchiivka speeds the pincers [src:isw-2023-01-24-roca].
  - **Withdrawal timing.** Blow the bridges, give up a district [src:isw-2023-03-03-roca].
- **Attrition mechanics.**
  - Wagner waves are cheap convicts with executions behind them. They break on house strongpoints, but each wave takes 30 m, and trained Wagner losses stay low [src:cnn-2023-lister-zombie-movie] [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Russian artillery control of the roads from February should raise Ukrainian casualty and supply costs. That is the point where holding stops paying, by RUSI's reading [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **Roads of life as a resource.** Model the T0504 and the Khromove road (O0506) as two supply lines with a fire-control meter. Engineers' bypass tracks and counterattacks at Ivanivske lower it [src:euromaidanpress-2023-zoria-road-of-life-cherevatyi] [src:isw-2023-03-19-roca]. April rain limits wheeled vehicles to paved roads [src:isw-2023-04-21-roca].
- **Politics as events.**
  - Zelensky's visit and "Fortress Bakhmut" raise morale but make leaving costlier [src:president-2022-address-indomitable-bakhmut] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-russia-takes-bakhmut].
  - Prigozhin's feud is a Russian supply modifier: shells cut in February and late April, restored after 7 May [src:reuters-2023-osborn-moscow-agrees-shells] [src:isw-2023-05-07-roca].
- **Pacing and weather.** The chapter runs from the wet autumn (100+ mm a month) through a frozen January–February, when the Soledar battle is fought at −13 °C. Then come the March thaw with snow, and April rain on 17 days [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-bakhmut-2022-23]. Daylight runs from about 8.3 hours in December to over 15 hours in May.

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Ukrainian losses** are the weakest number. Three counts should not be merged [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons] [src:meduza-2024-75000-dead] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-bakhmut]:
  - RUSI: about 10,000 killed or severely wounded.
  - UALosses (2024): about 4,600–5,500 named dead.
  - Wikipedia citing UALosses (2026): 14,235 killed and 16,569 missing, unchecked.
- **The Ukrainian order of battle is incomplete.**
  - No dated Ukrainian-outlet confirmation was found for the 24th, 30th, 60th, 71st or 4th Tank brigades inside the city. They come from Forbes' December list [src:forbes-2022-axe-eight-brigades].
  - The International Legion's role rests only on Wikipedia [src:wikipedia-battle-of-bakhmut].
  - At Soledar only the 46th and 77th Airmobile are officially confirmed. The 61st and 10th Mountain Assault brigades, often named there, were not verified [src:president-2023-zelensky-soledar-46-77].
- **The Soledar withdrawal date** is contested. The Kyiv Independent analysis puts the last Ukrainians out around 12 Jan, but official confirmation came on 25 Jan [src:kyivindependent-2023-ponomarenko-loss-of-soledar] [src:kyivindependent-2023-soledar-withdrawal-confirmed].
- **Which road is "the road of life"** differs by source: the T0504 via Ivanivske, or the Khromove road (O0506) [src:businessinsider-2023-baker-jankowicz-unyielding-city] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-bakhmut-flanks-hold].
- **Who blew the Khromove bridge** on 3 Mar is unresolved: Ukraine's own demolition or a Russian strike [src:isw-2023-03-04-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-bakhmut-troops-not-protected].
- **Location gaps.**
  - AZOM's internal layout and the "underground sections" Wagner assaulted are not described in any fetched source [src:isw-2023-03-13-roca].
  - The MiG-17 monument and "the last high-rise" were not found as OSM features [src:osm-2026-bakhmut-geodata].
- **Fire-rate figures** disagree by an order of magnitude between US and Ukrainian estimates (5,000 against 20,000 Russian rounds a day in January 2023) [src:cnn-2023-bertrand-russian-artillery-down-75].
- **The size of the May strip** is reported three ways: 3 km × 2.6 km (Biletskyi), 2 km × 0.5 km (Prigozhin) and 1,730 m × 700 m (the 3rd Assault Brigade, later) [src:pravda-2023-3rd-assault-72nd-flees] [src:ukrinform-2023-3rd-assault-700m].
- **Missing analyses.** No UNOSAT damage assessment for Bakhmut was found, and Planet imagery was not examined [src:reuters-2023-zafra-mcclure-bakhmut-destruction-analysis].
- **Missing ISW dailies.** Several dailies (December 2022; 4 Feb, 15 and 31 Mar, 2 Apr, 6 Jun 2023) could not be fetched. The 20 December visit and December fighting rest on other sources.
- **Unverified tactical claims.** The CNN-published Ukrainian intelligence report on Wagner tactics is single-source [src:cnn-2023-lister-wagner-tactics-report].
