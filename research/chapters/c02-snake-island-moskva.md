## 1. Summary and historical outcome

On the first day of the invasion, the cruiser *Moskva* and the patrol ship *Vasily Bykov* spent all of 24 Feb demanding over Channel 16 that Zmiinyi Island's garrison surrender. The garrison numbered about 80: border guards, marines of the 35th Brigade and a few others. Around 18:00 a border guard told the "Russian warship" to go f— itself. After a day of naval gunfire and Su-27/Su-34 air strikes, contact was lost about an hour later, and the whole garrison and the island's civilians were captured. First reports had them all dead [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:pravda-2023-deineko-16-border-guards]. The audio, published that night, became the war's rallying cry and a postage stamp. The prisoners came home over more than three years of exchanges, the last on 23 Jul 2025 [src:pravda-2022-romanenko-zmiinyi-audio] [src:ukrposhta-2022-warship-stamp] [src:pravda-2025-tyshchenko-last-zmiinyi-defender].

Act 2 begins on 13 Apr. A Ukrainian Navy coastal battery in southern Odesa Oblast fired two R-360 Neptune missiles at the *Moskva*, the Black Sea Fleet flagship and its long-range air-defence umbrella, about 120 km offshore. Low cloud hid the ship from aircraft and satellites; the battery's ordinary radar reached it anyway, apparently ducted under that cloud [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune] [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island]. A satellite radar image at 18:52 caught the burning ship at 45°10′43″N 30°55′31″E, about 80 nmi south of Odesa [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-moskva-sar]. Russia blamed a fire and an ammunition explosion and said the ship sank under tow "in stormy sea" on 14 Apr [src:reuters-2022-moskva-sunk]. The US confirmed the two Neptunes [src:dod-2022-04-18-background-briefing] [src:nbc-2022-dilanian-us-intel-moskva].

Without the *Moskva*, the island became an outpost Russia could not cover. Through May, Ukrainian Navy Bayraktar TB2s killed its Tor launchers and picked off the Raptor boats, a Serna landing craft and a Mi-8 bringing replacements and troops [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island] [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-curse-snake-island]. A HUR-led helicopter assault in early May was called off [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle]. From mid-June Ukraine switched to starving and shelling the island. Harpoons sank the tug *Vasily Bekh* on its way in (17 Jun), the gas rigs ("Boiko towers") were hit (20 Jun), and the prototype Bohdana howitzer, CAESAR and Uragan fired from the Danube-delta shore (21–30 Jun) [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-vasily-bekh] [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island]. In the coda, on 30 Jun, Russia evacuated the island's last troops in two speedboats and called it a "goodwill gesture" [src:ukrinform-2022-invaders-escape-snake-island] [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment]. Ukrainian troops planted the flag on 7 Jul [src:twz-2022-altman-flag-snake-island].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **The radio ultimatum (24 Feb).** Persuasion, then threat, every 15–20 min on Channel 16, timed to the ships' appearance on the horizon. Then gunfire, then air strikes [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
- **R-360 Neptune.**
  - A subsonic sea-skimmer, flying 3–10 m above the water and aimed just above the waterline, with a 150 kg warhead and up to 300 km range [src:kyivpost-2023-tuzov-neptune-moskva].
  - In February the missiles had to be flown at about 120 m over Odesa, which may explain why they failed [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - On 13 Apr the battery's ordinary radar reportedly saw the cruiser at about 120 km because low cloud ducted the beam [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - Neizhpapa: the first missile hit the galley above the engine room and the second the stern; AK-630 ammunition then exploded [src:intentpress-2025-neizhpapa-moskva-details].
  - Parry, an outside analyst, puts one or two hits below the aft Vulkan tubes, with fuel spreading the fire [src:guardian-2022-roth-moskva-photos].
- **The *Moskva*'s defences, and why they failed.**
  - Six AK-630s and the Rum Tub ESM should have given about 2 min of warning [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-moskva-negligence].
  - There is no record of chaff, jamming or manoeuvre. Explanations offered are poor alertness and training [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-moskva-negligence] and air defences that were "probably" not switched on [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - Damage control failed within hours [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-moskva-negligence]. The Russian court cites a six-hour rescue effort [src:rbcukraine-2026-court-moskva].
- **TB2 as a decoy or spotter (contested).**
  - Claimed by Ukrainian officials and the Wagner channel [src:forbes-2022-hambling-bayraktar-moskva] [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva], and by UP in Nov 2022 [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
  - Called "unlikely" [src:forbes-2022-hambling-bayraktar-moskva] and "unrealistic" [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-moskva-negligence].
  - UP in Dec 2022 says the TB2 operators refused to fly under the cloud [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune]. See §7.
- **TB2 against point defence on a rock.**
  - SHORAD with no area cover and no fighter cover on a treeless island let slow, low-signature TB2s kill the Tor, Strela-10 and ZU-23-2. They then hit the boats bringing replacements, which became the island's real weak point [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island] [src:twz-2022-payne-su27-snake-island].
  - Russia answered with a Tor fired from a boat deck, Pantsirs on tug sterns and EW [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island] [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island].
  - By June TB2s could not close inside 28–30 km [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island].
- **Toss bombing.** Su-27s came in under lighthouse height, popped up and lobbed high-drag bombs, dropping flares [src:twz-2022-payne-su27-snake-island].
- **Anti-ship interdiction, the A2/AD lever.** Neptune and Harpoon against resupply ships. The goal "was not the destruction of the garrison, but the complete severance of its supply lines" [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island]. A tug is "a higher-value target than the warships" [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-vasily-bekh].
- **Coastal artillery at its maximum range** [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island].
  - Bohdana (450 test rounds to 42 km) and CAESAR fired from beach positions about 40 km from the island.
  - The guns were camouflaged, and metal decoys hung in the trees made Russian radar "glow the whole coast".
  - Shoot-and-hide rotation: CAESAR, then Uragan, then Bohdana, with brass cleared away.
  - Fire was adjusted by Mavic/Matrix drones to about 20 m, with TB2 video going to OC South HQ.
  - Breakdowns: Bohdana's barrel seals burned out after three shots, and CAESAR leaked oil after 20.
- **Information operations.** On both sides the narrative was itself a weapon: the 13-dead myth [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle], the stamps [src:ukrposhta-2022-warship-stamp], Russia's "accident/storm" story [src:isw-2022-04-14-assessment] and the "goodwill gesture" [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **Channel 16, all day (24 Feb).** A horizon that slowly fills with ships. Surrender calls come every 20 minutes, then shells, then three hours of air strikes. Hotskyi splits his men across shelters so one bomb can't kill them all, and one old border guard runs back to stay with him. Around 18:00 comes the reply. On the mainland, a phone records it off a radio relay, with someone whispering "what, 'go f— yourself' too?" [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:pravda-2022-romanenko-zmiinyi-audio].
2. **The *Sapfir* trap (26 Feb).** Chaplains, a doctor and the crew sail with body bags to recover "the 13 dead". Russian marines board, make everyone kneel, and show them a video: everyone on the island is alive and captured. "Oh, so you're spies!" [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
3. **The blip under the clouds (13 Apr).** A basic radar shows a huge contact about 120 km out that it shouldn't be able to see. After "several minutes of pondering and consultations" two Neptunes leave the rails. Then about six minutes of not knowing. Later the cruiser turns at full speed for the Boiko towers, and four ships converge on it [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune] [src:pravda-2023-pohorilov-neptune-launch-video].
4. **Fire, tow and the night roll (13–14 Apr).** A tug hoses the listing cruiser; the life rafts are gone. An SOS at 01:05, a roll at 01:14, under by about 03:00. By the next day's calm sea there is only the "heavy black silhouette" of a ship going down [src:guardian-2022-roth-moskva-photos] [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva] [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
5. **Dawn hunt (2 May).** A TB2 strikes two Raptors 13 minutes apart as they zigzag [src:janes-2022-raptor-uav-strike].
6. **The landing ramp (7 May).** A Serna lowers its ramp with a Tor's radar still turning, and the TB2 bomb arrives. The same day two Su-27s skim in from the south past the lighthouse and toss-bomb the buildings [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island] [src:twz-2022-payne-su27-snake-island].
7. **The night assault and the Mi-14 (7–9 May).** Eight helicopters and armoured boats run in. A Tor lashed to a boat deck opens up and the assault pulls back. Over the coast, the standby rescue helicopter flown by Col. Bedzai is shot down [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:ppu-2024-bedzai].
8. **Two Harpoons (17 Jun).** TB2 video shows the second missile arriving seconds after the first fireball on the *Vasily Bekh* [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-vasily-bekh].
9. **Forty-six rounds from the dunes (30 Jun).** The Bohdana fires shell after shell, 90 seconds each to the island, waiting for the barrel to cool. Four rounds are held back for the pickup boats [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island].
10. **Two speedboats, then the flag (30 Jun – 7 Jul).** The island burns and the last Russians leave by speedboat. A day later come Russia's phosphorus runs. Then a motorboat at dawn on 7 Jul and a flag that reads "Russian warship, remember forever – Snake Island is Ukraine!!!" [src:ukrinform-2022-invaders-escape-snake-island] [src:aljazeera-2022-phosphorus-snake-island] [src:twz-2022-altman-flag-snake-island].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Garrison: surrender or hold (24 Feb).**
   - The garrison ignored a day of offers and threats [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
   - Of the civilians, only three took the two evacuation boats [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
   - The Navy says the defenders repulsed two attacks and could not go on without ammunition after communications were destroyed [src:ukrinform-2022-zmiinyi-defenders-alive].
   - In Kyiv, the government reported them dead and announced Hero titles. That error drove the next decision [src:guardian-2022-sauer-hrybov-medal].
2. **Sending the *Sapfir* (26 Feb).** It was sent on the belief that the garrison was dead and that a body-recovery deal existed. Result: 21 more captives [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
3. **Trusting the missiles (Mar).** After the February misses, the system's operator ordered every missile checked. Luch found and fixed a common fault. Sources split over whether it was sabotage [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
4. **Fire on 13 Apr.**
   - The operator launched after minutes of consultation, on a radar contact at extreme range [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
   - **Who confirmed the target is debated.** US officials say the US identified the ship as the *Moskva* and helped confirm its location, but wasn't told of the strike in advance [src:nbc-2022-dilanian-us-intel-moskva]. UP in Nov 2022 says NATO, including a P-8, confirmed "a certain object in a certain square" [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle]. Ukrainian military sources in UP (Dec 2022) and the Navy spokesman (2025) say Ukraine found it itself [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune] [src:ukranews-2025-herasimova-navy-moskva-rescue].
   - Neizhpapa says he expected it would take at least six missiles to sink it [src:kyivpost-2023-tuzov-neptune-moskva].
5. **Russia: save the ship or the crew (13–14 Apr).** A rescue group sailed within about 30 min. The cruiser was towed toward Sevastopol rather than abandoned at once, and people were taken off "from lunch to morning" [src:ukranews-2025-herasimova-navy-moskva-rescue]. The rescue effort lasted about 6 h [src:rbcukraine-2026-court-moskva]. Russia publicly denied a strike [src:isw-2022-04-14-assessment].
6. **Russia: pull back or cover the island (late Apr – May).**
   - The big ships kept a standoff from Odesa and the Ropuchas stopped coming [src:dod-2022-04-18-background-briefing] [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-curse-snake-island].
   - Russia kept feeding SHORAD to the island by small craft, which is what the TB2s hunted [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island].
   - ISW had predicted exactly this choice: add point defence or withdraw [src:isw-2022-04-14-assessment].
7. **Ukraine: assault the island (early May).**
   - Budanov's plan was to land, destroy equipment and block the bays. The orders were explicitly not to hold the island, to avoid leaving a target [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
   - It was aborted under fire from the boat-borne Tor [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle], and from aviation and naval air defence [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island].
   - Inside Ukraine it was criticised, with some calling Budanov "a butcher" chasing a 9 May flag. UP's intelligence sources put the losses at about 10 people and a boat, and say half the equipment was destroyed [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
8. **Ukraine: switch to starve-and-shell (late May – June).** The decision was to "oust the Russians … with massive missile and artillery strikes", using the newly arrived Western 155 mm guns from the delta coast [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle]. Coastal missile and artillery forces were tasked with preventing any resupply [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island].
9. **Russia: leave (30 Jun).** Russia withdrew and framed it as goodwill toward a UN grain corridor [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island] [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment]. Milbloggers had criticised the command for not retreating sooner [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment]. Russia then bombed the island with phosphorus, which Ukraine read as denying the abandoned equipment [src:aljazeera-2022-phosphorus-snake-island] [src:wikipedia-snake-island-campaign].
10. **Ukraine: garrison it or not (Jul).** "No one will risk people for the sake of a photo" (Humeniuk) [src:twz-2022-altman-flag-snake-island]. Instead a brief special-forces landing after demining raised the flag and left [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island] [src:twz-2022-altman-flag-snake-island].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Snake Island, 24–25 Feb.** As reported by Western and Ukrainian outlets:
  - The MoD (Konashenkov) said 82 Ukrainian servicemen surrendered voluntarily, signed pledges and would be released [src:wikipedia-snake-island-campaign].
  - Russia claimed it sank 6 of 16 Ukrainian boats that attacked on 25 Feb, and accused the US of helping. The US denied it [src:wikipedia-snake-island-campaign].
- ***Moskva*, 14 Apr.**
  - The MoD said a fire detonated ammunition, the fire was contained, and the crew was evacuated. Later that day: the ship "lost stability … in the stormy sea conditions" under tow and sank. It made no mention of a strike [src:reuters-2022-moskva-sunk] [src:isw-2022-04-14-assessment].
  - On 16 Apr the MoD showed Adm. Yevmenov meeting about 100 crew [src:meduza-2022-moskva-37-dead].
  - On 22 Apr it gave 1 dead, 27 missing and 396 rescued [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva].
  - A Sevastopol court declared 17 dead in Nov 2022 [src:meduza-2022-moskva-17-declared-dead].
  - In Jan 2026 a Moscow court acknowledged the Ukrainian missile, named Shubin, found 20 dead, 24 injured and 8 missing, and claimed 2.2 bn roubles [src:rbcukraine-2026-court-moskva].
  - State-TV figures criticised the navy, and some blamed Norwegian missiles or US electronics. Russia sent the 1915 salvage ship *Kommuna* to the wreck [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva].
- **May–June.**
  - The MoD claimed to have repelled the May assault and killed "more than 50" paratroopers [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
  - It claimed every Ukrainian drone and missile attack in June was defeated. ISW: "despite considerable evidence to the contrary" [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment].
  - Russian media gave 10 missing and 23 injured on the *Vasily Bekh* [src:twz-2022-newdick-harpoon-bekh].
- **30 Jun.** The MoD said the tasks were "completed", and that the withdrawal was a "goodwill gesture" so as not to obstruct UN grain-corridor efforts [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island] [src:pravda-2022-balachuk-zaluzhnyi-bohdana].
  - Milbloggers followed the line and ignored the strikes [src:isw-2022-06-30-assessment].
  - The DoD saw "no credence" in the goodwill claim [src:dod-2022-vergun-snake-island-retreat].
- **7 Jul.** The MoD said a 05:00 flag landing was hit by missiles, "killing some of the Ukrainian troops". Ukraine denied any deaths [src:twz-2022-altman-flag-snake-island].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Three phases with different player verbs.**
  - *Act 1 (one day):* a survival and morale scene with no tactical win. The scoring gate is "garrison refuses, survives the bombardment, is captured alive". Decision 2 (the *Sapfir*) is a classic trap event to script.
  - *Act 2 (one afternoon and night):* a detection, timing and launch puzzle.
  - *Coda (about 10 weeks, compressed):* an interdiction board where the player cuts supply rather than storms the island.
- **Act 2 mechanics.** All are sourced:
  - Neptune's radar horizon is about 18 km, but a weather modifier lets it reach about 120 km under low cloud [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - The salvo is two missiles with about 6 min flight [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - The cruiser's AD readiness is a hidden state [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-moskva-negligence]. Cloud also blinds TB2s and optical satellites [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
  - The target can be confirmed by US/NATO input or not, and the debrief shows the dispute [src:nbc-2022-dilanian-us-intel-moskva].
  - The TB2 "decoy" should be an optional, contested modifier, not a requirement [src:forbes-2022-hambling-bayraktar-moskva].
- **Sinking model.** Two hits on a ship expected to need six or more [src:kyivpost-2023-tuzov-neptune-moskva]. The damage chain is fire, then AK-630 or Vulkan ammunition, then a tow in sea state 3, then a roll [src:intentpress-2025-neizhpapa-moskva-details] [src:guardian-2022-roth-moskva-photos]. Overnight pacing: about 11–13 h from launch (14:10 or about 16:00) to sinking (about 03:00) [src:pravda-2023-pohorilov-neptune-launch-video] [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva].
- **Coda win band.** Russia abandons the island, with "no Ukrainian landing needed" and "assault fails" both historical.
  - Historical Russian attrition: about 4 Raptors, 1 Serna, multiple Tor/Pantsir/Strela-10, 1 Mi-8, 1 tug [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-tb2-ghost-snake-island] [src:navalnews-2022-ozberk-vasily-bekh] [src:ukrinform-2022-invaders-escape-snake-island].
  - Ukrainian costs: 1 Mi-14, about 10 assault troops and a boat, 1 Su-24MR [src:ppu-2024-bedzai] [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:wikipedia-snake-island-campaign].
  - Penalise holding the island with troops, which is not historical [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
- **Logistics as the target.** The island's supply should be a meter fed only by small craft and tugs. Each kill raises Russia's "cost to hold" until withdrawal triggers [src:zavhorodnii-2025-battles-snake-island] [src:dod-2022-vergun-snake-island-retreat]. After the *Moskva* is gone, Russian AD "umbrella" coverage drops to point defence only [src:navalnews-2022-sutton-curse-snake-island].
- **Artillery from the delta.** Guns at maximum range (40 km) move by pontoon at night (3–4 h each way). Metal decoys add radar noise, and the guns break down [src:militarnyi-2023-bohdana-snake-island].
- **Pacing.** Act 1 runs about 16 h of real events (03:00 to about 19:00); Act 2 about 11–13 h; the coda covers 15 Apr – 30 Jun with spikes on 2–8 May, 17 Jun and 20–30 Jun.

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Launch time.** 14:10 (Butusov) or about 16:00 (UP) [src:pravda-2023-pohorilov-neptune-launch-video] [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune]. The firing position is given only as "south of Odesa Oblast" or "the coast between Odesa and Mykolaiv" [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune] [src:wikipedia-sinking-of-the-moskva]. No coordinates.
- **The Neptune unit and crew.** No Ukrainian source names them. Only a Russian court names the 406th Artillery Brigade [src:rbcukraine-2026-court-moskva]. Launcher count on 13 Apr unknown.
- **TB2 role on 13 Apr.** Unresolved: UP contradicts itself between Nov and Dec 2022 [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle] [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-moskva-neptune].
- **US role in finding the target.** Unresolved. NYT's Mar 2025 "The Partnership" was not fetched [src:ukranews-2025-herasimova-navy-moskva-rescue].
- ***Moskva* losses.** Range from 1 to about 450, and the CO's fate is disputed. Treat as unknowable.
- **The May assault.** The date (7 May vs. the night of 8–9 May), the helicopter mix and the losses (the *Stanislav*?) are inconsistent across UP, Zavhorodnii and Wikipedia. Bedzai's death on 7 May suggests 7 May [src:ppu-2024-bedzai].
- **Raptors.** How many Raptors were lost at the island is unclear (2 + 2, or 2 + 3). Oryx's war-wide total is 3 destroyed and 2 damaged [src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses].
- **Who said the line.** Contested. The Guardian has border guard Roman Hrybov [src:guardian-2022-sauer-hrybov-medal]; Ukrposhta calls him "Marine Roman Grybov" [src:ukrposhta-2022-warship-stamp]; UP says it was an unnamed border guard, not Hrybov [src:pravda-2022-romaniuk-zmiinyi-battle].
- **Garrison count and prisoner ledger.** 80 vs 82, and 28 vs "about 30" border guards. The ledger from 19 (Mar), plus 1 (29 Oct), plus 3 (24 Nov), through 40 by Apr 2023, to the last on 23 Jul 2025 is incomplete.
- **Russian garrison.** Its size and units on the island were never established.
- **Light.** Sunrise and sunset times for the key days weren't sourced (the timeanddate.com fetch was blocked); add them from an astronomical table.
- **The *Moskva*'s track** on 12–13 Apr before the strike, and the Boiko-tower coordinates, have no fetched source.
- ***Admiral Essen*.** The claimed Neptune strike date conflicts: 2 Apr (Russian court) vs 4 Apr (Ukrainian claim via Forbes).
