## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Avdiivka is a small industrial city of about 32,000 pre-war residents, under 10 km from Donetsk. It was home to the Avdiivka Coke and Chemical Plant (AKHZ, "Koksokhim"), the largest coke producer in Ukraine [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka]. It had been a fortified front-line salient since 2014, with reinforced-concrete positions, trenches and tunnels [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka]. By autumn 2023 it was already semi-encircled [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].

- **The pincer, October.** At dawn on 10 October 2023 Russia opened the offensive with what Ukrainian soldiers called its strongest fire preparation in Donbas of the war. Armoured assault groups attacked the northern flank (Krasnohorivka–Stepove–Berdychi, towards the coke plant) and the southern flank (Sieverne, Vodiane–Opytne) to encircle the garrison [src:osw-2023-ber-battle-avdiivka-day-596] [src:isw-2023-10-11-roca]. A second wave followed on 19–20 October. Russia lost at least 109 vehicles to satellite count in the first ten days [src:frontelligence-2023-avdiivka-losses]. ISW judged these losses larger than those at Vuhledar [src:isw-2023-10-26-roca].
- **The grind, November–January.** Russia switched to dismounted infantry assaults [src:isw-2023-11-04-roca]. It took the waste heap (terrikon) north of the city in late October [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] and the fortified southeastern industrial zone ("promka") between mid-November and early December [src:isw-2023-11-14-roca] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. The coke plant held [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **The two breaches.** About 15 January Russian troops came up a service-water pipe behind the "Tsarska Okhota" strongpoint in the south. In early February they infiltrated through the northern dachas between the quarry and the lakes, bypassing the coke plant [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **The neck closes.** On 12–14 February the Russians reached Industrialnyi Avenue and cut the city in two, with the coke plant to the north and the centre to the south. The main supply road through Lastochkyne was lost [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Air and ammunition.** Glide bombs fell at 30–50 a day by early February (UK MoD) and about 60 a day by mid-February (Ukrainian units) [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. Ukrainian guns were starved by the stalled US supplemental [src:bbc-2024-lukiv-us-warns-avdiivka] [src:liga-2025-shell-ratio].
- **The last reserve.** The 3rd Assault Brigade was rushed in, and the exhausted 110th Mechanised Brigade began to rotate out [src:kyivindependent-2024-khalilova-reinforcing-avdiivka] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **The withdrawal.** Zenit, the southern bastion, was abandoned on 14–15 February. On the night of 16–17 February the new commander-in-chief, Oleksandr Syrskyi, announced the withdrawal "to avoid encirclement" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:isw-2024-02-16-roca]. It was completed on 17 February [src:pravda-2024-lykhovii-tavria-losses].

**The outcome the player must reproduce:**
- **Avdiivka holds about four months.** The battle runs from 10 October 2023 to the withdrawal on 17 February 2024 [src:euromaidanpress-2024-tril-avdiivka].
- **The coke plant holds to the end.** The 25th Assault Battalion of the 47th Mechanised Brigade was the last to leave it. The 3rd Assault Brigade had largely evacuated it by 05:00 on 17 February, and Ukrainian troops said no one was left behind there [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **No operational encirclement.** ISW and the Ukrainian command say the garrison withdrew "in mainly good order" [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca] [src:pravda-2024-tarnavskyi-soldiers-captured]. The withdrawal was nonetheless late and costly in places, above all at Zenit [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot].
- **Russian territorial gain is small.** The Russian MoD claimed an 8.6 km advance in depth [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca] and about 32 km² [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. Ukrainska Pravda says the line moved back 7–13 km [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. In the first two months the gain was only 1.5–2 km, per the Tavria group [src:kyivindependent-2023-fornusek-2km-20000].
- **The Russian cost is contested.** The band runs from about 16,000 to 47,000 or more men.
  - *Ukrainian claims.* Tarnavskyi: over 47,000 personnel, 364 tanks, 748 AFVs, 248 artillery systems and 5 aircraft [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca]. "Almost 50,000" [src:euromaidanpress-2024-tril-avdiivka]. 17,000 killed and 30,000 wounded (Tavria, via The Insider) [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].
  - *Russian sources.* The milblogger Andrei "Murz" Morozov put irrecoverable losses at 16,000 and lost equipment at 300 units [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca] [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].
  - *Independent counts.* The BBC Russian Service confirmed 6,641 Russian dead by name, a floor rather than an estimate [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka]. A US estimate put Russian killed and wounded at 13,000 for October–December alone [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].
  - *ISW's summary.* Ukrainian and Russian estimates range from 16,000 to 47,000 [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca].
  - *Vehicles, visually confirmed.* Naalsio counted 690 lost by 23 February: 465 destroyed, 197 abandoned and 28 damaged [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka]. The Insider puts Russian equipment losses at about nine times Ukraine's [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].
- **Ukrainian losses in the withdrawal are contested.**
  - *Ukrainian position.* Tarnavskyi admitted that "a certain number" were captured at the final stage [src:pravda-2024-tarnavskyi-soldiers-captured]. Lykhovii said the number was not in the "hundreds" and the day's losses were "similar to the average daily losses" [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot] [src:pravda-2024-lykhovii-tavria-losses].
  - *Estimates.* US officials told the Washington Post that Ukrainian officials privately estimated about 100 captured (via LWJ) [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. Mordvichev claimed about 200 surrendered, with 100 more expected [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
  - *The high end.* The NYT cited two soldiers saying 850–1,000 were captured or unaccounted for, a figure that includes the killed, wounded and missing [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca]. Shoigu claimed over 1,500 Ukrainian losses in 24 hours [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment].
  - *Assessment.* ISW saw no footage supporting mass captures [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca]. LWJ judges that "the bulk of the Ukrainian force ... escaped the pocket" [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Aftermath.** Lastochkyne fell on 24–26 February, and Stepove and Sieverne on 27 February [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:kyivindependent-2024-hodunova-stepove-sieverne]. Tarnavskyi said the line stabilised on Tonenke–Orlivka–Berdychi [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. Ukraine claimed 10 Su-34s, 2 Su-35s and an A-50 in February [src:pravda-2024-february-13-aircraft].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Footprint.** The diorama is a horseshoe salient about 12–15 km wide, projecting about 10 km into Russian lines [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka] [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal]. A box of about 20 × 15 km holds it: from Berdychi and Krasnohorivka in the north to Opytne and Vodiane in the south, and from Tonenke and Orlivka in the west to Kamianka and the industrial zone in the east. In November Kyiv Post described the central pocket as "funnel-shaped ... less than 15 kilometers wide" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district].

**Terrain.**
- **The ground.** The salient is flat and open apart from the coal and coke spoil heaps [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka]. The open field between Stepove and the railway was a kill zone [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. Treelines and concrete walls along the rails north of the coke plant gave Ukraine fire control [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-avdiivka-2023-11-26].
- **The terrikon.**
  - *Height.* Euromaidan Press calls it a "forty-meter-high slag heap" [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka]. Kyiv Post's "some 150 meters tall" is not supported by SRTM, which shows a top of about 231 m against about 180–200 m around the base, so about 30–50 m of relief [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka] [src:opentopodata-srtm30m].
  - *Its role.* Its defenders "saw every Russian move from the heights", and its steep face stopped assaults [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-spoil-tip].
- **The coke plant.** Its tunnels, railways and alleys made it "near-perfect" defensive ground. Hundreds of Ukrainian troops fell back into it in January, and it reportedly held about 1,000 troops in the last days [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. Barabash said bombing had left it "almost completely destroyed", so it was "not as easy to hold ... as when it was like a fortress" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district].
- **The industrial zone (promka).** Russian sources say it sits on a slight hill, from which artillery could be adjusted onto the city [src:isw-2023-11-26-roca] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district]. SRTM gives about 248 m there against about 219 m at the town node [src:opentopodata-srtm30m].
- **The pipe.** It is the main service-water pipe that fed the Donetsk filtration station, about 1.3–1.4 m high, and was mostly empty because the city's water had been cut since late 2021 [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. Russian sources describe a flooded 0.5 m drainage pipe and a 2 km infiltration [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. United24 gives 3.7 km and up to 1.4 m [src:united24-2026-kabachynskyi-pipelines].
- **Water.** Lakes and the flooded quarry lie north and northeast of the centre [src:isw-2024-02-06-roca] [src:osm-2026-avdiivka-geodata]. West of the city, the water between Berdychi, Semenivka and Orlivka was expected to slow the advance after the fall [src:isw-2024-02-27-roca].

**Roads and supply.**
- **The O0542 (H0542).** This was the main supply route and the only paved road in. It ran from the west through Lastochkyne, past the famous city sign, into Industrialnyi Avenue [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **The Avdiivka–Sieverne road.** The secondary route, running past the 9th quarter [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Dirt tracks to Lastochkyne and Sieverne.** These were the last routes in February [src:isw-2024-02-15-roca].
- **The O0562.** A GLOC 3.3 km from the southern Russian line on 12 Oct [src:isw-2023-10-12-roca].
- **The N20.** The Donetsk–Kostiantynivka highway, partly Russian-held from the start [src:isw-2023-10-11-roca].
- **Rail.** The Avdiivka–Pokrovsk railway runs past Stepove and the plant [src:osw-2023-ber-battle-avdiivka-day-596].

**Weather and light.** ERA5 reanalysis for 48.14 N 37.74 E, Kyiv local time; reanalysis, not observations [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-avdiivka-2023-24].
- **10–12 Oct 2023 (the first wave).** Dry and mostly clear (cloud 13–55%), lows about 0 °C, highs 7–12 °C. Sunrise about 06:40, sunset about 17:50.
- **19–20 Oct (the second wave).** Dry, warming to 18 °C, overcast by 20 Oct, with wind gusting to 33 km/h.
- **Late October to November.** Rain on 26 and 28 Oct. Milbloggers blamed rain for the lull on 26 Oct [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. In November mud made many roads impassable and fog cut aerial reconnaissance [src:isw-2023-11-13-roca]. Morning fog gave windows for infiltration [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-rain-fog].
- **19–27 Nov.** First snow (about 2 cm on 19 Nov), a frost to −7 °C on 23 Nov, then 25 mm of rain on 25–26 Nov and gusts to 38 km/h on 27 Nov. Barabash spoke of "blowing snow and high winds" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district].
- **January 2024.** Mostly around or below freezing and overcast, with light snow (sampled 11, 15 and 20 Jan). Kirby expected the frozen ground of late January and February to favour mechanised attack [src:isw-2023-12-21-roca].
- **5–13 Feb.** Snow on 5 Feb (about 4 cm), then a thaw: 15 mm of rain on 10 Feb and highs of 9–14 °C by 11–14 Feb, under 100% cloud. Fog on 10–11 Feb [src:euromaidanpress-2024-aerial-bombs-encirclement]. Barabash said the ground was too soft for tanks [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
- **15–17 Feb (the withdrawal).** A dry hard frost returns: lows of −2 to −6 °C, highs of 2–3 °C, mostly overcast. Sunrise about 07:35, sunset about 17:50. The Zenit walk-out was "pitch black" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. A Zenit veteran says it was "foggy" at the time evacuation should have happened [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].

## 4. Forces

### Russia

**Command.** The Central Grouping of Forces under Col. Gen. Andrei Mordvichev, whom Putin congratulated [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. Lt. Gen. Sergei Milchakov reportedly led the 1st DNR Army Corps; milbloggers complained he went unrecognised [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca]. Formal responsibility for the axis passed to the Central Grouping after the fall [src:isw-2024-02-27-roca].

**Formations** (as credited by Putin on 17 Feb, plus units reported during the battle):
- **1st DNR Army Corps (8th CAA, SMD).** The 1st ("Slavic"), 9th, 110th, 114th and 132nd Motor Rifle Brigades, the 1454th and 1487th Regiments and the 10th Tank Regiment. Irregular units were attached: "Kluny", Pyatnashka, Dikaya Diviziya and Arbat (including ex-Wagner men) [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment] [src:isw-2023-10-22-roca] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:isw-2023-10-19-roca] [src:isw-2023-10-28-roca] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
  - *The 114th* lost, per Kyiv Post, 190 dead and 160 wounded in October from 1,800–2,000 men [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
  - *The 1487th* was reduced to under 30% strength [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca].
- **41st CAA (CMD).** The 35th, 55th and 74th Motor Rifle Brigades, and the 90th Tank Division (6th, 80th and 239th Tank Regiments) [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. Ukraine claimed the 90th lost 2,500–3,000 men and 250 armoured vehicles by 12 Nov [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
- **2nd CAA (CMD).** The 15th, 21st and 30th Motor Rifle Brigades, moved laterally from the Lyman direction [src:isw-2023-10-28-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2024-fornusek-3rd-assault-15000].
- **Volunteer Corps.** The 60th "Veterany" Reconnaissance-Assault Brigade, which led the pipe infiltration [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. HUR says the corps of about 18,000 includes ex-Wagner men and uses Wagner-style assaults [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment].
- **Penal and special units.** Storm-Z units lost 40–70% [src:isw-2023-10-30-roca]. Storm-V units [src:ukrpravda-2024-isw-storm-v]. GRU spetsnaz and SSO ran night assaults and directed strikes [src:kyivindependent-2024-fornusek-3rd-assault-15000] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. The 24th Spetsnaz Brigade reached the city administration [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Air.** Su-25s close in; Su-34s and Su-35s with glide bombs from stand-off range [src:isw-2023-10-19-roca] [src:kyivpost-2024-orlova-six-aircraft-72h].

**Strength, as claimed.**
- *Ukrainian estimates of the Russian force.* About 40,000 (Shtupun, 29 Oct, and again in January) [src:isw-2023-10-29-roca] [src:isw-2024-01-21-roca]. About 50,000 (Lykhovii, Feb) [src:isw-2024-02-15-roca]. About 15,000 assault troops in the 3rd Assault's sector [src:kyivindependent-2024-fornusek-3rd-assault-15000].
- *Ratios in the final weeks.* Foka of the 3rd Assault: four Russian brigades rotating with two in reserve, and odds of 1:12 in infantry and 1:10 in artillery and ammunition [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. 3rd Assault statements: 7:1 [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal].
- *Forbes.* Two field armies of about 40,000 against about 10,000 defenders [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90].
- *Equipment.* The vehicle fleet was mostly Soviet-era. Only about 22% of tank losses were modern T-72B3, T-80BVM or T-90 types; 65 T-80BVs were lost; and 77 of 173 lost IFVs were BMP-1s [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].

**How it changed.**
- **October.** Regiment-plus mechanised waves [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **November–January.** Infantry assaults of 5–7 men at night, then attacks at sunrise [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
- **January.** The pipe and the southern residential block [src:isw-2024-01-25-roca].
- **February.** Armour returned inside the city, supporting the infantry [src:isw-2024-02-12-roca] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Russian mechanised waves (Oct).** Columns of 20–40 armoured vehicles led by tanks, 100–200 vehicles in all, drove across open ground into minefields [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka].
  - *What stopped them.* Drone spotting, mines, FPVs, ATGMs and artillery. Russian counter-battery fire and coordination between units were poor [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:isw-2023-10-18-roca].
  - *What followed.* A shift to dismounted assaults [src:isw-2023-11-04-roca] [src:frontelligence-2023-avdiivka-losses].
- **Ukrainian defence in depth.**
  - *Fortifications.* Concrete positions linked by tunnels [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka].
  - *Fires.* Cluster munitions to drive infantry into cover, then artillery to flush them out [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka].
  - *Bradleys.* Their 25 mm guns raked treelines and dismounting infantry [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-avdiivka-2023-12-07].
  - *Shell economy.* Artillery was saved for armour and guns, and FPVs were used against infantry [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
- **Glide bombs (KAB, FAB with UMPK).** ISW calls Avdiivka the first time Russia used them at scale to support advancing infantry, under "limited and localized air superiority" [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment].
  - *Rates.* 30–50 a day by 5 Feb (UK MoD) [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. 60 a day (3rd Assault, 16 Feb; a brigade, 17 Feb) [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal] [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. 80–110 a day (Foka) [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. Up to 500 "in recent days" [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment].
  - *Doctrine.* ISW later withdrew the term "close air support", since no close coordination with ground units was observed [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment].
  - *Effects.* Most troops in the city suffered concussions [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. New field positions could not be built under bombing without air defence [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
  - *The counter.* Shooting down the carriers (see the set pieces). After the second A-50 kill the carriers "no longer dare[d] to fly too close" [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50].
- **The shell famine.**
  - *Ratios.* A 110th gunner on 15 Feb: "we have two shells, but ... no charges ... we have run out of shells" [src:bbc-2024-lukiv-us-warns-avdiivka]. Troops reported 5:1 in February and 8–9:1 at worst in late November [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. The ratio reached 1:10 in February 2024 [src:liga-2025-shell-ratio].
  - *The cause.* The US supplemental was stalled from October 2023. The Senate passed the $95bn package that week, but the House only acted in April [src:bbc-2024-lukiv-us-warns-avdiivka] [src:crs-2026-if12040-us-security-assistance].
- **Infiltration and tunnelling (RU).** These evolved from a 160 m mine tunnel under a position (Nov) [src:isw-2023-11-04-roca] to the January pipe infiltration [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
  - *How it worked.* Oxygen cylinders for cutting torches, exit holes every 100 m, and noise covered by mortars [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
  - *How Ukraine answered.* Ukraine spotted the gathering at the pipe entry and shelled it, containing the first breach [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:kyivindependent-2025-zadorozhnyy-pipeline-tunnels]. But the same pipe was reused to reach Vinogradniki-2 on 15 Feb [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Infiltration under fog (RU).** Fog on 10–11 Feb and in November grounded Ukrainian drones [src:euromaidanpress-2024-aerial-bombs-encirclement] [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-rain-fog].
- **EW.** Russian armour was fitted with EW against drones by February [src:isw-2024-02-06-roca]. In the last days jamming cut Ukrainian links to higher echelons [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Air defence (UA).** Before the shoot-down spree, attack aircraft forced in close were the ones lost: five in 10 days, and seven Su-25s in a month [src:isw-2023-10-19-roca] [src:pravda-2023-zaluzhnyi-10000-avdiivka]. Troops in the city asked "Make it so that the enemy's aircraft does not fly" [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal].
- **Withdrawal under pressure (UA).** Units moved in small groups, often on foot along dirt roads, with drones guiding them and artillery covering. Tavria fired almost 400 fire missions to cover it [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:pravda-2024-tarnavskyi-soldiers-captured]. MWI calls it a "textbook example" of a security force covering the main body [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The first wave (10–12 Oct).** Dawn fire preparation, then columns of 20–40 vehicles roll across flat fields at the salient's shoulders. A 53rd Brigade video shows one column losing two vehicles and 100 men in ten minutes [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka]. An AFV is filmed falling into water [src:isw-2023-10-12-roca]. The scale: 70 vehicles in one day [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
2. **Storming the terrikon (~16 Oct).** Tanks line up at the foot of a 40 m wall of spoil while infantry try to climb it under machine guns and drone drops. One soldier gets three quarters of the way to the first terrace [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-spoil-tip]. By late October the heap is Russian, but Ukrainian fire stops them massing on it [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka].
3. **The coke plant under bombs.** The anchor, bombed to ruins but never taken. "The Russians were just banging their heads against the plant" (Samurai) [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. A Presidential Brigade soldier: they "kept throwing themselves at the coke plant, leaving piles of their corpses there" [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
4. **The promka falls (Nov).** A fortified hill of factories held since 2014 is ground away building by building near Yasynuvata-2, while Russian armour moves "freely" in its northern part [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district] [src:isw-2023-11-26-roca].
5. **The pipe (mid-Jan).** Men with oxygen cylinders and torches cut exits every 100 m under a mortar barrage, then emerge behind Tsarska Okhota [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:kyivpost-2024-york-graveyard-armour]. Ukrainian counterattacks retake some ground [src:isw-2024-01-25-roca]. See c17 for the mechanic.
6. **The Bradley duels (Nov–Jan).** See vignette v02. A T-90M is blinded by 25 mm fire at Stepove, and one Bradley kills three MT-LBs in about 30 seconds [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts] [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb].
7. **Cutting the neck (12–15 Feb).** Infiltration through the dachas in fog, the jump across the railway, then Industrialnyi Avenue and the "Brevno" restaurant at the O0542 junction [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
8. **The 3rd Assault's counterattack.** The brigade pushes back to the railway but cannot hold under artillery. "F**k Avdiivka, we'd rather fight in Mariupol again" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
9. **Leaving Zenit (13–15 Feb).** The order comes at 21:00. Men walk 1–2.5 km by night through a 120 m gap, following the sound of a drone. Six wounded and a Russian POW stay in the bunker, and the evacuation vehicle never comes [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. For the debrief: the later execution video [src:kyivpost-2024-zenit-pows-executed].
10. **The last night (16–17 Feb).** Syrskyi's announcement at about 01:00. The plant is emptied by 05:00, with maps and documents destroyed. The 9th quarter is held until dawn. A convoy is hit on the "road of death" [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
11. **Bombers falling (17–29 Feb).** Two Su-34s and a Su-35 on day one, then more Su-34s on 18, 19, 27 and 29 Feb, an A-50 on 23 Feb, and Cospas-Sarsat beacons in the Sea of Azov [src:kyivpost-2024-orlova-six-aircraft-72h] [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-su34-beacon] [src:pravda-2024-february-13-aircraft].

## 7. Decision points

- **Preparing for October (UA).** General Staff spokesman Kovaliov said Ukraine knew about the assault in advance and was ready [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. Kyiv moved the 47th from Zaporizhzhia to the northern flank in October, though it had little time to recover from the counteroffensive [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. The sources link its Bradleys to repeated defensive successes at Stepove [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove].
- **Russia changes method, twice.**
  - *After the October losses.* Mechanised waves gave way to infantry assaults [src:isw-2023-11-04-roca].
  - *From January.* Russia stopped attacking the coke plant and switched to the south and to the residential north. Samurai: "Perhaps that's why they changed the direction of their main attack" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:kyivpost-2024-york-graveyard-armour]. ISW reads this as abandoning encirclement for a "tactical turning movement" through the town [src:isw-2024-01-25-roca] [src:isw-2024-02-15-roca].
- **Watching the pipe (UA).** Ukrainian SSO soldiers said on 6 Feb: "The Russians are still using it ... Why our forces don't monitor it, I don't know". Ukrainska Pravda asked Tavria whether pipe control had been part of defence planning, and received no answer [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Committing the last reserve (UA).**
  - *Who ordered it.* The 3rd Assault was ordered in by Zaluzhnyi, so before 8 Feb [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. Syrskyi was also reported moving reserves on 9 Feb [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
  - *What it was for.* This is disputed. Foka, Butusov and an LWJ source say it was to retake and hold [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. A 3rd Brigade officer and MWI say it was the security force for the 110th's withdrawal [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal].
  - *How Ukrainians saw it at the time.* The volunteer commander Yevhen Dykyi gave it "two or three days" to clear the highway, or else it would be "organizing a corridor" [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal].
  - *What followed.* The counterattack reached the railway but could not hold [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Zenit: when to leave, and the wounded (UA).**
  - *The order.* It came on 13 Feb, "on their own" [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot].
  - *The wounded.* The 110th says it could not reach Zenit and asked the POW coordination centre to have Russia evacuate them. Russia agreed, and the men were then shot [src:kyivpost-2024-zenit-pows-executed].
  - *The soldiers' view.* Survivors say "instead of getting us out, they sent reinforcements" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. Two say no more than 40% of their company got out [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot].
  - *The brigade's view.* It says the losses were "minimal" [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot].
- **Timing the withdrawal (UA): the central decision.** On the night of 16–17 Feb Syrskyi, nine days in post, ordered it and announced it before it was complete, which was unusual compared with Sievierodonetsk and Bakhmut [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
  - *Too late.* Most of UP's sources said a day or two earlier [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. Four 110th soldiers and Western experts called it "late and disorganized" [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot]. LWJ says it "took too long to commence" [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
  - *In time.* Samurai and the 47th's 25th Battalion say they left without losses [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka]. ISW saw a "relatively controlled withdrawal" [src:isw-2024-02-16-roca]. MWI says Syrskyi's intent to preserve combat power was met [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal].
- **A second line (UA).** LWJ says Ukraine failed to build a solid line behind the city, which forced digging under fire [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. ISW on 18 Feb said imagery "does not support claims" that there were no prepared positions [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca].
- **Holding out vs husbanding.** Euromaidan Press framed the debate against Bakhmut, where Syrskyi was criticised for defending strategically unimportant towns [src:euromaidanpress-2024-aerial-bombs-encirclement]. Lykhovii said Avdiivka "has fulfilled its mission of draining the Russian reserve" [src:pravda-2024-lykhovii-tavria-losses].
- **US aid (outside the player's control).** Kirby tied the risk of falling directly to Congress's failure to pass the supplemental [src:bbc-2024-lukiv-us-warns-avdiivka]. ISW judged the delays helped Russia on several axes [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **The goals.** DNR nationalists long argued that taking Avdiivka would push Ukrainian guns out of range of Donetsk [src:isw-2024-02-15-roca]. Bielieskov and the Washington Post noted its value as a springboard towards Pokrovsk [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. ISW saw the timing as a pre-election victory for Putin, and called the fall "not ... operationally significant" [src:isw-2024-02-15-roca].
- **Controlling the story.** In mid-October milbloggers were told to stop detailing the fighting [src:isw-2023-10-13-roca]. They largely ignored the October vehicle losses, unlike the outcry after Vuhledar [src:isw-2023-10-12-roca]. Russian soldiers complained of poor counter-battery fire, foul trenches and a lack of surgeons and body bags in Donetsk [src:isw-2023-10-18-roca] [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-spoil-tip].
- **Claims of victory.** Shoigu reported "full control" on 17 Feb [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. The MoD claimed 8.6 km [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca]. Shoigu called it a success "with minimal losses". Gerasimov said it took "a fairly short time" after "a long period of preparation" [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca]. Putin credited the units listed in section 4 [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment].
- **The withdrawal narrative.** Shoigu claimed 1,500 Ukrainian losses in 24 hours, and milbloggers described a chaotic flight. ISW saw only a handful of POW videos and called the lack of footage "very unusual" [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment]. ISW calls the MoD's later campaign about a "chaotic" withdrawal an information operation [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca]. Mordvichev claimed 200 surrendered [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Dissent.** "Murz" (4th Brigade, 2nd LNR Corps) reported 16,000 irrecoverable losses. He was ordered to delete it and died by suicide on 21 Feb [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca]. Before that he had noted the tank units "distinguished" themselves by advancing a few kilometres in four months [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca]. Milbloggers also complained the MoD ignored Milchakov and the "Veterany" brigade [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca].
- **Aircraft losses.** Milbloggers blamed friendly air defence for the Su-34 and A-50 losses [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment].
- **The pipe.** Russian channels claimed 150 special-operations troops used a 0.5 m pipe for 2 km [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. The "Voenniy Osvedomitel" channel showed video from the tunnel on 25 Jan [src:kyivpost-2024-york-graveyard-armour].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **The win band.**
  - *Must happen.* The town holds until about 17 Feb (±2 days) and the coke plant is the last position given up. The withdrawal must avoid encirclement: no pocket of thousands, and captives in the band from about 100 to a few hundred [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:isw-2024-02-18-roca].
  - *Cost.* The Russian cost should land in the 16,000–47,000 casualty band and about 300–700 vehicles [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca] [src:theinsider-2024-meat-grinder-avdiivka].
  - *Failure (design choice).* An early withdrawal leaves Russian losses below the historical band. Staying long after the cut of the O0542 risks the "second Azovstal" that soldiers feared [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Three phases, three tempos.**
  - *October.* Two mechanised waves (10–12 and 19–20 Oct), which the defender should win heavily. Model about 100–200 Russian vehicles lost [src:frontelligence-2023-avdiivka-losses].
  - *November–January.* An infantry grind, with day ticks and slow loss of the flanks: the terrikon, then the promka, then Stepove [src:isw-2023-11-04-roca].
  - *February.* Collapse in about two weeks once the neck is cut [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Shell rationing is the player's main lever.** A shell budget falls towards 1:10 by February [src:liga-2025-shell-ratio]. Saving shells for armour and using FPVs on infantry is the historical policy [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal]. US aid is a scripted event that does not arrive in the window [src:crs-2026-if12040-us-security-assistance].
- **KAB pressure as a rising global meter.** About 30–50 a day by early February, about 60 a day at the end, with day-long pauses (19–20 Jan) [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment] [src:isw-2024-01-21-roca]. Bombed positions lose fortification value permanently, as the coke plant did [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district]. Shooting down a carrier gives only brief relief; the February spree came after the fall [src:pravda-2024-february-13-aircraft].
- **Hidden-axis events.** The pipe (mid-Jan) and the fog-covered dacha infiltration (early Feb) are scripted surprises. The player can blunt them by spending ISR on the pipe entry, which is what contained the first breach [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka].
- **Unit exhaustion.** The 110th's fatigue should degrade it until rotation [src:kyivindependent-2024-khalilova-reinforcing-avdiivka]. The 3rd Assault arrives as a one-time reserve card in early to mid February [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **The withdrawal mini-game.** Order it, set the security force (3rd Assault, 53rd), choose routes (dirt roads, the Sieverne road), decide on medevac, then run the night [src:mwi-2024-forte-avdiivka-withdrawal] [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis]. Zenit is a scripted dilemma with no good answer [src:kyivindependent-2024-terajima-avdiivka-wounded-on-foot].
- **Weather.** October is dry, so drones work well. November brings mud and fog, which favour infiltration. January is frozen, which favours armour. On 10–11 Feb fog grounds drones at the worst moment [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-avdiivka-2023-24] [src:euromaidanpress-2024-aerial-bombs-encirclement].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Zenit's coordinates.** The base is not tagged in OSM. Its position is known only as "1–2 km south" of the city, east of Opytne, near the "Cheburashka" crossroads on the Donetsk ring road [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka]. Descriptions also disagree on whether it is "southeastern" or "south-western" [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-zenit-withdrawal].
- **The date the terrikon fell.** Russian claims run from 12 Oct to 28 Oct, ISW saw no full control by either side on 26 Oct, and Kremlin channels claimed it again in late November [src:isw-2023-10-12-roca] [src:isw-2023-10-26-roca] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-factory-district]. Who held it in October is also contested: the 129th TD per Kyiv Post, the 110th per Euromaidan Press [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka] [src:euromaidan-2023-avdiivka-spoil-tip].
- **The 3rd Assault's arrival** ranges from 3 to 15 Feb (section 2), and its mission is disputed (section 7).
- **Ukrainian losses.** No official Ukrainian count of the whole battle or of the withdrawal exists in these sources. The 850–1,000 figure (NYT) versus about 100 (US officials via WaPo) is unresolved [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **The pipe's dimensions and route** conflict across sources: 0.5 m and 2 km, 1.3–1.4 m, or 3.7 km [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-last-days-avdiivka] [src:united24-2026-kabachynskyi-pipelines]. The entry and exit points are not geolocated here.
- **KAB counts** come from units and milbloggers. There is no single series, and the UK MoD's 30–50 a day reaches us only through Wikipedia [src:wikipedia-battle-of-avdiivka].
- **Kyiv Post's "25th Combined Arms Army" for the October assault** conflicts with ISW's 8th CAA and 1st AC [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-worst-defeat-avdiivka] [src:isw-2023-10-11-roca]. LWJ places 25th CAA vehicles there only later [src:lwj-2024-hardie-avdiivka-analysis].
- **Satellite imagery** of the destroyed plant and the city was not found in this pass. Commercial imagery (Maxar, Planet) is not cited here.
- **Pre-battle shell stocks**, and the actual number of tubes and drones with each Ukrainian brigade, are not in these sources.
