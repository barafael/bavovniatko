## 1. Summary and historical outcome

**Why this night.** It is the best-documented night of the 2026 strike war on the capital, and it shows every layer of the problem the chapter is about:
- **Scale and mix.** The Air Force counted 570 weapons: 74 missiles of five types and 496 drones of five types. Kyiv was the main target [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets][src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **A record ballistic salvo.** 28 ballistic missiles flew at Kyiv, the most ever in one attack on the capital (Ihnat) [src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed].
- **Many jet drones.** The Air Force named the large number of jet-powered drones as a defining feature [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:isw-2026-07-02-roca].
- **The Patriot shortage of e9b.** The night came at its height [src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-zelensky-patriot-140][src:atlanticcouncil-2026-kuskevics-patriot].
- **Good data.** The type-by-type counts are published, the alerts are timestamped to the minute, and the damage is reported district by district.

**Alternatives** (no dossier, but documented):
- **7 Sep 2025.** The record drone night of 2025: 810 drones, 4 ballistic and 9 cruise missiles across Ukraine. The Cabinet of Ministers building was hit for the first time. In Kyiv, Sviatoshynskyi and Darnytskyi were struck and two people were killed [src:wikipedia-kyiv-strikes-2022-present][src:isis-2026-anokhin-shahed-2025]. It is the better choice for a drone-saturation chapter with a working Patriot shield, but it has few missiles and was spread across the whole country.
- **19 Jul 2026.** The record ballistic barrage: 41 missiles, 35 of them ballistic (25 Iskander-M/S-400 and 10 Zircon), fired at Kyiv in under an hour, plus 125 drones. Only 18 missiles were downed. One person was killed [src:euromaidan-2026-murdoch-19-july-ballistic]. It is a pure ballistic-defence puzzle with few drones.

**What happened.**
- **Warning.** On 1 July Zelensky warned from Dublin of an imminent massed strike and flew home [src:pravda-2026-oliynyk-zelensky-warning].
- **Drone prelude.** From about 19:30, groups of Shahed-type and jet drones entered Kyiv Oblast from the north, east and south. Jet drones were over the city from about 22:50, and a drone set the historic Zektser building on Shevchenko Boulevard alight [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:pravda-2026-lobanok-zektser-building].
- **Ballistic salvo.** At 01:12 the Air Force warned of a ballistic threat from the north. From 01:53 to about 02:31 waves of ballistic missiles, Zircons among them, came in from Kursk Oblast (via Sumy and Chernihiv oblasts) and Bryansk Oblast (via Chernihiv Oblast) [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed].
- **Cruise wave.** About 02:50–03:50 groups of cruise missiles crossed Sumy and southern Chernihiv oblasts and reached Kyiv from the north, east and south-east [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv].
- **Dawn tail.** Banderol munitions and jet drones kept coming until morning, many of them down the Kyiv Reservoir from the north [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **The defence.** Aviation, surface-to-air missile (SAM) troops, electronic warfare (EW), unmanned-systems units and mobile fire groups downed or jammed 524 of the 570 weapons. But only 4 of the 28 ballistic missiles were stopped [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets][src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **The damage.** Hits were recorded in every district of Kyiv. In Darnytskyi a missile brought down the end section of a nine-storey block. The final toll was 31 killed and 102 injured in the city [src:pravda-2026-balachuk-30-sites][src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:pravda-2026-oliynyk-death-toll-31].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**The Russian package.**
- **Everything at once.** The Air Force's "distinctive feature": many weapon types used simultaneously from many directions, with many ballistic missiles and jet drones [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july]. Aviation analyst Dolintsev says using many weapon types in the same time window wears down the defence and makes the problem "exceptionally complex" [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv].
- **The sequence**, about 16 hours long:
  1. a drone and jet-drone prelude from about 19:30;
  2. a ballistic salvo from about 01:50 to 02:30 (with Zircons);
  3. cruise missiles from about 02:50 to 03:50;
  4. Banderols and jet drones until about 11:00.

  [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv]
- **Short ballistic warning.** Bryansk and Kursk are about 400 km from Kyiv [src:osm-photon-c18]. The alert "two missiles from Kursk Oblast" (01:58) was followed by "approaching Kyiv" two minutes later [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **Jet drones against the cheap layer.** They outrun interceptor drones and stress mobile fire groups, so missiles or fighters must be spent on them [src:pravda-2026-ihnat-jet-drones][src:isw-2026-07-02-roca].
- **EW adaptation.** Almost all the Shahed-types aimed at Kyiv flew on a new 3,900–4,100 MHz band, an apparent attempt to outpace Ukrainian EW (Beskrestnov) [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca]. From 2025 Gerans and Gerberas also used mesh modems [src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-xk-f358].
- **Decoys and saturation.** Parodiya and Gerbera decoys flew in the mix [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets]. Gerbera has no modem or camera, so it holds a stable altitude and does not "trigger the alarm" [src:pravda-2026-konyk-gerbera-fuel-tanks].

**The Ukrainian defence.**
- **Ration Patriot for ballistic missiles.**
  - Zelensky's rule of thumb: two interceptors per ballistic missile (140 for 70) [src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-zelensky-patriot-140].
  - Under shortage, Ukrainian crews moved to *one* interceptor per ballistic missile. They also used manual mode so as not to waste interceptors on drones, shoot-and-scoot, and decoys at about $30,000 each (NYT via EMP) [src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-patriot-playbook].
  - The MoD says NATO After Action Review practice "more than doubled" Patriot effectiveness against manoeuvring Iskanders [src:pravda-2026-bondarieva-fedorov-patriot-appeal].
  - The result that night: 4 of 28 ballistic missiles stopped [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets].
  - The 2026 trend: ballistic intercepts fell to 10–15% [src:csis-2026-jensen-two-strategic-attacks], with 0–50% per salvo [src:euronews-2026-vakulina-zero-intercepts].
- **Cheap layers for the piston drones.**
  - Interceptor drones, mobile fire groups, guns, helicopters and EW took 476 of 496 drones [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets].
  - Ihnat kept the interception rate "above 90%" despite the jets [src:pravda-2026-ihnat-jet-drones].
  - For comparison, Zelensky put the September 2026 figures at 57% for jet drones and 70% for all drones [src:united24-2026-popilnichenko-jet-57].
- **Missiles and fighters for cruise missiles and jets.** 44 of 46 cruise and air-launched missiles were downed [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets]. The kill per layer (SAM, fighter or gun) is not published.
- **Teaming F-16s and Patriot.** A week later, three F-16s and a Patriot battery ambushed a Su-35 (Russian channels: the Patriot radar cued the fighters) [src:euromaidan-2026-axe-f16-patriot-su35]. This shows how scarce assets were combined in mid-2026; it was not part of this night.
- **Civil defence as a layer.** Zelensky's public warning filled the shelters: a record in the metro, and shelters fuller than ever [src:pravda-2026-balachuk-metro-52500][src:pravda-2026-kutielieva-mathernova-hell].

**What got through, and what it hit.**
- **Ballistic missiles** caused the mass-casualty damage: the Darnytskyi block, a crater by a kindergarten, the Nyvky crater [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed][src:pravda-2026-kovalenko-kyiv-aftermath].
- **Drones** caused the hotel fire [src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed].
- **Debris from intercepts** caused the market fire [src:pravda-2026-vakuliuk-kyiv-overnight-damage].

## 6. Set pieces

- **The warning (1 July, afternoon).** In Dublin, the president says a massive strike is coming and flies home. WOG closes its pumps at 21:00. That night 52,500 people go down into the metro, the most in years [src:pravda-2026-oliynyk-zelensky-warning][src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed][src:pravda-2026-balachuk-metro-52500].
- **Jets at dusk (19:54–23:14).** Long before the missiles, jet drones probe from Boryspil, Vyshneve, Dymer and Boiarka. At 23:14 they are over the centre and the east. A drone sets the 1901 Zektser building on Shevchenko Boulevard on fire [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:pravda-2026-lobanok-zektser-building].
- **The ballistic hour (01:12–02:31).** First the warning from the north. Then a missile a minute, from Kursk via Sumy and from Bryansk via Chernihiv, with "unbelievably loud" blasts heard deep underground. There are only enough Patriot rounds for a few [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed][src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-zelensky-patriot-140].
- **Darnytskyi.** Two missiles land a few steps apart. One leaves a giant crater by a kindergarten; the other hits the end of a nine-storey block, which slides off the building's face. People may be sheltering in the basement. Ten bodies are recovered over two days [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-search-completed].
- **The cruise wave (02:48–03:49).** Cruise-missile groups track west over Krolevets and southern Chernihiv Oblast, then close on Kyiv from three sides. Of the night's 34 Kh-101s, 32 are downed [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets].
- **The ambulance station.** Medics called out during the alert are hit on the way to their vehicles. Nine ambulances are damaged and six crew injured [src:pravda-2026-pavliuk-injured-medics][src:pravda-2026-balachuk-six-missing].
- **Dawn down the Dnipro (05:36–08:20).** Jet drones keep coming south over the Kyiv Reservoir and past Kotsiubynske under a setting moon. In Obolon a petrol station burns, and 350 t of petroleum products spill into Lake Kyrylivske [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july][src:pravda-2026-kryzhnyi-kyrylivske-oil].
- **Small things.** Glass from the dome falls on turtles and crocodiles at Kyiv Zoo. A Red Cross warehouse holding UAH 79m of aid burns. A diplomats' residence catches fire [src:pravda-2026-kovalenko-kyiv-aftermath][src:isw-2026-07-02-roca][src:pravda-2026-kutielieva-mathernova-hell].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Warn the public, at the cost of tipping off Russia (1 July).**
   - *Decision:* Zelensky publicised the intelligence and cut his visit short [src:pravda-2026-oliynyk-zelensky-warning].
   - *What followed:* record sheltering [src:pravda-2026-balachuk-metro-52500]. EU Ambassador Mathernová credits the warning for the full shelters [src:pravda-2026-kutielieva-mathernova-hell].
2. **How to spend scarce Patriot rounds (the ballistic hour).**
   - *The constraint:* two rounds per missile would need 140 for 70 [src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-zelensky-patriot-140].
   - *The documented practice:* one round per missile, manual mode, and no Patriot shots at drones [src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-patriot-playbook].
   - *What followed:* 4 of 28 stopped, and 25 ballistic hits that caused most of the deaths [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets][src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv]. Four nights later, none of 29 was stopped [src:euronews-2026-vakulina-zero-intercepts].
3. **Spend missiles and fighters on jet drones, or keep them for missiles.**
   - *Ihnat's stated choice:* "missiles have to be used" against jet drones, because mobile fire groups and interceptors "can no longer be relied upon" [src:pravda-2026-ihnat-jet-drones].
   - *ISW:* this draws on the same critically short stock [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca].
4. **EW against a new band.** Russian Shahed-types switched to 3.9–4.1 GHz. Beskrestnov had flagged it on 21 June, and almost every drone aimed at Kyiv used it [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca]. How quickly Ukraine retuned is not public.
5. **Ask allies for stocks now, or wait for contracts (2 July).**
   - *Decision:* the MoD wrote to about 40 states asking for transfers "this month" [src:pravda-2026-bondarieva-fedorov-patriot-appeal].
   - *Zelensky:* asked the US for licences to produce Patriot missiles [src:pravda-2026-bondarieva-patriot-licences].
   - *What followed:* Trump's licence pledge at Ankara on 8 July, unbinding and without a timeline [src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-patriot-playbook]. At Ramstein on 8 Sep only Germany released stocks [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-patriot-1000].
6. **Russia's choice of package and timing.**
   - *Timing:* ISW reads the June lull as possible stockpiling for bigger strikes "at a later time of the Kremlin's choosing" [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca].
   - *Package:* Informational Resistance points to bomber wear and a shift toward jet drones [src:euromaidan-2026-mukhina-june-strikes-slowed].
   - *Approach:* the night's mix and routes (ballistic missiles from the north-east, a long jet-drone tail) follow ISW's assessment that Russia pairs jet Gerans with ballistic salvos to exploit the Patriot gap [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Targets and motive.**

It also claimed hits on military bases in central and eastern Ukraine [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv].

- **The Kremlin.** Peskov said only "military or quasi-military targets" were struck and that Putin had been briefed [src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed]. He added that Russia would "continue to increase pressure on the Kyiv regime" [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv].
- **Information operation.** Bryansk's acting governor claimed a Ukrainian drone hit a Minsk–Anapa bus. Ukraine's General Staff denied it and called it a diversion from the Kyiv strike. ISW notes Russia used a similar claim in June to justify strikes [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca].
- **Ukrainian rebuttal.** FM Sybiha called it "immoral" to justify the strikes as a response [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv]. A wounded resident told the BBC: "This is not retaliation… This is a residential area" [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv].
- **Missing.** No Russian count of launches or claimed hits, and no milblogger battle damage assessment, was found in the fetched sources.

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Shape.** A single night of about 16 hours, compressed, in four scripted phases:
  1. drone and jet prelude (19:30–01:00);
  2. ballistic hour (01:12–02:31);
  3. cruise wave (02:48–03:49);
  4. dawn tail (04:00–11:00).

  Russian launches follow the Air Force alert log (§2). Each alert is a spawn event with an axis (north, north-east, east, south-east, south-west) [src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **Win band** (reproduce history, do not beat it):
  - about 92% of all weapons downed or suppressed;
  - about 96% of drones, about 94–100% of cruise missiles;
  - about 14% (4 of 28) of ballistic missiles;
  - 25 ballistic and 12 drone hits on 33 sites;
  - 30–31 dead in Kyiv.

  A run that saves more ballistic intercepts than the stock allows is out of band. The debrief sets the MoD's "90% Shahed-type" and "77 missiles" beside the Air Force's figures [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets][src:pravda-2026-bondarieva-fedorov-patriot-appeal][src:pravda-2026-oliynyk-death-toll-31].
- **The core lever: magazine allocation.**
  - A fixed, small stock of Patriot rounds, one or two per ballistic target [src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-zelensky-patriot-140][src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-patriot-playbook].
  - A separate SAM and fighter pool for cruise missiles and jet drones.
  - Cheap layers (interceptor drones capped at about 300 km/h, mobile fire groups, guns, helicopters, EW) that only work on piston Shaheds and decoys [src:pravda-2026-ihnat-jet-drones].
  - Spending SAMs on jets visibly drains the pool available against Kh-101s.
- **Speed classes.** Gerbera at about 160 km/h, piston Shahed, jet Geran at 300–600 km/h, Kh-101, then ballistic missiles with about two minutes' warning from the alert [src:pravda-2026-konyk-gerbera-fuel-tanks][src:united24-2026-place-jet-gerans][src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **EW mini-game.** Russian drones switch bands mid-campaign (3.9–4.1 GHz). Jamming counts as "suppressed", not as a kill [src:isw-2026-07-02-roca][src:isis-2026-anokhin-shahed-monthly].
- **Civil-defence meter.** A public warning raises shelter occupancy (52,500 in the metro) and lowers casualties per hit. Debris from intercepts can still start fires (the market) [src:pravda-2026-balachuk-metro-52500][src:pravda-2026-vakuliuk-kyiv-overnight-damage].
- **Map and light.** Clear sky, near-full moon from 22:31, a short night (civil dusk 21:59, dawn 04:05). Corridors run down the Kyiv Reservoir and Dnipro, across Chernihiv and Sumy oblasts, and through Boryspil and Vyshneve [src:openmeteo-era5-archive][src:pyephem-c18-moon][src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **Coda hooks.** The 5–6 July night (0 of 29 ballistic intercepted) and the 19 July record barrage can be harder variants. The NATO-summit licence pledge is the strategic epilogue [src:euronews-2026-vakulina-zero-intercepts][src:euromaidan-2026-murdoch-19-july-ballistic][src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-patriot-playbook].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Jet versus piston drones.** The Air Force does not split the 496 drones by type. Neither the jet share nor the jet interception rate for this night is known [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets].
- **Kill per layer.** No public breakdown by Patriot, IRIS-T, NASAMS, fighters, interceptor drones, mobile fire groups or EW. "Downed/suppressed" combines kills and jamming [src:isis-2026-anokhin-shahed-monthly]. F-16 and Mirage sorties that night are not reported.
- **Count reconciliation.**
  - 28 ballistic launched, 4 downed, 25 "ballistic" hits; two Kh-101s unaccounted for [src:pravda-2026-romanenko-570-targets].
  - The MoD's 77 missiles against the Air Force's 74 [src:pravda-2026-bondarieva-fedorov-patriot-appeal].
  - Kh-101 launch areas: the Air Force says Vologda Oblast.
- **Impact times and weapons per site.** No source maps each hit to a time and weapon, except "two missiles" in Darnytskyi (BBC) and the drone hit on the hotel (witness) [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed].
- **BBC time error.** BBC gives missiles at "01:00 (23:00 GMT)". Kyiv is UTC+3 in summer, and the Air Force's first missile alerts come at 01:53 [src:bbc-2026-rainsford-most-massive-kyiv][src:uaf-2026-kpszsu-alerts-1-2-july].
- **Desnianskyi.** Klitschko reported a nine-storey collapse in Desnianskyi, but later DSNS and Ukrainska Pravda reporting places the collapse and the recovered bodies in Darnytskyi [src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed][src:pravda-2026-pohorilov-search-completed].
- **Energy damage.** DTEK and Ukrenergo reported damage and outages in Kyiv without naming facilities, and no CHP is reported hit [src:kyivindependent-2026-fenbert-kyiv-attack-31-killed][src:pravda-2026-kryzhnyi-power-outages].
- **Patriot battery count and sites** are not public. Nor is the size of the PAC-3/PAC-2 stock on 1 July.
- **Russian targeting claims.** Which "military plants" were meant is unspecified, and there is no independent battle damage assessment of military targets in Kyiv.
