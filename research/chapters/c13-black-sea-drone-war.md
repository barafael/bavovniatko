# The Black Sea drone war: sea drones, helicopters, and air defence on sea drones
Oct 2022 – May 2025, peaking Feb – Mar 2024. The western and southern Black Sea off occupied Crimea (Sevastopol, Donuzlav, Alupka, Cape Tarkhankut, the Kerch Strait) and Novorossiysk, with operators in Kyiv and launch areas off Odesa Oblast.

Research base for *bavovniatko*. The player is Ukraine. Accessed 2026-10-01. Sources are cited inline by id in square brackets. Figures from HUR, the SBU, the Navy and Russian officials are claims, and the text says who makes each one.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Ukraine lost its surface navy in the first days of the invasion. It then fought the Black Sea Fleet (BSF) with missiles and with uncrewed surface vessels (USVs): explosive-laden, remote-controlled speedboats that ride low in the water [src:gssr-2026-swarm-at-sea][src:navalnews-2023-sutton-385th-brigade]. The first swarm raided Sevastopol bay on 29 Oct 2022. It hit the frigate *Admiral Makarov* and the minesweeper *Ivan Golubets* but sank nothing [src:pravda-2022-geoconfirmed-admiral-makarov][src:navalnews-2023-sutton-385th-brigade]. The programme then split:
- The SBU built the **Sea Baby**, a heavy-warhead boat for static targets: the Crimean Bridge, and the *Olenegorsky Gornyak* in Novorossiysk.
- HUR took the original maker's design forward as the **Magura V5**, a smaller and faster boat built to hunt ships at sea [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate][src:wikipedia-sea-baby].

Through 2023, missiles and sea drones together drove most of the fleet from Sevastopol to Novorossiysk (October 2023) [src:pravda-2023-mt-anderson-bsf-novorossiysk]. Ukraine opened its own export corridor along the western coast on 16 Aug 2023 [src:pravda-2023-first-ship-corridor].

The climax came in Feb – Mar 2024, when HUR's Group 13 used night swarms of Magura V5s to sink three ships in five weeks:
- the corvette *Ivanovets*, off Donuzlav on 1 Feb;
- the landing ship *Tsezar Kunikov*, off Alupka on 14 Feb;
- the patrol ship *Sergey Kotov*, off the Kerch Strait on 5 Mar.

The BSF commander was sacked, and the Russian Navy limited its operations to the eastern Black Sea [src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-six-maguras][src:isw-2024-02-14-roca][src:isw-2024-03-05-roca][src:pravda-2024-ukdi-sokolov][src:maritimeexec-2024-bsf-new-measures].

Russia answered with helicopters, Su-30SM jets, gunboats, booms and FPVs that hunted the drones at sea. Ukraine in turn armed its drones with missiles:
- **31 Dec 2024:** R-73 "SeeDragon" missiles fired from a Magura V5 downed at least one Mi-8 off Cape Tarkhankut. HUR later claimed two.
- **2 May 2025:** AIM-9M Sidewinders fired from Magura V7s downed at least one Su-30SM west of Novorossiysk. HUR claims two [src:gur-2024-mi8][src:eurosd-2025-magura-mi8-kills][src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9].

**Terrain and structures.**
- **Harbour defences.** Sevastopol was layered with nets, floating booms and a chain of barges. Other harbours were too, and so was the Kerch Bridge after Jul 2023 [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts].
- **Where to strike.** Budanov judged Sevastopol and Novorossiysk "comprehensive but [able to] be overcome" defences. He preferred to catch ships at sea, on duty or moving [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
- **The corridor** runs inside the territorial waters of Ukraine and its neighbours [src:hromadske-2024-ryzhenko-bsf]. The first ship hugged the coast toward Romania [src:maritimeexec-2023-joseph-schulte].

**Sea and season.**
- The developers say the Black Sea suits drone testing from late March to early November [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].

**Ukrainian tactics.**
- **Hunt at sea, not in harbours.** The Magura was designed "to intercept ships". It is deployed for a specific mission, not for constant patrol [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
- **Swarms with roles.** Groups of more than five drones, each with a place in the order of attack [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]:
  1. The first strike immobilises the target, usually by hitting the engines or the stern.
  2. Then the most vulnerable spot is hit.
  3. Follow-on drones are steered into the blast holes at the waterline before they detonate.

  The attacks come at night, stern first, then amidships, to cause flooding and capsizing [src:maritimeexec-2024-bsf-new-measures].
- **Evasion.** On the *Ivanovets* video, drones zig-zag through AK-630M fire. One hits where an earlier drone had torn a gash [src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets]. A "chasing splashes" manoeuvre, steering toward the last impact point to upset gunnery correction, is described by a low-reliability source [src:gssr-2026-swarm-at-sea].
- **Persistence.** At the *Ivan Khurs* the link dropped about 100 m from the ship. Operators then shadowed it for about six hours, waiting for it to run out of ammunition, and struck at about 07:00 [src:pravda-2024-hur-ivan-khurs-details].
- **Retargeting.** When the intended convoy target moved hours before contact, the swarm switched to the *Kunikov* [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
- **Mines.** Sea Babies laid mines once booms blocked Sevastopol Bay. The SBU and WSJ say at least four ships were damaged [src:wikipedia-sea-baby][src:wikipedia-385th-usv-brigade].
- **Air defence from the sea.**
  - R-73s on V5s (seen from May 2024; first kill 31 Dec 2024). Air-launched, the R-73 has at least 30 km range and an 8 kg warhead [src:aviationist-2024-durso-magura-mi8].
  - AIM-9Ms on V7s (from Jan 2025). The hull turns to aim the missile horizontally, and the seeker's 60–67° field covers elevation [src:euromaidan-2025-zoria-magura-v7][src:defenseexpress-2025-magura-v7].
  - The AIM-9M lacks the R-73's high off-boresight seeker, so the boat must point at its target [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9].
  - Mixed swarms combine kamikaze boats, anti-air boats and gun boats [src:defenseexpress-2025-magura-v7].
  - The lure: probably a feint toward Sevastopol on 31 Dec 2024 (ESD's speculation) [src:eurosd-2025-magura-mi8-kills]. A Russian analyst said of the Su-30 kill that Ukraine "lured it out and caught it" [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9].

**Russian counters and how well they worked.**
- **Booms, nets and barges** were moderately effective at harbour mouths [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts]. Two USVs jumped the Sevastopol boom on 22 Mar 2023 [src:twz-2024-altman-fpv-helicopters].
- **Aircraft** were the main threat to USVs at sea:
  - Be-12 spotting cued the helicopters [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts];
  - helicopters and jets "significantly reduced USV survivability in mid-2024" (low-reliability source) [src:gssr-2026-swarm-at-sea];
  - a Ka-29 killed an R-73-armed USV in May 2024 [src:twz-2024-altman-fpv-helicopters].
- **EW** jamming was moderately effective [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts].
- **Convoys:** escorts, with AIS switched off [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts].
- **FPVs:** first from patrol boats, then from helicopters (Sep 2024). Russian commentators say FPVs "effectively destroy" USVs [src:twz-2024-altman-fpv-helicopters][src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9].
- **Ship self-defence failed against swarms.** The *Kunikov* crew fired back and manoeuvred "for a long time" [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. Milbloggers asked why the *Kotov* lacked anti-drone systems after three earlier attacks [src:isw-2024-03-05-roca].
- **Camouflage** (black bows and sterns, painted decoys) was judged unlikely to cut losses by UK DI [src:maritimeexec-2024-bsf-new-measures].
- **Relocation** to Novorossiysk, and leaving the western sea, was the decisive counter [src:pravda-2023-mt-anderson-bsf-novorossiysk][src:isw-2024-02-14-roca].

## 6. Set pieces

- **Sevastopol, first light, 29 Oct 2022.** Six to eight drones slip into the bay before dawn. One weaves among moored ships, U-turns and rams a target. Another hits a Grigorovich-class frigate, the *Admiral Makarov*, and the minesweeper *Ivan Golubets* is hit too. A boom in Pivdenna Bay is damaged. The drone cameras cut out at impact [src:pravda-2022-geoconfirmed-admiral-makarov][src:wikipedia-2022-sevastopol-drone-attack]. Prologue: Starlink fails over Crimea and the September mission is lost [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
- **The six-hour chase of the *Ivan Khurs* (24 May 2023).** A 500 km transit and a lost link at 100 m. The drones shadow the ship under fire until morning, run low on fuel, and score a hit at the stern [src:pravda-2024-hur-ivan-khurs-details][src:ukrinform-2023-ivan-khurs].
- **Novorossiysk Bay under a bright moon (3–4 Aug 2023).** A 450 kg drone hits the *Olenegorsky Gornyak*, which is towed out listing at dawn. The *Sig* is holed off Kerch hours later [src:pravda-2023-novorossiysk-olenegorsky-gornyak][src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-novorossiysk][src:shipandbunker-2023-sig-tanker][src:usno-rstt-oneday].
- **The *Ivanovets* at Donuzlav (1 Feb 2024).** The corvette is under way and its CIWS is firing. Drone 1 cuts to the stern. Drone 2 zig-zags in. Drone 3 hits the existing gash. The ammunition detonates and the ship sinks stern-first, bow up [src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets][src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-six-maguras].
- **Off Alupka before dawn (14 Feb 2024).** The original convoy target slips away, so the swarm turns on the *Tsezar Kunikov*. Flashes are visible from the shore through haze; holes open along the port side. Mi-8s and a Ka-27 circle afterwards [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate][src:gur-2024-kunikov][src:pravda-2024-kunikov-sunk].
- **The Kerch approaches (5 Mar 2024).** Five hits on the *Sergey Kotov*, a failed tow, and the Crimean Bridge closed overnight [src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-kotov-sunk][src:isw-2024-03-05-roca][src:pravda-2024-shapps-kotov].
- **The hunter becomes prey, Tarkhankut (31 Dec 2024).** Helicopter gunfire splashes around the drone. It answers with an R-73. One Mi-8 pumps flares, is hit and falls into the sea; a second limps home [src:aviationist-2024-durso-magura-mi8][src:gur-2024-mi8]. A companion piece: the SBU's machine-gun Sea Babies in Kerch Bay on 6 Dec 2024 [src:euromaidan-2024-mukhina-sea-baby-machine-guns].
- **The first jet kill (2 May 2025).** Three V7s off Novorossiysk. An AIM-9 breaks up a Su-30SM; the pilots eject and a container ship picks them up [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9][src:maritimeexec-2025-drone-boat-fighter][src:wikipedia-magura-v5].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Starlink over Crimea (Sep 2022).**
   - *Decision:* Musk refused coverage around Crimea [src:wikipedia-starlink-war], and the first mission failed [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
   - *Response:* both programmes moved to redundant links [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate][src:wikipedia-sea-baby]. Starlink terminals still appear on Maguras in later photos [src:aviationist-2024-durso-magura-mi8].
2. **The SBU–maker split (end 2022).**
   - *Decision:* after disputes over quality, specification and budget, the SBU built its own Sea Baby and the maker went to HUR [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
   - *Consequence:* two families of drones with different design briefs (static targets vs ships at sea), plus inter-agency rivalry. HUR describes operators refusing to share target coordinates; it says results were best when the agencies co-planned, as at Novorossiysk [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
3. **Ships at sea vs harbours (2023–24).** Budanov chose to intercept ships "on duty or being repositioned" rather than force the harbour defences at Sevastopol and Novorossiysk [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. All three 2024 sinkings were of ships outside the main harbours [src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-six-maguras][src:pravda-2024-yusov-kunikov-crew][src:isw-2024-03-05-roca].
4. **Russia moves the fleet (Sep–Oct 2023).** The move followed the missile and USV strikes, including the 22 Sep strike on the BSF HQ. Satellite imagery shows the shift to Novorossiysk [src:wikipedia-storm-shadow][src:pravda-2023-mt-anderson-bsf-novorossiysk]. ISW credits the 2023 campaign with "sequestering" the BSF in the east [src:isw-2024-02-14-roca]. The alternative base at Ochamchire was too small (Ryzhenko) [src:hromadske-2024-ryzhenko-bsf].
5. **Ukraine opens its own corridor (Aug 2023).** Russia had declared merchant ships hostile. Kyiv registered ships and sent the *Joseph Schulte* on 16 Aug [src:maritimeexec-2023-joseph-schulte][src:pravda-2023-first-ship-corridor]. Volumes grew through 2024 [src:pravda-2024-corridor-45mt][src:osw-2024-record-exports].
6. **Retarget mid-mission (14 Feb 2024).** HUR's rule: don't interfere with the operators, and decide quickly when the plan breaks. The swarm switched to the *Kunikov* [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate].
7. **Russia changes commanders (Feb – Mar 2024).** Sokolov went after the *Kunikov* and Yevmenov after the *Kotov*. Shoigu ordered drills and extra guns [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-sokolov][src:hromadske-2024-ryzhenko-bsf][src:maritimeexec-2024-bsf-new-measures]. Milbloggers debated the "bureaucratic inertia" behind the losses [src:isw-2024-03-05-roca].
8. **Air cover against USVs leads to missiles on USVs (2024).**
   - *Russia* shifted to helicopters and Su-30SMs hunting drones offshore [src:isw-2024-12-31-roca].
   - *Ukraine:* Budanov had already named air defence on the drones as "the next step" in Mar 2024 [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. R-73s were seen in May 2024 [src:aviationist-2024-durso-magura-mi8], and the first kill came on 31 Dec 2024 [src:gur-2024-mi8].
9. **R-73 or AIM-9 (Jan 2025).** HUR began fitting AIM-9s and says "the best results [come from] the AIM-9" [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9]. Group 13 still plans R-73 fits as an alternative [src:euromaidan-2025-zoria-magura-v7].
10. **Russia's response to the jet loss (May 2025).** Milbloggers argued for FPVs and ZALA–Lancet pairs instead of jets "firing cannons at USVs like in World War II". ISW records the call for coastal FPV integration [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9][src:isw-2025-05-03-roca]. Russia's own Katran USV is reported as a threat to Maguras [src:defenseexpress-2025-magura-v7].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

These Russian claims are taken from Ukrainian and Western reporting.

- **Denial and minimisation.**
  - *29 Oct 2022:* the Russian MoD admitted only "minor" damage to *Ivan Golubets* and a boom, and said the ships guarded the grain corridor. Russia blamed British specialists in Ochakiv and suspended the grain deal [src:wikipedia-2022-sevastopol-drone-attack][src:pravda-2022-geoconfirmed-admiral-makarov].
  - *24 May 2023:* "all three" boats were destroyed by gunfire at the *Ivan Khurs* [src:pravda-2023-ivan-khurs-russian-mod].
  - *4 Aug 2023:* the attack on Novorossiysk was "repelled" [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-novorossiysk].
  - *14 Feb 2024:* six USVs were destroyed; there was no comment on the *Kunikov* [src:wikipedia-tsezar-kunikov][src:pravda-2024-kunikov-sunk].
  - *31 Dec 2024:* Razvozhayev said 4 aerial and 2 naval drones were repelled, and did not mention the Mi-8 [src:isw-2024-12-31-roca].
  - *2–3 May 2025:* the MoD claimed 170 drones, 11 missiles and 14 sea drones destroyed [src:maritimeexec-2025-drone-boat-fighter].
- **Milbloggers confirm the losses.**
  - VoenkorKotenok on the *Ivanovets* ("three hits from naval drones") [src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets].
  - Aleksandr Kots on the *Kotov* ("a story without a happy ending"). He says the ship had repelled three attacks in Jul – Sep 2023 [src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-kotov-sunk][src:isw-2024-03-05-roca].
  - Rybar on the Su-30 (he says it was an R-73, not an AIM-9) [src:maritimeexec-2025-drone-boat-fighter].
  - Kotenok in May 2025: the fleet is "huddled in harbors and bays", and Ukraine has "an overwhelming advantage" in USVs [src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9].
- **Crew losses are contested.** For the *Kunikov*, HUR says most of the crew died, while a former sailor told the BBC that all 89 were evacuated [src:pravda-2024-yusov-kunikov-crew][src:wikipedia-tsezar-kunikov]. ## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Three acts.**
  1. Prologue: Sevastopol, 2022. The swarm can damage but not sink. Starlink fails.
  2. Main act: the 2024 hunt. Pick the night, the target and the swarm size; reproduce three sinkings in five weeks.
  3. Coda: the hunters are hunted, Dec 2024 – May 2025, as anti-air Maguras ambush helicopters and jets.

  The world advances by itself: Russian relocation (Oct 2023), Shoigu's counter-measures (Mar 2024), helicopter hunting (2024), FPVs from helicopters (Sep 2024) [src:pravda-2023-mt-anderson-bsf-novorossiysk][src:maritimeexec-2024-bsf-new-measures][src:twz-2024-altman-fpv-helicopters].
- **Swarm mechanics.** Each drone gets a role (immobiliser, breacher, finisher) and an order of attack: stern or engines first, then the vulnerable point, then into the hole [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. Hits needed to sink: the *Ivanovets* took 6 and the *Kotov* 5. Ambrey counted 3 drones on the *Kunikov* video [src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-six-maguras][src:euromaidan-2024-zoria-kotov-sunk][src:wikipedia-tsezar-kunikov].
- **Link and latency layer.**
  - Operators sit in Kyiv on satcom or mesh links [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate][src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets].
  - A link-loss event near the target is historical: the *Ivan Khurs*, at 100 m [src:pravda-2024-hur-ivan-khurs-details].
  - EW raises the chance of losing the link [src:navalnews-2023-sutton-russia-adapts].
  - Missions take a day or two of real time, so time compression is needed.
- **Sea state and light as gates.** The V5 is limited to sea state 2 and the V7 to sea state 3, and the season runs March to November [src:euromaidan-2025-zoria-magura-v7][src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. Historical nights were mostly dark or overcast with low waves (§3 table) [src:openmeteo-era5-archive][src:usno-rstt-oneday].
- **Air threat loop.** Helicopters and Su-30s hunting drones reduce survivability [src:gssr-2026-swarm-at-sea]. Unlocking R-73 and then AIM-9 boats turns the player's swarm into an ambush; a decoy feint draws out the hunters [src:eurosd-2025-magura-mi8-kills][src:twz-2025-altman-su30-aim9]. AIM-9 aiming means turning the hull, a nice manual-skill mechanic [src:defenseexpress-2025-magura-v7].
- **Win band.**
  - *Required:* the *Ivanovets*, *Kunikov* and *Kotov* sunk in Feb – Mar 2024, and the fleet relocated or kept east.
  - *Coda:* one or two Mi-8s and one or two Su-30s.
  - *Debrief:* lay HUR's tallies (9 vessels vs "16 warships") beside Oryx's photo-confirmed list and the Navy's "one third" [src:defenseexpress-2025-magura-v7][src:ukrinform-2025-magura-v7][src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses][src:pravda-2024-pletenchuk-third-of-fleet].
- **Strategic meter.** Corridor exports (from 16 Aug 2023; 45 Mt by May 2024) rise as the fleet is pushed east [src:pravda-2023-first-ship-corridor][src:pravda-2024-corridor-45mt].
- **Pacing.** Long, tense, low-information transits (hundreds of km), then minutes of chaotic terminal attack under CIWS fire. The *Ivan Khurs* chase shows the endgame can last hours [src:pravda-2024-hur-ivan-khurs-details][src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Launch points and transit routes** are undisclosed. Budanov declined to say whether a mothership was used [src:twz-2024-altman-ivanovets]. There are no sourced coordinates for the strike positions apart from the place names (Donuzlav raid, 4 km off Alupka, 5 km off Cape Takil, about 50 km west of Novorossiysk).
- **Times** of the 2024 sinkings and of the air kills are not given to the hour, apart from the 2022 and 2023 Russian claims.
- **The Olenegorsky Gornyak drone** is contested: Malyuk says Sea Baby, HUR says the SBU's Mamai [src:wikipedia-sea-baby][src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate]. Oryx's naval list does not appear to include the ship (our reading of the page) [src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses].
- **Which ships were hit on 29 Oct 2022** beyond the *Makarov* and *Ivan Golubets* is unknown. Ukrainian channels said four [src:wikipedia-2022-sevastopol-drone-attack].
- **The Serna and Akula fate.** HUR says destroyed. Oryx lists one Serna destroyed, one Serna damaged and one Ondatra damaged, without dates in the list text [src:pravda-2024-romaniuk-magura-target-eliminate][src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses].
- **The second Mi-8 and second Su-30** have no public visual confirmation. We could not identify a 2–3 May 2025 Su-30SM entry in Oryx [src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses].
- **The type of the R-73-armed boat** in the May 2024 Ka-29 video is unclear: Sea Baby per Wikipedia, Magura per The Aviationist [src:wikipedia-sea-baby][src:aviationist-2024-durso-magura-mi8].
- **Not covered here:**
  - the US/CIA targeting support reported by the NYT [src:maritimeexec-2025-drone-boat-fighter];
  - Magura's sea-state limits as measured in practice;
  - Russian helicopter and aircraft orders of battle by unit and base, apart from Kacha;
  - corridor attack statistics.
- **Kyiv Independent coverage** could not be fetched (a client-rendered page), so the chapter leans on UP, EMP and Ukrinform for the Ukrainian voice.
- **Post-window coda for another chapter:** Sea Baby strikes on tankers off Turkey (Nov 2025) and the underwater Sea Baby against a submarine at Novorossiysk (Dec 2025) [src:kyivindependent-2025-zadorozhnyy-kairos-virat][src:kyivindependent-2025-myronyshena-sub-sea-baby].
