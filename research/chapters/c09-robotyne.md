# Robotyne: the 2023 counteroffensive on the Orikhiv axis

4 Jun – 30 Nov 2023 (shaping and first breach 4–12 Jun; attrition June–July; second mechanized push 26 Jul; Robotyne taken 22–28 Aug; Verbove breach 20–25 Sep; positional end-state November), the Orikhiv–Robotyne–Tokmak axis, Polohy raion, western Zaporizhzhia Oblast. Epilogue: Popov's fate, and the loss of Robotyne in 2024.

Loss and area figures are **claims**, and the claimant is always named. ## 1. Summary and historical outcome

The 2023 counteroffensive was planned around one main effort: a push south from Orikhiv to Tokmak and then Melitopol, to cut Russia's land bridge to Crimea [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months] [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons]. The US–Ukrainian war games behind it imagined Ukraine reaching the Sea of Azov in 60–90 days [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].

- **The defence.** Facing it was the 58th Combined Arms Army behind the best-prepared sector of the "Surovikin line": three belts about 30 km deep [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons]. ISW had the 42nd and 19th Motor Rifle Divisions heavily committed, with Spetsnaz brigades as the counterattack force; RUSI lists the 7th VDV and 42nd Divisions forward [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca] [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca] [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **The first breach failed.** On 7–8 June the Western-equipped 47th Mechanised Brigade, with Leopard 2A6s of the 33rd, tried to breach south of Mala Tokmachka. Mines, artillery, ATGMs and Ka-52 helicopters stopped the columns. The photographs of abandoned Leopards and Bradleys became the offensive's emblem [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses] [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca].
- **The switch.** Within days Zaluzhnyi stopped frontal mechanized assaults. Ukraine turned to dismounted platoon and company assaults, counter-battery fire and deep strikes [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-counteroffensive-lurches].
- **The second push.** A renewed mechanized attack on 26 July broke into the first positions east of Robotyne but did not hold its furthest gains [src:isw-2023-07-26-roca] [src:isw-2023-07-27-roca]. Reserves, the 82nd Air Assault and 46th Airmobile Brigades, were committed in mid-August [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months] [src:kyivpost-2023-york-82nd-in-action].
- **Robotyne.** The 47th entered Robotyne on 22 August and raised the flag on 23 August, and Deputy Defence Minister Maliar declared the village liberated on 28 August [src:isw-2023-08-22-roca] [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-advance-from-robotyne] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-robotyne-liberated].
- **Verbove.** Ukrainian infantry then pushed east along the Russian trench belt to Verbove. ISW judged the field fortifications west of Verbove broken on 23 September. Al Jazeera reported Ukrainian and Western sources saying the "Surovikin Line" had been cut [src:isw-2023-08-30-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca] [src:aljazeera-2023-psaropoulos-surovikin-line].
- **No breakthrough.** Russia fed in VDV divisions and the 41st Army. The salient stopped short of Novoprokopivka and Verbove, and by November the front was positional. Zaluzhnyi called the war a "stalemate" [src:isw-2023-09-01-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-vdv-robotyne] [src:isw-2023-11-06-roca] [src:abcnews-2023-burridge-stalemate].
- **Russian command.** Maj. Gen. Ivan Popov, the 58th Army commander who had celebrated the June minefield defence, was fired on about 11–12 July after complaining about counter-battery fire and the lack of rotation [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca] [src:politico-2023-camut-popov-fired].

**The outcome the player must reproduce (chapter window, to 30 Nov 2023):**
- **Robotyne is taken.** Ukraine announces it on 28 Aug (flag on 23 Aug), and holds it to the end of the window [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-robotyne-liberated] [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-advance-from-robotyne] [src:isw-2023-11-30-roca].
- **The salient stops short.** At best it is a bulge of about 7–9 km across and about 7 km deep (a Russian milblogger's estimate, 19 Sep), about 10 km from the start line by Kofman and Lee [src:isw-2023-09-19-roca] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months]. Its tip reaches the northern edge of Novoprokopivka and the western outskirts and northern part of Verbove, but neither village is cleared [src:isw-2023-09-22-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca] [src:euromaidan-2023-rfu-novoprokopivka-verbove].
- **No breakthrough to Tokmak.** Tokmak, about 23 km south of Robotyne, was Tarnavskyi's "minimum goal" and stays Russian [src:cnn-2023-cotovio-pleitgen-verbove] [src:osm-overpass-2026-c09-places].
- **Area won is a contested band**:
  - Ukrainian MoD: 256.5 km² by 11 Sep and 260 km² by 18 Sep in the whole south (the Orikhiv and Velyka Novosilka axes together) [src:kyivindependent-2023-court-4-8-km2] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-vdv-robotyne].
  - DeepState-derived: a gross Ukrainian recapture of 350 km² in Q2 and 294 km² in Q3 2023 for the whole front [src:deepstate-api-history].
  - The Orikhiv salient alone is a fraction of that. No source found here gives its area.
- **Western armour losses are heavy but survivable.** The main band is set by the 8 Jun breach (§4).
  - Forbes counted 25 vehicles there: 17 Bradleys, 4 Leopard 2A6, 3 Leopard 2R and 1 Wisent [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles].
  - DeepState counted irrecoverable losses of at least 4 APCs and 1 Leopard [src:euromaidan-2023-shandra-leopards-mala-tokmachka].
  - Oryx, as reported on 9 Jun, logged 16 Bradleys and 3 Leopard 2A6 in the area, of which 5 Bradleys and 1 Leopard were destroyed [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
  - By 6 Sep, five Leopard 2s had been destroyed in the counteroffensive (Forbes, via the Kyiv Independent), and the first Challenger 2 was lost near Robotyne [src:kyivindependent-2023-fornusek-challenger-destroyed].
- **Russian equipment losses are visually confirmed at about 156 items on the axis.** These are WarSpotting entries tagged to the Orikhiv axis for 1 Jun – 30 Nov 2023: 41 tanks, 62 IFVs and APCs, and a Ka-52M [src:warspotting-api-russian-losses].
- **Command consequences.** Popov is dismissed (12–13 Jul) and his successor, Lt. Gen. Denis Lyamin, is reported removed on 9 Oct [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca] [src:isw-2023-10-10-roca]. On the Ukrainian side the 47th's commander, Oleksandr Sak, is replaced on 16 Sep [src:pravda-2023-sak-removed].

| Tokmak | 47.2550, 35.7092 | ~47 | 22.7 km SSW | the objective, ringed by its own fortifications [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line] [src:isw-2023-09-13-roca] |

| Polohy | 47.4785, 36.2531 | — | 32.3 km E | eastern end of the N08 sector [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca] |

**Terrain.**
- **Uphill all the way.** The ground climbs from the Kinska valley at Orikhiv (about 51–53 m) and Mala Tokmachka (about 68 m) to about 120–140 m on the Robotyne–Kopani ridge, peaking at about 152 m just south of Robotyne [src:opentopodata-srtm30m]. The attackers advanced up a long, open slope under observation.
- **Open steppe.** The Zaporizhzhia steppe is flat to gently rolling, which makes for long sight lines [src:cepa-2023-kallberg-treelines].
- **A grid of shelterbelts.** In the OSM and Sentinel-2 sample box around Robotyne, tree lines run roughly E–W and N–S. Main belts are about 0.5–0.6 km apart and cross-belts about 1.0–1.2 km apart, at 1.34 km of mapped belt per km² [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample] [src:eox-2021-s2cloudless]. Kofman and Lee: "a war of tree lines, with shifts in the line often counted in hundreds of meters" [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
- **Ravines.** Balkas separate the "platforms", as described at Rivnopil on the neighbouring axis [src:euromaidan-2023-rivnopil-heights]. The Balka Uspenivka southeast of Orikhiv was a named fighting area [src:isw-2023-08-22-roca].
- **Dams.** A Ukrainian mine-action officer reported that Russia was regularly blowing up small dams in the south to disrupt Ukrainian operations [src:isw-2023-06-13-roca].

**The defence belts.**
- **Ukrainian description.** Col. Petro Chernyk described three echelons: minefields "several kilometers deep", then a line with artillery and troops, then rear positions [src:isw-2023-08-17-roca].
- **Overall depth.** RUSI puts the Orikhiv–Tokmak defence at about 30 km deep in three lines [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **Robotyne's place in it.** Robotyne marked the southern edge of the densest minefields [src:isw-2023-08-17-roca] [src:isw-2023-08-23-roca].
- **The next belt.** Beyond Robotyne lay "a relatively more contiguous array" of anti-tank ditch, dragon's teeth and minefields with fighting positions behind them [src:isw-2023-08-26-roca]. West of Verbove this belt runs about 1.7–3.5 km from the village [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca].
- **The trenches.** Assault troops described the Surovikin-line trenches as "usually 2-3 meters deep, reinforced with wood and earth" [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line]. Anti-tank ditches were 4 m deep and 6 m wide [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder].
- **The minefields.** They grew from 120 m to about 500 m deep, with mines stacked two high [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak]. Barrier minefields ran "sometimes as much as 5 or more kilometers deep" [src:kyivpost-2023-brown-landmine-problem].

**Roads** (OSM refs) [src:osm-overpass-2026-c09-places]:
- **T0408** (Т-04-08) runs north–south: Orikhiv – Novodanylivka – Robotyne – Novoprokopivka – Tokmak. It passes through all three villages and is the axis of the whole chapter.
  - Shtupun noted Robotyne sits on "the highway to Tokmak" [src:isw-2023-08-29-roca].
  - By 4 Oct Ukraine held a trench along it between Robotyne and Novoprokopivka [src:isw-2023-10-04-roca].
- **N08** (Н-08) shares the T0408 out of Orikhiv to Novodanylivka, then runs east past Novopokrovka (about 2 km) and Novofedorivka (about 1.3 km), about 6 km north of Verbove, towards Polohy. This is the "N08 Polohy–Voskresenka highway" that Ukrainian forces crossed on 8 Jun and later claimed near Verbove [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-21-roca].
- **T0815** (Т-08-15) runs through Mala Tokmachka towards Novopokrovka.
- **Tracks.** Mapped tracks are sparse, at 0.34 km per km² in the sample box. Movement was along field edges and tree lines [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample].

**Weather and light** (ERA5, 47.47 N 35.85 E, local time) [src:open-meteo-historical-archive]:
- **7–10 June.** Dry and warm: highs 24–27 °C, lows 9–17 °C, no rain. Cloud rose from 69% on 7 Jun to 79% on 8 Jun and 100% on 10 Jun. Sunrise was 04:40 and sunset 20:30. Rain followed on 11–12 Jun (27 mm).
  - Night lasted only about eight hours, so an assault that slipped two hours, as one did in the opening assaults, ran into dawn [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - Russian sources reported attacks rising "due to improved weather" by 15 Jun [src:isw-2023-06-15-roca].
- **July–August.** Hot and dry. August means were 30 °C high and 19 °C low, with a peak of 36.8 °C and only 32 mm of rain. 26 Jul reached 30 °C, 23 Aug 27 °C and 28 Aug 33 °C. Soldiers fought without body armour in the heat [src:bbc-2023-beale-western-armour].
- **September.** Dry and clear: 24 mm, highs 25–27 °C on 20–23 Sep, sunset falling to about 18:35.
- **October–November.** Cooling. November brought 101 mm of rain on 19 days and a storm on 26–27 Nov (gusts to about 46 km/h, then a low of −1.9 °C). Milbloggers said the weather impeded vehicles and artillery but not drones [src:isw-2023-11-30-roca].

## 4. Forces

### Ukraine

**Command.**
- Commander-in-chief: Gen. Valerii Zaluzhnyi [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].
- The Tavria group, commanding the southern front: Brig. Gen. Oleksandr Tarnavskyi. Spokesmen: Valerii Shershen, then Oleksandr Shtupun [src:isw-2023-06-05-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-fornusek-melitopol-1-5-km] [src:isw-2023-08-29-roca].
- The 47th Mechanised Brigade: Lt. Col. Oleksandr Sak until 16 Sep, then Col. Oleksandr Pavlii [src:pravda-2023-sak-removed].

**The plan.**
- RUSI: 12 armoured and mechanised brigades on a 30 km front were to isolate Tokmak in seven days. In the event only a couple of brigades were properly equipped [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- The US wanted one massed southern axis. Ukraine split its effort across three axes (Orikhiv, Velyka Novosilka, Bakhmut) [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
- A secondary NYT account (2025), relayed by Wikipedia, says Zelensky split the 12 new brigades seven to Tarnavskyi and five to Syrskyi. This was not verified here [src:wikipedia-2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive].

**Order of battle on the Orikhiv axis** (Kofman and Lee, unless noted) [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months]:
- **First echelon, 9th Corps.**
  - **The 47th Mechanised Brigade "Magura".** It was built from scratch around Markus's volunteer "brand", and its troops trained in NATO countries [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line].
    - Equipment: M2A2 ODS Bradleys and Leopard 2s [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line]; Soviet T-64s fitted with mine rollers [src:bbc-2023-beale-western-armour]; an engineer element with Leopard 2R and Wisent breaching tanks and a BMR-2 [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses] [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles].
    - Its casualties are reported at about 2,000 of 5,000 by September (El País via Wikipedia, not verified here) [src:wikipedia-2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive].
  - **The 33rd Mechanised Brigade.** It supplied the Leopard 2A6s seen on 8 Jun [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
- **Supporting units.** The 65th Mechanised Brigade (photographed near Robotyne on 1 Oct) [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-out-of-steam], the 128th Mountain Assault Brigade and the 15th National Guard Brigade.
- **Second echelon, 10th Corps, committed at the end of July.** The NATO-trained 116th, 117th and 118th Mechanised Brigades and the 3rd and 14th National Guard Brigades.
- **Reserves, committed in mid-August.** The 46th Airmobile Brigade and the 82nd Air Assault Brigade.
  - Per the leaked US documents the 82nd had 40 Marders, 90 Strykers and 14 Challenger 2s, with crews from the 25th and 80th Brigades trained in the UK [src:kyivpost-2023-york-82nd-in-action].
- **Special forces.** Elements of the 73rd Naval Special Operations Centre fought at Robotyne.

**Strength and fires.**
- At most about 55 Ukrainian 155 mm guns fired on the main axis, at about 70 rounds a gun a day at peak and as few as 10 [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons]. About 7 GMLRS a day were available for the whole theatre [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- Storm Shadow and SCALP were used against headquarters [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons] [src:cnn-2023-lister-popov-dismissed]. US cluster munitions (DPICM) arrived in July [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-counteroffensive-lurches].
- On 10 Sep Tarnavskyi claimed 1,391 artillery engagements in one day [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-robotyne-1-5-km2].
- Breaching kit was scarce, and the M1150 Assault Breacher was usable only from October [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].

**Western vehicles in the field, and lost.**
- **Arrivals.** About 109 M2A2 ODS Bradleys and 21 Leopard 2A6s had been delivered by June [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses], and all six Finnish Leopard 2Rs went to Ukraine [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles]. Abrams arrived only on 23 Sep, too late [src:isw-2023-09-25-roca].
- **Losses on 8 Jun.** See §1. In "an hour or two" of fighting Ukraine lost about a fifth of its Bradleys and 2A6s and half its 2Rs (Forbes, via Censor) [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles].
- **Recovery.** Many vehicles were immobilised rather than destroyed and were later recovered and repaired [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses] [src:bbc-2023-beale-western-armour]. A 47th assault commander claimed "every last vehicle was successfully retrieved" from the row of eight disabled Bradleys and a Leopard [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line].
- **Later losses.** By 6 Sep five Leopard 2s were destroyed (Forbes) and one Challenger 2 [src:kyivindependent-2023-fornusek-challenger-destroyed]. By mid-December the 47th had lost about 30 Bradleys and had about a dozen Leopard 2A6s left [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb] [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove].
- **Oryx whole-war totals** (to Oct 2026, not the window): 13 Leopard 2A6, 4 Leopard 2R, 3 Challenger 2, 189 M2A2 ODS-SA Bradleys and 12 Wisent 1 [src:oryx-ukrainian-losses].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**The Russian defence: minefields, a forward line, counterattacks.**
- **Doctrine.** ISW judged the 8 June response "doctrinally sound": the first echelon slowed the attack with mines and strongpoints, fell back to a second line, and reserves retook the first line [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca].
- **Weight forward.** Russia put its weight forward: 60% of its effort in the first belt, by Tarnavskyi's count [src:isw-2023-09-03-roca].
- **Surovikin against Gerasimov.** Kofman contrasts Surovikin's positional defence with Gerasimov's "active defence" of constant flank counterattacks [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-russian-blunders].
- **Mines.**
  - Fields were deepened to about 500 m, with stacked anti-tank mines and mixed anti-tank and anti-personnel fields [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - The Zemledeliye system scattered mines remotely [src:euromaidan-2026-mukhina-zemledeliye], and artillery-delivered mines made night resupply through the lanes deadly [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Remote-detonated mines were left in empty trenches [src:bbc-2023-beale-western-armour].
  - Russia re-mined cleared ground in October [src:kyivindependent-2023-amran-remining].
  - A 47th T-64 commander: rollers normally survive "up to four explosions", but stacked mines destroyed one after a single blast [src:bbc-2023-beale-western-armour].
- **The kill chain.**
  - Drones spot a column, and artillery, ATGMs, Ka-52s, Lancets and FPVs follow. "In many cases, a tank or armored vehicle will become immobilized after hitting a mine and then be destroyed by attack helicopters or drones" [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - Russia moved to Krasnopol and Lancet strikes on lead elements, "effect over volume" [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - EW disrupted Ukrainian GPS, radios and drones [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca] [src:isw-2023-06-15-roca].
  - Guided glide bombs pounded Orikhiv and Verbove [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line] [src:japcc-2026-zanier-glide-bombs].
- **Ka-52 lob fire and LMUR.**
  - The Ka-52 was "one of the most influential Russian weapons systems" against the offensive (UK MoD) [src:elpais-2023-ka52-counteroffensive].
  - It struck with Vikhr from 8–10 km, often with an EW escort, and its strikes were heralded by the lifting of Russian GPS jamming [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - The Ka-52M carried LMUR to about 15 km, beyond Ukrainian tactical air defence [src:kyivindependent-2023-court-ka52m-lmur] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - Lofted S-8 rockets gave a further 6–7 km [src:twz-2022-newdick-helicopter-lob].
  - On 8 Jun the column foundered under Ka-52 Vikhr fire [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
  - **Counters.** MANPADS from the 47th (its eighth Ka-52 on 17 Aug) [src:ukrinform-2023-ka52-robotyne], and ATACMS on the Berdiansk base on 17 Oct [src:isw-2023-10-17-roca]. Russian sources say Ka-52 use fell after mid-August losses [src:isw-2023-09-28-roca].
- **Lancet.** It hunted guns and lead vehicles. A Lancet-3 targeted an abandoned Leopard 2A6 after 8 Jun, with ambiguous results, and another hit a Stryker of the 82nd east of Robotyne on 15 Aug [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses] [src:kyivpost-2023-york-82nd-in-action] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-lancet].

**The Ukrainian breach (June).**
- **The design.** A company-size combined-arms breach behind mine-clearing vehicles, using the Leopard 2R, Wisent, BMR-2, M58 MICLIC, UR-77 and Meteorit line charges [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:euromaidan-2023-rfu-mala-tokmachka-assaults] [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
- **What went wrong**:
  - Lanes were too shallow, and crews strayed off them [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Columns stacked up on single lanes [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka].
  - One unit was late, another fired on friendly troops, and another was disoriented at night [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - Ukraine fired only 3% of its fire missions as smoke, because smoke blinded its own drone feeds [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - Training did not match reality. A Leopard crew had trained on minefields of "100 by 200 meters" and faced "hectares" [src:kyivpost-2023-brown-landmine-problem]. A soldier said the Americans taught Stinger drills from rooftops that would not survive the first tank shells [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line].

**The Ukrainian adaptation (July – November).**
- **Dismounted assault groups.** Platoons and companies of 10–15-man squads moved along tree lines, mostly on foot [src:cnn-2023-cotovio-pleitgen-verbove] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - The pace was 700–1,200 m every five days [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - Each battalion yielded only about two platoons of assault-capable troops [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
  - Engineers cleared mines by hand at night [src:isw-2023-09-03-roca].
- **Bradleys as "taxis".** They delivered and recovered assault groups and survived mines and a near-direct 120 mm mortar hit [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line].
- **Tank raids.** Leopard 2A6s fired HE into tree lines and pulled back at the first Russian round. Tanks ran along tree lines to set off anti-personnel mines for the infantry [src:euromaidan-2023-rfu-tank-raids].
- **The counter-battery war.** It used GMLRS and FPVs [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-counteroffensive-lurches], radar-hunting [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months], and night bombing with converted agricultural drones [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak]. Popov's complaint is the Russian side of this [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca].
- **Deep strikes.** Storm Shadow hit HQs and generals [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months] [src:cnn-2023-lister-popov-dismissed].
- **What countered Ukraine.** Russian FPVs took the edge at small-unit level by September. A 47th sergeant: "Our enemy has learned and adapted" [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line]. Air cover was missing against glide bombs and helicopters [src:kyivindependent-2023-kossov-out-of-steam].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The breach south of Mala Tokmachka (7–9 Jun).** *This is the Leopard–Bradley vignette.*
   - **The column.** Twenty vehicles in two groups move up a wide muddy track between hedgerows behind a Leopard 2R and a BMR-2 [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:euromaidan-2023-shandra-leopards-mala-tokmachka].
   - **First contact.** The first group hits mines and gives itself away. Drones then find the second, and shells and Ka-52 missiles fall on vehicles bunched "too close together near a minefield" [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
   - **Inside the kill zone.** Infantry run through the smoke between vehicles. A Bradley crew pops smoke and covers the dismounts as they climb onto it, wary of mines beside the track [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
   - **The reinforcement.** Four more Bradleys drive into the kill zone to retrieve troops [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
   - **The aftermath.** The drone image of a Leopard 2A6 and eight Bradleys in a row becomes the offensive's emblem, and the Russian MoD later parades a captured Leopard [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line] [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka].
   - **The toll.** Markus says five soldiers were lost [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka]. Most crews escaped through open hatches and lowered ramps [src:popularmechanics-2023-roblin-minefield-losses].
2. **The pre-dawn height (8 Jun).** An assault group slips between two Russian companies and takes a tactical height by 05:00. It shells the next height for three hours, and a soldier on the ground can see Polohy through binoculars [src:euromaidan-2023-rfu-mala-tokmachka-assaults].
3. **Spartacus's voice memo (12 Jul).** The fired 58th Army commander addresses his "gladiators": "we were hit from behind by our highest boss, treacherously and vilely". It comes a day after a Storm Shadow strike on his reserve command post [src:politico-2023-camut-popov-fired] [src:cnn-2023-lister-popov-dismissed] [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca].
4. **The 26 July push.** Company-size mechanized groups break into the first positions east of Robotyne, until Bradleys and a T-72 are left 2.5 km due east of the village. A lone vehicle reaches the anti-tank positions near Verbove [src:isw-2023-07-26-roca] [src:isw-2023-07-27-roca].
5. **Robotyne in a day (22–23 Aug).** An assault group briefed to take positions outside the village is told mid-move to take the first houses, then told again that friendly troops are further in. "In the space of a day, we moved forward more than two kilometers" [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line]. The 47th films civilians being led out with journalists present, and raises the flag on 23 Aug [src:isw-2023-08-22-roca] [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-advance-from-robotyne].
6. **The Ka-52 that did not come back (17 Aug).** A single MANPADS launch by the 47th downs a Ka-52 near Robotyne, the brigade's eighth. WarSpotting logs a Ka-52M lost near Novoprokopivka that day [src:ukrinform-2023-ka52-robotyne] [src:warspotting-api-russian-losses] [src:isw-2023-08-17-roca].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Ukraine and the US: one axis or three (spring 2023).**
   - US planners urged a massed mechanized assault on the southern axis alone [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].
   - Ukraine split its forces across three axes to stretch Russia [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis]. Kofman and Lee read it as probing for the weakest point [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
   - US officials later blamed the dispersal and continued investment around Bakhmut. Ukrainians replied that the US misunderstood the drone-saturated battlefield and withheld air power [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].
   - RUSI: Ukraine went in with only a couple of properly equipped brigades [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
2. **Timing.**
   - The Pentagon wanted mid-April. Kyiv waited for equipment and training, and the attack began in June [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].
   - Zelensky said the delay gave Russia time to fortify (relayed by Wikipedia) [src:wikipedia-2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive].
   - RUSI adds that poor operational security meant Russia knew the axis and timing [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
3. **Where to breach.** The 47th, untested, was given a frontal attack where the Russian lines were most concentrated [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line]. Western officials and analysts argued any force would have struggled without air superiority or enough breaching kit [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months] [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka].
4. **Stop the armour (about 11–12 Jun).**
   - On "the fourth day" Zaluzhnyi halted frontal assaults to save Western vehicles and switched to infantry-led attrition (WaPo, via Euromaidan Press) [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis].
   - Kofman and Lee describe the same switch [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
   - The trade-off was fewer armour losses, a slower advance and more time for the defender to reset [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak].
5. **Committing the second echelon (late July) and the reserves (mid-August).**
   - The 10th Corps was meant to pass through a breach that the 9th Corps had not made [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
   - The NYT reported much of the reserve committed on 26 Jul [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-counteroffensive-lurches].
   - The 82nd and 46th then gave quicker results, "enabled by other units having spent weeks of fighting over those areas" [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
   - RUSI judged a breakthrough likeliest in early August [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
6. **Russia: Popov's appeal and dismissal (July).**
   - Popov raised counter-battery and rotation problems "at the highest level" and was fired by order of the defence minister [src:politico-2023-camut-popov-fired].
   - ISW read it as an attempt to go over Gerasimov to Putin. It judged Russia had no reserves to grant rotation, and the effect on the defence marginal [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca].
7. **Russia: lateral reinforcement over rotation (August – September).** Rather than rotating exhausted units, Moscow moved in the 7th and 76th VDV and the 41st Army, accepting risk at Kherson and Kreminna. These were the third Russian lateral transfer of the counteroffensive and the second in a few weeks [src:isw-2023-08-17-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-01-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-vdv-robotyne].
8. **Ukraine: Tokmak or bust (September).**
   - Tarnavskyi set Tokmak as "the minimum goal" and promised to fight through winter on foot [src:cnn-2023-cotovio-pleitgen-verbove].
   - A secondary NYT account (2025), relayed by Wikipedia, says the aim had already shrunk from Melitopol to Tokmak. It says US suggestions to replace Tarnavskyi or send the marines to Robotyne were overruled, and Zaluzhnyi sent the marines across the Dnipro instead (see the Krynky chapter). Not verified here [src:wikipedia-2023-ukrainian-counteroffensive].
9. **Declaring the end (November).** Zaluzhnyi's "stalemate" essay in The Economist signalled that the offensive was over and opened a rift with Zelensky [src:abcnews-2023-burridge-stalemate] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khrebet-rudenko-zaluzhnyi].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Official triumph in June.**
  - The Russian MoD praised the Southern Military District with "an uncharacteristic degree of coherency" [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca].
  - Romanchuk said the attack was telegraphed by artillery preparation. Popov credited the 58th Army's minefields [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca].
  - The MoD claimed 150 Ukrainian tanks and AFVs were used and 30 tanks destroyed, including 3 Leopards. Unofficial Russian sources stressed that Ukraine was using far more wheeled vehicles than tanks [src:euromaidan-2023-rfu-mala-tokmachka-assaults].
  - ISW called the Russian claims of destroyed vehicles "highly inflated" [src:isw-2023-06-09-roca], and DeepState said Russia recirculated the same footage from new angles [src:euromaidan-2023-shandra-leopards-mala-tokmachka].
- **Milbloggers' explanations.** They cited EW, an "unprecedented" amount of helicopter support, landmines and layered fortifications [src:isw-2023-06-08-roca], and a "strategic defense" to attrit Ukraine on the first line [src:isw-2023-06-15-roca]. Some prematurely declared the counteroffensive failed [src:isw-2023-06-09-roca].
- **Popov's revolt.** "The armed forces of Ukraine couldn't break through our army on the frontline; we were hit from behind by our highest boss" [src:politico-2023-camut-popov-fired]. Milbloggers called him "intelligent" and "competent", and none disputed his complaints [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca]. In 2025 he was jailed for stealing metal meant for the very fortifications his army defended [src:kyivindependent-2025-fornusek-popov-jailed].
- **Alarm in August.** Milbloggers wrote of exhausted, unrotated units [src:isw-2023-08-25-roca], called the situation at Verbove "very dangerous" [src:isw-2023-08-29-roca], and worried about the Robotyne–Verbove line [src:isw-2023-08-28-roca]. Shoigu called Zaporizhzhia "the tensest" direction [src:isw-2023-09-05-roca].
- **Denial in September.** A Kremlin-affiliated milblogger insisted the "Surovikin" line west of Verbove was unbroken [src:isw-2023-09-22-roca]. "Storm Ossetia" and "Alania" called breakthrough reports false [src:isw-2023-09-28-roca]. Russian sources later credited the Ka-52 with disrupting the early assaults [src:isw-2023-09-28-roca].
- **Claimed totals.** Shoigu claimed over 90,000 Ukrainian killed and wounded, almost 600 tanks and about 1,900 armoured vehicles across the counteroffensive. ISW called this "implausible" [src:isw-2023-10-30-roca].
- **Western analysis.**
  - RUSI calls the e5 defence "textbook" (three belts, rotated regiments, cached ammunition, Lancets and helicopters, early glide bombs) [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].
  - Kofman and Lee note that, unlike in Kherson in 2022, Russia manned its forward positions most heavily [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Win band.**
  - **Hard conditions at 30 Nov.** Robotyne is Ukrainian, Tokmak is Russian, and the salient's tip is at Novoprokopivka's northern edge and Verbove's western outskirts [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-robotyne-liberated] [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca].
  - **Score the date Robotyne falls.** 22–28 Aug is historical, and earlier is a bonus [src:isw-2023-08-22-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-khalilova-robotyne-liberated].
  - **Score Western armour preserved against the 8 Jun benchmark.** The floor is DeepState's irrecoverable count (at least 4 APCs and 1 Leopard). The ceiling is Forbes's 25 vehicles [src:euromaidan-2023-shandra-leopards-mala-tokmachka] [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles].
  - **Russian attrition.** About 156 confirmed equipment losses on the axis is the historical yardstick [src:warspotting-api-russian-losses].
- **Self-running script.**
  - A mechanized breach on 7–9 Jun fails. Armour assaults stop about 11–12 Jun, followed by six weeks of 100 m-a-day infantry grinding [src:euromaidan-2023-wp-analysis] [src:isw-2023-07-20-roca].
  - The 10th Corps push on 26 Jul, and the 82nd and 46th committed about 15 Aug [src:isw-2023-07-26-roca] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months].
  - Robotyne falls on 22–28 Aug, Verbove is probed from 30 Aug and the Verbove belt broken on 20–23 Sep, then the front goes positional from October [src:isw-2023-08-30-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca] [src:isw-2023-11-06-roca].
  - Scripted Russian events: the Popov dismissal on 12 Jul, the 7th VDV from about 16 Aug, the 76th VDV from about 26 Aug, the 41st Army from 1 Sep, and re-mining from October [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca] [src:isw-2023-08-22-roca] [src:isw-2023-08-26-roca] [src:isw-2023-09-01-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-amran-remining].
- **Core mechanic: breaching order.** The player chooses:
  - lane count and depth against minefields 120–500 m deep with stacked mines [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak]
  - whether to commit breaching tanks (scarce: 6 Leopard 2R, a few Wisents) [src:censor-2023-forbes-25-vehicles]
  - H-hour against a short June night, with sunrise at 04:40 and a late unit landing in daylight [src:open-meteo-historical-archive] [src:warontherocks-2023-kofman-lee-three-months]
  - smoke against its own drone picture [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak]
  - **Failure modes:** stacking on one lane, and Ka-52s at 8–15 km [src:guardian-2023-beaumont-wintour-mala-tokmachka] [src:kyivindependent-2023-court-ka52m-lmur].
- **Core mechanic: dismounted against mounted.** After the switch the player trades speed (mechanized, high vehicle loss) against attrition (infantry at 700–1,200 m per five days, about two assault platoons per battalion). Bradleys are better used as armoured "taxis" than as assault vehicles [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-47th-surovikin-line].
- **Ka-52 lob fire as a layer.** The helicopter threat should dominate the June map and decline after mid-August as MANPADS kills mount and ATACMS hit Berdiansk (17 Oct). The GPS-jamming lift is a tell for the player [src:rusi-2023-watling-stormbreak] [src:isw-2023-09-28-roca] [src:isw-2023-10-17-roca].
- **Counter-battery as the hidden win condition.** Russian artillery degradation, Popov's complaint, is what lets the infantry advance. Model it as a meter fed by GMLRS, FPVs and radar kills [src:isw-2023-07-13-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-farrell-counteroffensive-lurches].
- **Terrain.** The attack runs uphill from about 55 m to about 150 m, along a grid of shelterbelts 0.5–1.2 km apart. The T0408 is the spine, and the high ground south of Robotyne is the Russian last stand [src:opentopodata-srtm30m] [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample] [src:isw-2023-09-23-roca].
- **Pacing.** The window runs from heat (to 37 °C) through a dry September to November mud and storms. Milley's "30 to 45 days" from 11 Sep is the in-game clock warning [src:open-meteo-historical-archive] [src:kyivindependent-2023-court-4-8-km2].
