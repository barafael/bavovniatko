# The battle for Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad

July 2024 – February 2026 (the main window is 2025). Pokrovsk and its twin mining town Myrnohrad (Dymytrov until 2016) in western Donetsk Oblast, with the approach from Avdiivka and the Dobropillia salient to the north.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

After Avdiivka fell in February 2024, Russia drove west for Pokrovsk, the road and rail hub of western Donbas. It broke through at Prohres in July 2024 and took Novohrodivka and Selydove that autumn [src:forbes-2024-axe-prohres-breakout] [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-novohrodivka-72h] [src:kyivindependent-2024-york-selydove]. Frontal attacks stalled at the city's edge. Through the winter of 2024–25 Russia tried to turn the towns from the south-west, then from the east, cutting the highways to Kostiantynivka and Mezhova in January 2025 [src:isw-2025-01-13-roca]. Ukrainian counter-attacks in February 2025 won back a few villages [src:kyivpost-2025-struck-feshchenko-kotlyne].

From July 2025 the battle became the war's defining drone fight. Russian FPV teams, Rubikon among them, put every road into Pokrovsk under fire control, and ISW judged that this had already denied Ukraine the city as a logistics hub [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months]. Russian infantry no longer stormed. It seeped through in groups of two to three and by the hundred a day, walking for days from Selydove [src:nv-2025-horova-deepstate-150-saboteurs] [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-pokrovsk-poised]. On 11 August such groups opened a 15 km wedge towards Dobropillia. The 1st Azov Corps and other reserves cut it off and cleared it by late October [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-deepstate-wedge] [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons]. Meanwhile infiltrators accumulated inside Pokrovsk itself: about 200–250 by late October, then more than 300 in the November fog [src:pravda-2025-kyrylenko-250-russians-pokrovsk] [src:euromaidan-2025-axe-foggy-advance]. The garrison was squeezed into the northern quarters and Myrnohrad onto a single drone-watched road [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad]. Russia announced the capture of Pokrovsk on 1 December and of Myrnohrad on 27 December. Ukraine held parts of both towns into late January 2026 [src:cnn-2025-stockwell-capture-claim] [src:isw-2025-12-28-roca] [src:isw-2026-02-25-roca].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Map box.** About 45 × 45 km. It runs from Kucheriv Yar and Zolotyi Kolodiaz (the tip of the Dobropillia salient) in the north to Selydove and Novohrodivka in the south, and from Udachne in the west to Prohres and Ocheretyne in the east [src:osm-photon-2026-pokrovsk-places]. Avdiivka, the start line of February 2024, sits 45 km south-east of Pokrovsk, just off the box. Pokrovsk stands about 60 km south-west of Kramatorsk and 100 km east of Pavlohrad, the rear railhead used for the evacuation [src:osm-photon-2026-pokrovsk-places] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-pokrovsk].

**The towns: mining-town geometry.**
- *Footprints.* In OSM, Pokrovsk's town boundary spans about 8.6 × 7.3 km and Myrnohrad's about 5.1 × 8.5 km [src:osm-photon-2026-pokrovsk-places]. Syrskyi put the city at 29 km² [src:united24-2025-litnarovych-155000].
- *Building types.* Both are coal towns that pair mine shafts and spoil heaps (terykony) with apartment blocks and districts of private houses [src:wikipedia-battle-of-pokrovsk]. The Donetsk railway cuts east–west through central Pokrovsk and became the front line inside the city [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-20-bodies] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-russian-bmp].
- *Mines and heaps in OSM* [src:osm-photon-2026-pokrovsk-places]:
  - the Tsentralna mine (48.312, 37.263) and the spoil heaps of mine No. 3 (48.294, 37.272) and of Tsentralna (48.335, 37.276), in Myrnohrad;
  - the Rodynska mine (48.355, 37.222) at Rodynske;
  - the Kapitalna mine and the Myrnohradska washery (48.329, 37.308) to the east;
  - the Pokrovske mine administration (48.259, 37.061) near Kotlyne;
  - the heap of mine No. 21 (48.257, 37.150) on Pokrovsk's southern edge.
- *Why the heaps matter.* They give fire control over the approaches: Russian forces worked to bypass Selydove's slag heaps, whose capture would give fire control over Pokrovsk's surroundings [src:wikipedia-battle-of-pokrovsk]. On 14 November Russians planted a flag on a ruined mine on Myrnohrad's south-eastern edge [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-flag-on-mine]. In March 2026 Russian units hung antennas and repeaters on Myrnohrad's high-rises, and those emitters drew Ukrainian strikes [src:isw-2026-03-10-roca].
- *The two towns differ.* Pokrovsk is easy to reach from the south through villages such as Zvirove, where infiltrators use the ruins. Myrnohrad sits among fields with clearer lines of sight [src:euromaidanpress-2025-rfu-black-hawk-corridor].
- *The pocket.* By 29 October the gap between the Russian pincers north of Pokrovsk and south-west of Krasnyi Lyman was about 3 km [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-encirclement-what-we-know], and by late November about 1 km [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-raiders-425th].

**Roads and rail.** Pokrovsk sits where several supply highways meet, and its railway station was regionally significant until it closed on 5 September 2024 [src:wikipedia-battle-of-pokrovsk] [src:kyivindependent-2024-court-train-station].
- *Cut in January 2025:* the T-0504 to Kostiantynivka (the H-32) and the T-0406 to Mezhova [src:isw-2025-01-13-roca].
- *Under drone attack from July 2025:* the T-0515 north through Rodynske and Bilytske to Dobropillia, and the T-0514 Dobropillia–Lyman road, which was closed to civilians from 16 July [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai].
- *The western flank:* the M-30/E-50 to Pavlohrad, held by the 32nd Brigade [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad].
- *The southern approach:* the same highway runs from Selydove into the city; this is where the November fog column came in [src:bbc-2025-abdurasulov-fog-pokrovsk].
- *Into Myrnohrad:* in December only roads through Rivne and Svitle remained [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-13-sq-km].
- *Rivers:* the Kazennyi Torets and the Vovcha are the named river lines [src:wikipedia-battle-of-pokrovsk]. Novoekonomichne's reservoirs, river line, industrial zone and two spoil heaps made it "a fortress" [src:euromaidan-2025-martyniuk-matveyev-final-battle].

**The open ground.**
- *Measured west of Pokrovsk* [src:osm-overpass-2026-landscape-sample]:
  - 67% of the 100 km² sample box is mapped as farmland;
  - fields have a median of 65 ha and median dimensions of 625 × 1,348 m;
  - shelterbelts run NNW–SSE and are under-mapped, at only 0.28 km per km² in OSM;
  - tracks run at 0.55 km per km², primary to tertiary roads at 0.13 km per km².
- *Settlements.* The same box holds 2,758 buildings in eight settlements [src:microsoft-global-ml-building-footprints], and villages sit along wooded balkas [src:eox-2021-s2cloudless].
- *Treelines.* A 1 km treeline near Pokrovsk was the unit of defence: an exposed 80 × 30 m trench in it was destroyed, while two concealed holes held for 10 days [src:wotr-2026-maurin-front-line].
- *Ravines.* The August infiltrators used ravines running north–south and the summer foliage [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons].

**Weather and light.**
- *Spring 2025.* Mechanised assaults resumed by mid-April 2025 as the ground hardened [src:spire-2025-soil-moisture].
- *The Dobropillia breach, 7–14 August 2025.* It came in dry, clear summer weather, 25–30 °C with little rain, and sunrise about 05:20 local [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26]. Foliage and thermal blankets, not fog, hid the infiltrators [src:suspilne-2025-ivantsiv-how-breakthrough].
- *October 2025.* The armoured attacks on Shakhove were timed to rain and morning fog (6, 9 and 13 October) [src:isw-2025-10-14-roca].
- *The November fog, 5–12 November.* ERA5 shows overcast, near-saturated air (daily mean relative humidity 86–96%, light wind), and our fog proxy counts 6–10 hours a day on 5–7, 10 and 12 November. November 2025 had the most fog-proxy hours of the winter: 84, against 52 in December and 14 in January [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26]. Reported effects:
  - more than 300 Russians got in [src:euromaidan-2025-axe-foggy-advance];
  - the column of cars and bikes drove in on 9 November [src:bbc-2025-abdurasulov-fog-pokrovsk];
  - wet weather disadvantaged light FPVs [src:aljazeera-2025-psaropoulos-pokrovsk];
  - up to 10 infiltrators a day got in on sunny days and 30–40 on rainy ones [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad].
- *1–2 December 2025.* Overcast, 4–6 °C, with 7 mm of rain on 2 December [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26], during the Russian claim and the turtle-tank assault in fog [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-turtle-tanks-myrnohrad].
- *Light.* Sunset about 16:40 in December, giving about 8.5 hours of daylight [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26].
- *January 2026.* The fall came in deep cold: −15 °C on 17 and 20 January and −18 °C on 4 February, with a thin snow cover of 3–7 cm [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26]. The 7th Corps said the cold caused Russian hypothermia and frostbite losses [src:united24-2026-litnarovych-winter]. Icing hampers fibre-optic FPVs [src:euromaidan-2026-kossov-icy-fibre].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **The kill zone (both sides).**
  - *Depth.* The contested zone near Pokrovsk was about 1 km deep on 1 January 2025 and five to eight times wider by 2026 [src:wotr-2026-maurin-front-line]. ISW reported in August 2025 that Ukrainian sources put the kill zone at "at least 10 kilometers" from the line, against 0.5–2 km in 2024 [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai]. In the same month soldiers spoke of a "20-kilometre kill zone" [src:suspilne-2025-ivantsiv-how-breakthrough]. By January 2026 soldiers near Myrnohrad said both sides' kill zones had grown "to about 30 kilometers" [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-if-you-stop-you-die]. In December the National Guard put kill zones on the Pokrovsk and Dobropillia axes at up to 30 km deep, with the most intense band 5–10 km [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-kill-zones-everywhere]. By July 2026 the 7th Air Assault Corps measured 20–25 km on its sector, with drones causing 70–80% of damage [src:euromaidanpress-2026-vivdych-kill-zone-corps].
  - *Roads under fire control.* From July 2025 Russian drones achieved battlefield air interdiction against the supply roads, and ISW judged that this already denied Ukraine the use of Pokrovsk as a logistics hub [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-putin-claims-isw]. The key roads were the T-0514 Dobropillia road and the T-0515 through Rodynske [src:isw-2025-08-12-roca]. "Virtually every vehicle heading into the city comes under attack… The main losses aren't at the positions, but on the road" (Serhii Sternenko, November) [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-time-to-retreat-myrnohrad]. Medics said vehicles could get no closer than 10–15 km [src:isw-2025-11-16-roca], and troops walked 10–15 km to their positions [src:pravda-2025-kyrylenko-250-russians-pokrovsk].
  - *When it closed.* Russian FPVs, fibre-optic ones included, "controlled all of the entrances to Pokrovsk from the sky" by the beginning of summer 2025 [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-pokrovsk-brink]. On the T0515 through Rodynske, Russia hit "two or three Ukrainian vehicles daily" in late July [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai]. By 6 November less than 5 km of drone-infested fields linked Myrnohrad to the rest of the front [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-knife-edge].
  - *Russian means.* Longer-range FPVs, thermobaric warheads and "waiter" drones parked along roads [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months]. Radio and fibre-optic FPVs with thermal cameras made night only slightly safer than day [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-drone-strike-rubicon]. Russia was said to hold a 10:1 drone advantage in October [src:euromaidanpress-2025-murdoch-outnumbered-8-to-1].
- **Rubikon (RU).**
  - The Center for Advanced Unmanned Technologies, founded on 2 August 2024, had about 1,450 people in March 2025 and about 5,000 by spring 2026 [src:fpri-2026-lee-putiata-rubicon]. More than a quarter of its strikes hit Ukrainian drone teams [src:rferl-2025-rubicon].
  - Rubikon and Sudny Den arrived in this part of Donetsk Oblast "over the spring" of 2025; a 68th Brigade pilot said "in a day they torched eight of our cars" [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-24h-pokrovsk]. Some of the crews that had worked against the Kursk salient moved near Pokrovsk [src:united24-2025-wesdorp-rubikon].
  - After cutting off Sudzha in February–March 2025, it was moved to the Pokrovsk direction to interdict logistics and kill drone operators [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-drone-strike-rubicon]. A 7th Corps spokesman confirmed its presence: "Their tactic is to cut off the Ukrainian Defense Forces' logistical routes" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-20-bodies]. Ukrainian soldiers said Pokrovsk was "like a holiday" before it arrived [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-rubikon-holiday].
  - Its FPV teams held the single road into Myrnohrad "almost constantly under… fire control" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad]. Other named Russian drone units on the axis were Sudny Den (Judgment Day), the "Irish" (Grom-Kaskad) and Archangels [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-flag-on-mine] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-myrnohrad-encircled].
  - *Counters.* HUR struck a claimed Rubikon base near Avdiivka in early November with long-range FPVs [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-drone-strike-rubicon]. In January 2026 Starlink-equipped Rubikon drones reaching the E-50 behind Pokrovsk triggered the Starlink whitelist [src:isw-2026-02-01-roca].
- **Infiltration (RU).**
  - *The walk.* In July 2025 three groups of about 50 walked 14 days from Selydove to the city, about 600 m a day, supplied by drone drops and wearing cloaks. About 30 of 150 arrived, and the rest were killed by drone drops [src:nv-2025-horova-deepstate-150-saboteurs]. The August Dobropillia force (200–300) had also walked about two weeks from Selydove [src:kyivpost-2025-korshak-reserves-contain].
  - *Sizes.* Groups of three "with the expectation that two will be destroyed", up to 300 men a day [src:aljazeera-2025-psaropoulos-pokrovsk]. About 100 such groups a day in November [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-pokrovsk-poised] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months]. Single men by January 2026 [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-one-by-one].
  - *Weather.* Up to 10 men got in on a sunny day and 30–40 on a rainy one [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad].
  - *Targets.* Drone crews first, engaged at close range so that they could not launch [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months]. Infiltrators carried two radios, one to pass the line and one to correct fire from a "burrow" [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-burrow]. Some wore civilian clothes [src:aljazeera-2025-psaropoulos-pokrovsk].
  - *Doctrine.* Mashovets describes three phases: preparation (Spetsnaz find supply roads and drone sites), execution, then exploitation by many small groups [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months].
- **Motorcycles, buggies, civilian cars and quad bikes (RU).** On 9 November, in fog, a column of cars and motorbikes drove into southern Pokrovsk on the Selydove highway [src:bbc-2025-abdurasulov-fog-pokrovsk]. Motorcycle groups of 6–8 bikes carried 2–3 EW sets, and few groups survived [src:frontelligence-2025-motorcycle-assaults]. In March 2026 quad bikes carrying up to four men replaced motorcycles for flanking runs near Myrnohrad [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-myrnohrad-quad-bikes].
- **Mechanised assaults in fog and rain (RU).**
  - *Dobropillia.* Company to reinforced-battalion columns on Shakhove on 6, 9, 13, 16 and 27 October, timed to rain and fog [src:isw-2025-10-14-roca] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-29-vehicles-volodymyrivka].
  - *Myrnohrad.* Turtle tanks of the 40th Naval Infantry Brigade near Razine on 2 December, with 11 vehicles lost [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-turtle-tanks-myrnohrad].
  - *Inside Pokrovsk.* ISW predicted that infiltration alone could not generate enough mass to clear the city, forcing Russia to bring troops in by vehicle in bad weather [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-isw-fix-from-west].
- **Glide bombs (RU).** 850 KABs on Pokrovsk in October, and 156 in one autumn day [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-20-bodies].
- **Ukrainian logistics by robot and bomber drone.**
  - *Where the share was highest.* Some brigades moved most forward supply to UGVs and heavy Vampire multicopters. One Air Assault brigade flew 10–12 t a month, about 80% of its deliveries [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-robotic-logistics].
  - *Inside the towns.* From the first weeks of the city fight in October, no Ukrainian vehicle could get through the corridor to Myrnohrad, so every supply run was made by drone or UGV, and the UGVs "usually don't survive the trip" [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-myrnohrad-encirclement] [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-knife-edge]. Large bomber drones could still lift 60 mm mortar bombs but not 120 mm ones, and keeping drone teams in fuel took many sorties [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad]. In January 2026 the defenders of both towns were supplied by "heavy drones and ground robotic complexes" [src:interfax-2026-skhid-northern-pokrovsk]. NATO said Myrnohrad was being resupplied by drone [src:eurointegration-2025-nato-95-percent].
  - *Gun robots.* The 93rd Brigade used gun-armed UGVs in the Dobropillia counter-attack [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-armed-robots].
  - *Limits.* UGVs cannot cross rivers and stick in mud and craters [src:euromaidan-2026-murdoch-robotic-medevac].
- **Casualty evacuation.**
  - *Robots.* On 15 October a Zmiy UGV (500 kg payload, 10 km/h, 20 km range) carried a wounded man out under 152 mm fire, a mine blast and an FPV hit, after losing its link for 1.5 hours [src:euromaidanpress-2025-kossov-zmiy-evacuation]. SSO's Sword Group cleared a blocking position so that a ground robot could evacuate three wounded [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-sword-group]. A 25th Brigade UGV took a direct FPV hit and still brought out two wounded in a six-hour mission [src:euromaidanpress-2026-murdoch-ugv-evacuation].
  - *Waiting times.* Near Pokrovsk in July the wounded had to "wait days" [src:cnn-2025-walsh-summer-offensive]. By November some seriously wounded men lay in position for two weeks, and Red Cross-marked UGVs drew fire [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-bakhmut-to-pokrovsk-tactics] [src:isw-2025-11-16-roca].
  - *The general picture.* In 2025, UGVs typically brought wounded out from 2–5 km at night to medical posts 7 km or more behind [src:rusi-2025-watling-combined-arms]. By late October, medics said evacuation of the wounded was nearly impossible because all the bridges had been blown [src:kyivindependent-2025-terajima-8-to-1].
- **Net tunnels.** On the drive into Pokrovsk in June 2025 the nets were "helpful, but enemy drones simply need to find the entrance or create one", and one net hung down across the road [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-24h-pokrovsk]. Syrskyi ordered "anti-drone tunnels" and barriers against "total infiltration" on 2 August [src:kyivindependent-2025-fenbert-total-infiltration]. By October Russian FPVs "cut through anti-drone netting" on the Myrnohrad route [src:ukrainskapravda-2025-kyrylenko-five-observations]. Russia had netted its own roads since February 2025 [src:defenseexpress-2025-russian-net-tunnels].
- **Fog: the decisive weather.** It hurts the side that relies on drones and helps the side moving on the ground [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad].
  - *For Russia:* fog let more than 300 Russians into Pokrovsk on 7–12 November [src:euromaidan-2025-axe-foggy-advance].
  - *For Ukraine:* fog was the only cover under which Ukrainian vehicles could reach Myrnohrad [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad] and under which Ukrainians could leave Pokrovsk, as a milblogger admitted [src:isw-2025-11-16-roca].
  - *Ukrainian counters:* UGVs as ground spotters, SIGINT direction-finding and AI-assisted FPVs [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-turtle-tanks-myrnohrad].
- **Search-and-strike in the ruins (UA).** Joint teams of SSO, HUR, SBU, Military Law Enforcement and police hunted infiltrators building by building [src:kyivindependent-2025-myronyshena-hur-raid]. Radio intercepts gave away call signs, and in one Myrnohrad clearance Ukrainian troops called out in Russian, "Kedr, we're your own", and walked in [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-myrnohrad-encircled]. Loudspeaker drones broadcast surrender instructions [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-hryshyne-clearing].
- **Flag raising for the cameras (both sides).** Russian flags appeared on the western entrance sign on 29 October (destroyed within an hour), on a ruined mine in Myrnohrad on 14 November, and in the Pokrovsk centre in the MoD video of 1 December [src:pravda-2025-pohorilov-flag-destroyed] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-flag-on-mine] [src:cnn-2025-stockwell-capture-claim]. ISW treated the December flags in Myrnohrad as infiltration missions that "did not change control of terrain" [src:isw-2025-12-28-roca]. Ukraine answered with a flag on a live drone feed in northern Pokrovsk (9 December) [src:bbc-2025-beale-flag-pokrovsk].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The 14-day walk** (July 2025). Three groups of about 50 men move from Selydove to the Pishchane industrial zone, then creep about 600 m a day towards Zakhysnykiv Ukrainy Street. Drones drop their water and food, and cloaks hide them from thermal cameras. About 120 die from drone drops, and some 30 reach the city [src:nv-2025-horova-deepstate-150-saboteurs]. The player's drone line sees some groups and misses others.
2. **"The brigade ran out of infantry."** On 21 July DeepState reports that Russians walked in through Zvirove because one brigade had no infantry left and positions were misreported. The 155th and 68th brigades have to plug the hole [src:pravda-2025-up-russians-entered-pokrovsk].
3. **The Dobropillia "rabbit ears"** (11–17 August). A 15 km wedge appears on the map in five days. DeepState and the General Staff publicly disagree about it [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-deepstate-wedge] [src:pravda-2025-shumilin-military-deny-breakthrough]. The Azov Corps arrives, and the 93rd Brigade's tanks and gun robots clear Zolotyi Kolodiaz, Hruzke and Vesele [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-armed-robots].
4. **The Kucheriv Yar pocket** (22 October). The 132nd Reconnaissance Battalion retakes the village and captures more than 50 Russians, and civilians are found in basements [src:pravda-2025-romanenko-kucheriv-yar] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-murdoch-kucheriv-yar].
5. **The railway station** (about 20 October). An infiltration team reaches the centre, kills civilians and is destroyed inside a station building [src:rbc-2025-sabotage-team-railway-station].
6. **The flag on the entrance sign** (29 October, 09:40–10:40). A Russian flag goes up on the western entrance sign by the Pavlohrad highway and is destroyed within an hour [src:pravda-2025-pohorilov-flag-destroyed].
7. **The Black Hawk** (late October). HUR special forces land by helicopter in the northern part of the city, in an operation overseen by Budanov, to hold the corridor out [src:reuters-2025-dysa-balmforth-special-forces-pokrovsk]. Russia claims all 11 were killed and Ukraine denies it [src:kyivindependent-2025-myronyshena-hur-raid].
8. **Mad Max in the fog** (9 November). Russian infantry on civilian cars and motorbikes drive up the Selydove highway into southern Pokrovsk under fog, and 68th Brigade drones partly destroy the column [src:bbc-2025-abdurasulov-fog-pokrovsk].
9. **The road into Myrnohrad** (November – January). A single road, 1–3 km of corridor, Rubikon FPVs overhead. Vehicles can run only on the foggiest days, and a stretch of clear weather could doom the garrison [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-raiders-425th].
10. **Turtle tanks at Razine** (2 December). A Russian naval-infantry column in fog hits an anti-tank ditch, and 11 vehicles burn [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-turtle-tanks-myrnohrad].
11. **The flag on the drone feed** (9 December). Skala soldiers wave a Ukrainian flag in northern Pokrovsk for the BBC eight days after Moscow's claim [src:bbc-2025-beale-flag-pokrovsk].
12. **Robots under fire.** The Zmiy evacuation under 152 mm fire [src:euromaidanpress-2025-kossov-zmiy-evacuation], and gun robots in residential yards [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-armed-robots].
13. **Coda: the commandant's office** (28 January 2026). Russian command posts and a commandant's office move into Myrnohrad. Ukrainian marines in the north are told to "not let them out of the city", and there is no order to leave [src:pravda-2026-kyrylenko-myrnohrad-staffs].

## 7. Decision points

- **Leave trenches empty to rebuild brigades (summer 2025).**
  - *What was decided:* according to the American analyst Andrew Perpetua, Ukrainian commanders deliberately left some trenches north-east of Pokrovsk empty so that brigades could refit: "Sacrifice ground for time while refitting and then you can attack later" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-155th-marines-himars].
  - *What followed:* the Dobropillia breach came through those gaps [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-armed-robots]. Kyiv Independent places the weak point at the seam between two exhausted National Guard brigades and blames false reporting up the chain [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons].
- **Commit the Azov Corps to Dobropillia, not to Pokrovsk (August).**
  - *What was decided:* Syrskyi sent the last large reserve, the 1st Azov Corps, against the salient [src:kyivindependent-2025-hodunova-azov-deployed].
  - *What followed:* the salient was gone by late October [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons].
  - *The debate:* David Axe, citing the analyst "Delwin", argues that the salient was a "counter-fixation axis" that kept Azov out of Pokrovsk itself [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-dobropillia-diversion]. CIT judged both towns "effectively lost" because the reserves went to Dobropillia [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-time-to-retreat-myrnohrad]. Farrell calls the operation costly "fire-fighting" rather than a successful counter-offensive [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons].
- **Hold or withdraw (October – January).**
  - *The case for withdrawal:* Black Bird Group said Ukraine should have withdrawn by about mid-summer [src:kyivindependent-2025-terajima-8-to-1]. On 4 November CIT said "It is time… to abandon Myrnohrad and the area south of it" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-time-to-retreat-myrnohrad]. Clément Molin wrote that observers "struggle to understand Syrskyi's strategy" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad]. Sternenko pointed to a pattern of late withdrawals at Bakhmut, Avdiivka and Sudzha [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-time-to-retreat-myrnohrad].
  - *The command's line:* Syrskyi said there was "no encirclement or blockade" and "Pokrovsk – we hold. Myrnohrad – we hold" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-syrskyi-no-encirclement]. Soldiers told CNN on 8 November and UP on 28 January that there was no order to withdraw [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-pokrovsk-poised] [src:pravda-2026-kyrylenko-myrnohrad-staffs].
  - *What followed:* a staged exit. The 68th and 155th brigades began leaving Pokrovsk on foot from about 27 October [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-commandos-escape-corridor]. On 8 December came an "organized maneuver" out of the salient south of the city [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-salient-withdrawal]. By February 2026 Ukraine had fallen back a short distance north of Pokrovsk [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-falling-back-north]. No official order to withdraw from either town was found (§11).
- **Send special forces in rather than a brigade (late October).**
  - *What was decided:* Syrskyi ordered SSO, HUR, SBU and military-police groups into the city [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-syrskyi-no-encirclement].
  - *The alternative argued:* DeepState called for a mop-up "by a full brigade, not by small special-forces groups" [src:pravda-2025-deepstate-infiltrating-pokrovsk].
- **Silence versus maps.** DeepState's public depiction of the Dobropillia breach prompted faster reaction from higher command than official channels, according to a battalion commander [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-front-line-mapping]. The Dnipro group's spokesman, by contrast, told viewers not to believe unofficial maps [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-dobropillia-lessons].
- **Rob Pokrovsk to save Zaporizhzhia (January – February 2026).** After the Starlink cut-off, Kyiv moved units including the 425th Assault Regiment south to counter-attack at Huliaipole, accepting the loss of ground north of Pokrovsk [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-falling-back-north]. Axe judged this "a hard decision, but one that has paid off" [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-falling-back-north].
- **Russia: encircle or storm.** ISW judged that Russia was trying to encircle and storm the towns at the same time. In November it seemed to shift to closing the ring from the west, while fixing Ukrainian troops inside to prevent an orderly withdrawal [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-isw-fix-from-west]. Russian armour was held back until infantry had accumulated, then committed in fog [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-raiders-425th].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **The claims and their timing.** Gerasimov said on 26 October that up to 10,000 Ukrainians were encircled, which pro-war bloggers and ISW disputed [src:moscowtimes-2025-tenisheva-gerasimov-encirclement]. He reported the capture of "Krasnoarmeysk" to Putin on 30 November, and Peskov announced it on 1 December, just before the Witkoff talks [src:cnn-2025-stockwell-capture-claim] [src:dw-2025-shepeleva-is-ukraine-losing]. Putin called the city a "good base" for advancing in "any direction" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-putin-claims-isw]. Solodchuk claimed Myrnohrad and Rodynske on 27 December [src:isw-2025-12-28-roca]. Ukrainian officers say Russia used Pokrovsk as leverage in the US-led talks rather than for its own value [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-20-bodies].
- **Units credited** by Russian officials: for Pokrovsk, the 27th Motor Rifle Division (2nd CAA) with the 506th and 1435th regiments and the 30th Brigade; for Myrnohrad, the 5th Brigade (51st CAA) and the 1435th Regiment; for Rodynske, the 9th Brigade [src:isw-2025-12-02-roca] [src:isw-2025-12-28-roca].
- **Milblogger admissions.** No encirclement existed in October [src:moscowtimes-2025-tenisheva-gerasimov-encirclement]. Fog let Ukrainians exit Pokrovsk [src:isw-2025-11-16-roca]. The base of the Dobropillia penetration was too narrow for logistics [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-isw-fix-from-west]. A Russian brigade commander described about 3 km of advance towards Myrnohrad in four months [src:isw-2025-12-28-roca].
- **A report withdrawn.** A Ukrainian officer said Russia "officially announced the capture of Myrnohrad last week — and then quietly removed the report" (February 2026) [src:ukrinform-2026-liskovych-front-spring].
- **The Russian naming.** Russia still uses the pre-2016 names, Krasnoarmeysk for Pokrovsk and Dimitrov for Myrnohrad [src:dw-2025-shepeleva-is-ukraine-losing] [src:interfax-2016-rada-renames].
- **What ISW concluded.** The fall of the towns did not open the way north. Russia had not exploited Pokrovsk as of late February 2026, Hryshyne 2 km north-west still held, and the Fortress Belt was untouched [src:isw-2026-02-25-roca]. The 51st and 2nd CAAs were "severely degraded" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-putin-claims-isw].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Win band: lose slowly.**
  - *The towns:* Pokrovsk holds, at least in part, until on or after 1 December 2025, and Ukrainians are still inside until about 28 January 2026 [src:cnn-2025-stockwell-capture-claim] [src:isw-2026-02-25-roca]. Myrnohrad falls no earlier than January 2026 [src:isw-2026-02-04-roca].
  - *The salient:* Dobropillia is erased by late October [src:pravda-2025-romanenko-kucheriv-yar].
  - *Beyond the towns:* Hryshyne and the line north of the towns hold [src:isw-2026-02-25-roca].
  - *Russian pace:* an average advance of no more than about 70 m a day [src:csis-2026-jones-grinding-war].
  - *Russian cost:* at least the 2025 national benchmark of about 85 casualties per km² gained [src:euromaidan-2026-tril-cost-per-km].
  - *Failure modes:* an encirclement of the Myrnohrad garrison (the 1,000–1,500 men Hromadske's officer feared for) is a loss [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad]. So is clinging on past February.
- **Logistics is the battlefield.**
  - *Roads cost something to use:* each road carries a fire-control value, and vehicles die on it [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-time-to-retreat-myrnohrad].
  - *The kill zone grows:* model it from about 1 km in January 2025 to 10 km by August and about 30 km by January 2026 [src:wotr-2026-maurin-front-line] [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai] [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-if-you-stop-you-die].
  - *The player's levers:* UGV runs (500 kg, fragile, and their share falls in winter mud), bomber-drone lifts (60 mm rounds yes, 120 mm no), net corridors, and rotations on foot of 10–25 km that take a day to two weeks [src:euromaidanpress-2025-kossov-zmiy-evacuation] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-robotic-logistics] [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad] [src:kyivindependent-2026-terajima-if-you-stop-you-die].
  - *Wounded:* casualty evacuation is a queue, with waits of days to two weeks [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-bakhmut-to-pokrovsk-tactics].
- **Infiltration as a flow, not an assault.**
  - *The flow:* Russian infantry arrives in groups of 2–3, up to about 100 groups a day, and accumulates in ruins [src:cnn-2025-kottasova-pokrovsk-poised].
  - *Detection:* the drone line catches a share, about 120 of 150 in the July walk [src:nv-2025-horova-deepstate-150-saboteurs].
  - *Weather:* the inflow rises 3–4 times in rain and fog [src:hromadske-2025-butsko-encirclement-myrnohrad].
  - *Targets:* infiltrators go for Ukrainian drone crews first, which degrades the player's detection [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-isw-21-months].
- **Rubikon as a scripted arrival.** An elite counter-logistics and counter-drone layer arrives on the axis in spring–summer 2025 and raises road lethality step-wise [src:kyivindependent-2025-farrell-24h-pokrovsk] [src:isw-2025-08-07-bai]. Striking its bases (HUR, November) is a lever with limited effect [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-drone-strike-rubicon].
- **Fog as the master event.** Fog days ground both sides' drones. They let Russian infantry and vehicles in, and let Ukrainian vehicles reach Myrnohrad [src:euromaidan-2025-axe-foggy-advance] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-axe-road-into-myrnohrad]. Weight the fog random events to November (ERA5 proxy: 84 hours in November 2025, against 14 in January) [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-pokrovsk-2025-26].
