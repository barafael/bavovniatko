# Vuhledar: the Russian mechanised assaults on the ridge town

28 Oct 2022 – 31 Mar 2023 (prelude at Pavlivka, Oct–Nov 2022; main battle 24 Jan – 15 Feb 2023; infantry phase to end of March), Vuhledar, Volnovakha raion, southwest Donetsk Oblast. Epilogue: the fall of the town, Aug – 2 Oct 2024.

Accessed 2026-10-01. English-language sources, with Ukrainian outlets paired with Western analysis and OSINT. Russian statements appear only as Ukrainian, Western or independent Russian outlets report them. Loss figures are **claims**, and the claimant is always named.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Vuhledar is a coal-mining town of about 14,000 pre-war residents. Its Soviet-era apartment blocks stand on high ground above flat, open fields, about 2 km north of Pavlivka [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar] [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt]. From those roofs Ukrainian observers overlooked the T-0509 highway and threatened the Donetsk–Volnovakha railway, Russia's only rail link between Donbas and the south [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar] [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost].

- **Prelude.** In late October 2022 the Pacific Fleet's 155th and 40th Naval Infantry Brigades attacked Pavlivka, just south of the town. They took most of it by mid-November, but in four days the 155th lost more than 300 men and half its equipment. Its men then wrote a public letter blaming their commanders [src:isw-2022-10-29-roca] [src:isw-2022-11-07-roca] [src:isw-2022-11-14-roca].
- **The main battle.** On 24 January 2023 Russia attacked again, from Pavlivka and Mykilske, with the same marines plus Eastern Military District motor-rifle and tank brigades [src:isw-2023-01-24-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-25-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca]. The Ukrainian 72nd Mechanised Brigade and its supporting artillery had been in place for months [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].
- **How it went.** For three weeks Russian columns drove down the same dirt roads into a prepared kill zone. The roads were left clear, the shoulders were mined, ATGM teams waited in the tree lines, and artillery was cued by drones [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. Artillery also fired US RAAM mines onto corridors that Russian mine plows had just cleared [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam] [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam].
- **The footage.** Ukrainian drone footage of columns piling up on mines went viral. The worst day was the assault of 6 February [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca] [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade].
- **Culmination.** By mid-February the offensive had culminated, and from late February Russia attacked with infantry alone [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca] [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. Attacks in March gained nothing, and Russia's Eastern Military District commander, Col. Gen. Rustam Muradov, was reported dismissed on 3 April [src:isw-2023-03-26-roca] [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed].
- **Epilogue.** Ukraine held Vuhledar for another 18 months. It fell on 1–2 October 2024 to an attack on its flanks, not on its front [src:isw-2024-10-01-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2024-denisova-withdraw-vuhledar].

- **The 155th Brigade ends the window combat-ineffective.** The UK MoD called its losses "extremely high", and from 23 Feb Ukraine reported elements refusing to attack [src:nv-2023-ukmod-155th-satellite] [src:isw-2023-02-23-roca].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Footprint.** The diorama is a box of about 13 × 10 km. It runs from Prechystivka in the west to Mykilske and Vodiane in the east, and from Yehorivka in the south to Bohoiavlenka in the north. Most of the 2022–23 fighting sits within about 6 km of the town; only the flank attacks towards Prechystivka and Bohoiavlenka reach further [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca]. ISW measured Russia's best effort as falling short of a 4 km advance [src:isw-2023-03-05-roca].

**Terrain.**
- **The ridge.** Vuhledar sits about 30–50 m above the Kashlahach valley 2–3 km to its south (SRTM: about 190 m at the northwest blocks and 174 m at the town council, against 142–148 m at Pavlivka) [src:opentopodata-srtm30m]. Russian milbloggers said Ukraine used "elevation differences between Pavlivka and Vuhledar to their advantage" [src:isw-2022-11-14-roca].
- **The plain.** The ground around the town is flat, open farmland, which Cooper called "a big, tall fortress in the middle of an empty, flat desert" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines]. Russian milbloggers blamed the open terrain for making advances hard [src:isw-2023-02-15-roca].
- **Tree lines.** Shelterbelts divide the fields. Satellite imagery shows "intensive patterns of impacts along tree lines" where Russian tanks tried to advance [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys]. Ukrainian ATGM teams and tanks hid in the tree lines [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].
- **The Kashlahach river.** In OSM it flows west, past Mykilske (about 47.753 N) and Pavlivka (about 47.759 N), to Prechystivka [src:osm-copyright]. It is "too narrow" to be an obstacle opposite Mykilske and Shevchenko [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles]. In November 2022 Russia tried to dislodge Ukrainians "from behind the Kashlahach River" [src:isw-2022-11-14-roca].
- **Dacha areas.** Garden-plot settlements lie south and southeast of the town, the "Mykilske dacha area" among them, along with Verkhni Dachi. They were fought over block by block and levelled by artillery by 13 Feb [src:isw-2023-02-14-roca] [src:pravda-2023-zaluzhnyi-35th-marines].
- **Ruins.** By February the town was largely destroyed and without water or power. Wikipedia, citing the deputy mayor, says "one hundred percent of the buildings damaged" [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar].

**Roads and rail.** Road refs and extents are from OSM [src:osm-copyright].
- **T-0509** (OSM: Т-05-09) runs east–west just south of the town. It links Velyka Novosilka and Novotroitske and is the road Vuhledar overlooks [src:isw-2023-01-26-roca] [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar].
- **T-0524** (OSM: Т-05-24) enters the town from the northeast. On 4 Feb Russian armour tried to reach it from Mykilske [src:isw-2023-02-04-roca].
- **O-0532** runs west towards Vodiane and Kostiantynivka [src:kyivindependent-2024-denisova-withdraw-vuhledar].
- **The Pavlivka–Mykilske road**, along the river, was the Russian lateral. Russia claimed it on 17 Nov 2022 [src:isw-2022-11-17-roca].
- **The approach routes were dirt roads across the fields.** Ukrainian crews "studied the roads they used" [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. Girkin: Russian columns had to advance "along narrow roads" [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade].
- **The railway.** The Donetsk–Volnovakha railway passed near the town [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost]. Holding Vuhledar let Ukraine threaten it [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].
- **Wider geometry.** Vuhledar is about 24 km from Marinka, Kurakhove and the N15, so even its capture would not have linked up with the Donetsk City axis [src:isw-2023-03-05-roca].

- **12–18 Feb.** Repeated light snow (about 13 cm in total).
- **From 19 Feb.** Intermittent thaw (frost returns on 22–24 Feb), with highs of 11–14 °C on 26–27 Feb. March was mostly above freezing, with rain on 7 and 11 Mar.
- **Light.** The NYT's crew describes an ambush ordered "during the gray pre-dawn" [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].

## 4. Forces

### Russia

**Command.**
- The Eastern Military District and Eastern Grouping of Forces were under Col. Gen. Rustam Muradov, appointed 5 Oct 2022 [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed] [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].
- The 155th Brigade was commanded by Col. Zurab Akhmedov in Nov 2022 [src:isw-2022-11-07-roca].
- Unofficial Russian sources reported a special forces colonel, Sergey Polyakov, killed while commanding in the assault [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].

**Main units.**
- **The 155th Guards Naval Infantry Brigade.** Pacific Fleet, from Vladivostok and Primorsky Krai [src:dfrlab-2023-vuhledar-war-report].
  - It had been rebuilt after Kyiv and after Pavlivka. A captured marine said it was 80–90% mobilised men [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca].
  - Its strength is contested. Ukraine claimed about 5,000 [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade]. Kyiv Post put it at about 2,000 at full strength [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines]. Forbes said the 155th and 40th together had about 6,000 at their peak [src:forbes-2022-axe-marines-pavlivka].
- **The 40th Naval Infantry Brigade.** Pacific Fleet, from Kamchatka, an Arctic-trained unit [src:forbes-2022-axe-marines-pavlivka] [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar]. Each marine brigade has motorised battalions, a tank battalion, engineers, artillery and air defence [src:forbes-2022-axe-marines-pavlivka].
- **Eastern Military District army units.**
  - The 29th Combined Arms Army's 36th Motor Rifle Brigade [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca].
  - The 36th Combined Arms Army's 37th Motor Rifle and 5th Guards Tank Brigades [src:isw-2023-03-01-roca].
- **The 3rd Army Corps.** This was a volunteer corps formed in 2022. It included the 72nd Motor Rifle Brigade with the Tatar "Alga" battalion (about 500 men at the highest estimate) and the Chuvash "Atal" battalion [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca] [src:dfrlab-2023-vuhledar-war-report].
- **Others named by a Ukrainian reserve officer:**
  - the 136th Motor Rifle Brigade (Southern Military District, which arrived in March [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca])
  - the DNR's "Kaskad" and "Vostok" units
  - BARS (Combat Reserve) volunteers
  - the "Patriot" private military company [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca]
- **Also reported:** the 14th Spetsnaz Brigade and Akhmat-North elements [src:dfrlab-2023-vuhledar-war-report], the 5th Brigade of the 1st Corps in March [src:novayagazeta-2023-5th-brigade-appeal], and T-80/T-90s of the "90th Tank Regiment" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].

**Strength.** Tom Cooper's estimate, quoted by CNN, is "about 20,000 troops, 90 MBTs, perhaps two times as many IFVs, and about 100 artillery pieces" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys]. No official figure was found.

**Equipment seen or reported.**
- Tanks: T-80BV/BVM, T-72B3 and T-90 [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys] [src:militarnyi-2023-72nd-basurmanin] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].
- Infantry vehicles: BMP-1/2, BMP-1AM "Basurmanin", BMP-3 and BTR-82 [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:militarnyi-2023-72nd-basurmanin] [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles] [src:dfrlab-2023-vuhledar-war-report].
- Mine plows on lead tanks [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam].
- TOS-1A thermobaric launchers [src:isw-2023-01-27-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].
- A 2S4 Tyulpan 240 mm mortar (Dec 2022) and Ka-52 helicopters. Ukraine claimed 5–6 Ka-52s shot down on 24–28 Jan, a figure relayed by Tom Cooper [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar].
- Front-line aviation supporting the attacks [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles].

**How the force changed.**
- **Late January – early February.** The best units were spent in the first days [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca]. By 6 Feb a Ukrainian reserve officer called the 155th and 40th "largely destroyed" [src:isw-2023-02-06-roca].
- **6–9 February.** Volunteer battalions replaced the marines as the main assault force [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca].
- **Late February.** With most of its armour gone, Russia attacked with infantry alone [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle], and the 155th was refusing missions [src:isw-2023-02-23-roca].
- **March.** Russia fed in mobilised and BARS infantry and the 136th Motor Rifle Brigade [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-31-roca].

### Ukraine

**Command.**
- The Tavriia (Tavriisk) operational direction, whose spokesman was Col. Oleksiy Dmytrashkivskyi [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade] [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca].
- The 72nd Separate Mechanised Brigade "Black Zaporozhians", commanded from summer 2022 by Col. Ivan Vinnyk [src:pravda-2024-vinnyk-transferred] [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles].

**Units.**
- **The 72nd Mechanised Brigade** held the town and its approaches. Its subunits included the 1st Mechanised Battalion, whose deputy commander was Lt. Vladislav Bayak. The brigade laid the minefields on the main approaches from Pavlivka [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle] [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt].
- **The 55th Artillery Brigade** is named by Forbes as co-defending [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam].
- **SOF teams** worked with the 72nd behind the lines [src:pravda-2023-sof-ambush-t80].
- **The 35th Marine Brigade and the 56th Brigade** fought on the approaches. They took Verkhni Dachi by April 2023 [src:pravda-2023-zaluzhnyi-35th-marines].
- **Also reported, not independently confirmed here:** the 68th Jaeger Brigade and the Belarusian Kalinouski Regiment, whose activist Eduard Lobau was killed nearby on 26 Jan [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar].
- **Reserves** were moved in quickly in late January. Russian milbloggers claimed some came from Bakhmut [src:isw-2023-01-27-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].

**Strength.** No published Ukrainian figure was found for 2023. In 2024 the new commander described a 1:9 ratio against the brigade, but that refers to the 2024 battle [src:hromadske-2024-okhrimenko-withdrawal].

**Equipment.**
- ATGMs: Javelin and Stugna-P [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle] [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles].
- Artillery: M777 and Caesar howitzers, and HIMARS [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].
- Tanks: T-64s, used from hides at stand-off range [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].
- Drones: DJI Matrice and similar quadcopters for spotting, plus small drones dropping charges [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles] [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].
- Mines: TM-62 anti-tank mines, laid by hand or by GMZ minelayer, and the US M718 RAAM artillery-delivered mine, of which about 6,000 shells were delivered in late 2022 [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam] [src:taskandpurpose-2023-slayton-vuhledar-mines].
- Zaluzhnyi named artillery ammunition as the binding constraint [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].

**Rotation.** The 72nd was not rotated out from February 2022 to its withdrawal in October 2024 [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar] [src:kyivindependent-2024-hodunova-bbc-marines].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**The Ukrainian kill zone.** Lt. Bayak described it in detail [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]:
- A drone video feed spots the column (about 15 tanks and APCs).
- The dirt road is left free of mines and the fields around it are seeded, "to entice the Russians to advance while preventing tanks from turning around".
- Javelin and Stugna-P teams in the tree lines open fire. Panicking drivers swerve onto the mined shoulders, and the wrecks block the road.
- Artillery then fires on the stalled column and on crews climbing out.
- Once, HIMARS was used against a stationary column.

One Ukrainian tank crew stopped about three miles short of its ambush site, out of range of return fire, and shot at targets it could not see, with a drone pilot calling coordinates over the radio [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].

**Mines.**
- **Two kinds.** There were deliberate TM-62 belts, and RAAM shells fired onto corridors Russian mine plows had just cleared, "filling up the cleared corridor" according to a moderator of the pro-Russian forum Lost Armour [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam].
- **UK MoD.** Ukraine's successful use of RAAM was the reason behind the heavy losses, as Novaya Gazeta Europe relays the update. It was sometimes fired over and behind advancing units, to trap them on withdrawal [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam].
- **Surprise.** Kyiv Post called artillery-delivered mines a tactical surprise and the first large-scale use in the war [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].
- **Typical footage.** One vehicle detonates a mine and the next drives past without slowing, only to hit another [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines] [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].
- **Training.** A captured marine said the mobilised men could not identify minefields or react to them [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca]. A tank milblogger blamed rushed mine clearing [src:isw-2023-03-15-roca].

**Drone-spotted artillery.** "Day after day, Russian armored columns were halted and then crushed by accurate shell strikes adjusted by" drones or by observers on Vuhledar's tallest buildings [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].
- **Smoke backfired.** On 6 Feb the Russians smoked their advance with exhaust-injected fuel, but vehicles bunched up so closely behind the smoke that artillery could hit by firing at its head [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt].
- **Depth.** Fire also hit the Russian rear, cutting supply and withdrawal routes [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].

**The Russian columns.**
- **Why columns.** Moscow Calling argued that tanks without vision upgrades and units without communications or situational awareness could only move in "slender columns": "If the Russian Armed Forces try to disperse, they will shoot each other" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys]. The Moscow Times says tanks went single-file for lack of demining equipment [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed].
- **The assault formation.** Armoured groups were one tank and 3–4 IFVs. They drove to within 100–150 m and dismounted, and attacked from the front and both flanks [src:militarnyi-2023-vuhledar-vehicles].
- **Training, not command.** ISW judged the "highly dysfunctional tactics" to reflect poorly trained mobilised men more than poor command [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].

**The Russian shift to infantry.** After the armour was spent, Russian infantry advanced in waves and dug in behind burned-out vehicles. A 72nd soldier: "They'd come in groups of five. We'd kill four, one would stay and dig in" [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost].

**Russian fires.** TOS-1A thermobaric launchers, a military-district-level asset, were used throughout, which ISW read as a sign of priority [src:isw-2023-01-27-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].

**Lasting effect.** RUSI later noted that the destruction of a naval infantry brigade in an armoured thrust at Vuhledar reinforced Russian caution about using tanks for breakthroughs [src:rusi-2023-watling-meatgrinder]. ISW judged that the Vuhledar losses prevented sustained mechanised assaults elsewhere in winter–spring 2023 [src:isw-2023-10-26-roca].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **Pavlivka in the mud (late Oct – mid-Nov 2022).** Two marine brigades attack a village across the Kashlahach in rain and mud. They take it street by street and lose half their equipment in four days. The episode ends with their men's public letter of protest [src:isw-2022-11-03-roca] [src:isw-2022-11-07-roca].
2. **The SOF ambush (3 Feb 2023).** Two 155th tanks move up to firing positions. The lead T-80 hits a mine and burns, and SOF and the 72nd capture two crew [src:pravda-2023-sof-ambush-t80].
3. **Black Monday (6 Feb 2023).** A daylong assault by about two battalions under exhaust smoke, led by a plow tank, drives into the minefields. Artillery fires into the smoke, about 30 vehicles are left behind, and the Alga battalion is mauled [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar].
4. **"The second one went exactly the same route" (filmed early Feb).** In a video published by Ukrainian marines, artillerymen comment as one column blows up and a second follows the same path [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade]. This is the signature shot. In the filmed engagement that ISW counted, 13 tanks and 12 BMPs were lost [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca].
5. **The RAAM corridor.** A plow tank clears a lane, and minutes later 155 mm shells scatter magnetic mines back into it. The followers are trapped between old and new mines [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam] [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam].
6. **HIMARS on a column.** Guided rockets, normally used against depots, strike a stalled column [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. In a related strike reported on 13 Feb, HIMARS hit the Vostok battalion's headquarters [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca].
7. **The Wanderer.** In the grey pre-dawn, a Ukrainian T-64 crew says a prayer and drives out against a column of 16 Russian tanks and armoured vehicles. It stops about three miles short of the ambush site and fires on coordinates from a drone pilot as the column stalls on mines [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].
8. **The officer's notebook (1–5 Mar 2023).** An infantry-phase coda: one assault group's daily tallies of 100, 116, 103 and 115 men sent and 16, 23, 15 and 3 back [src:forbes-2023-axe-diary-1100].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Russia: attacking Pavlivka in late October 2022.**
   - At the time, Khodakovsky called it "premature" [src:isw-2022-10-31-roca]. ISW judged that Russia had "impaled an insufficient concentration of mobilized personnel" and would have done better to wait [src:isw-2022-11-03-roca].
   - The 155th blamed Muradov, Akhmedov and Gerasimov [src:isw-2022-11-07-roca].
   - What followed: Pavlivka was taken, the brigade gutted, and the same command repeated the plan in January [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].
2. **Russia: the purpose of the January 2023 attack.** The sources disagree.
   - ISW first read it as an information operation or spoiling attack to distract from Bakhmut [src:isw-2023-01-24-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-25-roca].
   - The UK MoD saw a "concerted offensive assault" to open a new axis [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca].
   - Kyiv Post's sources later said the aim was to cut off the whole Ukrainian Donbas grouping and break through here rather than at Bakhmut [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost].
   - The UK MoD also suggested the MoD wanted a success of its own to rival Wagner [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam].
3. **Ukraine: rapid reinforcement in late January.** A Kremlin-affiliated milblogger judged that the Russians lost the initiative by the end of January "due to Ukrainian forces' rapid transfer of reserves" [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca]. Pushilin likewise blamed reserves "in large quantities" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys]. Which reserves came from where is not public.
4. **Ukraine: the kill-zone design.** Leaving the roads clear and mining the shoulders, rather than blocking the roads, was a deliberate choice to draw columns in [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. Firing RAAM into freshly cleared lanes broke with the Soviet practice of laying narrow belts just before an attack [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam].
5. **Russia: columns on the same routes.**
   - The repeated choice to send columns down the same narrow approaches drew fire from milbloggers, who wanted Muradov tried [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].
   - Some argued the units had no alternative: no communications, no situational awareness, "blind, deaf tanks" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys], or a lack of demining equipment [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed].
   - ISW put the cause in training [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].
6. **Ukraine: spending HIMARS on tactical targets.** Commanders called in HIMARS against a stalled column, though it was normally reserved for depots and barracks [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle].
7. **Russia: switching to infantry (late February).** After losing "so many machines", Russia attacked only with infantry [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle], at the cost the notebook records [src:forbes-2023-axe-diary-1100].
8. **Russia: "at any cost" (4–5 March).** Ukraine's General Staff reported that Shoigu ordered Muradov to take Vuhledar at any cost amid a dispute between them. ISW judged a renewed offensive impossible without new reserves [src:isw-2023-03-05-roca]. Muradov's reported dismissal followed on 3 April [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed].
9. **Epilogue, Ukraine: when to leave (Sep – Oct 2024).**
   - The Khortytsia group says higher command authorised the withdrawal [src:kyivindependent-2024-denisova-withdraw-vuhledar].
   - A 72nd soldier told Kyiv Post that "the order to withdraw was never given", and analyst Kuzan said rotating the brigade was discussed but no reserves existed [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost].
   - The new brigade commander called the town untenable with the forces available [src:hromadske-2024-okhrimenko-withdrawal].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Official optimism.** On 7 Feb Defence Minister Shoigu said Russian forces were "successfully developing" an offensive near Vuhledar [src:isw-2023-02-07-roca]. In a broadcast on 12 Feb Putin said "naval infantry is working as it should right now" and that the Pacific and Northern fleets were "heroically fighting" [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca]. The MoD stayed silent on the losses [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca].
- **Early claims.** Russian sources claimed breakthroughs and positions inside Vuhledar (26–30 Jan) that footage did not confirm [src:isw-2023-01-26-roca] [src:isw-2023-01-31-roca]. Pushilin claimed a foothold on the southern outskirts [src:isw-2023-02-10-roca].
- **The milblogger revolt.**
  - Girkin: "Ukrainian artillery shoots exceptionally accurately... the Ukrainians shot the attackers like in a shooting gallery" [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade]; "Only morons attack head-on in the same place" [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].
  - Wagner's Grey Zone channel attacked Muradov personally [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys] [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines].
  - Rybar called for "systemic changes" [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar].
  - A Kremlin-affiliated milblogger admitted the offensive had culminated [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].
  - Moscow Calling said 31 armoured vehicles of the 155th were destroyed in one assault [src:reuters-2023-ukmod-vuhledar-dozens].
- **From the ranks.**
  - A survivor of the 155th said eight men were left of his company [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed], and one soldier said "up to 500" were killed [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar].
  - Relatives estimated 250–300 casualties in the Alga battalion [src:rferl-2023-eckel-what-happened-vuhledar].
  - The 5th Brigade's video appeal to Putin [src:novayagazeta-2023-5th-brigade-appeal].
- **Command fallout.** The Moscow Times' MoD sources said Muradov was relieved for the Vuhledar failures. One called him someone who would "order soldiers to go to certain death" [src:moscowtimes-2023-muradov-dismissed].
- **Claims about Ukrainian losses.** No Russian official figure was found in an acceptable source. Wikipedia's infobox cites a pro-Kremlin outlet claiming 200 Ukrainian killed and wounded on 24–25 Jan [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar]. That outlet is not used here.
- **Western analysis.** RUSI places Vuhledar at the start of an "ill-prepared offensive using under-trained troops [that] was extremely costly for the Russians" [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons]. ## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Win band.**
  - **Hard conditions.** Vuhledar is still held at 31 Mar 2023, and the Russian line ends no more than about 2–4 km closer: Pavlivka, Mykilske and parts of the dachas fall [src:isw-2023-03-05-roca] [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam].
  - **Armour scoring band** for Russian vehicles destroyed or abandoned in the main battle:
    - floor of about 30 (UK MoD's single incident; the 13+12 footage)
    - middle of about 100 (Oryx, 8–10 Feb, Avdiivka and Vuhledar)
    - ceiling of about 130 (Ukrainian claim)
  - The debrief names each claimant [src:reuters-2023-ukmod-vuhledar-dozens] [src:insider-2023-oryx-103-vehicles] [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade].
- **Self-running script.** The AI should repeat the historical pattern:
  - probes on 24–27 Jan
  - a first-week drive from Mykilske towards the T-0524
  - the large mechanised push of 6 Feb
  - repeated column attempts to about 15 Feb
  - armour exhausted by late February, then infantry waves into March
  - The scripted events are the arrival of the Alga battalion, the 136th Motor Rifle Brigade in March, and Shoigu's "at any cost" order. Sources: [src:isw-2023-01-25-roca] [src:isw-2023-02-04-roca] [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle] [src:isw-2023-03-10-roca] [src:isw-2023-03-05-roca].
- **Core mechanic: the kill zone.** Pre-planned minefields with a deliberately clear road, ATGM teams in tree lines and pre-registered artillery. The player's levers are:
  - where to leave lanes
  - when to spring the trap, waiting for the column to commit
  - RAAM missions onto cleared corridors or behind columns
  - Source: [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle] [src:forbes-2023-axe-minefield-raam] [src:novayagazeta-2023-ukmod-raam]
- **Observation as a resource.** High-rise OPs and quadcopters convert the open plain into a fire-control advantage, and losing an OP or a drone should degrade fire accuracy [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines] [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys]. Russian TOS-1A and air strikes on the blocks are the counter [src:isw-2023-01-27-roca].
- **Scarcity.** Shells, RAAM rounds and ATGMs are limited, which is Zaluzhnyi's binding constraint, and HIMARS is a rare high-value call [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca] [src:nyt-2023-kramer-epic-tank-battle]. The player should be tempted to over-spend on the first columns.
- **Russian AI behaviour.** Columns in single file along known dirt roads, smoke screens, a plow lead and poor reaction to mines. Mobilised crews panic and leave the road [src:forbes-2023-axe-vuhledar-halt] [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys] [src:isw-2023-02-13-roca].
- **Weather and pacing.** The window runs from mud (Nov) through frozen ground (late Jan – mid-Feb, frost to −11 °C) to thaw (late Feb – Mar) [src:open-meteo-historical-archive]. Daylight is about 07:50–17:40 in early February. Reserves arriving in late January are the Ukrainian "save" that ends Russia's window of opportunity [src:isw-2023-02-11-roca].
- **Epilogue framing.** The debrief can show the 2024 fall by envelopment of the flanks (Prechystivka, Vodiane), with glide bombs and an unrotated brigade. The town that columns could not take frontally fell to its flanks [src:kyivpost-2024-kostezh-why-lost] [src:isw-2024-09-30-roca].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **No Oryx list for Vuhledar alone.** Oryx's master list [src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses] does not tag locations. The only Oryx-derived figure found covers Avdiivka and Vuhledar together for 8–10 Feb [src:insider-2023-oryx-103-vehicles].
  - Wikipedia cites WarSpotting at "107+ armoured vehicles (17+ tanks)" for the whole 2022–24 battle [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar]. This was not checked directly, and a location-filtered WarSpotting export would settle it.
- **The Ukrainian order of battle is thin.** The roles of the 68th Jaeger Brigade and the Kalinouski Regiment rest on Wikipedia's citations of the Washington Post [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar]. Battalion dispositions, artillery numbers, RAAM rounds fired, and minefield extents and locations are not public.
- **Ukrainian personnel losses for Jan–Mar 2023 are unknown.** Wikipedia cites a Times figure for Sep 2022, a CBC figure for one battalion, and BBC Ukrainian name counts, none of them checked [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar].
- **Exact dates of the iconic videos.** The "second column on the same route" video and the 13+12 engagement can be dated only to early February from their posting dates [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade] [src:isw-2023-02-09-roca]. GeoConfirmed's geolocations could give exact positions.
- **The Ka-52 claims** (5–6 shot down on 24–28 Jan) and the strike on a building of reinforcements at "Kyrylivka" come via Tom Cooper's blog, relayed by Wikipedia. They are unverified [src:wikipedia-battle-of-vuhledar].
- **Sergey Polyakov's** death and brigade are reported only by unofficial Russian sources via CNN [src:cnn-2023-lister-vuhledar-turkeys].
- **The size of the 155th** before the battle is disputed: about 2,000 (Kyiv Post) against 5,000 (Ukrainian spokesman) [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-vuhledar-marines] [src:politico-2023-melkozerova-155th-brigade].
- **The "Pivdennodonbaska No. 3"** mine near Vodiane needs matching to its OSM feature [src:isw-2024-09-30-roca] [src:osm-copyright].
- **Not examined:** satellite imagery of the battlefield (Sentinel-2 or commercial) and the UK MoD image itself.
