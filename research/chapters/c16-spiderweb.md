# Operation Spiderweb (Pavutyna): the trucks, the cabins and the bomber bases

About 22 November 2023 – 1 June 2025 (the long game), with the strike on Sunday 1 June 2025 and its aftermath to 2026: a warehouse in Chelyabinsk and five Russian bomber bases across three time zones. Olenya (Murmansk Oblast), Dyagilevo (Ryazan), Ivanovo Severny (Ivanovo), Belaya (Irkutsk) and Ukrainka (Amur).

Accessed 2026-10-01. New sources are in `db/staging/sources/c16-spiderweb.yaml`; sources already in the knowledge base are cited by their existing id. Citations are inline source-id tags. The player side is Ukraine. Russian claims are given as Ukrainian, Western and independent Russian outlets in exile report them. Computed figures (distances, time zones, weather, light) are labelled with their method. The drivers and the alleged agents are private people, so the dossier describes them but does not name them, even where sources do.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Operation Spiderweb (Ukrainian *Pavutyna*) was a covert strike by the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) on Russian long-range aviation at its home bases [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb]. Zelensky said it took "one year, six months, and nine days from the start of planning to effective execution" and that he supervised it, while SBU head Lt. Gen. Vasyl Maliuk and his staff carried it out [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed]. The method:
- the SBU smuggled FPV quadcopters into Russia, and later the mobile wooden cabins [src:bbc-2025-adams-spiders-web-strike];
- inside Russia the drones were hidden under the cabins' roofs, and the cabins were loaded on trucks [src:kyivindependent-2025-york-sbu-bombers-burning];
- Russian drivers who thought they were hauling prefabricated houses drove the trucks to car parks and petrol stations a few kilometres from the bases [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-spiderweb] [src:kyivpost-2025-brown-manhunt-drivers];
- the roofs opened remotely and the drones took off [src:guardian-2025-mazhulin-visual-guide].

Pilots in Ukraine flew the drones over Russian mobile networks, and some drones fell back on onboard autonomy when the link dropped [src:ssu-2025-pavutyna-video-41] [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation]. On 1 June 2025, the eve of the second Istanbul talks, 117 drones hit four bases: Olenya, Belaya, Dyagilevo and Ivanovo [src:bbc-2025-adams-spiders-web-strike]. The fifth truck, bound for Ukrainka in the Far East, caught fire and exploded on the highway near Seryshevo [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck] [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb]. The damage at Olenya and Belaya is clearly imaged. At Dyagilevo and Ivanovo it is thin or disputed (§3, §9). Russia's Ministry of Defence admitted fires at two bases and called the operation a "terrorist attack" [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed]. Russia then moved bombers to remote bases and started building bomber shelters. The losses also showed in its next air-launched cruise-missile salvo, which used only five Tu-95MS and two Tu-160 [src:moscowtimes-2025-bombers-relocated] [src:twz-2026-newdick-bomber-shelters] [src:defenseexpress-2025-seven-bombers-kh101].

## 4. Forces

**Ukraine (SBU).**
- **Command:** Zelensky supervised and approved the operation; SBU head Lt. Gen. Vasyl Maliuk led it [src:kyivindependent-2025-york-spiderweb] [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed]. Zelensky said it was achieved "solely by Ukraine", using only what Ukraine produces [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-spiderweb].
- **Compartmentation:** only a few people knew the whole plan. Maliuk: the cabin maker did not know the cabins would go to airfields, and the drone maker did not know the targets [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Agents in Russia:** a Ukrainian-born couple who ran a logistics front in Chelyabinsk. Russian investigators later placed them on a wanted list [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb] [src:meduza-2026-court-frees-drivers]. They were extracted before the strike [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck]. Zelensky said the operation's "office" was next to a regional FSB building [src:bbc-2025-adams-spiders-web-strike].
- **Drivers:** five unwitting Russian truckers, all from Chelyabinsk [src:meduza-2025-mediazona-drivers].
- **Equipment:**
  - **Trucks:** 5 trucks carrying 8 cabins [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
  - **Drones in the cabins:** about 36 drones per roof compartment, per Wikipedia [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb].
  - **Drone counts:** 150 drones assembled (WSJ) or smuggled (Butusov); 117 launched by the official figure (116 per Butusov); "a corresponding number of drone operators" [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation] [src:kyivindependent-2025-york-sbu-bombers-burning].
- **Pilots:** some of the SBU's best FPV pilots, taken from the front, who trained all day for muscle memory without knowing the target. Some grumbled at being taken off the front [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**The drone.**
- **Type:** Ukrainian-made quadcopters (Wikipedia says *Osa*) with a payload of just over 3.2 kg [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb]. The WSJ says the drones were "about the size of a large dinner plate" and carried up to 1.8 kg of explosive, with a charge that burned through the skin to ignite fuel [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Cost:** Wikipedia says about $2,000 each [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb].

**The cabin launcher.**
- **Design:** wooden mobile cabins that looked like prefabricated houses, with drone bays between the roof beams [src:guardian-2025-mazhulin-visual-guide].
- **Opening:** remotely opened roofs on specially designed locks [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Power:** batteries and solar panels kept the link to Ukraine up and the drones charged [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Self-destruction:** the trucks burned after launch in Irkutsk and Murmansk, and a container exploded when a police officer entered it [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck] [src:bbc-2025-adams-turning-point].

**Control link.**
- **Network:** control ran over Russian telecom networks with "auto-homing" (Butusov) [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation]. The drones flew autonomously to the airfield, and then a pilot in Kyiv took over through local cell towers (WSJ) [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb]. Russian analyst Rogozin thought SIM cards carried the control and video [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main].
- **Autopilot:** the open-source ArduPilot autopilot supports dead reckoning without satellite navigation [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb]. An analyst read a "PosHold" mode on an ArduPilot screen as autonomous navigation followed by manual terminal flight [src:twz-2025-altman-what-we-know-spiderweb].

**AI elements (the SBU's claim).**
- **Fallback on link loss:** "some UAVs, due to signal loss, switched to performing the mission using artificial intelligence along a pre-planned route", and on contact with the designated target the warhead triggered automatically [src:ssu-2025-pavutyna-video-41] [src:twz-2025-newdick-confirmed-losses].
- **Training data:** reported, not confirmed by the SBU, as photographs of the Poltava museum's bombers [src:kyivpost-2025-brown-ai-museum].

**Targeting.**
- **Briefing:** pilots were shown diagrams with weak points marked [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb], and Maliuk was photographed with satellite images of the five bases and X-marked Tu-95MS and Tu-22M3 profiles [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb].
- **Aim points:** drones landed on wings beside the fuel tanks and went for pylons carrying Kh-101 [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite] [src:kyivpost-2025-brown-ai-museum].
- **Loaded aircraft:** several Tu-95MS were already loaded with Kh-101 and fuelled [src:twz-2025-newdick-confirmed-losses].

**Why it worked, as analysts say.**
- **Range:** launching beside the base evaded EW and denied defenders reaction time [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca].
- **Detection:** small quadcopters flew below detection in daylight [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation] [src:barentsobserver-2025-chentemirov-olenya-attack].
- **No response on video:** the SBU footage shows no Russian defensive action [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite]. Mediazona read remote control from Ukraine as a lack or failure of EW countermeasures at the bases [src:mediazona-2025-deep-web].
- **No alert:** there were no air alarms near the airfields [src:rbcukraine-2025-deadly-spiderweb].

**What countered it, or might have.**
- **On the day:** the only direct action was civilians throwing stones [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb]. The tyres on the wings did not save the aircraft, and some observers mistook them for scorch marks [src:twz-2025-newdick-confirmed-losses].
- **Afterwards, as analysts advise or Russia did:**
  - truck inspections [src:twz-2025-altman-what-we-know-spiderweb];
  - mobile air-defence groups and wider coverage [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca];
  - control of mobile communications and network traffic around strategic sites (Rogozin) [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main];
  - dispersal [src:moscowtimes-2025-bombers-relocated];
  - hardened shelters [src:twz-2026-newdick-bomber-shelters].
- **Lutsenko's view:** an exploit this good is hard to repeat [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation].

## 6. Set pieces

- **The Chelyabinsk warehouse.** A rented shed on Sverdlovsky Trakt. Two agents build 150 quadcopters and eight cabins under video-call instruction from Kyiv, and one roof hinge keeps tearing loose [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb]. SBU photos show rows of black drones under the roof beams [src:bbc-2025-gozzi-how-ukraine-did-it] [src:guardian-2025-mazhulin-visual-guide].
- **"There are drones under the roof."** About 300 km out on a 3,200 km run, a roof slides off. The driver phones the agent, the agent phones his SBU handler, and Maliuk improvises the hunting-cabin story. The driver fixes the roof with a local tractor driver's help [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb]. This is the long game's signature risk event.
- **The reveal.** On 1 June Maliuk tells the pilots the target only then, showing maps and aircraft diagrams. One pilot recalls: "there was no alarm" [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Roofs open at Olenegorsk.** A truck by a petrol station 6–7 km from Olenya releases drones. Black smoke rises over the runway, residents count at least 10 explosions, and the driver runs around shouting that "someone would meet me" [src:barentsobserver-2025-chentemirov-olenya-attack] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-bombers-ablaze]. On the southern apron four Tu-95MS and a propeller transport burn [src:barentsobserver-2025-chentemirov-olenya-attack].
- **Stones against drones at Usolye.** Drivers and onlookers at a Siberian car park climb onto the truck and hurl stones to pin the drones down [src:bbc-2025-gozzi-how-ukraine-did-it] [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed].
- **"This is what Belaya looks like now."** Maliuk's voice over drone video of Belaya burning, the first strike ever in Siberia [src:pravda-2025-petrenko-pavutyna-first] [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca]. A full single-drone path video runs from the cabin roof to a Tu-22M3 at Belaya [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb].
- **Two radomes at Ivanovo.** Drones touch down on the radar domes of two A-50s, one sandbagged and one under tyres. The feed cuts at impact [src:telegraph-2025-barnes-a50-spy-planes] [src:bbc-2025-adams-turning-point]. Whether either was operational is debated (§9).
- **The burning truck at Seryshevo.** The fifth truck, already troubled by its unstable link, burns on the Amur highway. It explodes as a man opens the trailer, and the driver dies [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck] [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **The applause room.** In Kyiv, hit after hit lands. Later pilots simply climb and steer for the black smoke [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].

## 7. Decision points

- **Launch from inside Russia, not from Ukraine.** The SBU chose short-range FPVs brought to the fence over long-range drones. The Guardian notes that launching from Ukraine would have meant huge distances through Russian air defences [src:guardian-2025-mazhulin-visual-guide]. ISW credits the choice with operational surprise and a minimal Russian response window [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca].
- **Ukrainian means only, and no notice to the US.** Zelensky wanted "to use only what we produce" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-tril-spiderweb]. Ukraine and the US both say Washington was not told in advance. Axios first reported the opposite and corrected itself [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed] [src:reuters-2025-stewart-ali-us-estimate].
- **Choosing and vetting agents.** The SBU polygraphed the couple in Lviv before giving them the assembly phase. They then had to pass an FSB questioning at the border. The drivers had to be "reliable but not curious" and were secretly vetted by the SBU [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **The strike date.** The original date was about 9 May, and it slipped because of the drivers. The trucks then left on 23–26 May [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb]. The strike fell the day before the Istanbul talks, which the BBC and others read as deliberate leverage [src:bbc-2025-adams-spiders-web-strike] [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main]. Zelensky said it would not have happened under a ceasefire [src:bbc-2025-adams-turning-point]. He also cited intelligence on a Russian massed strike being prepared [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main], and a Russian outlet had trailed bombers waiting at Olenya and Engels [src:texty-2025-zeus-lightning].
- **The roof-slip crisis.** Maliuk chose a plausible cover story over aborting [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Swapping the broken truck.** Replacing the truck kept the schedule but left an unstable link. The SBU tried to talk the driver through the cabin wiring, and the truck later burned. Ukrainka was never hit [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Target and timing on the day.** Daylight, small quadcopters and aim points on fuel tanks and loaded pylons [src:texty-2025-butusov-pavutyna-preparation] [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite]. The bases were struck while Tu-95MS stood fuelled with Kh-101 [src:twz-2025-newdick-confirmed-losses].
- **Withdraw first.** All operatives and helpers were extracted on the eve [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck]. SBU sources pre-empted any Russian arrests as "staged" [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed].
- **What to publish.** Zelensky told the SBU to release what could be disclosed. The SBU spokesman: "We disclose the details only when it will not hamper the future operations" [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed] [src:twz-2025-altman-what-we-know-spiderweb]. The BBC notes that almost all information came from the SBU itself [src:bbc-2025-adams-turning-point].
- **Russia's choices.** Russia kept bombers in the open with tyres rather than shelters. Milbloggers and Gurulev blamed the command and the security services [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca] [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main]. Afterwards Russia chose dispersal, a "retaliation" salvo flown by a reduced bomber force, and shelter construction [src:moscowtimes-2025-bombers-relocated] [src:defenseexpress-2025-seven-bombers-kh101] [src:twz-2026-newdick-bomber-shelters].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **The MoD line.** The MoD called it "a terrorist attack using FPV drones against airfields in the Murmansk, Irkutsk, Ivanovo, Ryazan and Amur regions". It said all attacks in Ivanovo, Ryazan and Amur were repelled, that "several units of aircraft caught fire" in Murmansk and Irkutsk after launches "from the territory located in the immediate vicinity", that the fires were extinguished, that there were no casualties, and that "participants" had been detained [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed]. The Kremlin was silent on the day. State TV merely quoted the governors, and by Monday the story had left the bulletins [src:bbc-2025-gozzi-how-ukraine-did-it] [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main].
- **The governors.**
  - Kobzev (Irkutsk): the source "has already been blocked. It's a truck. The main thing is not to panic" [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed].
  - Chibis (Murmansk): asked people not to post videos [src:barentsobserver-2025-chentemirov-olenya-attack].
  - Malkov (Ryazan): a drone fragment damaged one roof [src:rbcukraine-2025-deadly-spiderweb].
  - Severomorsk's mayor: denied claims of an attack on the Northern Fleet [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed].
- **Downplaying the numbers.**
  - FighterBomber: "34% … is pure bullshit. The number of planes hit is in the single digits", but even one bomber is a huge loss for a country that does not make them [src:twz-2025-altman-what-we-know-spiderweb].
  - Military Chronicle: only one Tu-95MS lost and one badly damaged at Olenya, because the aircraft were empty for maintenance. It also relayed "other sources" giving 8 Tu-95MS [src:twz-2025-altman-what-we-know-spiderweb].
- **Blame.**
  - Milbloggers called it "Russia's Pearl Harbor" and "a black day" [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck] [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main].
  - Rybar: "serious miscalculations in the work of the special services" [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main].
  - Gurulev blamed the security services for letting trucks near the bases [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca].
  - Others mocked tyres as the bombers' only cover [src:istories-2025-airfield-attacks-main].
  - Rybar praised the stone-throwing drivers [src:twz-2025-altman-rogoway-bombers-destroyed].
- **Nuclear rhetoric.** State media and milbloggers framed the strike as grounds for nuclear use under the doctrine. ISW reads this as rhetoric meant to deter Western support [src:isw-2025-06-01-roca]. Putin told Trump that Russia would respond [src:bbc-2025-adams-turning-point].
- **The retaliation.** The 5–6 June barrage was billed as payback, but Defense Express counts only seven bombers in it [src:defenseexpress-2025-seven-bombers-kh101] [src:twz-2025-altman-revenge-strikes].
- **The A-50 at Ivanovo, contested on Russia's side of the ledger.**
  - **Hit:** Kyiv Post published Mizarvision imagery of a blackened A-50 and other wreckage [src:kyivpost-2025-spiderweb-a50-ivanovo]. The Telegraph's footage shows drones on two radomes [src:telegraph-2025-barnes-a50-spy-planes]. The NYT's Western officials counted one A-50 [src:isw-2025-06-03-roca]. A NATO official counted at least one A-50 among those hit or destroyed [src:eurointegration-2025-sydorenko-nato-count] [src:moscowtimes-2025-kozlov-nato-retaliate].
  - **Not proven:** BBC Verify found the Ivanovo wreckage predated the attack [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite]. MAIAR saw eight A-50s in unchanged positions [src:abc-2025-skinner-spiderweb-satellite]. TWZ notes weathered radomes and missing engines, which suggest stored airframes [src:twz-2025-newdick-confirmed-losses].
- **The drivers' case.**
  - Four drivers were arrested and charged with a group terrorist act. A fifth died in the Amur fire [src:meduza-2025-mediazona-drivers] [src:unian-2026-shenderovskyi-drivers-charged].
  - The drivers said they themselves called the police and tried to bring the drones down [src:meduza-2026-court-frees-drivers].
  - On 16 Sep 2026 the Moscow City Court freed them after the Prosecutor General's office found no direct evidence [src:meduza-2026-court-frees-drivers].
  - Russian investigators allege that the SBU recruited the organisers, who are on a wanted list [src:meduza-2026-court-frees-drivers]. Russian outlets reported that the organisers left for Kazakhstan days before the strike [src:meduza-2025-mediazona-drivers].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Two halves, two clocks.**
  - **The long game:** 18 months compressed into perhaps a dozen decisions. It has few hard dates, so let the historical plan run itself: ready by end of April, a slip from about 9 May, departures on 23–26 May, the strike on 1 Jun [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
  - **The strike:** five parallel dioramas, each a few minutes long, with launch points 5–10 km from the aprons (§3).
- **The detection meter, with sourced risk events:**
  - vetting the agents (polygraph, FSB questioning at the border);
  - recruiting drivers (20 interviewed);
  - smuggling parts through customs;
  - the roof slip in transit;
  - a mechanical failure forcing a truck swap that degrades the link;
  - drivers' drinking delaying the date [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
  - Each event offers the historical choice (a cover story, a swap, a delay) against abort.
  - Speed, scale and risk are the player's lever; 8 cabins in 5 trucks is the historical scale.
- **The link-latency model.** The drones fly autonomously to the airfield, then a pilot takes over through local cell towers. On link loss an AI fallback flies the pre-planned route with a contact trigger [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb] [src:ssu-2025-pavutyna-video-41]. The pilot gets bonus accuracy on fuel tanks and loaded pylons [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite]. The first fires give later drones a smoke beacon [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **The Russian reaction timer.** Historically no SAM or EW response appears on video, and the only "defence" was civilians with stones [src:bbc-2025-brown-spencer-satellite] [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb]. A fair design is a slow local-guard response and a fast civilian-interference event at the launch truck.
- **The win band** from §1:
  - **Historical floor:** about 10 heavy bombers destroyed, at Olenya and Belaya.
  - **Par:** 12–13 destroyed, the independent counts.
  - **Ukraine's claim:** 41 hit.
  - **Debrief:** show the claims side by side: SBU 41, NATO 40+/10–13, US 20/10, imagery 12–13, FighterBomber "single digits".
  - **Ukrainka:** the historical result is a loss en route, so reaching it should be possible but rare.
- **Systemic payoff.**
  - **Short term:** the next Kh-101 salvo uses about 7 bombers instead of 11 or more [src:defenseexpress-2025-seven-bombers-kh101]; Tu-160s are pulled into routine strikes [src:militarnyi-2025-liuksikov-tu160-strike].
  - **Medium term:** the bombers disperse far from Ukraine [src:moscowtimes-2025-bombers-relocated].
  - **Long term:** Russia builds shelters, which raises the difficulty of later deep strikes [src:twz-2026-newdick-bomber-shelters].
  - **Limits:** Western officials said it did little to change the ground war [src:telegraph-2025-barnes-a50-spy-planes].
- **Coupling with c12 and c05.** The A-50 radomes at Ivanovo tie into the A-50 hunt [src:kyivpost-2025-spiderweb-a50-ivanovo], and two days later the SBU hit the Crimean Bridge [src:pravda-2025-prots-third-strike].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Exact strike times.** No source fetched gives the launch minute at any base. The first reports were published from 10:52 UTC (§2), Barents calls it "Sunday morning", and TWZ "this afternoon" for Belaya. Whether the four launches were simultaneous in absolute time, and what local hour each was, remains open.
- **The long game's dates.** Almost all dated logistics rows rest on one WSJ article, read through NV's summary. The Wikipedia/Baza and Kyiv Post/Baza dates for the company and the trucks (October versus December 2024) disagree. When and how the drones crossed into Russia, and from where (Kazakhstan has been speculated [src:euromaidanpress-2025-zoria-trojan-truck]), is unpublished.
- **The drone and its payload.** Is it the *Osa* (Wikipedia), and is the payload 3.2 kg or 1.8 kg (WSJ)? Were there 150 drones and 300 munitions (Butusov)? Is 117 versus 116 a counting difference?
- **The AI's role.** The only primary claim is the SBU's own statement [src:ssu-2025-pavutyna-video-41]. The Poltava museum training story is second-hand [src:kyivpost-2025-brown-ai-museum].
- **Aircraft present.** Pre-strike counts conflict, especially at Olenya: 2 Tu-95MS on 26 May (AviVector) against a Russian media claim of 40 Tu-22M3 and 11 Tu-95MS. The Belaya breakdown does not add up (52 versus 48).
- **Be-12.** The brief lists the Be-12 among the aircraft present. No source reviewed here reports a Be-12 at any Spiderweb base or among the aircraft hit. The SBU's own list is A-50, Tu-95, Tu-22, Tu-160, An-12 and Il-78 [src:ssu-2025-pavutyna-video-41].
- **Tu-160 and Il-78 damage.** Both are claimed by the SBU, but no imagery confirms them. Analysts think the Tu-160 fleet is largely intact [src:wikipedia-operation-spiderweb].
- **The A-50s.** At least one was hit on video. Whether either was operational, and whether the imagery shows new damage, is unresolved (§9).
- **The Ukrainka failure.** Was it a wiring or charging fault, sabotage, or an accident? The WSJ says only that "something provoked a fire" [src:nv-2025-semenova-wsj-spiderweb].
- **Not pursued here.** Claims of an Il-76 near Olenya and a Northern Fleet strike at Severomorsk were denied locally and not pursued. Bortnikov's later claim of British involvement appears only in non-English wikis and was not sourced here.
- **Weather and light.** The values are reanalysis, not station observations, and are not tied to a strike hour.
