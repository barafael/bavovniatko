## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Russia's Beriev A-50/A-50U Mainstay is its airborne radar picture of southern Ukraine. Ukrainian officials say it directed strikes on Ukrainian air defences and aviation and cued Russian missile and glide-bomb attacks [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment] [src:militarnyi-2024-a50-lost-eyes]. In early January 2024 Russia began flying it constantly, apparently because Ukrainian strikes were threatening Crimea [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment]. On the evening of 14 January 2024 Ukrainian air defence ambushed two Russian command-and-control aircraft over the Sea of Azov:
- an Il-22M11 airborne command post was hit at about 21:00 over the Strilkove area and limped to Anapa with a dead commander;
- the A-50U RF-93966 was shot down at about 21:10–21:15 as it entered its patrol zone off Kyrylivka [src:euromaidanpress-2024-zoria-a50-azov] [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims] [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns].

A US Army air-defence officer later said the shooter was a German-supplied, truck-mounted Patriot used in a "SAMbush" at the very edge of its kinematic range [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed].

Russia then moved the next A-50 orbit east, over land near Krasnodar [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy]. On 23 February 2024 a second A-50U was nonetheless brought down over Krasnodar Krai, about 200 km or more from the front. Ukraine attributed the kill to a refurbished Soviet S-200 under a joint Air Force–HUR (military intelligence) operation [src:pravda-2024-kravets-a50-s200] [src:twz-2024-newdick-second-a50]. Both kills came in Ukraine's best month for air defence since October 2022: 10 Su-34s, 2 Su-35s and the A-50 claimed in February [src:pravda-2024-february-13-aircraft]. For more than a week afterwards no A-50 was seen near Ukraine, and glide-bomb carriers "no longer dare[d] to fly too close" [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50]. Russian milbloggers blamed friendly fire both times [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment] [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment].

**Outcome the player must reproduce:**

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**The map** is the northern Sea of Azov: shallow, enclosed water bounded by the Russian-occupied Zaporizhzhia coast (Prymorsk, Berdiansk, Kyrylivka), the Arabat Spit and Henichesk to the west, Crimea and the Kerch Strait to the south-west, and Krasnodar Krai (Yeysk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, Kanevskaya) to the east. The Ukrainian-held front ran through the Zaporizhzhia steppe south of Robotyne, about 55 miles from the Azov coast [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims]. The A-50s flew orbits over the water and the coast, behind the front.

## 4. Forces

**Ukraine (Air Force Command, HUR)**
- **Command.** Commander-in-Chief Gen. Valerii Zaluzhnyi; Air Force Commander Lt. Gen. Mykola Oleshchuk; spokesman Col. Yurii Ihnat; HUR chief Lt. Gen. Kyrylo Budanov, with spokesman Andrii Yusov [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment] [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-second-a50].
- **Patriot.** The first batteries arrived in April 2023 [src:oryx-heavy-weaponry-supplied-ukraine], and Germany's second system had deployed by December 2023 [src:militarnyi-2023-kushnikov-three-su34]. By mid-2024 the US estimated "about a battalion" in Ukraine, some guarding static sites and some highly mobile. The German truck-mounted systems were the ones used to ambush the first A-50, after German–Ukrainian night mobility drills in Poland in April 2023 [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed]. The same arm claimed the 13 May 2023 Bryansk kills [src:liga-2023-dzheripa-bryansk-patriot]. Patriot launchers can fire remotely, well forward of the radar and control station [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov].
- **S-200.** A Soviet 1960s long-range SAM, restored from May 2022 by recalled operators and engineers [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50] and credited with the second A-50 and a Tu-22M3 at 308 km [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200]. A Russian court names the 138th Anti-Aircraft Missile Brigade and its commander, Col. Mykola Dzyaman [src:kyivpost-2024-dzyaman-court].
- **S-300.** The legacy force: in 2022, 35 S-300PS/PT divisions, 3 S-300V, 9 S-125 and 15 Buk-M1 [src:rusi-2022-zabrodskyi-preliminary-lessons]. By The Times's account, S-300 radars were used as bait [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50].
- **Fighters.** MiG-29 and Su-27 (the 40th Tactical Aviation Brigade over Kyiv in 2022) [src:kyivpost-2022-neplii-ghost-of-kyiv]. In 2024 they had no long-range air-to-air missile that could reach the A-50 [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns], and the F-16 arrived only in August 2024 [src:kyivindependent-2024-court-f16]. Su-24Ms struck Crimea and may have shaped the January geometry (Cooper's hypothesis) [src:nexta-2024-cooper-a50-trap].

**Russia (VKS)**
- **A-50/A-50U Mainstay.** Il-76-based, with the Shmel radar. Militarnyi gives detection of aircraft "up to 700 km", air-defence systems at 400 km and armour at 450 km, a crew of 15–16, and a home base at Ivanovo-Severny with the 610th Centre for Combat Use and Retraining [src:militarnyi-2024-a50-lost-eyes]. Ukrainska Pravda gives a crew of 19 (5 pilots, 11 radio engineers, 3 technical engineers) [src:pravda-2024-balachuk-a50-azov]. For fleet size see §1. Known A-50U board numbers include RF-92957, RF-50602, RF-94268, RF-93952 and RF-50610 [src:militarnyi-2024-a50-lost-eyes]. The lost aircraft were RF-93966 "Sergey Atayants" (14 Jan) [src:militarnyi-2024-a50-lost-eyes] and, by ASN's search listing, RF-50610 "42 Red" (23 Feb) [src:asn-2024-a50u-rf50610].
- **Il-22M11 (Il-20M family) airborne command post and radio relay.** Fewer than a dozen in service [src:theaviationist-2024-cenciotti-a50-il22]. One was damaged, RF-95678 [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns]. In the Russian C2 chain the Il-20M relays the picture from command posts to fighters and SAMs [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
- **Strike and escort aircraft.**
  - **Su-34 and Su-35S** fly glide-bomb runs and CAPs from bases such as Millerovo [src:pravda-2024-petrenko-a50-five-aircraft], and Su-30 crews fly over the Azov [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims].
  - **Su-34 losses:** Oryx confirms 42 Su-34s lost over the war [src:oryx-russian-equipment-losses].
  - **The ground layer:** S-400s in Crimea and Belarus [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war] and, by Russian claims, near Mariupol [src:militarnyi-2024-kushnikov-second-a50].
- **Industrial base.** The Beriev TANTK plant at Taganrog, 150–160 km from the front, is the only plant that repairs and modernises A-50s [src:armada-2025-oliver-berievs] [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-taganrog-a50]. The A-100 successor had still not entered production in 2026 [src:theaviationist-2026-satam-new-a50u].

**How the window changed the forces.** In mid-January the Russian fleet lost one A-50 and one Il-22M. From 17 January the orbits went east, over land [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy]. In late February a second A-50 was lost, and in March the A-50s were absent and the KAB carriers stood further back [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50]. On the Ukrainian side the S-200 crews stayed forward for a week before 23 February [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200]. In 2025–26 the remaining fleet was pulled deep inside Russia [src:united24-2026-khomenko-a50u-patrols].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **What the A-50 does.** It feeds early warning and vectoring against low-flying Ukrainian jets, and in 2022 it flew 2–3 sorties a day over Donbas and the south [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. Ukrainian officials say:
  - it directed strikes on Ukrainian air defences and aviation, and its loss "will at least postpone" missile strikes (Humenyuk) [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment];
  - it gave target designation for Russian aviation, kept round-the-clock border duty, and worked in tandem with Kinzhal-carrying MiG-31Ks [src:militarnyi-2024-a50-lost-eyes];
  - from Belarus it was "the primary type of jet that Russia uses to guide missile strikes" [src:euromaidanpress-2023-shandra-machulishchy].
- **Why it flew close, and what that cost it.** "The closer it flies to our coast, the further it can conduct radar reconnaissance in the airspace of our country" (Ihnat) [src:euromaidanpress-2024-zoria-ihnat-a50-crew]. An air-defence officer at West Point read the forward Azov orbit as urgent need (full coverage of the front, and perhaps the expected F-16s) and as a sign of Russian risk acceptance [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov].
- **The A-50's limits.** RUSI found Ukraine could degrade the A-50 "fairly easy" by electronic attack. Because the VKS is subordinated to the ground forces, its picture went via district or army command posts and Il-20M relays rather than straight to fighters or S-400s, which slowed the kill chain [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
- **The glide-bomb link.** A KAB has limited range, so carriers must come close. Without the A-50's warning of Ukrainian SAMs and fighters, Su-34s and Su-35s "can no longer feel free" [src:ukrinform-2024-ihnat-a50-sixth-day] and "no longer dare to fly too close" [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50]. The February spree hit aircraft on glide-bomb runs [src:kyivpost-2024-orlova-six-aircraft-72h].
- **The SAMbush (Patriot).**
  - **The move:** a mobile battery is moved forward to the edge of the front, fires at the limit of its kinematics, then leaves [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed]. Defense Express's model is a "wandering Patriot" 15–25 km behind the line [src:espreso-2024-defenseexpress-wandering-patriot].
  - **Emissions:** remote launchers can sit far forward of the radar. The intercept could also have been cued by passive sensors picking up the A-50's and Il-22's own emissions [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov].
  - **Bait and timing:** Cooper hypothesises S-300 radars switched on only briefly [src:nexta-2024-cooper-a50-trap], and The Times describes S-300 radars used as bait to draw Russian aircraft into Patriot range [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50].
- **The S-200 at long range.** It is a huge, old semi-active-homing SAM suited to big, slow targets [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov]. Ukraine restored it, missed once in autumn 2023 when the A-50 turned away, and learned to hide its launchers fast [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50]. It then waited forward for a week [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200]. Kyiv Post lists possible modifications (more fuel, new guidance, a forward illuminator), but they are undisclosed [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200].
- **Countermeasures seen.** In its last seconds the 23 Feb A-50 dropped infrared flares [src:twz-2024-newdick-second-a50] [src:militarnyi-2024-kushnikov-second-a50]. The A-50 that evaded in autumn 2023 changed course [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50]. The strategic countermeasure was geographic: moving the orbit east and inland, then deep into Russia [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy] [src:united24-2026-khomenko-a50u-patrols].
- **Battle damage assessment by beacon.** Oleshchuk published Cospas-Sarsat emergency-beacon positions of downed crews in the Sea of Azov as evidence of the kills [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-su34-beacon].
- **Kyiv 2022, the low-level fight.**
  - **Russian overmatch:** Su-35S and Su-30SM radars, and R-77-1 shots from over 100 km, outclassed the Ukrainian jets' semi-active R-27s, and Ukrainians were outnumbered up to 15:2. Ukrainian pilots flew "extremely low" to use ground clutter and terrain masking [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - **Who downed whom:** Buk TELARs used as solitary pop-up threats were the best Ukrainian SAMs against jets, and MANPADS punished the Russians when they went low [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
  - **Kyiv's counterpart to the A-50:** a Podlet-K1 radar in Belarus covered the Kyiv axis down to about 15 ft at over 150 km. Ukrainian low-level ambushes on high Russian patrols suggest it had low resolution or poor dissemination [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].

## 6. Set pieces

- **"Night over Kyrylivka" (14 Jan 2024).** A radar-only screen with total overcast and no moon. Two Russian tracks settle into orbits: the Il-22M off the Arabat Spit and the A-50 heading for its patrol box off Kyrylivka. At about 21:00 the Il-22 is hit and turns for Anapa. Minutes later the A-50 vanishes from the scope and stops answering, and a Su-30 pilot reports a fire falling into the sea [src:euromaidanpress-2024-zoria-a50-azov] [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims]. Ihnat's line that the Il-22 was hit while the A-50 was the target makes this a two-for-one moment [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment].
- **The shoot-and-scoot.** A German truck-mounted Patriot runs to the edge of the front, shoots at the kinematic limit and is gone before Russian counter-strikes arrive [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed].
- **Zaluzhnyi's flight tracks.** Two tracks "suddenly disappear" near Prymorsk and on the way back to Russia. It can serve as the debrief cinematic [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims].
- **"Bayan has done its time" (23 Feb 2024).** The S-200 crew has waited a week at the front [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200]. An A-50 that took off from Akhtubinsk at 15:50 radiates near Primorsko-Akhtarsk. At 18:45 its radar dies [src:pravda-2024-petrenko-a50-five-aircraft]. Dusk videos show flares, then a fireball over Kuban farmland [src:twz-2024-newdick-second-a50]. Afterwards five Russian jets over Millerovo are called home [src:pravda-2024-petrenko-a50-five-aircraft].
- **The beacon map (19 Feb 2024).** Oleshchuk posts emergency-beacon pings from the Sea of Azov: Russian rescuers report a Su-35 pilot "floating face down", with the Su-34 crew's beacon next to it [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-su34-beacon].
- **The Bryansk five minutes (13 May 2023), prequel.** Over Bryansk Oblast a Patriot downs a Su-34, a Su-35 and three Mi-8s in five minutes [src:liga-2023-dzheripa-bryansk-patriot].
- **Machulishchy (26 Feb 2023), prequel.** Partisan drones reach the A-50 parked 12 km from Minsk. A bag of drone remote controls is later found [src:euromaidanpress-2023-shandra-machulishchy] [src:wikipedia-machulishchy-attack].
- **Vignette: the air war over Kyiv (Feb – Mar 2022).** Ukrainian MiG-29s and Su-27s hug the ground under high Su-35 patrols, then pop up to fire. The deconfliction rule changes from time to area after friendly-fire losses on 3 March. From early March Buk and S-300 make the high sky lethal for the VKS, and MANPADS make the low sky lethal [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. The debrief reveals the "Ghost of Kyiv":
  - the viral clip was DCS World footage and the "photo" a 2019 MoD image;
  - the Air Force itself said the Ghost is "a superhero-legend whose character was created by Ukrainians", a collective image of the 40th Brigade's pilots;
  - the fallen Maj. Stepan Tarabalka "is not the Ghost of Kyiv, and he did not shoot down 40 planes" [src:kyivpost-2022-neplii-ghost-of-kyiv] [src:theaviationist-2022-cenciotti-ghost-of-kyiv].

  RUSI notes that the "viral pieces of footage" of air-to-air combat were "quickly recognisable as fakes created using commercial flight simulator software" [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Ukraine restores the S-200 (May 2022).** Ukraine chose to revive a retired 1960s system, find its old operators and rebuild launchers and missiles [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50]. The payoff came nearly two years later, at ranges Patriot could not reach [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200].
2. **Russia flies the A-50 closer (late 2023 – Jan 2024).** In November 2023 the UK MoD warned that Russia might accept more risk with the Mainstay [src:theaviationist-2024-cenciotti-a50-il22]. Constant sorties began in early January over the Crimea threat [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment]. MWI reads the forward orbit as urgent need and risk acceptance [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov].
3. **Ukraine pushes Patriot to the front (Jan 2024).** Using a scarce strategic SAM as a forward ambush weapon, rather than for city defence, was what the US officer called "stretching the very, very edges" of its envelope [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed]. The precedent was Bryansk in May 2023 [src:liga-2023-dzheripa-bryansk-patriot].
4. **The target order on 14 Jan.** Ihnat says the A-50 was the priority target and the Il-22M was hit in the same engagement ("caught in crossfire") [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment] [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns]. Sources say nothing of an alternative plan.
5. **Russia's response: move the orbit east (from 17 Jan).** Russia traded coverage for survival, "at the expense of their overall efficacy in Ukraine" (UK DI) [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy].
6. **Ukraine answers with range and patience (Feb 2024).** Rather than chase the new orbit with Patriot, HUR and the Air Force set an S-200 ambush and waited a week [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200]. They struck on 23 February, Russia's Defender of the Fatherland Day, a date HUR made a point of [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-second-a50].
7. **Russia grounds or withholds the A-50 (late Feb – Mar 2024).** It recalled strike sorties on 23–24 February [src:pravda-2024-petrenko-a50-five-aircraft] and flew no A-50s near the front for over a week. Oleshchuk reported pilots' "discussions" about being sent "one way" [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50].
8. **Ukraine goes after the repair base (Mar 2024 onward).** Ukraine struck Taganrog, the only A-50 repair plant, in March 2024 [src:pravda-2024-ivashkiv-taganrog], December 2024 [src:armada-2025-oliver-berievs] and November 2025 [src:euromaidanpress-2025-mukhina-taganrog-a50]. In June 2025 Spiderweb reached the home base at Ivanovo [src:kyivpost-2025-spiderweb-a50-ivanovo].
9. **Kyiv 2022 (vignette).**
   - Ukraine switched deconfliction from time to area after friendly fire [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
   - Russian pilots went low, then refused missions beyond the lines, and the VKS switched to night attacks from 9 March [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war].
   - On 30 April the Air Force chose to debunk its own myth after The Times misidentified the Ghost [src:kyivpost-2022-neplii-ghost-of-kyiv].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Official silence.** The Russian MoD did not comment on 14 January [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns]. UK DI read the eastward redeployment as "tacit Russian acknowledgement" of the loss [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy].
- **Friendly fire, twice.**
  - **14 Jan:** Russian Telegram channels (Rybar, Colonelcassad, Kotenok and others, as reported) blamed S-400s guarding the Crimean bridge [src:wikipedia-2024-a50-il22-shootdowns]. A self-described security insider called this a "duck" (a planted story) meant to reassure pilots that the Azov and Black Sea were still safe [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment].
  - **23 Feb:** Russian sources again blamed their own air defence, firing from the Mariupol area [src:militarnyi-2024-kushnikov-second-a50]. The milbloggers extended the friendly-fire claim to the recent Su-34 and Su-35 losses [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment].
  - **ISW's view:** if the A-50 really fell to Russian air defence, that would itself be "a calamitous failure" [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment].
- **Blame on commanders.** The aviation channel FighterBomber blamed commanders without the right background, published the Il-22's shrapnel damage and called its crew "real heroes" [src:isw-2024-01-15-assessment] [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims].
- **Scarcity.** Military Informant called the loss "a huge loss for domestic aviation" [src:twz-2024-altman-rogoway-a50-claims]. A milblogger noted that the A-50 and its specialist crews cannot easily be replaced [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment].
- **The court version.** As Kyiv Post reports it, a Moscow court framed the 23 Feb aircraft as patrolling over Krasnodar Krai, "not directly intended for combat operations in Ukraine" and unarmed, and charged Col. Dzyaman for an S-200 kill at about 300 km [src:kyivpost-2024-dzyaman-court].
- **Belarus.** Lukashenko denied the Machulishchy attack, then minimised it, then admitted the A-50 needed repair in Russia [src:wikipedia-machulishchy-attack].
- **Taganrog.** The Russian MoD claimed 47 drones intercepted on 9 March 2024, 41 of them over Rostov Oblast [src:pravda-2024-ivashkiv-taganrog].
- **2026.** FighterBomber publicised another refurbished A-50U handed over in May 2026 [src:theaviationist-2026-satam-new-a50u].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Radar-only diorama.** The map is the Azov basin at night, with track symbols and range rings. The player sees what Ukrainian radar and passive sensors see. The A-50 is itself a huge emitter, and passive cueing on its emissions is a sourced possibility [src:mwi-2024-mitchell-awacs-azov]. Each Ukrainian radar switch-on is a risk the player chooses.
- **Two different ambushes, two acts.**
  - **Act 1 (Patriot, January):** move a mobile battery to within about 15–25 km of the line [src:espreso-2024-defenseexpress-wandering-patriot], wait for the orbit, fire at the edge of the envelope and leave.
  - **Act 2 (S-200, February):** after Russia moves the orbit east [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy], only a long-range, one-shot-and-hide weapon reaches it, and the crew must wait days [src:kyivpost-2024-tuzov-s200].
- **Win band.**
  - **Required:** both A-50 kills, on 14 Jan and 23 Feb.
  - **Bonus:** the Il-22M damaged.
  - **Debrief:** the contested fleet sizes (3/6 to 10) and the 160–300 km range claims.
  - **Fail states:** the battery found and struck, or the A-50 turning away as in autumn 2023 [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50].
- **Pacing.** Long quiet waits (a week forward) are broken by a few minutes of engagement. Between the two kills, a glide-bomb pressure meter rises (Avdiivka falls on 17 Feb) [src:isw-2024-02-17-assessment], with the Su-34/Su-35 spree as interstitial targets [src:kyivpost-2024-orlova-six-aircraft-72h] [src:pravda-2024-february-13-aircraft].
- **Systemic payoff.** Killing or pushing back the A-50 should lower the effectiveness and boldness of Russian KAB carriers for a while [src:pravda-2024-romanenko-no-a50] [src:ukrinform-2024-ihnat-a50-sixth-day] and briefly reduce Azov tactical aviation [src:isw-2024-02-23-assessment]. Model this as a fading modifier, not a permanent one.
- **Russian AI levers.** Orbit distance versus coverage, which is the sourced trade-off [src:pravda-2024-ukdi-a50-redeploy]. Recalling strike packages when the AEW goes down [src:pravda-2024-petrenko-a50-five-aircraft]. Flares and evasive turns [src:twz-2024-newdick-second-a50].
- **The Kyiv vignette** plays as a short low-level dogfight-and-SAM tutorial with the "Ghost" debunk as its debrief [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war] [src:kyivpost-2022-neplii-ghost-of-kyiv].
- **Coda.** The A-50s retreat to Ulyanovsk and Samara by 2026 [src:united24-2026-khomenko-a50u-patrols]. This links to Spiderweb (chapter 16), where an A-50 at Ivanovo is a target [src:kyivpost-2025-spiderweb-a50-ivanovo].

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Which weapon on 14 January.** A US officer's public statement (Patriot) is the strongest attribution [src:twz-2024-newdick-patriot-a50-confirmed]. Ukraine never officially named the system for the first kill, and the friendly-fire claim persists in Russian sources. Was the Il-22M hit by the same battery? The exact missile type (PAC-2 GEM-T or other) is unconfirmed.
- **Positions and ranges.** No source gives the aircraft's coordinates at the moment of the hit. Range claims run from 160 to 300 km (§3). The time zone of HUR's 18:45 is unstated.
- **Identities and crews.** The 14 Jan aircraft is RF-93966 in Militarnyi, Wikipedia and ASN but "RF-50601" in Euromaidan [src:euromaidanpress-2024-zoria-a50-azov]. The 23 Feb identity (RF-50610) rests on a search-only ASN listing. The 14 Jan death toll runs from 11 to about 15.
- **Fleet baseline and what remains.** The counts are mutually inconsistent (§1). Spiderweb's A-50 damage is imaged but not quantified. The 2026 refurbished A-50U may be a returned airframe rather than a new one [src:theaviationist-2026-satam-new-a50u].
- **The Times's S-300 bait account.** It is known here only through Babel's summary [src:babel-2024-perepechko-times-a50]; the original article is paywalled and was not read. It is unclear which engagements the bait tactic applied to.
- **The "May 2024 Patriot ambushes on Su-34s and Ka-52s near the border".** No such event was found in Ukrainian or Western sources. The well-documented border Patriot ambush is **13 May 2023** over Bryansk (Su-34, Su-35, three Mi-8s) [src:liga-2023-dzheripa-bryansk-patriot]. The 2024 Patriot-linked Su-34 losses found are 22 Dec 2023 (south, suspected), the February 2024 spree (system not stated), and 1 Nov 2024 (Belgorod, a milblogger's guess) [src:kyivpost-2023-struck-feshchenko-three-su34] [src:kyivpost-2024-orlova-six-aircraft-72h] [src:kyivpost-2024-su34-two-majors]. The 2024 Ka-52 losses seen in search were to MANPADS, not Patriot. This needs a targeted pass if the chapter wants a 2024 helicopter ambush.
- **Machulishchy damage.** It is attested only by the partisans and by Lukashenko's eventual admission; the imagery was inconclusive [src:ukrinform-2023-machulishchy-satellite].
- **Weather.** Values are reanalysis, not station observations. Cloud base and the moon's altitude were not checked.
- **Kyiv 2022 numbers.** Air-to-air kill counts are uncertain because of overclaiming on both sides [src:rusi-2022-bronk-russian-air-war]. Tarabalka's date of death differs between sources (13 vs 23 March).
