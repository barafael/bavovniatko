# Operation Vivaldi

About mid-April – 30 September 2026 (with the Starlink cutoff of 29 Jan – 5 Feb 2026 and the southern counteroffensive as context). The former Russian salient north of Lyman, Donetsk Oblast, between the Nitrius and Zherebets rivers, from Lyman north to Nove and Novomykhailivka.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

From spring 2025 the Russian 20th Combined Arms Army (CAA) pushed a salient into the ground north of Lyman, between the Nitrius and Zherebets rivers. Its aim was to bypass Lyman from the north, reach Sviatohirsk and the Siverskyi Donets, and form the northern pincer of an envelopment of the Donetsk fortress belt (Sloviansk, Kramatorsk, Druzhkivka, Kostiantynivka) [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240] [src:isw-2026-09-12-roca]. In DeepState's polygons the Russian-occupied area in a 1,096 km² box around the salient grew from 60 km² (1 Mar 2025) to 350 km² (15 Jan 2026) [src:deepstate-api-history]. Ukraine's 3rd Army Corps under Brig. Gen. Andrii Biletskyi first stabilised the line and stopped the infiltration [src:twz-2026-altman-vivaldi]. Then, under "maximum information silence", it attacked the salient's northern flank at Nove, with the first visible attack on 22 May 2026 [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. Before and during the ground attacks it ran a drone and EW campaign: it blinded Russian drones, hit Rubikon's operators and launch sites, struck logistics 50 and then 120 km deep, and cut a diesel pipeline from Voronezh Oblast [src:isw-2026-09-15-roca] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting]. Its first-phase method was to kill the ATV and motorcycle reinforcements sent to stranded Russian infiltration groups, not to chase every infiltrator [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment].

By about 21 August the corps had cut across the salient to Kolodiazi. Most of the Russians inside escaped toward the Zherebets [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. Biletskyi revealed the operation only on 15 September, named after Vivaldi's *Four Seasons*: "Vivaldi, as we know, has four seasons, and this is only the first of them" [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi]. Three "seasons" were made public by 28 September [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**Map box.** The box is about 33 × 33 km: 48.95–49.25 N and 37.55–38.00 E, or 1,096 km² [src:osm-2026-vivaldi-geodata]. It runs from Lyman in the south to Novomykhailivka in the north, and from Korovii Yar and Oleksandrivka in the west to the Zherebets in the east. The salient sat between the Nitrius and Zherebets rivers [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. Its size is quoted as 10 × 10 km [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-trap] and as 100 km² [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. DeepState's occupied polygon in the box was much larger, 350 km² at its peak, because it included ground east of the Zherebets [src:deepstate-api-history].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **Preparation first.**
  - *Restoring command:* rebuild command, replace commanders, set up the surveillance-and-strike system and stockpile [src:twz-2026-altman-vivaldi].
  - *EW:* nine months of EW preparation to blind Russian drones [src:euobserver-2026-vasilko-vivaldi].
  - *An air-defence and EW "dome":* over the assault area, so Russian drones "could not read the operation's design" [src:euromaidan-2026-mukhina-vivaldi-ugv-drop].
  - *"Protective Drone":* Zhorin's EW system, credited with about 20,000 Russian drones [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-isw-drones-out].
- **Counter-drone first.**
  - *Rubikon as the priority target:* reconnaissance identified Rubikon as the unit able to isolate the area. A senior Russian commander pulled it back "just as the assault began" [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting].
  - *Launch sites:* the 60th Brigade hit Russian drone launch points at set times to open windows for the infantry [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-akhmet-stealth].
- **Middle strike on logistics.**
  - *Depth:* strikes went to 50 km, then 120 km [src:isw-2026-09-15-roca], and over 205 km into Luhansk Oblast [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-luhansk-skies]. Hornet drones hit river crossings more than 150 km deep [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi].
  - *The diesel pipeline:* strikes on both ends of the Voronezh–Luhansk pipeline forced Russia to dismantle it, and fuel now goes by truck on a 400 km run [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting]. Russian drone units could not refuel the generators that charge their batteries, and Russian EW ran in reduced mode [src:isw-2026-09-22-roca].
  - *Repair bases:* the repair base of the 20th CAA and the 144th MRD was pushed to Voronezh Oblast, a 250 km route [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting].
- **Kill the reinforcements, not the infiltrators.** Fires went into the ATV and motorcycle runs sent to stranded groups, which were then cleared by "strike-search" teams [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment] [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi]. Many infiltrators surrendered without food or water [src:euobserver-2026-vasilko-vivaldi].
- **C2 decapitation by robot.** Corps and brigade reconnaissance mapped Russian command nodes. Heavy bomber drones then dropped kamikaze UGVs onto them more than 10 km behind the line, forcing Russia to divert troops to guard its headquarters [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting]. The 93rd Brigade's "Desantnyk" uses the same marsupial idea [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-desantnyk-drop].
- **Robots first.**
  - *Demining:* UGVs cleared 30 km of routes in two nights [src:euromaidan-2026-vivdych-robot-demining].
  - *Bullspike UGVs:* armed UGVs fire into shelters and then detonate [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-bullspike-ugv].
  - *APC VBIEDs:* remote BTRs packed with TM-62 mines are driven into bunkers. The corps and Euromaidan say BTR-60, ISW says BTR-80 [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-btr60-vbied] [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].
  - *Robot share:* the corps aims to replace 30% of its infantry with robots by the end of 2026 [src:euromaidanpress-2026-mukhina-robots-30pct].
- **Limited mechanised manoeuvre returns.** Mechanised manoeuvre came back in a limited way once drone overmatch was won: armour entered Serednie with no losses [src:isw-2026-09-18-roca] [src:isw-2026-09-20-roca]. "Vehicles didn't win it, they showed up because the conditions… were already gone" (Vitaly) [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240].
- **Deception and OPSEC.** The corps kept an information blackout and used camouflage, disinformation and the season 3 feint [src:isw-2026-09-25-roca] [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].
- **The Starlink context.** Losing Starlink grounded Russia's deep strike drones and blinded its HQs but not its fibre FPVs [src:euromaidan-2026-axe-starlink-counterattacks] [src:euromaidan-2026-zoria-air-assault-south]. On 22 May only fibre-optic drones could still fly [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. No source ties Vivaldi directly to the cutoff [src:isw-2026-feb-26-assessment].
- **Russian counters.**
  - *Infiltration:* small groups and flag-planting, including flags flown in by drone [src:isw-2026-09-22-roca].
  - *Glide bombs:* on Izium and Raihorodok [src:isw-2026-09-17-roca].
  - *Blind bombing:* of windbreaks [src:isw-2026-09-25-roca].
  - *Reserves:* sent to hold the Zherebets [src:isw-2026-09-22-roca].

## 7. Decision points

- **Stabilise before attacking (2025 – June 2026).** Kudriashov: "our first task was not an offensive". The corps replaced commanders and rebuilt NCO chains, and only then shaped and attacked. Biletskyi's principle was that "a commander must be responsible for the entire cycle" [src:twz-2026-altman-vivaldi].
- **Silence.** The corps and the high command kept a blackout, and OSINT mappers held back until late August [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240]. Biletskyi complained that the attention "is not helping our operations" [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi]. Zhorin credits the silence for misleading the Russian leadership [src:isw-2026-09-17-roca].
- **Strike the reinforcements, not the infiltrators.** This set the season 1 method [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment] [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi].
- **Rubikon.** Ukraine chose to hunt the drone units first. A Russian commander chose to pull Rubikon to the rear to recover, "at what turned out to be the worst possible moment" [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting].
- **Russian false reporting.** Commanders' "beautiful reports" reached Putin (the Sviatohirsk claim) and stalled reinforcement decisions [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca] [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi]. On 12 Sep ISW set out the Russian options: redeploy from Lyman, or double down on Lyman to recreate the pincer [src:isw-2026-09-12-roca]. In the event Russia kept attacking toward Dobropillia, sent reserves to the Zherebets and shifted toward Mykolaivka [src:isw-2026-09-14-roca] [src:isw-2026-09-22-roca] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-thomas-vivaldi].
- **How far to go.** Biletskyi said the aim is "favourable defensive lines", not kilometres [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-barvinkove-pincers]. MWI argues the tactical win was not turned into operational exploitation [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi]. Thorkill says it will not change the Kramatorsk fight [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240].
- **The season 3 feint.** The main effort was put on Nove while Russia was shown Zelena Dolyna [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].
- **National level.** Fedorov's middle-strike and "Logistics Lockdown" campaigns and his push to switch off Starlink for Russian forces shaped the battlefield before his July removal [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi]. Kyiv then gave Biletskyi the wider fortress-belt command [src:isw-2026-09-01-roca].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **Win band.**
  - The salient between the Nitrius and Zherebets is eliminated, and the line ends near the Zherebets.
  - Lyman and Sviatohirsk are never lost.
  - Area retaken is 125–240 km²: the corps claims 125 km² after season 2 and 176 km² after season 3, against 151–250 km² from mappers [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240] [src:mwi-2026-rose-vivaldi].
  - Settlements per season as in section 1.
  - Russian losses in the low thousands, with about 250 prisoners.
  - The Serebrianka forest and the east bank of the Zherebets stay Russian [src:deepstate-api-history].
- **The lever is the reinforcement stream, not the infiltrators.** Model Russian infiltration groups as cheap, self-replenishing pockets fed by an ATV and motorcycle pipeline. Interdicting the feed (routes, depots, the diesel line, launch sites) makes the pockets starve, surrender and get cleared [src:isw-2026-09-26-assessment] [src:euromaidanpress-2026-zoria-vivaldi-targeting].
- **Drone overmatch as a gate.**
  - *The gate:* mechanised movement is suicidal until Russian drone launch sites, Rubikon and the EW fuel supply are degraded; then armour and VBIEDs can reach fortifications [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].
  - *Meters:* "Russian drone density" and "Russian fuel at the front", both driven down by middle strikes [src:isw-2026-09-22-roca].
- **An information layer.**
  - *Ukraine's track:* an OPSEC meter (announcing early costs momentum) [src:kyivindependent-2026-farrell-vivaldi].
  - *Russia's track:* a "beautiful reports" fog that delays its reinforcement decisions in proportion to how far the front has moved [src:isw-2026-09-28-roca].
  - *Debrief:* compare Putin's Sviatohirsk claim with the real front [src:isw-2026-09-19-roca].
- **Pacing.**
  - *Long build-up:* April to mid-August, with stabilisation, strike campaigns and probing raids such as 22 May; one tick per day is fine.
  - *Fast collapse:* from 17–18 Aug to 30 Sep [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-lyman-salient-240].
  - *Announcements:* the three public "seasons" are scripted on 15, 20 and 28 Sep, and can serve as the chapter's acts.
  - *Weather:* dry August and clear skies, then cloudier late September [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-lyman-2026].
- **Starlink as a prelude.** An optional opening act (29 Jan – 5 Feb) in which Russian deep drones and HQ links degrade by 20–40% while fibre FPVs carry on. Set the recovery curve at partial within 1–2 months [src:isw-2026-feb-26-assessment].
- **Robots.** UGV demining, the air-dropped kamikaze UGV and the APC VBIED are distinct unit types with clear trade-offs: cheap, no casualties, and dependent on Ukraine's EW dome holding [src:euromaidanpress-2026-axe-btr60-vbied].
