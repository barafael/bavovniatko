# Bradley vs T-90M near Stepove (vignette)

Probably 11 January 2024; the footage was published on 12 January 2024. Stepove, on the northern flank of Avdiivka, Donetsk Oblast. A tank-duel set piece inside chapter 11 (Avdiivka), with the wider record of Bradleys against Russian armour from November 2023 to February 2024.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

The 47th Mechanised Brigade was moved from Zaporizhzhia to Avdiivka in October 2023, and its M2A2 ODS Bradleys anchored the northern flank around Stepove [src:euromaidan-2023-havrylets-avdiivka] [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb]. A Russian T-90M "Proryv", Russia's most capable frontline tank, attacked near Stepove, where Ukrainian troops held a trench under tank fire [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner] [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts]. Two Bradleys took it on. The T-90M fired first and missed, and the first Bradley's run "didn't work out" [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90] [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner]. The second Bradley's gunner-commander, "Serhiy", closed in with the M242 25 mm cannon and fired armour-piercing rounds into the rear of the tank. When those rounds gave trouble, he switched ammunition and shot out its optics [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner] [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts]. Blinded, the T-90M popped smoke, backed away with its turret spinning and ran into a tree. Its crew bailed out, and an FPV drone finished the tank off [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].

**The outcome the player must reproduce:**
- The T-90M is **mission-killed by 25 mm fire** from two Bradleys and then **destroyed by an FPV**. The crew flees on foot [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
- The Ukrainian trench holds. No Bradley loss is reported in this engagement [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
- **Contested date.** The brief's "Oct–Nov 2023" is not supported by the sources:
  - Forbes reports the video circulating on Friday 12 Jan 2024 [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90].
  - Newsweek relays the War Archive channel's claim that it happened "on Thursday", i.e. 11 Jan 2024 [src:newsweek-2024-cole-bradley-t90].
  - The gunner says he had returned from Bradley training in Germany in December, so the duel cannot predate that month [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
- **Who finished the tank.** Forbes suggests the 25 mm fire killed its driver and commander [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90]. A master gunner says only that "might have" happened, and the second video shows the crew fleeing [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].

## 4. Forces

**Ukraine: 47th Separate Mechanised Brigade "Magura".**
- *Fleet:* the brigade was the main Bradley user, receiving about 190–200 M2s [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb] [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90]. It had lost about 30 by mid-December 2023 [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb] and at least 31 by January 2024 [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90]. It also had about a dozen Leopard 2A6s left [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove].
- *The crew:* the second Bradley was commanded and gunned by "Serhiy", a former infantryman, with driver "Oleksandr", who had driven for a supply unit. It was their second mission together [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner]. The first Bradley's crew is not identified.
- *Nearby units:* the 110th and 57th Brigades in the garrison [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove]; the 109th Territorial Defence Brigade's infantry near Stepove [src:twz-2024-situation-report-2024-01-22].
- *Other Bradley users at Avdiivka:* the 3rd Assault and 67th Mechanised Brigades are also reported using Bradleys there [src:euromaidanpress-2024-frontline-avdiivka-2024-02-15] [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-avdiivka-2023-12-05]. This contradicts Forbes's "sole user".

**Russia.**
- *The tank:* a single T-90M "Proryv" with a 125 mm gun, about 51 t, crew of three [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90]. Its unit is not identified in these sources.
- *The wider force:* two field armies with about 40,000 troops against about 10,000 defenders [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

- **M242 Bushmaster 25 mm.**
  - *Ammunition and rate:* it feeds AP (sabot) or HE from two ready boxes, with single or burst fire at about 200 rounds a minute. The gun is stabilised, so the reticle stays on target while the hull moves [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
  - *How it was used:* the experts read the crew as aiming at the rear (engine and fuel), then at the sights, for a mission kill. Doctrine prefers the TOW against tanks, and it is unclear why the crew did not fire one [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
- **Paired manoeuvre.** One M2 hammers the tank as it speeds past, and the second, running the other way, takes over at close range [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90]. The two Bradleys "trapped" the T-90 between them [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
- **Drones.** A drone observed the duel, and an FPV finished the abandoned tank [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
- **The wider pattern at Stepove.** Mines laid at night on the approaches, then drones, then Bradleys against whatever got through [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove]. There were far ambushes on MT-LB columns [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb], and machine-gun fire on infantry as it dismounted [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-avdiivka-2023-12-07].
- **Countermeasures on both sides.** The T-90M's smoke grenade discharge [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts]. The Bradley's own smoke while reversing [src:twz-2023-situation-report-2023-11-22]. Russian recovery of an abandoned M2 [src:twz-2023-altman-captured-bradley].

## 6. Set pieces

1. **The tank in the village.** A T-90M fires on a Ukrainian trench. The first Bradley's pass fails [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
2. **The second run.** Serhiy: "We fired with all we could." When the AP rounds give trouble, he goes for the optics: "I started blinding him so he couldn't leave" [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
3. **Spin-out.** Smoke, reverse, the turret spinning, a tree. The crew runs and an FPV dives on the tank [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
4. **Optional prelude: three MT-LBs in 30 seconds** at night outside Stepove [src:forbes-2023-axe-bradley-three-mtlb].

## 7. Decision points

- **Commit the second Bradley.** "There was another crew that came first. It didn't work out, so we had to muster up the courage and go" [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
- **Switch from AP to blinding fire.** The gunner changed rounds and aim point after a problem with the AP ammunition, choosing a mission kill over penetration [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
- **25 mm instead of the TOW.** This is debated: an expert says the TOW is the trained answer, "unless they were out". A master gunner says the 25 mm can be "just as good as the TOW in certain situations" [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **A duel of minutes**, with 2 Bradleys against 1 T-90M in a village with trees. The tank shoots first. The player sequences the two IFVs, picks AP or HE, aims at the hull rear or the optics, and decides whether to use the TOW [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts] [src:forbes-2024-axe-bradleys-t90].
- **The win band:**
  - a mission kill on the T-90M (optics or turret drive) with no Bradley lost;
  - the FPV coup de grâce as a scripted follow-up [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts];
  - the first Bradley's failed pass is historical and not a loss condition [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-gunner].
- **Model both kills.** Optics and turret drive fail separately from a hull penetration, and a blinded tank loses orientation and drives into obstacles [src:twz-2024-altman-bradley-t90m-experts].
- **Weather:** snow cover and overcast skies on about 11 Jan 2024 [src:open-meteo-2026-era5-stepove-jan-2024].
- **In the Avdiivka chapter**, Bradleys work as a mobile fire brigade: they react to a detected assault, suppress dismounts and hunt light armour [src:euromaidanpress-2023-frontline-avdiivka-2023-12-07] [src:forbes-2023-axe-21-tanks-stepove].
