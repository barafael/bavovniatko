# The Krynky bridgehead
Kherson Oblast, the Dnipro's left (east) bank between the Antonivskyi bridges and Korsunka; prelude Feb – Sep 2023 (island raids, the Antonivskyi bridge lodgement, the Kozachi Laheri raid), main window 10–17 Oct 2023 to 17 Jul 2024, coda to mid-Aug 2024.

Casualty, loss and strength figures are **claims**, so the claimant is always named. Russian statements are given only as ISW and Ukrainian outlets report them.

## 1. Summary and historical outcome

Ukrainian troops had raided the Dnipro islands since February 2023 [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless]. After Russia destroyed the Kakhovka dam on 6 June 2023, they held a small lodgement under the Antonivskyi road bridge, and ISW confirmed it on 27 June [src:isw-2023-06-27-roca]. On 8 August a boat raid struck Kozachi Laheri [src:isw-2023-08-08-roca].

The main operation was planned with British help. The newly created Marine Corps under Lt Gen Yurii Sodol carried it out through a temporary "Katran" group [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. It began around 10–16 October 2023, with three brigades on three footholds: the 36th at the Antonivka railway bridge and Poima, the 38th at the Pishchanivka–Pidstepne solar farm, and the 35th at Krynky [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. Within about two weeks the two western footholds were given up, and the effort narrowed to Krynky and the woods around it [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].

Ukraine first confirmed a "foothold" through Andriy Yermak on 13 November [src:isw-2023-11-14-roca]. The Marine Corps and the General Staff announced "several bridgeheads" on 17 November [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca]. The best depth reached was about 2.5 km from the river, in December [src:kyivindependent-2024-york-krynky-ongoing].

From winter 2023–24, Russia did four things [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]:
- massed glide bombs, FPV drones and fresh assault units (the new 104th VDV Division among them);
- learned to hit the boats at river bends and at the landing points;
- flattened the village;
- made the crossing a "one-way trip" for many [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell].

Brigades left in stages. The 36th went first, in winter, and the 35th last, in July 2024 [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. On 17 July the Defence Forces of the South conceded that the main positions in Krynky were "completely destroyed", while saying that fighting on the left bank went on [src:kyivindependent-2024-york-krynky-ongoing]. Wikipedia dates the battle 20 October 2023 to 17 July 2024 [src:wikipedia-battle-of-krynky].

RUSI's verdict is that the lodgement was never exploited, because a larger force could not be sustained across the river [src:rusi-2024-watling-offensive-lessons].

**The outcome the player must reproduce**

- **The foothold.** Hold a foothold on the left bank in and around Krynky from mid-October 2023 until about late June to mid-July 2024, roughly nine months.
  - The marines probably never controlled all of Krynky. They held about half of it [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - Soldiers put the bridgehead at 300–500 m to 2.5 km deep [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless]. A Russian milblogger claimed 10 km² on 16 November 2023 [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca].
  - The M-14, 5–7 km inland, was never reached [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **The withdrawal.** By mid-July 2024 the village is lost, and fighting moves to the islands and to positions north of Krynky [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell] [src:kyivindependent-2024-hodunova-krynky-new-positions].
- **Ukrainian losses (contested).**
  - Police data: 788 missing and 262 dead recovered and buried, October 2023 to end of June 2024 [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell] [src:euromaidan-2024-martyniuk-krynky-missing].
  - "More than 1,000" feared killed [src:telegraph-2024-barnes-krynky-1000].
  - UALosses, as of 21 June 2026: 306 named killed, 730 missing and 54 or 64 captured. The article gives both figures [src:wikipedia-battle-of-krynky] [src:ualosses-2026-soldiers].
  - One marine brigade lost about 700 dead and wounded [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Russian losses (Ukrainian claims).**
  - 1,216 killed and 2,217 wounded by mid-November 2023 (Marine Corps / General Staff) [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-marines-bridgeheads] [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca].
  - Russian losses "four to five times higher" than Ukraine's (Lykhovii) [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost].
  - Equipment: 58 Ukrainian and 271 Russian items to 14 June 2024 (OSINT researcher Naalsio) [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell].
- **Russia's claim.** Shoigu claimed "up to 3,500" Ukrainians killed on the east bank by February 2024 [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca].
- **The pinning effect (contested).** The Russian Dnepr grouping is said to have grown from about 64,000 in autumn 2023 to 100,000–120,000 by July 2024 (Viktor Kevliuk) [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost]. Oleksandr Kovalenko puts the number of Russian troops pinned at 30,000–40,000 [src:nyt-2024-meheut-krynky-lost].

## 3. The diorama: geography, terrain, weather

**The bounded map.** The map is a strip of the lower Dnipro, about 35 km long, from the Antonivskyi bridges near Kherson upstream to Korsunka. The planned assault frontage, from the rail bridge to Krynky, was about 30 km [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].

- **Right bank.** The Ukrainian launch bank: Kherson city and the villages opposite Krynky.
- **Middle.** A belt of islands and channels: the Konka, the Krynka and others.
- **Left bank.** A low, wooded and marshy fringe, then the M-14/E-58 highway and dry steppe beyond [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:isw-2023-11-14-roca].

**The water.**
- **The river.** The main river is "around a kilometer wide in its southern parts" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **The route.** The marines' route was longer than one channel. They crossed the Dnipro, landed on islands, walked "a few kilometres" across them, sheltered in holes, and then crossed more channels such as the Konka or the Krynka. One soldier felt it was "twelve and a half kilometres" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Crossing time.**
  - Before December 2023 a crossing took several hours to a full day.
  - From December, once the small rivers were cleared of mines, the route was continuous and took 40–50 minutes [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - The Telegraph gives "a 30 to 60-minute boat journey through open water" [src:telegraph-2024-barnes-krynky-1000].
- **The danger points.** These were the bends, where boats slow down, the Konka–Dnipro junctions and the landing points [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **The last leg.** In March 2024 marines walked 2–3 km of no man's land at night between the village and the boats [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].

**Terrain after the dam.**
- **The flood.** The dam was destroyed on 6 June 2023. About 600 km² of Kherson Oblast flooded, 68% of it on the Russian-held side, and the reservoir shrank from 2,155 km² to 509 km² by 20 June [src:wikipedia-kakhovka-dam-destruction].
- **Effects in the field.**
  - A Russian milblogger said the flood gave Ukrainian forces "freedom of movement" [src:isw-2023-06-27-roca].
  - Allies asked why the marines did not exploit the washed-out first Russian line, but the floods also eroded the islands and banks [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - The islands are swamp, craters and fallen trees, and a dug hole fills with water at once [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. "You can only go down half a meter before hitting water" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
  - Flood debris caught in the trees gave some cover from drones [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - Snowmelt partly re-flooded the reservoir upstream in March 2024 [src:defenceexpress-2024-kakhovka-refill].
- **Woods and mines.** There is dense forest on both sides of Krynky, toward Kozachi Laheri and toward Korsunka. Russian logistics behind them were "sound" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. Russian forces mined the area heavily [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca]. The low ground and woods keep Russian vehicles on the roads [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].
- **Roads.**
  - the Kozachi Laheri–Krynky–Korsunka road through the village [src:isw-2023-10-20-roca];
  - the Oleshky–Nova Kakhovka road, a drone hunting ground for the 501st Battalion [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in];
  - the E97 to Armiansk and the P57 from Hola Prystan to Skadovsk further south [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **Bridges.** Both Antonivskyi bridges are wrecked. Ukrainian strikes hit the road bridge in 2022, and Russia blew two spans during its retreat [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones]. No assault bridge was ever tried [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-retreat].
- **The village over time.** In Oct–Nov 2023 the houses still had roofs. By December the riverbank was "a mass of mud and splintered trees" [src:nyt-2023-gall-marines-suicide-mission]. By July 2024 it was "a pile of rubble" [src:pravda-2024-lykhovii-krynky-not-critical].

- No heavy equipment, except what the 38th brought [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. A Russian source claims the marines had only 80 mm mortars [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky]. Light mortars did cross later [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in].
- **Boats.** The Navy provided some. Most were bought by the brigades through volunteers.
  - The 36th had only eight boats of its own [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - Craft included "good US ones reinforced with inflatable pontoons" that self-inflate when holed. They survive shrapnel but not a direct FPV hit.
  - One inflatable from the Prytula Foundation cost 388,000 UAH (about US$9,400).
  - At first boats carried 8–10 people. After losses, three-person boats took over [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Electronic warfare.** There was "hardly any" EW on the boats, only a large EW system protecting the landing points [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Cargo drones.** The Prytula Foundation bought eight hexacopters (20 kg payload) and 40 "Mamont" 10-inch FPV carriers. Each of the four brigades got two hexacopters and ten Mamonts. They carried ammunition, food, water and even blood [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Fires.** Artillery and HIMARS fired from the right bank [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in] [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca]. Sodol promised that "HIMARS will fire like machine guns". A 36th Brigade source says the promise was not kept [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].

## 5. Systems and tactics in use

**Russian.**
- **Glide bombs.**
  - Su-34s dropped glide bombs on Krynky from about 50 km away, beyond Ukrainian air defence in the sector [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky].
  - The UMPK kit gives FAB bombs a range of 30–90 km, typically 60–70 km [src:japcc-2026-zanier-glide-bombs] [src:rusi-2025-watling-third-year].
  - Glide bombs flattened much of the village from winter 2023–24 [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold], and they hit Marine positions "with near-impunity for months" [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-retreat].
  - A reconnaissance soldier on KABs: "the least scary because you understand that they will hit, and you won't feel anything" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **FPVs and recon drones on the crossings.** Russian drones "are always on the hunt" and boat motors are loud. Spotted boats draw FPVs or mortars [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
  - By March 2024 Russia launched up to 80 FPVs a day at Krynky, including a new winged long-range FPV [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].
  - From late winter, fire at the river mouths and landing points meant boats were hit on approach or on exit [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Mortars and artillery on the landing points.** It "usually takes a few minutes" for a landing to be passed to mortar crews [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless]. The landing points were among the deadliest places [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Other strike weapons.**
  - Iskander missiles hit troops massed at the rail bridge [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
  - The TOS-1A thermobaric launcher was used at close range [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in].
  - Drones dropped anti-tank mines on positions [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell] and, in summer, incendiaries [src:kyivindependent-2024-hodunova-krynky-new-positions].
  - Marines reported gas attacks [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell]. Kyiv Independent reports "prohibited chemical weapons" among the tactics [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost].
- **Assaults.**
  - "Human wave" assaults without armour (Humeniuk, Feb 2024) [src:kyivindependent-2024-krynky-human-waves].
  - Seven to eight attacks a day, by groups that shrank from 6–7 men to 3–4. The assault groups included VDV and Spetsnaz (a Ukrainian platoon commander) [src:isw-2024-07-18-roca].
  - By late July 2024, 2–4 assaults a day, sometimes a dozen (Lykhovii) [src:pravda-2024-lykhovii-krynky-destroyed].
- **Defence in depth.** The Russians did not mass their forces at the very front [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca]. They had "elaborate dugouts" and mines, and an "exceptionally fortified" line east of Krynky [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].

**Ukrainian.**
- **Small-boat night crossings and casevac.** Disembarkation was drilled (the 501st recce dropped life jackets because taking one off on shore costs about 30 seconds). "Camels", or "logisticians", waited at the shore to guide new arrivals [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold]. Men keep apart so that "at least one of them survives to call in an evacuation" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **Hobby and FPV drones.** Ukraine had "particularly effective use of small attack uncrewed aerial vehicles" (UK MoD) [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-uk-intel-krynky]. One example was a drone carrying an RPG-7 warhead that hit a vehicle 7–8 km inland [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in].
  - Brovdi claims 2,900 Russian drones downed or jammed since October and about 77% of incoming drones defeated. Some were hijacked and flown back [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].
- **Counter-battery and deep fires from the right bank.** HIMARS struck the 35th MRB column at Hladkivka [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in]. A regimental commander said Russian long-range artillery was "heavily suppressed" by December [src:nyt-2023-gall-marines-suicide-mission].
- **Drone resupply** to the left bank by hexacopters and Mamonts [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **What was missing.** Air defence against glide-bomb carriers, enough boats, EW on the boats, long-range fires and armour [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless]. The US aid delay of early 2024 "likely only aggravated the problem" [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost].

## 6. Set pieces

- **The night crossing.** Inflatables and three-man boats run a 40–50 minute route (from December) through channels and islands, with loud motors and Russian drones overhead. The boats must slow at the bends, the most dangerous points, and land where mortars follow within minutes [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **The Iskander strike on the rail-bridge embankment (~19 Oct 2023).** The 36th Brigade asked for a chain of 10–15 supply posts along a 3.5 km embankment with swamp on both sides. It was told to keep pushing to Poima. Iskanders, air strikes and tank fire then cost it 90 killed or wounded [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **The first landing in Krynky (27 Oct 2023).** Thirteen men leave a three-man boat and sink waist-deep in silt. There is no guide. One marine shouts to the next crews to follow his voice. They take over abandoned Russian trenches and fight street by street toward Kozachi Laheri [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **The woods.** Marines hold the forest on both flanks for up to two months, under branches in the cold. The forest floor is strewn with Russian dead [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **The FPV-struck evacuation (11 Dec 2023).** A wounded marine's boat is hit. He swims one-armed to an island and paces the shore, soaking wet, from midnight to 06:00 so as not to freeze [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **Shoigu's flag (20–21 Feb 2024).** Shoigu reports Krynky cleared. A clip shows two Russian soldiers raising a flag at the edge of the village and then hurrying back. Ukraine's Southern Command says "this information is not true" [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca] [src:kyivpost-2024-shoigu-krynky-denial].
- **The cellars.** By March 2024, 60–100 marines live underground through the daylight drone hours and come out at night to meet the boats [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].
- **The last swim.** When no boat can get through, drones drop life vests to the last men, who swim back [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].

## 7. Decision points

1. **Holding the marines back after the dam (Jun 2023).** Officers say the landing was planned for summer 2023 and postponed after the dam was destroyed. The Marine Corps went to Velyka Novosilka instead. Allies asked why the marines did not exploit the washed-out first line. UP reads the move as a way to "break in" the new brigades on land [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
2. **The objective and the plan (Sep–Oct 2023).**
   - The maximum objective was Perekop, about 80 km away. The minimum was the left-bank line and the M-14 (sources in the 36th Brigade and the Rada defence committee) [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-krynky].
   - The plan gave three brigades on foot 3–4 days for a 30 km strip, with no second echelon.
   - A brigade commander says he "wouldn't have undertaken this operation" and that it "came from political leadership" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
   - Ukrainian higher command never passed the marines' spring 2023 equipment list on to partners [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
3. **Concentrating on Krynky (late Oct – early Nov 2023).** Unable to hold three footholds, Katran gave up the rail-bridge and solar-farm footholds and reinforced Krynky [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
4. **Going public (13–17 Nov 2023).** Yermak announced the foothold in Washington. The Marine Corps and General Staff then declared bridgeheads, while Humeniuk urged "informational silence" [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-marines-bridgeheads].
   - Soldiers and analysts saw a political motive. Kofman said the operation "appears to have political objectives" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
   - Kastehelmi said it became "more politically motivated" by winter [src:nyt-2024-meheut-krynky-lost].
   - The General Staff's stated aim was to push Russian guns out of range of the west bank [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca].
5. **Russian command changes and reinforcement (Oct–Dec 2023).** Teplinsky reportedly replaced Makarevich, and the 104th VDV Division was committed [src:isw-2023-10-29-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2023-fornusek-104th-vdv-krynky]. Russia chose massed fires and attrition rather than early mechanised counterattacks [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky].
6. **No bridging.** Commanders never tried to bridge the Dnipro. Analysts say Russian bombers would have destroyed any attempt [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-retreat].
7. **When to leave (winter 2024 – Jul 2024).** The withdrawal was gradual: the 36th went first, in winter, and the 35th last, in July [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
   - Most UP sources think the operation ran "at least several months longer than it should have". One officer: "We had to withdraw in spring at the latest, during the foggy season" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
   - Kastehelmi: "a fair question to ask if the operation should have been ended faster" [src:nyt-2024-meheut-krynky-lost].
   - A 35th Brigade company commander defends holding the bridgehead as preventing Russian landings on the right bank [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell].
8. **Accounting after the withdrawal (Jul 2024).** The General Staff did not confirm the withdrawal officially [src:euromaidan-2024-tril-krynky-withdraw]. It later called its loss data a "state secret" [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
   - MP Solomiia Bobrovska said she would put Krynky before the Security, Defence and Intelligence Committee, saying that personnel had gone "without being given a proper task" and "without the appropriate support" [src:unn-2024-bobrovska-krynky-committee].
   - Families say their brigades stonewalled them [src:rferl-2024-krynky-missing-marines] [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell].
   - One interviewee took part in interrogations in a criminal case against his commander for disobeying an order [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].

## 9. Russia's side as reported

- **Concern, then blame.** From 18 October 2023 one prominent milblogger led alarmed reporting of an "offensive across the Dnipro" and accused the MoD of ignoring the threat [src:isw-2023-10-18-roca]. Milbloggers blamed Makarevich for the repeated landings [src:isw-2023-10-29-roca].
- **Putin's framing.** On 18 October Putin called the activity the "next counteroffensive" and a failure [src:isw-2023-10-18-roca]. In December he framed the defence as luring Ukrainians in to attrit them [src:isw-2023-12-16-roca], and said Russian troops had pulled back "several meters" [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless].
- **Saldo's denial.** Kherson occupation head Saldo denied the Kozachi Laheri landing, likely to avoid panic [src:isw-2023-08-08-roca].
- **Command failures.** Russian sources reported poor coordination, too few drones, weak artillery support and unevacuated wounded in the 810th Brigade [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky]. They also reported desertions from the 144th MRB (a General Staff claim) [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-marines-dug-in].
  - A pro-Kremlin channel: the Ukrainians "have no chance of expanding the occupied bridgehead" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky].
  - A pro-Russian blogger: "constant attacks by small aircraft (hobby drones)... There are a lot of them" [src:kyivpost-2023-korshak-glide-bombs-krynky].
- **The February claim.** Shoigu claimed that Krynky was cleared and up to 3,500 Ukrainians killed, and called it the end of the 2023 counteroffensive. ISW judged the claim premature and timed for the March election [src:isw-2024-02-20-roca]. Milbloggers accused him of lying to Putin [src:isw-2024-02-21-roca]. A Kremlin claim in March that the whole left bank was cleared angered milbloggers, who called it fake [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-retreat].
- **The July claims.** On 17 July Russian sources claimed the Ukrainian withdrawal from Krynky before Ukraine acknowledged it. They also reported small-arms fighting on the Dnipro delta islands [src:isw-2024-07-17-roca].

## 10. Game/sim relevance

- **The win band.** Keep a presence on the left bank from mid-October to about late June–July 2024 and withdraw before total loss. Do not take the M-14.
  - Score Russian forces pinned against the 64k → 100–120k range [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost].
  - Score Ukrainian losses against the 262 recovered / 788 missing police band [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell].
  - Holding longer than history, or with fewer losses, sits at the margin. A breakout to Perekop sits outside the band.
- **The core loop is boat logistics.** Each night has a limited boat count, with 8–10 seats falling to 3 as craft are lost. The resource chain runs crossing time → river-bend exposure → landing-point exposure, and the wounded take priority for seats [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:slidstvo-2024-stanko-krynky-hell]. Cargo drones (20 kg hexacopters) are an unlock in the volunteer economy.
- **Terrain rules.** Marsh cells cannot be entrenched (water at 0.5 m). Shelter means cellars, craters and flood debris. Vehicles can only use the roads. The islands are a transit layer that cannot be fortified [src:kyivindependent-2023-terajima-dnipro-hopeless] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold].
- **A scripted Russian escalation curve.**
  - Oct: weak second-line units.
  - Nov: the 810th and 144th counterattacks, plus glide bombs.
  - Dec: the 104th VDV.
  - Winter: FPV saturation and fire on the crossings.
  - Feb: the Shoigu information event.
  - Spring onward: boats cannot get through.
- **Light and weather.** Night is the only crossing window and daylight is drone-lethal. The 26–27 Nov storm and January frost (25 frost nights) drive exposure casualties. Spring fog is a withdrawal window. Summer heat drives incendiary fires [src:openmeteo-2026-krynky-weather] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:kyivindependent-2024-hodunova-krynky-new-positions].
- **The political meter.** Public announcements (Yermak, 17 Nov) raise allied-perception points but lock in the commitment. Holding longer lifts the meter and adds to the missing count, and the debrief shows the 788/262 data and the Bobrovska committee question [src:unn-2024-bobrovska-krynky-committee].
- **Pacing.** The prelude raids play as tutorial vignettes. Act 1 (Oct–Nov) is a three-foothold manoeuvre. Act 2 (Dec–Feb) is the attritional hold. Act 3 (Mar–Jul) is managed decline and extraction.

## 11. Open questions/gaps

- **Exact withdrawal date.** "Several weeks" before 16 Jul 2024 (Suspilne). The last reported ground combat was on 11 Jul. Wikipedia uses 17 Jul. No official date exists [src:euromaidan-2024-tril-krynky-withdraw] [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-retreat].
- **Ukrainian losses.** Official figures are a state secret. There is no breakdown of losses on the river versus in the village, or by brigade beyond "about 700" for one. The UALosses captured count is 54 or 64 [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:wikipedia-battle-of-krynky].
- **Russian losses after November 2023.** Only Lykhovii's ratio and Naalsio's equipment count exist. The November claim is variously printed as 1,216 or 1,126 KIA, and as 14 "watercraft", "boats" or "aircraft" [src:euromaidan-2023-zoria-marines-bridgeheads] [src:isw-2023-11-17-roca] [src:isw-2023-12-16-roca] [src:kyivindependent-2024-ostiller-krynky-lost]. The boats reading fits the Marine Corps release.
- **The Antonivskyi footholds.** Mashovets's 9 Nov claim of continuous control from the rail bridge to the road bridge conflicts with UP's account that both western footholds were abandoned by early November. A March 2024 return to the road bridge is reported only by Russian bloggers and Saldo [src:isw-2023-11-09-roca] [src:pravda-2024-kyrylenko-krynky-untold] [src:kyivpost-2024-korshak-krynky-drones].
- **Boat types and counts.** No full inventory: hulls, motors and losses per month. "US boats with pontoons" is not identified by model.
- **Russian strength figures** (64k → 120k) are expert estimates with no stated method.
- **Weather.** Fog days and river state (current, ice) were not sourced. The temperatures are modelled.
- **Timing of the raids before Krynky.** The Feb–Jun 2023 island raids and the unit roles in the summer raids are thinly documented. The best sources are UP and KI soldier testimony.
- **The parliamentary follow-up.** It is not known whether the defence committee ever held the hearing Bobrovska proposed.
- **Chemical weapons.** Testimony only, with no OPCW finding sourced here.
