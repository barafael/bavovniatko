## The choice

Three episodes from the dossiers fit "humans vs drones vs other drones" best.

**A. The road into Myrnohrad (c15, November 2025 – January 2026).** The player is a drone team of the Myrnohrad
garrison, inside a pocket that one road and a corridor 1–3 km wide connect to the rest of the front (c15 §3 "the
pocket", §6.9). Nothing on wheels gets through in clear weather: the road is under Rubikon's fire control almost
constantly, ammunition, food and fuel come by ground robot and heavy bomber drone, and the wounded go out by robot,
when they go at all (c15 §5). Russian infantry seeps in by twos and threes, ten a day in sun and thirty to forty in
rain, and goes for the drone crews first (c15 §5 "infiltration"). Fog grounds both sides' drones and is the only
cover under which our vehicles reach the town (c15 §5 "fog"). This is the era in its purest form: the kill zone
that by January was about 30 km deep (c15 §5), men on foot against drones, robots instead of trucks, fibre-optic
FPVs that EW cannot touch (c15 §2, 16 Jan 2025; 01 e8), and the drone units hunting each other (01 e8: over a
quarter of Rubikon's strikes on Ukrainian drone teams). It is the campaign's row 12, "the drone line, then the road
into Myrnohrad" (`campaign.md`). **Engine:** most of the parts exist: the drone and its thermal channel, the FPV and
the FPVs as a stock, the drop, the EW field, the enemy staff with its Orlans and Lancets, posts with ammunition,
areas held, night and flares, Supply's Columns and the evacuation runs, OSM maps with buildings and spoil heaps.
What is missing is the heart: their drones as a force over the ground, infiltration as a flow, robots, and fog in
the battle rather than only in the picture. **Sources:** the richest dossier of the era (coordinates, ERA5 weather
by day, units, rates of infiltration by weather, robot payloads and the bombers' limits). Thin: no published name
for the corridor's road (§3 says only "roads through Rivne and Svitle"), no Ukrainian losses, no order to withdraw
and no fall date but ISW's inference (§11).

**B. Operation Vivaldi (c19, May – September 2026).** The player is the 3rd Army Corps' strike and drone cell:
blind their drones with nine months of EW, kill Rubikon's operators and launch sites, strike the ATV and motorcycle
runs that feed the stranded infiltration groups rather than chase the groups, then drive armour in once the
drones are beaten (c19 §5, §10). It is the era's other face, the side that won the drone fight, and the
campaign's limited win (row 14). Drones fought drones at its core: about 20,000 Russian drones neutralised by the
corps' claim, and Rubikon withdrawn (c19 §1). **Engine:** it needs everything A needs, turned around (their drone
density as a target, a reinforcement pipeline, UGV demining and air-dropped UGVs, APC VBIEDs), and its scale is a
33 × 33 km salient over five months (c19 §3): a map-table chapter more than a field diorama; only the Nove raid of
22 May is a field day (c19 §6.1). **Sources:** strong on method, thin on the ground: the start date is contested
from mid-April to August, the July narrative contradicts itself, DeepState shows nothing in the box until 14
September, the Russian order of battle and strength are unknown, and the loss claims do not add up (c19 §11).

**C. The Kupiansk pipeline (c17, August 2025 – January 2026).** The player is Khartiia's search group's drone
eyes: the pipe's exits are hidden, moving spawn points; a drone that finds one and fire that holds it drive its
throughput to almost nothing (about 600 tried in 100 days and eight got through), then the town is cleared block
by block (c17 §6, §10). It is humans against drones at their starkest, and the campaign's recovery beat after
Pokrovsk (row 13). Drones against drones are a side note (UGVs lasting three or four missions against dedicated
hunter crews, c17 §5; Molniya motherships only in March 2026, c17 §2). **Engine:** the flow of infiltrators and
the drone-watched kill zone are A's new pieces; the urban clearance needs the town builder at block scale.
**Sources:** the exit points were never published, the force inside is given as 40 to 500, and the date the pipe
was cut as 21 October or 12 December (c17 §11).

Not chosen as candidates: the night over Kyiv (c18) is drones against drones with no front-line humans, and it is
the campaign's finale with a radar-and-city engine of its own; the road of death out of Sudzha (c14 §6.9) is the
kill zone's prototype of early 2025, before the fibre layer was mature; the Black Sea (c13) is a sea chapter.

**Recommendation: A, the road into Myrnohrad.**
- It is the owner's description almost word for word: the deep kill zone, infiltration in pairs and threes,
  logistics by robot and by night, fibre beating EW, Rubikon hunting the drone crews, and fog as the one weather
  that matters (c15 §5).
- It is a bounded, fixed outcome with a clear Record: the town is lost slowly and the garrison is not encircled
  (c15 §1, §10). Winning means holding as long as history did, at the margin.
- It reuses Avdiivka's engine (the turned map, the spoil heaps, the season, the drop, the FPV stock, the areas) and
  Hostomel's (posts with ammunition, night, thermals, flares), and Command's Supply and Columns become the robots.
- What it builds is what B and C need next: their drones as a field (B inverts it into a target, C uses it as the
  exits' kill zone), infiltration as a flow (C's pipe is a spawn of it; B's groups are its stranded end), robots,
  and fog. Build A first, then C and B reuse it, as Robotyne reused Vuhledar.
- Its sources are the best of the era's dossiers, and where they are thin the plan says so.

The cost: it is the campaign's bleakest field chapter, with no order to withdraw (`campaign.md`, "where harsh was
chosen"). It must be followed by something clean, which C is (`campaign.md`, recovery beats).

## Context

The battle for Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad runs from July 2024 to February 2026 over a 45 × 45 km box (c15 §3). This plan
builds its last act on one map: the corridor between the two towns, Rivne and Svitle, and Myrnohrad's western
quarters, from the November fog to the January frost. The summer drone line, the Dobropillia wedge and the fight in
Pokrovsk itself are listed at the end as not now.

What the engine already has, and where this plan takes it from:
- the drone with its thermal channel (I), the feed's analogue and thermal modes: Vuhledar, Hostomel;
- the FPV in the goggles, the FPVs as a daily stock, the drop with the day's wind: Robotyne, Avdiivka;
- the EW field (jammers on the ground, the link fraying past the jam line): Robotyne;
- the enemy staff (Spotted, Orlan passes, Lancets, the guns, KABs through `Act::Bombs`): Command, Avdiivka;
- posts with ammunition, return fire, a dry post pulling out, areas held by whoever is in them: Hostomel, Avdiivka;
- night, flares, the night's lights, sight shortened in the dark (`fight::Seeing`): Hostomel;
- Supply, Columns from a road head with the way's length through the grey zone, orders over the net: Command;
- the evacuation runs and the wounded calling for help (`evac.rs`): Robotyne;
- OSM maps with buildings, the turned box (`Anchor::bearing`), `Spoil` heaps on a `Mound`, the seasons: Avdiivka.

What it adds, each piece reusable by later chapters:
- **fog in the battle**: one visibility for men, drones and FPVs, from light and weather together (Avdiivka's grind,
  Kupiansk, Kursk);
- **their drones as a force over the ground**: crews with launch points, RF and fibre FPVs, waiters on the
  shoulders, a hunting preference, nets (Kursk's road, Kupiansk's exits, Vivaldi as the target);
- **infiltration as a flow** of twos and threes that hide in ruins and go for the drone crews (Kupiansk, Vivaldi,
  Avdiivka's grind);
- **robots**: the UGV as a Column on Supply with cargo, the casevac by robot, the heavy bomber's sorties, and a
  new view, the robot's own camera (Vivaldi's demining and air-dropped UGVs, Krynky's hexacopters);
- **drones against drones**: our interceptors against their reconnaissance wings, our strikes on their launch
  points and relays, EW domes on both sides (the night over Kyiv, Vivaldi);
- **a pocket**: the enemy on three sides and our way in through one gap (Avdiivka's neck, Kursk's salient).

**The pocket's shape.** Our way in is the H-32 from the west (c15 §3: "in December only roads through Rivne and

### 1. The map and the set
- Buildings from their footprints (`look/buildings.rs`): Myrnohrad's blocks at their storeys where the OSM has them
  (nine nine-storey blocks tagged), the rest by footprint; the villages' houses, which the map has none of, placed
  along their streets; the Tsentralna headframe over the shaft node and the heaps (Avdiivka's `Heap` on the map's
  two spoil outlines); the disused spur; the woods and fields.
- **A pocket.** The grey zone and the enemy's side become polygons in the file (Command's data already plans
  `grey_zone: [polygon]`, `command.md`), so a position, a route's grey length and the staff's spotting stop
  assuming one front at +z. Where the older days use `defence_z`, `foothold_z` and `jam_z`, the polygons are built
  from them, to the bit.
- **Light and weather:** the sun for 48.30 N on days 314, about 338 and 20 (UTC+2), the moon for III; ruins
  smoking; snow cover in II and III with Vuhledar's winter palette.

### 2. Fog in the battle
- **One visibility.** `fight::Sight` (the night's) becomes `Seeing` (Bevy has a `Visibility`): daylight × weather, asked by the posts'
  rifles, the attackers' return fire, the observers, the staff's eyes, the drone's picture and the FPVs. The file's
  `fog: [(from, to, metres)]` sets the weather's term through the day; the look's `DistanceFog` follows the battle's
  value instead of a constant.
- **What fog does** (c15 §5 "fog"): men see a few hundred metres; the drone's day camera sees little and its thermal
  some; light FPVs do not fly (c15 §3), heavier and fibre ones fly worse; vehicles drive. It hurts the side that
  relies on drones and helps the side moving on the ground, both ways.
- **The lift.** The fog thins on its schedule, and the net says so before it goes.
- Reused by: Avdiivka's grind, Kupiansk's 20 November, Kursk's overcast days, Vuhledar's real 6 February.

### 3. Their drones over the ground
- **`bav_sim::killzone`.** Their drone crews are entities at launch points beyond the +z edge and the jaw (named
  for Rubikon, Sudny Den, the "Irish", the Archangels, c15 §4), each with a kind (RF FPV, fibre FPV, a waiter that
  sits on a road's shoulder until something passes, c15 §5), a tempo by light and weather, and a reach (the whole
  box: fibre reached 40–60 km by 2025–26, 01 e8, e9).
- **What they hunt.** A sortie goes for what their picture holds (the staff's `Spotted`, which already fades) and
  what its own camera finds near the road. Preference by the record: drone teams first, then EW, then vehicles and
  robots, then men (01 e8: over 25% of Rubikon's strikes on drone teams, 15% on EW; c15 §5: "in a day they torched
  eight of our cars"). An FPV is drawn from launch to impact, so the player can see it coming.
- **What stops them.** An RF FPV in one of our EW domes loses its link (`ew.rs` turned to our side: domes of
  200–300 m at posts, 02 e8); a fibre one does not, but its cable can snag and, in III, the ice stops the spool
  (02 §6). Nets over a stretch of road make a vehicle under them harder to hit until the FPVs cut through (c15 §5:
  "helpful, but enemy drones simply need to find the entrance"; by October they cut the netting). Fog grounds the
  light ones.
- **Rubikon as a scripted arrival** of tempo, not a unit to beat (c15 §10). In the Record the tempo is the day's;
  in Command the staff spends it.
- Reused by: Kursk's road of death, Kupiansk's exits, Vivaldi with the roles reversed.

### 4. Infiltration as a flow
- **`Act::Seep`**: from a stretch of an edge, groups of 2–3 at a rate per hour that the weather multiplies (about
  10 a day in sun, 30–40 in rain or fog, c15 §5), walking along belts, ravines and ruins (c15 §3) to a goal, then
  going to ground in a building, a **burrow**, as a hidden post (Hostomel's posts, with a few rounds and a radio).
- **What they do there.** They go for our drone crews first, at close range so the crews cannot launch (c15 §5);
  the second radio corrects their guns onto what they see (c15 §5 "two radios": a burrow feeds the staff's
  `Spotted`). A group of three is sent expecting two to die (c15 §5).
- **Seeing them.** Cloaks lower the thermal picture's chance at range, and moving cancels most of it (02 §6,
  partial masking). The drone, the observers and the posts find them; the drop, the FPV, the bomber and a post's
  fire kill them; in Command a search-and-strike team clears a burrow (c15 §5: joint SSO, HUR, SBU and police teams;
  "Kedr, we're your own").
- **The areas** (Avdiivka's): Svitle, Rivne, the western quarters, the Tsentralna mine, the south-eastern edge.
- Reused by: Kupiansk (the pipe is a `Seep` with a hidden, moving mouth), Vivaldi (the stranded groups), Avdiivka's
  grind.

### 5. Robots
- **The UGV** as a vehicle kind (a tracked cart: 500 kg, 10 km/h, 20 km range, c15 §5 the Zmiy), sent as a Column on
  Supply from a road head to a position. Its **cargo** is ammunition for a post's `Ammo`, food and water, fuel and
  batteries for a drone team (c15 §5: keeping drone teams in fuel took many sorties), or a **wounded man** back:
  `evac.rs`'s calls for help become a queue that only a robot can empty (c15 §5 casualty evacuation). Its link
  frays in EW and can drop for a long time (the Zmiy lost it for 1.5 hours, c15 §5); it sticks in craters and mud
  and cannot cross water (c15 §5 "limits").
- **The robot's camera** (key 7): the player can drive one, or leave it to its route.
- **The heavy bomber** (a Vampire, 01 §3: about 15 kg over 20 km, about a dozen sorties in its life): a sortie
  from our drone team's position, mostly at night, with a bundle for a post or 60 mm bombs to drop (the drop with a
  bigger blast); 120 mm is too heavy (c15 §5).
- Reused by: Vivaldi (demining runs, the UGV air-dropped from a bomber, c19 §5), Krynky's hexacopter resupply,
  Kupiansk.

### 6. Drones against drones
- **Their reconnaissance wings become things in the sky.** The staff's Orlan pass turns from a strip into an
  aircraft on a path (Orlan, Zala, a Molniya over the road), which spots as before. **Our interceptor** (an FPV in the
  goggles, or by itself when radar-cued) can bring it down: interceptors wore the Orlans down from 2024 (01 e7), and
  by March 2026 claimed 33,000 Russian drones (01 e9). Russia had interceptors of its own from mid-2025 (02 e8): a
  rule for the staff, later.
- **Their launch points and relays are targets.** An FPV or a 155 mm mission on a crew's launch point stops its
  sorties for a while; the HUR's strike on a Rubikon base had limited effect (c15 §5, §10), so it is a lever, not a
  win. Every emitter shows (02 §6, "emit and die"): our domes and our drone team's ground station too, which is
  what the existing `team_found` and M already model.
- **Our EW blinds our own drones** inside its domes (02 §6, the friendly-fire toggle): a dome over the road is
  switched off for our FPV to fly through (02 §6: EW pauses scheduled for a strike).
- Reused by: the night over Kyiv (interceptors on Shaheds), Vivaldi.

## Risks
- **The killzone is the heart and the hardest.** Hundreds of sorties a day must be legible (the player sees each
  one coming), cheap (entity counts; `perf.md`'s budget) and deterministic (their order in the frame). If the player
  only watches robots die, the margin is not legible: the domes, the nets, the fog's timing and the interceptors must
  each visibly move the count.
- **The flow** is many small groups pathing through ruins; the burrow's rules (when they move, when they fire) set
  the band, and the detection model must be partial, never a switch.
- **The pocket** touches every system that assumes the enemy at +z; phase 1 makes the polygons reproduce the old
  values to the bit before anything else.
- **The clock.** Hours of rates (groups an hour, sorties an hour) compressed into about 25 minutes of play, as every
  diorama does, but here the rates are the content.
- **Tone.** The town falls with no order; III's Record is thin. The debrief must count and not comment
  (`campaign.md`, psychology).
