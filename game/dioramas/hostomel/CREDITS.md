# Hostomel: map data

The roads, taxiways, railway, tree rows, land cover and building footprints in `osm.ron` are made by
`tools/osm/osm2ron.py` from an Overpass extract (`tools/osm/data/hostomel.json`, 2026-10-19) of
OpenStreetMap.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL):
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

OpenStreetMap shows the place as it is now. Buildings named as built after 24 February 2022 are dropped by
the script, and the rest is checked by eye against what the sources describe (design/hostomel.md).
